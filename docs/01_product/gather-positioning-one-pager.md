# Gather — Positioning One-Pager

## Who

My customer is Kate. 45, organizing the family Christmas lunch for up to 40 family members. She’s always done it and its assumed she will this year too.

## Problem

She carries the emotional, relational, and reputational weight that comes with organizing a complicated gathering and making it a success. Last year, the spreadsheet and massive WhatsApp thread didn’t work. She believed it was her fault apparently. (More importantly, there was no potato salad.)

## Solution

An ai enabled platform that separates Kate from the thinking/planning/dreaming from the doing/communicating/chasing.

## Difference

It is different because it puts Kate in complete control. She’s the hero, she never spends her family goodwill. The app is the annoyingly organised assistant who not only gets Kate’s ideas out of her head into a comprehensive plan and also has no problem chasing, harassing badgering family members mercilessly.

## Why

They would choose it to actually enjoy the organizing, avoiding performance anxiety and actually being able to enjoy the event.

## Monetise

We will charge per event and the number of invitations to be sent.
