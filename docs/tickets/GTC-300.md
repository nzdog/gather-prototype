# GTC-300 — `isValidNZNumber` rejects Australian numbers that `sendSms` routes to TNZ

**Status:** Open — filed, not executed. Filed 2026-09-13 | **Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [ ] High [ ] Medium [x] Low — proposed at filing; the founder's to set.

Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] No

**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from [[GTC-189]] *Founder answers — the slice 1 flags*,
answer 3, in the founder's words:

> +61 NUMBERS — leave as built, and flag it as a defect rather than a decision. isValidNZNumber
> rejecting Australian numbers while sendSms routes them to TNZ is a real inconsistency, and the

**Confirmed by reading the code, NOT reproduced** — reproducing it is the executor's first step, and
Stop Condition 3 applies if it does not reproduce.

**Prior record — named, never filed.** [[GTC-229]] audit C records that `normalizePhoneNumber`
rejects `+61` and mangles AU local format into a bogus NZ number, and [[GTC-288]]'s *Do not touch*
says a widening of `normalizePhoneNumber` / `isValidNZNumber` *"is its own ticket"*, noting 13 call
sites and two test files pin current behaviour. This is that ticket, for the predicate. Audit C is
the capture and inbound half, and is cited below rather than re-derived.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
One answer to *"can this number be texted?"*, and it agrees with what `sendSms` will actually do.

**Actual — three answers.**

- **`sendSms`** in `src/lib/sms/send-sms.ts` accepts any E.164 number (`isE164`), routes `+64` and
  `+61` to TNZ (`TNZ_COUNTRY_CODES`, `shouldUseTnz`) and everything else to Twilio, which
  `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` lists as optional.
- **`isValidNZNumber`** in `src/lib/phone.ts` accepts `+64` followed by 8 to 10 digits and nothing
  else. It gates `findNudgeCandidates` (the direct auto-nudge skips the person),
  `findProxyNudgeCandidates`, `chooseManualNudgeChannel` (the host's nudge falls to email), and
  [[GTC-189]] slice 1's `chooseAskRoute` and `chooseChaseRoute` in
  `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` (dark; the number reads `PHONE_UNUSABLE`).
- **`findDecideByFollowupCandidates`** in `src/lib/sms/decide-by-eligibility.ts` accepts any
  non-null `phoneNumber` and leaves validity to `sendSms` — *"deliberately not isValidNZNumber,
  which rejects the +61 numbers send-sms.ts routes to TNZ on purpose."* The workaround is in the
  tree, commented, and is the evidence that the predicate is the defect.

So one Australian guest would get the decide-by follow-up by text, never an auto-nudge, a host's
nudge by email, and under the press a line on the host's list.

**Smallest failing case**
`isValidNZNumber('+61412345678')` is `false`, and `sendSms` with `to: '+61412345678'` routes to TNZ.

---

### The same root, further in (by reading, as of the slice 1 commit)

- **Capture.** `normalizePhoneNumber` in `src/lib/phone.ts` validates through `isValidNZNumber`, so
  it returns `null` for a `+61` number. `PATCH` in `src/app/api/p/[token]/contact-info/route.ts`
  refuses with 400; `PATCH` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/[personId]/route.ts` writes
  `phoneNumber: null`. The host UI refuses first — `AddPersonModal` and `Moment1InputForm` say
  *"International numbers not supported yet. NZ numbers only."*
- **⚠ Opt-out, Zone 7 adjacent — latent, because the route is.** `POST` in
  `src/app/api/sms/inbound/route.ts` normalises the sender through `normalizePhoneNumber` and, when
  that returns `null`, answers 200 and records nothing, so a STOP from a `+61` number would write no
  opt-out. [[GTC-229]] section D records that route as effectively dead — it reads Twilio's form
  fields and nothing sends it any. **The live form of this bullet is [[GTC-288]]'s TNZ inbound
  writer: if it normalises the sender the same way, the gap arrives with it.** The two opt-out facts
  themselves are [[GTC-301]].
- **Width.** `normalizePhoneNumber` has thirteen call sites (counted in the `From` note of
  `ENVELOPE_PROVENANCE` in `src/lib/sms/tnz-webhook-envelope.ts`), so changing it is a wider change
  than changing the predicate.

### Why Low, proposed

- **Latent in the data.** gather_dev (measured 2026-09-13) holds 0 `Person.phoneNumber` values
  starting `+61`, 9 starting `+64`, and 3 in local `021…` form, which every one of the three
  definitions rejects (a separate question, not this ticket's).
- **Latent at capture.** The UI refuses international numbers, so today an Australian number
  reaches the senders only through a fixture, a script or the API.
- **Not closed, because each new reader picks a side.** Slice 1's chooser is the latest; slices 3,
  5 and 8 will read it.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- `isValidNZNumber` in `src/lib/phone.ts`, or a successor predicate that answers *"can `sendSms` text
  this?"*, and its readers: `findNudgeCandidates`, `findProxyNudgeCandidates`,
  `chooseManualNudgeChannel`, and the chooser's `askChannelOf` and `chaseChannelOf`.
- `findDecideByFollowupCandidates`, to read the same predicate and drop its workaround comment.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` are binding. **Zone 7:** the inbound STOP
  path is named above and is not this ticket's to change without explicit approval.
- `TNZ_COUNTRY_CODES` and `shouldUseTnz` — the routing is the reference the predicate should agree
  with, not the thing to move.
- `normalizePhoneNumber`, unless Unknown 1 is answered first.
- The chooser's behaviour beyond reading the fixed predicate. Its `[DEFECT GTC-300]` assertion in
  `tests/channel-chooser-test.ts` is **expected** to change with this fix; that is where it should
  change.

### Unknowns

1. **Whether capture should accept `+61` at all.** The UI copy says NZ only. That is a product
   question, and it decides whether `normalizePhoneNumber` changes or only the predicate does.
2. **Numbers outside NZ and AU.** Twilio is optional per `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`, and
   [[GTC-189]] ruling P records *"cannot"* as a deployment fact. Whether a predicate should consult
   provider configuration is the question `chooseManualNudgeChannel`'s own docstring (GTC-214)
   answered no to.
3. **TNZ delivery to Australian numbers on this account** is not verified.
