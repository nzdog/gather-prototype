### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-10 at `ebdfe41`)

The questions are `src/components/plan/Moment2Step1Modal.tsx`. The setup page returns that screen
alone at its stage, so nothing else is on screen with it. Top to bottom: the occasion question and
the event's details; "Find a dish" ([[GTC-373]]); a "Food" group (Dietary requirements L763, the
"📝 Notes" box L772, then the food sections L782); "Show N more food categories" (L859) and the
sections it reveals (L872); an "Other jobs" heading (L921) over "🛠️ Set up", "🧹 Clean up" and
"📋 Other" (L945); and a bar fixed to the foot of the screen (L956 to L980) holding the way back
and "Generate plan →". The scroll area leaves `pb-32` (L670) for that bar.

**Notes (41).** `NotesAccordion` (L1184) is the "📝 Notes" box: id `other`, stored as `otherNotes`,
no "Still deciding?" link ([[GTC-364]] item 18). [[GTC-363]] (item 25) renamed the old "📝 Other"
box and lifted it to just after Dietary (his ruling then: "Rename and lift the existing box, with
leftovers as the example"). Where it sits on screen does not change what the AI reads: the text
goes into the plan request as "Other food notes from Kate" (`src/lib/ai/prompts.ts` L695).
"📋 Other" is a different box, the last of the three under "Other jobs". Pinned by
`tests/walkthrough-batch1-test.tsx` 25a to 25c (its words, its hint, "just after Dietary
requirements and before the first food section", its id and `otherNotes`) and
`tests/walkthrough-batch2-test.tsx` 18a and C9 (no "Still deciding?" link).

**Entrée & Starters (42).** The food sections are drawn in one fixed order, held in two copies:
`OPTION_TREE_FOOD_CATEGORIES` in the questions (L112 to L122: Mains, Entrée & Starters, Sides &
Salads, Dessert, Cake, Alcoholic Drinks, Non-Alcoholic Drinks, Table Snacks, Breakfast & Brunch)
and `FOOD_CATEGORY_ORDER` in `src/lib/ai/plan-categories.ts` (L32, "Order in which food categories
appear in the modal (and so in the plan)"). The second is read by `src/lib/ai/plan-input.ts` L173,
which lists the categories handed to the AI, and the AI is told "CATEGORIES TO GENERATE (in this
order)" (`prompts.ts` L721); `regenerate-plan/route.ts` L29 uses it only as the set of valid keys.
The plan's sections are `Team` rows: `applyPlanSections` (`src/lib/ai/plan-write.ts` L105 to L127)
gives a new team the next `displayOrder`, so teams are created in the order the AI returns them,
and keeps a team it finds by name where it stands; Moment 3 sorts its categories by that stored
`displayOrder` (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/setup/page.tsx` L110). The plan view and the other screens
that list the plan are for the plan to trace. Cowork's reading, for the plan to confirm or
correct: a plan drafted after the change comes out in the new order and every screen that reads
the stored order follows it; a plan already drafted keeps the order it has. A third list,
`ALL_CATEGORY_KEYS` in `GuidedPlanBuilder.tsx`, belongs to the old dashboard's modal, which is
[[GTC-381]]'s. Where Entrée & Starters sits on screen today (read from
`src/lib/ai/plan-option-tree-config.json`, ten event kinds): second, after Mains, for the five
kinds that show it up front with Mains (Christmas, adult birthday, wedding reception, engagement
party, anniversary); first and alone for a baby shower (Mains is behind "Show more"); behind "Show
N more food categories", below every up-front section, for BBQ, Easter and farewell (Mains is up
front there); and for a child's birthday, where both are behind "Show more".

**Select all (43).** There is none in the questions. Every list is drawn by `OptionTree`
(`src/components/shared/OptionTree.tsx`). In today's config a section's first list is styles
("What style of dessert?"), and each style that is ticked opens its own list of dishes right under
it ("Which desserts?", grouped under the style); a few sections have only the first list (Cake:
"What type of cake?"), and Alcoholic Drinks adds a third ("Anything else to consider?"). No list
anywhere has more than six options. Every list in today's config is tick boxes (the component draws
radios if a list says `multiSelect: false`; none does). Each tick goes through one function
(`emit`, L80) that unticks a style's dishes when the style is unticked (`withoutOrphanedChoices`,
[[GTC-364]]), then saves after half a second and ends "Still deciding?" for that section; "Find a
dish" ([[GTC-373]]) ticks through the same path. A select-all has to do the same.

**The summary (44).** Only Dietary's row says what was picked, open or closed ([[GTC-364]]: for
example "Vegetarian, Nut allergy" or "No dietary needs"). The food sections' rows show their names,
so what is ticked inside a closed section is out of sight, and nothing sums up the picks before
"Generate plan →". The picks live in the screen's own state: `dietaryData`, `mainsData`,
`extendedCategoriesData[section].selections` (list number to ticked options and the Other box's
text), `stillDeciding` for each section, and the free texts (`otherNotes`, `setUpData`,
`cleanUpData`, `otherJobsOtherData`). The button is in the bar fixed to the foot of the screen, so
"above" it is either the foot of the scrolling content, just clear of the bar, or inside the bar.

This is Cowork's first pass, not a complete one.
