# GTC-479 - Multi-day: Moment 1's sessions card, and who comes to which session

Base: origin/main 3d74d6d (24 Nov 2026, the merge of pull request #120,
GTC-478). The founder's multi-day rulings are in [[GTC-476]]; the foundation
(the session order, the "not at this session" table, the session routes and
the `GATHER_SESSIONS` switch) is [[GTC-478]]. In the code a session is a `Day`
row; the screens say "session" (Q1). All dates and times are NZ time.

Everything here is behind `GATHER_SESSIONS`. With it off, nothing a host or
guest sees changes. Nothing in Moments 2, 3 or 4, the guest page, the send,
the nudges, the decide-by clock, the board or the drafting changes: those are
GTC-480 to GTC-482.

## The plan (as approved, 24 Nov 2026)

**Decision 1, the date rule.** Two forms of a bare date exist: the seed stores
a session at NZ midnight ("25 Dec" held as 24 Dec 11:00 UTC); GTC-478's
routes, the demo copy and an event made at checkout store midnight UTC ("25
Dec" held as 25 Dec 00:00 UTC). Both read as the right day in NZ time, but
they are 13 hours apart. The rule:

- A session's date is a calendar date. Writers store it at midnight UTC of
  that date, as GTC-478's routes already do.
- Every reader turns it into a date through ONE helper,
  `src/lib/sessions/dates.ts`, which takes the NZ calendar day of what is
  stored. That reads both forms correctly, so no existing row changes: no
  data change, no migration.
- Anything that compares, groups or sorts sessions by date uses the helper's
  answer, never the stored time. Three narrow edits to GTC-478's routes: the
  days GET sorts by it (its reply unchanged), the days PUT's date-order check
  compares by it, and the session PATCH's "has the date changed" test
  compares by it.
- Every later ticket reads session dates through the helper.

What reads a session's date today: the days GET, PUT and PATCH (above); the
coordinator page's list (sorts by stored time, shows names; left as it is,
a note for GTC-480); the guest page, the host's team page and the coordinator
page (show the name only); the two text exports (print the first date on the
server with no time zone; a housekeeping finding); the AI drafting (the
number of sessions only); templates (saving drops dates, making uses the
event's start); "invites only" (keeps what is stored); the decide-by (counts
from the event's start today; for GTC-480, Q8 must turn the helper's date
into a moment in one place, never use the stored time).

**Decision 2.** With the switch on, the card shows on every event's summary,
one-day or not.

**Decision 3.** The card starts empty (everything for the whole event, as
today). On an event over 2 to 14 dates, an empty card offers "Add one session
for each date": one session per date, named by its date ("Thu 24 Dec").

**Decision 4.** Every named person chooses (adults, guests, kids with jobs).
Kids without jobs are a number on the household, so they come with their
household; the card says so.

**Decision 5.** Marking someone as not at a session where they are down for a
dish on that session: the server answers with the dishes and writes nothing;
the card warns; only "Mark as not coming" writes the mark. Nothing about the
dish changes. Before and after the send. Dishes for the whole event never warn.

**Decision 6, the words.**

The card:

- Heading: "Sessions"
- Empty, an event over more than one date: "Is this more than one meal? Add a
  session for each one, like Christmas lunch or Boxing Day dinner. Until you
  add one, everything is for the whole event."
- Empty, a one-day event: "Lunch and dinner on the day? Add a session for
  each. Until you add one, everything is for the whole event."
- Button: "Add one session for each date" (names each "Thu 24 Dec")
- Button: "+ Add a session"
- The form: labels "Session name" and "Date"; placeholder "e.g. Christmas
  lunch"; buttons "Save session" and "Cancel"
- On each session: its name, its date ("Thu 24 Dec"), buttons "Edit" and
  "Remove"; on two or more sessions sharing a date, "Move up" and "Move down"
  (read aloud as "Move Christmas lunch up")
- A session outside the event's dates (Q11): "Outside the event's dates"
- Remove, asked first: "Remove Christmas lunch?" buttons "Remove" and "Keep it"
- Remove refused (GTC-478's words): "This session still has items. Move them
  to another session or to the whole event first."
- GTC-478's other route words, shown as the route gives them.
- A save that fails: "That didn't save. Please try again."

Who comes to which (shown once there are two or more sessions):

- Heading: "Who comes to which session"
- Line: "Everyone comes to every session unless you say otherwise."
- On each household, titled by its main contact's name: "Coming to: every
  session"; or "Coming to: Christmas lunch, Boxing Day dinner" when everyone in
  it comes to the same ones; or "Coming to: it differs by person"; or "Coming
  to: no sessions"
- Buttons: "Change" and "Done"
- Opened: each named person, with one tick box per session, labelled by the
  session's name. Ticked means coming.
- With kids without jobs: "Kids without jobs come with the household."
- The dish warning: "Sam is down to bring the pavlova to Christmas lunch. Mark
  Sam as not coming to Christmas lunch anyway? Nothing changes about the
  dish." Buttons "Mark as not coming" and "Keep as coming". (Two dishes: "the
  pavlova and the ham".)

The new route's words: "That person is not on this event", "That session is
not on this event" (GTC-478's), "Say whether they are coming to this session".
