# GTC-448 - A copy deleted by its visitor is never cleaned up

> Filed by Claude Code on 1 Nov 2026 as a stub, from the founder's
> ruling on GTC-442's question 3 that day. Branch feat/demo-public.
> Planned, not built. The sample does not go public (GTC-447) until
> this is fixed.

## Why

GTC-442's clean-up finds an expired copy by its event: a sample event
whose host's account is on the made-up copy domain. A visitor can
delete her own copy (DELETE /api/events/[id] admits the event's host).
Once she does, the clean-up never finds it again, so her demo-copy
account, the host Person "Sam", her session (until it expires, and its
row after), and every person she typed (each stamped with the deleted
copy's id, GTC-441) stay in the database for good. W58 tells her
"Only you can see your sample. It, and everything you type into it, is
deleted the next day." For her that is untrue.

## Scope (to plan, not decided)

- Options to plan: a second pass in the hourly sweep that finds
  demo-copy accounts and stamped people whose copy event no longer
  exists, past the same age as GTC-442's rule, with the same skip
  guards (a WrapUpLink; a person with a membership on another event; an
  account that is not a copy's made-up address).
- How "past the same age" is read once the event row is gone (the
  account's or the people's own creation time) is for the plan.
- W58 stays as approved.

## Zone stops

- Zone 2: deleting copy accounts and their sessions, on GTC-269's
  reading, as GTC-442 does. Asks first at the build.
