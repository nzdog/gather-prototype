## Decisions made while building

- Item 1, the openings: the strip goes inside each page's centred
  column, directly above the title, in an `mb-6` wrapper (the plan view's
  and Moment 3's spacing). The top row is left exactly as it is (on a
  computer it sits at the top of the overlay, on a phone in its own room
  above the column), so the strip is under it on both. On a computer the
  column is centred, so the strip sits with the title block.
- Item 1, the summary: `currentMoment={1}` with no completed Moments,
  as Moment 1's form draws it, and `doorsFor(1)`: "What's the plan?" is
  then a door forward (the same rule as "On to the plan →") and "Who's
  coming?" is the current Moment.
- Item 2: the opening's way back ("← Back to the plan") goes to the
  plan screen as the working screen's own does (her approval stays). It
  sits in its own file, so the setup page's overlay count is unchanged.
- Item 3: `jumpMoment1ToTop` was read and not reused: it scrolls Moment
  1's overlay through a ref the page holds, while Moment 3's working
  screen is not an overlay (the window scrolls there), and the one place
  that knows whether she landed on one dish is the view's own arrival
  effect. So the jump is one `else` there: `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`.
- Item 5: while a folding step is open its header's tick slot stays,
  empty (same width), so the row and its "Show"/"Hide" cue do not move;
  the box sits at the foot of the open content, right-aligned, under a
  thin rule. The arrival (`goToStep`) still brings the step's top to the
  top of the screen and focuses and rings its box, now found at the
  foot. On a long step that box may sit below the screen when it is
  focused (the focus does not scroll). A question for his eye: keep it
  so (default), or let the arrival bring the box itself into view.
