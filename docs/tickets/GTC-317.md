*Filed on founder instruction, 2026-09-18, at the [[GTC-191]] shape proposal: "The reminder
offer and the §3 list follow slice 5, and the reminder becomes its own ticket — you are right
that it is a new outbound path wearing a button."*

meets. It proposes no shape.

---

### What it is — Hinge §3

> The ask is closed; the response is open: after a **yes**, the assistant makes its first offer
> — *want a reminder closer to the day?* Jake's to take or leave, costing nothing to decline. A
> requested reminder is the guest inviting Gather's voice back — it serves, where a nudge
> chases. This is also the only lever the product will ever have on Kate's deepest unknowable:
> *will they actually bring it.*

Two things in that passage are load-bearing and neither is a UI detail:

- **It serves, where a nudge chases.** The reminder is invited. Everything else outbound in the
  tree is sent because the system decided to send it.
- **Declining costs nothing.** Which means the decline must not be a second decision the guest
  has to make — the shape of the offer is the substance of it.

---

### Why it is not [[GTC-191]]'s, and not a precondition of [[GTC-189]] slice 5

- **It fires after a yes. The press produces no yes.** Ruling D item 3's rule is that the press
  must not send a link to a page that cannot answer what the message asks. This page will
  answer the ask with or without the offer.
- **It is not a screen change.** A requested reminder is an outbound message and needs
  everything an outbound message needs.

---

### What it meets — measured against the tree, 2026-09-18 at `87a35ef`

**Nothing of it exists.** No column, no route, no scheduler, no sender, no UI. A grep across
`src` and `prisma/schema.prisma` for reminder-offer machinery returns nothing.

What it would have to reach, named so the scoping meets it rather than discovers it:

- **Storage.** There is nowhere to record that a guest asked for one, or when it is due.
  `PersonEvent` carries `nudgeMark` and the attendance columns; neither is this.
- **A sender.** `src/lib/email.ts` carries `sendMagicLinkEmail`, `sendNudgeEmail` and
  `sendWelcomeEmail` and no reminder sender. `sendSms` in `src/lib/sms/send-sms.ts` is the text
  side.
- **A schedule.** "Closer to the day" is a clock. The three cron routes in `src/app/api/cron/`
  are the precedent for how one runs; none of them is this.
- **A channel decision.** `chooseAskRoute` and `chooseChaseRoute` in
  `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` are the two that exist, and the reminder is neither
  an ask nor a chase — it is invited, which is a third thing. Whether it gets its own chooser or
  borrows one is a ruling, not an implementation choice.

⚠ **DO-NOT-TOUCH ZONE 7 (SMS opt-out).** The reminder sends a message, so `Person.smsOptedOut`
and the `SmsOptOut` row both bind. The narrow question: **does an opted-out guest who asks for
a reminder get one?** Ruling O in [[GTC-189]] settled the analogous case for the chase — *"a no
to one channel is a no to being chased"* — but a reminder is asked for, which is the one thing
a refusal of unsolicited contact does not obviously cover. **Not ruled. Do not decide it in
passing.**

---

### Unknowns

1. **The words**, and the shape of the decline. §3 says it costs nothing to decline; whether
   that means a second button, a dismissable line, or no decline at all is not ruled.
2. **When "closer to the day" is**, and whether the guest picks it.
3. **The channel**, and whether an opted-out guest who asks for one gets one — the Zone 7
   question above.
4. **Whether it survives a changed answer.** A guest who says yes, takes the reminder, then
   changes to no. Nothing rules what happens to the pending reminder.
5. **Whether the host sees it.** §3 calls it *"the only lever the product will ever have on
   Kate's deepest unknowable"*, which reads as though it is hers to know about; nothing says
   she is shown it.
