## Readings taken (each is the plain reading; say if one is wrong)

1. **Each paragraph is one run of text.** The ruling's line breaks are the
   brief's own wrapping (under 80 characters), not breaks inside a
   paragraph. Each is drawn as one paragraph, its words exactly as ruled.
2. **The apostrophe.** Paragraph 1's "Elizabeth's" is drawn with the curly
   apostrophe (’), as the line under the heading ("won’t") and the steps
   ("Who’s coming?") are. Paragraph 2 has none.
3. **"Directly under" is the next thing on the page.** The two paragraphs
   sit straight after the block that holds the first button and the line
   under it, and before the steps' heading. That block itself is not
   changed: it still holds only the button and its line.
4. **Quiet body text in the page's existing style** is the steps' own body
   text: the explainer's introduction is set `text-sm leading-relaxed
   text-warm-grey-700`, and the two paragraphs take those three classes,
   with `text-balance` (the balanced wrapping the two lines above them
   have) and the steps' own gap between paragraphs (`space-y-3`). That is
   smaller than the headline (the serif `text-xl` line under "Gather") and
   the same size as the line under the button. No new colour:
   `text-warm-grey-700` is already on the page. No border, background,
   shadow or picture.
5. **Centred, in the button's column.** Everything above the steps is
   centred, in a column as wide as the button (`max-w-sm`, 384 pixels);
   the paragraphs follow both. On a phone that column is the screen's width
   less the page's margins, as now.
6. **Shown only with the demo on.** The paragraphs are drawn in the demo
   start switch's on-branch, as everything else under the heading is since
   GTC-486. With the switch off the page is still "Gather" and "Under
   construction" only (GTC-430).
7. **The words live in `src/app/demo/page.tsx`**, as two constants beside
   `LEAD` and `START_LINE` (`STORY` and `STORY_GATHER`). No new import, no
   new component, no new file under `src`.

## The layout change (ruling of 28 Nov 2026, after the merge)

Readings (each the plain reading; say if one is wrong):

1. **The block was already the button's width.** The button fills its own
   block (`w-full`), and that block and the paragraphs' block carry the
   same width classes (`mx-auto w-full max-w-sm`), so the triangle came from
   the centring and the balanced wrapping, not the width. The width stays;
   the tests now hold it as "the same width classes as the button's block,
   neither padded at the sides", which is what puts the text's left edge on
   the button's.
2. **Left-aligned** is `text-left` on the paragraphs' block (the page's
   container centres everything; the block overrides it for its own two
   paragraphs only). **Ordinary wrapping** is `text-balance` taken off, and
   no other wrapping class put on. Readings 4 and 5 above (balanced
   wrapping, centred) are superseded by this ruling.
3. **The same small grey text and gap** are the classes as they were:
   `text-sm leading-relaxed text-warm-grey-700` and `space-y-3`.

What changes: `src/app/demo/page.tsx`, the paragraphs' block's classes
only (`text-left` in, `text-balance` out) and a dated note.
`tests/demo-potatoes-test.tsx`: R1 compares the block with the button's
block (width, centring, no side spacing); R3 adds the gap; a new R4 holds
the left alignment and ordinary wrapping. No other test, no package line.
