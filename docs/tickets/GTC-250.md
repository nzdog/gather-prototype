# GTC-250 — Reconcile Hinge/Moment 4 spec dietary language with the counts-are-sufficient ruling

**Status:** Open | **Branch:** TBD | **Commit:** [pending] | **Effort:** low

**Priority:** Low — editorial only, not blocking. No open ticket depends on the specs reading
correctly; [[GTC-185]], [[GTC-188]], and [[GTC-228]] all carry the ruling inline already.

### Context

Two founder rulings (GTC-184, 2026-08-15; extended explicitly to GTC-185, 2026-08-23) settled
that per-person dietary capture is not being built: dietary needs are event-level counts-of-kind,
counts are sufficient to verify coverage, and per-person identity would only be required for
portioning or for telling an individual guest which dish is theirs — a different product.

The spec documents were never updated to match and still describe the per-person design in full:

- `docs/03_specs/gather-hinge-spec-v1.md:20` (§1) — "**Dietaries** — re-verified by name. Dietary
  needs are a safety condition in this product, not a preference; the last check before exposure
  re-confirms the one thing that must never be wrong."
- `docs/03_specs/gather-moment-4-spec-v1.md:144` (§10.5) — "Per-person dietary is captured at
  Moment 1. Dietary needs are facts about people, and Moment 1 is where Kate tells Gather about
  her people: an optional per-person note at household capture, costing seconds, only for those
  with needs. Moment 2 consumes rather than asks — generation reads per-person needs plus the
  event-level answer; the dietary three-state remains the safety gate. The pre-flight's by-name
  re-verify then shows her what she entered, against the plan."

Both lines currently describe a feature that will not be built. The contradiction is already
recorded inline in [[GTC-185]] (which cites both quotes and marks them superseded), so no live
ticket is at risk of being misled by the stale spec text. This ticket exists to close the loop at
the source rather than leave the correction living only in a downstream ticket.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- `docs/03_specs/gather-hinge-spec-v1.md` §1 — strike the "re-verified by name" clause. Replace
  with language describing an event-level dietary re-verify at the pre-flight, consistent with
  the ruling: dietary needs are a safety condition re-confirmed before exposure, verified as
  event-level counts-of-kind, not by name.
- `docs/03_specs/gather-moment-4-spec-v1.md` §10.5 — rewrite in full to match the ruling: no
  per-person capture at Moment 1, no per-person consumption at generation, the pre-flight
  re-verify shows the event-level three-state answer for confirmation/correction.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` (not applicable to this ticket's surface,
  noted for completeness).
- No code changes of any kind — this is a documentation-only ticket.
- The historical record: `docs/04_roadmap/moment4-hinge-discovery-report.md` and
  `docs/04_roadmap/moment4-hinge-report-accuracy-audit-2026-08-04.md` are **deliberately left
  describing the original per-person plan**. They are dated audit artifacts of what was decided
  *at the time*, not living specs — rewriting them would erase the record of what the discovery
  process actually found and recommended, which is the same reasoning `gather-change-control`
  uses for never renumbering history. Do not edit either file under this ticket.

**Out of scope:**
- Any other spec sections. This ticket touches exactly the two dietary passages named above.
- Re-opening whether per-person capture should be built — both rulings stand; this ticket only
  brings the spec documents into agreement with them.
