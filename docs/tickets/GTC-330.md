### Why it is its own ticket

The add paths are several, and each needs its own design:

- the host's own edit;
- batch import;
- a guest's self-join through `src/app/api/join/[token]/claim/route.ts`, where **no host is present
  to answer**.

The last one is the hard case. A question that needs the host cannot be asked at a moment she is
not there.
