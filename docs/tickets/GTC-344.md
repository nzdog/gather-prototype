# GTC-344 — the chase's "nowhere to send" on a child's card

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-09-30 at [[GTC-336]] on founder ruling (plan question 3:
*"CHASE_UNREACHABLE children: out of scope, noted in Evidence, and file a stub for it in this commit
so it's on the list. Leave its severity for me."*). Not scoped.
**Severity:** not set at filing — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

[[GTC-336]] gave a red child whose carrier's message failed a sentence naming the carrier and
pointing at the carrier's card, because the child's card offers no door (Q1). It covered the door's
two reasons, `NOT_DELIVERED` and `UNREACHABLE`, and no other.

A child can also read red for a third reason with the same shape. When the carrier's channel is lost
AFTER the ask arrived (a hard bounce with no mobile, an address removed, a number that cannot take
texts and no email), the chase chooser refuses the carrier, and `CHASE_REFUSAL_MEANS` in
`src/lib/glance/chase-fact.ts` maps `EMAIL_BLOCKED`, `NO_CHANNEL` and `PHONE_UNUSABLE` to
`CHASE_UNREACHABLE` ([[GTC-251]] Q5). The child's strip then reads *"nowhere to send"* or *"can't
email"* (`WHY_LINES` in `src/components/glance/strip.ts`).

That red is not a door reason (`doorOffered` in `src/lib/glance/actions.ts`, and `REMIND_WITHDRAWN`'s
note there), so the child's card has no door — correctly. But unlike GTC-336's two reds, it also
carries no sentence saying whose channel was lost or that the fix is on the carrier's card.
`carrierNote` is null for it by GTC-336's ruling.

### Unknowns

1. Whether this red wants words on the child's card at all, and if so which: GTC-336's W2 and W3 are
   about the ASK's message, and here the ask arrived.
2. What the carrier's own card offers for it. The adult reads `CHASE_UNREACHABLE` too, which is not
   a door reason either (the ask arrived, so the resend door would answer `NOTHING_FAILED`).
