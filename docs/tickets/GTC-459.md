# GTC-459 - The /demo explainer: what each Moment is for, and pictures for Moments 3 and 4

**Status: built, committed on `gtc-459-explainer-moments` and open as a pull request (8 Nov 2026),
approved by the founder to commit and push.** Not merged. The commit hash is backfilled after merge.

All dates are NZ time.

## What and why

The founder (8 Nov 2026), after GTC-457 and GTC-458 were written: "I want this to say what happens
throughout the moments: what each is for and what happens when you've done it. A shorter form of
what's on each moment screen. Use titles for each moment." Then: "Also include some graphic element
for moment 3 and 4. Show the board for 4."

This was first built on GTC-457's component. GTC-458 (PR #91, the Gather restyle with a fold-out per
step) reached `main` while it was waiting, so that version was not pushed: it would have undone
GTC-458. The same changes are made here on top of `main` as it stands.

## What changed

- `src/lib/demo/explainer-words.ts`: each Moment's fold-out opens with two labelled lines, "What this
  does" and "When it's done", short forms of the lines on that Moment's own screen
  (`MOMENT_WORDS`, GTC-368). The two labels are the screens' own (`MOMENT_HEADINGS`), drawn by the
  component so they cannot drift. GTC-458's practical paragraphs and pictures stay under them. Where
  GTC-458's first paragraph said the same thing as the new line (Moments 2, 3 and 4), the new line
  replaces it. The titles are GTC-458's (the label "Moment 3" over "Who's on what?").
- Two pictures, `public/explainer/m3-whos-on-what.png` and `public/explainer/m4-board.png`, each with
  a caption and words for a screen reader. They are NOT captures of the live sample (GTC-458 left
  Moments 3 and 4 without pictures because both need a generated plan, which makes an AI call). They
  are the real components (`Moment3AssignView`; `GlanceBoard` with the sample note, the Host view /
  Guest view pair and the Moments strip) drawn from made-up data (Sam and the Ngata, Sharma and
  Brennan households) with the repo's own Tailwind. They go stale if either screen changes: redo them.
- `src/components/demo/DemoExplainer.tsx`: draws the two labels; a picture now opens full size in a
  new tab (the phone problem GTC-458 listed as decision 3: at 390 wide the small print is unreadable).
  This applies to all seven pictures. A closed step's picture is not reachable by keyboard.

## Corrections made while doing this

- Moment 3 said "Tap a job, then a person". The screen says "Tap a person, then tap the dishes
  they're bringing" (`ASSIGN_HOW`, `src/lib/moment3/words.ts`). Now "Tap a person, then tap what
  they're bringing."
- Moment 4's sentence about Gather showing what changed "when you come back to your laptop" is not
  in the screen's own words, so it is out. The founder asked for a shorter form of the screen.
