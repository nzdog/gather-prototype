# GTC-356 — the household contact is told what her household has been asked to bring

**Status:** Open — **scoped 2026-10-03 (the ruling and seven scoping rulings below); plan next.**
**Precondition of the deploy** (founder ruling S7, 2026-10-03).
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

## How it came up

Scoping [[GTC-355]] (Moment 3), the founder asked why teams come after launch. The answer: his own
Moment 3 document (`docs/moment-3-flow-document.md`, Build Priority) puts the team builder in
Phase 2, after launch, and its two-layer team messages change who Gather messages and chases. A
middle path was offered: a team builder screen without changing the messages. The founder asked
instead about "the middle layer": an extra message to team leads letting them know what their team
has been asked to bring.

Asked in Cowork, 2026-10-03: *"What's a team lead's job in this middle layer?"* Options: *Kept in
the loop (Recommended)* — told what their team has been asked to bring, at the send, and asked to
do nothing: Gather still asks and chases each person; *Helps chase* — leads also hear when someone
goes quiet or says no, and see guests' answers; *Runs the team* — the lead decides who brings what
within the team, the document's Phase 2. The founder asked for the pros, cons and build
consequences of all three; they were given in chat (option 1: an addition, not a change, about one
GTC-350-sized ticket; option 2: a privacy change, two chasers, roughly three times the size;
option 3: a redesign of the send and Moment 4 for team events, weeks).

## The ruling (founder, 2026-10-03, verbatim)

> Option 1. I think that households will probably make up teams and the team lead or household
> lead can be the same. If Mum knows that her partner Ross has to bring some cabbage, she can
> follow him up irl and buy the cabbage when she gets her stuff.

### What it settles

- **The lead is told, not asked.** Gather still asks and chases each person directly, as today.
  Nothing about who is asked, the reminders, the replies or the board changes.
- **The team is the household, and the lead is its contact.** The household contact is chosen in
  Moment 1 already (`Household.contactPersonEventId`), and she already receives her children's asks
  in her own message (the carried asks). This extends what she is told to the other adults in her
  household: Mum learns that Ross has been asked to bring the cabbage.
- **No lead-picking step in Moment 3.** [[GTC-355]] needs no team or lead UI for this. The team
  builder (the document's Phase 2) stays after launch.

## Scoping rulings, 2026-10-03 (in Cowork, verbatim)

**S1.** *"Where should Mum see what her household has been asked to bring?"* Options: *In her own
message (Recommended)*; *A separate message*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"In her own message
(Recommended)"** — *"Her usual message gets one more part, listing what the others in her household
have been asked to bring, alongside her own items and her children's. One message, and she sees the
whole household at once."* Settles open item 1.

**S2.** *"If Mum hasn't been asked to bring anything herself and has no children's items to carry,
should she still get a message with her household's list?"* Options: *Yes, just the list
(Recommended)*; *No*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Yes, just the list (Recommended)"** — *"She
still gets a message telling her what the others in her household have been asked to bring, so she
can follow Ross up. It costs one extra message for that household."* Settles open item 2. Open item
3 is settled by the ruling itself: the list says what each other adult was asked to bring, never
their answers.

**S3.** *"When Mum's message goes by text, how much of the household list should the text carry?
(By email it's always written out in full, at no extra cost.)"* Options: *Written out
(Recommended)*; *Names only, list on the link*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Written out
(Recommended)"** — *"The text lists what each person was asked to bring, "Ross: cabbage", so she
sees it at a glance. It may add one text part, about 10 cents. A very long list is cut short, with
the rest on her link."* Settles open item 4; the plan proposes where "very long" starts.

**S4.** *"After the send, if someone in Mum's household is given something new or an item moves,
should Mum be told too?"* Options: *No, just at the send (Recommended)*; *Yes, tell her about
changes*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"No, just at the send (Recommended)"** — *"Mum's list
reflects the moment of the send. Later changes reach the person they're about, as they do today,
but not Mum. Simplest, and later changes are uncommon."* Settles open item 6. Open item 5 needs no
ruling: the pre-flight shows this part of her message like every other (the Hinge spec, section 1).

**S5.** *"Should the privacy page tell guests that their household's contact sees what they've
been asked to bring?"* Options: *Add a sentence (Recommended)*; *No sentence*. The founder chose,
verbatim: **"Add a sentence (Recommended)"** — *""If you're part of a household, Gather tells its
contact person what you've been asked to bring." It keeps the privacy page true, as you did for
replies in GTC-350."* Settles open item 7: that sentence, as written; the plan proposes where it
sits on the page.

**S6.** *"If Ross has no email or mobile, his item goes on your list today, for you to ask him
yourself. With this change, what should happen?"* Options: *Keep it on the host's list
(Recommended)*; *Carry it in Mum's message*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Keep it on the host's
list (Recommended)"** — *"Ross's item stays on your list as it is today, and Mum's household list
still shows it, so she knows about it too. Nothing about who is asked or chased changes, as option 1
promised."* Settles open item 8.

**S7.** *"Last one. Which of these must be done before the 25 October launch? I'd tick all three:
together they give you the flow from Moment 1 to 4 through the Hinge."* Options (several could be
chosen): *Moment 3 (GTC-355)*; *Household list (GTC-356)*; *A way into the board*. The founder
chose all three, verbatim: **"Moment 3 (GTC-355)"**, **"Household list (GTC-356)"** and **"A way
into the board"**. Settles open item 9: **a precondition of the deploy**, recorded in [[GTC-189]]'s
gathered list. Built after [[GTC-355]].

**All nine open items below are now settled (S1 to S7, and the ruling itself). Ready for a plan.**

## Open, to scope with the founder one at a time

1. Whether the household list goes inside her own message, beside her own items and her children's,
   or in a separate message.
2. Whether a contact with nothing of her own still gets a message with only the household list.
3. What the list says: each other adult in the household and what they were asked to bring. Never
   their answers (that is option 2).
4. Text and email: a long list in a text runs to several paid parts.
5. The pre-flight shows it, like every message (the Hinge spec: she reads everything before it
   goes).
6. Changes after the send (someone added, an item moved): told or not.
7. The privacy page: a sentence in the founder's words, if any.
8. An adult in the household with no email or mobile goes on the host's list today. Should their ask
   be carried in the contact's message, like a child's? A change to the recipient ruling.
9. Launch: recommended to build right after [[GTC-355]]; not yet ruled a deploy precondition.
