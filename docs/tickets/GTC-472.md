**Status: built, committed locally, gated; not pushed.** Built on `feat/gtc-472-share-a-dish`, made from

## The founder's words (22 Nov 2026), verbatim

First:

> Need to build this urgently. Moment 3. Assign more than one item to several
> people - eg, both mum and Joanna bring salad

Then:

> I want to divide the dish being shared

## The Person

Kate, the host, in Moment 3 ("Who's on what"). She is DECIDING who brings what.
She has given the green salad to her mum, and she knows her mum cannot bring
salad for twenty on her own: Joanna will bring half. She has just done the
sensible thing a person does with a list, and the list has no way to say it.

She is feeling the small irritation of a tool that makes her work around it:
"Anything that needs three people becomes three items" was the first version's
answer, and it left her to type the same name three times and to do the
arithmetic herself. She needs to feel that two people bringing the salad is
as easy to say as one.

## The Broken Promise

Moment 3 promised that Kate could say who brings what. GTC-355 Ruling 2 chose
"One person per item" and said "Several people per item can come later". The
founder's flow document has a paragraph for it ("Multiple people per item")
that was not built. What Kate was left with was a list where a dish held by two
people was two unrelated rows she had to make, name and size herself.

What Gather already knows and did not say: the amount. Twenty servings between
two people is ten each. Gather can do that sum, and show it before it saves.

## The Cost

Kate carries the sum, and the risk of getting it wrong: two people each told to
bring the whole dish, or neither told a number. Each wrong amount is a dish
too big or too small on the day.

## The Fix

On a dish that already has a person, the Details box offers "Share with
someone". Pressing it and picking a person makes a COPY of the dish, placed
directly under the original in the same section, gives the copy to that person,
and divides the amount between everyone sharing it. Before anything is saved,
Kate sees the proposed split, one line per person, and can change each number.
Nothing is written until she confirms.

Example: "Green salad, serves 20: Mum" shared with Joanna becomes "Green salad,
serves 10: Mum" and "Green salad, serves 10: Joanna". Shared with a third
person, all three are re-divided from the total.

Why copies, not one shared item: `Assignment.itemId` is unique and
`Item.assignment` is singular; the send, the nudges, the decide-by clock and the
board all rely on it. GTC-355 Ruling 2 stands. This ticket makes the "three
items" workaround one action and divides the amount. It does not reverse the
ruling.

## Known limits (recorded, not built)

- A split cannot be reopened later with all sharers side by side. She changes a
  row's amount on Moment 2, or shares with another person, which re-divides.
- A drafted dish also holds a words note such as "serves 4-6". Words are never
  divided, so both rows keep it, and Moment 2's plan view shows it.
- The app now reads the ledger to decide something (which rows share a dish). A
  plan cloned into another event loses its share links; the rows then count as
  separate dishes. Nothing wrong is saved because she sees every number first.
- A parent who carries a child's ask, where both hold shares of one dish, could
  read the dish named twice in one message.
- A guest reads a share of one with the plural unit word as the host typed
  it, for example `x1 pavlovas` (a cosmetic limit, left as it is on the
  founder's ruling of 22 Nov 2026).
- The row order shifts a share makes (the rows after the copy move down one
  place) are not ledgered, as ruled.
