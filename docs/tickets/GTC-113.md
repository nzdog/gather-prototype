# GTC-113 — New sub-forms appear at top of section — CLOSED

**Status:** Complete | **Branch:** feat/moment-one-redesign | **Commit:** 4ef0935

### What was found

When Kate clicked "Kid with a job" or "Add Guest", the new sub-form appended to the bottom of its section. Each new entry pushed the form further down the page, forcing Kate to chase her cursor. The buttons stayed at the top but the new form appeared below all existing entries.

### What was changed

**File:** `src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx`

- **Helper button handler:** Changed from `[...prev, emptyGuest()]` to `[emptyGuest(), ...prev]` — new kid-with-a-job sub-forms prepend to the top of the helpers list
- **`addGuest` function:** Same change — new guest sub-forms prepend to the top of the guests list
- **`autoFocus` for helpers:** Changed from `i === helpers.length - 1` to `i === 0` — focus targets the first (newest) entry
- **`autoFocus` for guests:** Same change — focus targets the first (newest) entry

Partner is unaffected — there's only ever one.

### Known limitations

None. Edit mode populates helpers and guests in their original order (oldest first). Only the "add new" action prepends.
