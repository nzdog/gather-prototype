# GTC-302 — A job is stored and read as a dish, and item names are stored in no consistent form

**Status:** Open — filed, not executed, not scoped. Filed 2026-09-13 | **Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [ ] High [x] Medium [ ] Low — **confirmed by the founder:** *"GTC-302 —
MEDIUM CONFIRMED."* Proposed at filing as Medium; the frontmatter records the confirmed value.
Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] No

**Two defects in one sentence, and the worse one leads.** Filed on the founder's instruction from
[[GTC-189]] *Founder answers — the slice 2 words*, where the ask was ruled a sentence: *"Would you
bring the pavlova?"*. The founder asked for the stored names to be checked before that sentence was
written; the check found the naming problem, and beside it a job being asked for as a dish. On
reading both, in the founder's words:

> ⚠ THE JOBS FINDING IS THE WORSE HALF and should lead GTC-302 rather than sit under the naming
> problem. "Would you bring the Wash the dishes?" is a different defect from "the The pavlova": one
> is untidy data, the other is a sentence written for a dish being used for a chore. The founder
> ruling behind this whole model has children doing jobs, so the common case for a carried ask IS a
> job, and the carried sentence is the one most likely to hit it. Say whether an item knows it is a
> job — a kind, a flag, anything — and if it does not, say so plainly, because that decides whether
> slice 3 can filter or whether something has to be added.

**The job sentence has since been ruled** — [[GTC-189]] *Founder answers — the slice 2 words*,
answer 6 — in the founder's words:

> RULE THE JOB SENTENCE. A job is DONE, not brought — that is the whole ruling and it changes one
> word.
>
> Own job: "Would you do the dishes?"
> Carried job: "Ollie has been asked to do the dishes. Would you also answer on Ollie's behalf?"

> ⚠ THE FIELD EXISTS AND THE DATA DOES NOT. Item.kind is ITEM or TASK, zero TASK rows in a
> thousand, and only plan generation ever sets it. So this sentence is ruled on a case that has
> never occurred, and the first job assigned by hand will be stored as ITEM and read "bring".

> ⚠ AND DO NOT FILTER JOBS OUT. Your own finding: a filtered job reaches nobody, and a child's job
> reaching an adult is exactly what the carried ask exists for. The answer was always a sentence,
> never a filter.

**So this ticket's first half is no longer the missing sentence — composition has it. It is what
keeps the sentence unreachable:** a job typed by a host is stored as a dish. Half 1 below is
rewritten on that footing; the sentence itself is recorded in [[GTC-189]].

**The sharpest part, said plainly.** The plan generator's prompt gives *"Wash the dishes"* as its own
example of a job name, so the first generated job reads **"Would you do the Wash the dishes?"**. In
the founder's words:

> The naming problem and the verb problem are the same defect arriving twice, and neither is fixable
> in the composer — a sentence cannot tidy its own nouns.

So Half 2's *"the The pavlova"* and Half 1's *"do the Wash the dishes"* are one defect — a name
stored in a form the sentence cannot use — met once by a dish and once by a job. The fix is where
names are made, not where sentences are.

Both halves were **found by reading and measuring gather_dev on 2026-09-13, NOT reproduced on a real
event**. Composition is at [[GTC-189]] slice 2.

## Half 1 — a job stored and read as a dish, so the ruled sentence cannot reach it

### Signal

**Expected**
A job reaches its owner, or its owner's carrier, in the ruled sentence: *"Would you do the dishes?"*,
*"Ollie has been asked to do the dishes."* Never filtered out.

**What composition now does** ([[GTC-189]] slice 2). `whatIsAsked` in
`src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` says *"do"* of a job and *"bring"* of a dish, and one person
holding both is one sentence — *"Would you bring the pavlova and do the dishes?"*. It is handed the
two kinds apart, as `AskRecipient.jobNames` and `CarriedChildAsk.jobNames`. It cannot tell them
apart itself: composition is handed names only ([[GTC-187]] decision 1).

**Actual — three ways a job still reaches a guest wrong, the first this ticket's defect:**

1. **A job a host adds is stored as a dish.** No route that creates a row sets `kind` (see the
   limits below), so a typed job is an `ITEM`, arrives in `itemNames`, and reads *"Would you bring
   the Wash the dishes?"* — carried, *"Ollie has been asked to bring the Wash the dishes."* **This is
   what makes the ruling unreachable today.** And it is reachable now: neither `POST` in
   `src/app/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/assign/route.ts` nor `POST` in
   `src/app/api/c/[token]/items/[itemId]/assign/route.ts` reads `kind`, so a job can be assigned by
   hand the moment it is typed; only `POST` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/auto-assign/route.ts`
   leaves `TASK` rows out.
2. **The preview reads no kind.** `GET` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/pre-flight/message/route.ts`
   selects a person's assignments with no `kind`, so every caller of `composeAsk` passes
   `jobNames: []` and even a generated `TASK` row reads *"bring"* on the pre-flight page. **[[GTC-189]]
   slice 3's to split** — split, not filter. Layer D of `tests/carried-ask-composition-test.ts` pins
   the empty list until then.
3. **The generator names jobs as verb phrases.** `buildPlanGenerationPrompt` in
   `src/lib/ai/prompts.ts` asks for day-of jobs with *"normalise the phrasing"* and gives *"Wash the
   dishes"* as the example name. The ruled sentence wants a noun phrase — *"do the dishes"* — so the
   first generated job, once split, reads *"Would you do the Wash the dishes?"*. Pinned
   `[DEFECT GTC-302]` in layer J. **This is Half 2's defect arriving a second time**, and like Half 2
   it is not fixable in the composer.

**⚠ For slice 3, not this ticket, and recorded so it is not rediscovered:** the chooser's
`ChooserMembership.holdsItems` in `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` must count jobs. A child
whose only row is a job, counted as holding nothing, is `CHILD_WITHOUT_ITEM` and carried to no one —
a filter by another name. It is written into slice 3's entry in [[GTC-189]]'s build shape as well.

**Why the carried sentence is the one most exposed.** The recipient model carries a child's ask and
nothing else ([[GTC-189]], THE ASK), and the model has children doing jobs — Hinge §4 has children as
*"owners of tasks, never recipients of messages"*, and Moment 4 §6 is *"one assignment machine, two
kinds of rows"*. So the carried ask's common case is a job.

### Does an item know it is a job? **Yes.**

- **`Item.kind`**, of type `RowKind` — `ITEM` or `TASK` — added by [[GTC-171]]. The schema docstring:
  *"Task rows are Item rows, so `Assignment` reaches both through the identical relation,
  unchanged."*
- **It is already read to tell them apart:** `POST` in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/auto-assign/route.ts` takes only `kind: 'ITEM'` rows; `GET` in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/summary/route.ts` counts the two kinds separately.
- **So slice 3 can split on it. No field has to be added to tell a job from a dish** — for the rows
  that carry it.

**Three limits, stated plainly because they decide what the flag is worth:**
1. **Only plan generation marks a job.** `POST` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/finalize-plan/route.ts`
   is the one writer of `kind: 'TASK'` in `src/` — the plan's set-up, clean-up and other-jobs
   buckets. No row-creating route reads a kind from its request, so a job a host types through
   `POST` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/teams/[teamId]/items/route.ts` or `POST` in
   `src/app/api/c/[token]/items/route.ts` is stored as `ITEM`, the default, and nothing marks it.
   **The flag is reliable for generated jobs and absent for typed ones.**
2. **gather_dev has never held a job.** 0 of 1,017 rows are `TASK`, so no job has been assigned,
   composed or carried in this database, and everything in this half is by reading. A search of
   `ITEM` names for job verbs (*wash, clean, set up, pack, tidy, carve, serve, …*) found none that
   read as a job.
3. **Not a filter — a sentence, and the sentence is now ruled.** A job filtered out of the message is
   a job the message no longer asks for, and a child's job filtered out of a carried ask reaches no
   adult — which is what the carried ask exists to prevent. The founder: *"The answer was always a
   sentence, never a filter."* Ruled as [[GTC-189]] answer 6, on a case gather_dev has never held.

## Half 2 — untidy data: item names in no consistent form

### Signal

**Expected**
A name that reads correctly after *"the"*, mid-sentence: *"Would you bring the pavlova and the
trifle?"*

**Actual — what gather_dev holds, 1,017 `Item` rows, measured 2026-09-13**

- **Generated, 823 rows** (`source = GENERATED`): every one capitalised, none beginning with an
  article, and 546 in Title Case (*Garden Salad*, *Roast Kumara*, *Sparkling Grape Juice*) against
  82 in sentence case.
- **Manual, 194 rows** (`source = MANUAL`): 182 capitalised, 90 in sentence case (*Beef sausages*,
  *Christmas cake*), 14 in Title Case, and **56 that already begin with an article** (*The
  pavlova*, *the glazed ham*). All 56 are on four GTC-192 seed boards (*GTC-192 glance — mixed*,
  *replay — arrival*, *phase 7 — the two variants*, *replay — all green*). The strings are written
  by `buildBoard` in `scripts/seed-gtc192-replay.ts` and `build` in `scripts/seed-gtc192-phase7.ts`.
- **Qualifiers inside the name**, 324 rows with `( & / + , : ;` — *Serving Platters (large)*, *Butter
  (for table)*, *Glazed Ham (bone-in leg)*, *Sparkling Wine / Prosecco*.
- **Proper nouns and brands** — *Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc*, *Bluff oysters*, *Whittaker's
  chocolate*, *Beer (DB Export, Speights)*.
- **Names that are already lists** — *Guacamole and Corn Chips*, *Crackers and dips*.
- **Characters outside GSM-7**, 15 rows, among them em dashes inside names (*Seafood on the Grill
  (mixed — prawns, fish fillets, mussels)*) and accents (*Rosé*, *Crudités Platter*).
- **Fixture rows** — *Row 12-0*, *Item Team A 2*.

Of 185 assigned rows, 143 belong to a non-child, non-host member — the people the pre-flight preview
lists today.

**No write site normalises a name.** By grep and by reading each one's name handling:
`applyPlanSections` in `src/lib/ai/plan-write.ts` writes the generated `item.name`; `POST` in
`src/app/api/events/[id]/teams/[teamId]/items/route.ts` and `POST` in
`src/app/api/c/[token]/items/route.ts` write the typed name; `PATCH` in
`src/app/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/route.ts` writes `body.name`. The JSON examples in
`src/lib/ai/prompts.ts` show `"name": "Item Name"`, and `buildPlanGenerationPrompt` gives
`"name": string` with no guidance on form.

**The founder chose to see this rather than hide it.** Offered three ways on, the founder chose to
build the ruled sentence with each name exactly as stored and file the data, to be fixed **before
[[GTC-189]] slice 5 sends anything**. Not taken: holding the colon list, and fixing names inside the
composer — the papering over the founder refused, which also breaks proper nouns.

**Smallest failing case**
`composeAsk` with own items `['The pavlova', 'Berry Trifle']` composes *"Would you bring the The
pavlova and the Berry Trifle?"* — pinned as `[DEFECT GTC-302]` in
`tests/carried-ask-composition-test.ts`. On other gather_dev names: *"Would you bring the Berry
Trifle and the Beer (DB Export, Speights)?"*, and *"the Pavlova and the Guacamole and Corn Chips"*.
