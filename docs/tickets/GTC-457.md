# GTC-457 - The /demo page: a closed explainer under "Try it yourself"

**Status: built, committed on `gtc-457-demo-explainer` and open as a pull request (8 Nov 2026), approved
by the founder to commit and open it.** Not merged. The four test suites listed below are still red by
design. The commit hash is backfilled after merge.

All dates are NZ time.

## What and why

The founder (8 Nov 2026), on the page visitors reach from "See it in action": it opens with the
heading and "Try it yourself". He asked for "a detailed explainer in accordion (default collapsed)
that guides users through the platform", and then asked that Moment 4 say that Gather sends the
invites and asks, that the demo does not, and that the guest side can be seen by clicking the
toggle (the board's Host view / Guest view pair, GTC-444).

This overrides one earlier ruling on purpose: GTC-395 items 1 and 2 (20 Oct 2026) left the front
page with the heading and one button and nothing else. Four test suites pin that (below).

## What changed

- `src/lib/demo/explainer-words.ts` (new): the words. One title, an introduction and five steps
  (Start your event; Moments 1 to 4).
- `src/components/demo/DemoExplainer.tsx` (new): one fold-out row, closed on every visit, built from
  the pre-flight's pieces (`FoldBody`, `FoldCue`, the shade and the ring in `AccordionShell`) so it
  opens like every other fold-out. `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and the closed text is
  `aria-hidden`. No hooks beyond one open flag; no setting read; no request.
- `src/app/demo/page.tsx`: draws it under the button, inside the button's own branch, so it follows
  the demo start switch and never shows beside "Under construction". The container is `max-w-xl`
  (was `max-w-sm`) for the explainer, and the button sits in its own `max-w-sm` wrapper so it keeps
  its width. `isDemoStartOn()` is still read once; `TRY_IT_YOURSELF` is unchanged.

No schema, no migration, no route, no sign-in code, nothing in a Do-Not-Touch Zone.

## The words, and what each was checked against (8 Nov 2026)

Every name quoted was read in the code, not recalled:

- Moment names: `MomentArc.tsx`.
- "Add a crowd" (30 made-up people in 11 households, once her own household is saved):
  `src/lib/demo/crowd.ts`.
- "Fill in demo answers", "Generate plan →", "Write the list myself": `typical-answers.ts`,
  `Moment2Step1Modal.tsx`, `Moment2Opening.tsx`.
- "Move on →", the pretend Send ("Here's what Send would have done"), "See the board as it would
  look →": `Moment3AssignView.tsx`, `pretend-send.ts`, `sample-board.ts`.
- "Host view" / "Guest view", "← Back to the board", "Make the guests reply": `sample-board.ts`,
  `replies-words.ts`.
- The guest's answers are Yes, No, Maybe: `src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx`.
- Email first, text if there is no email: `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` (line 322 picks
  email when the person has one that is not blocked; the text route follows).

Two corrections to the first draft shown to the founder, both from this check: it said one person
answers for a whole household (not true: adults with their own email or mobile hear from Gather
themselves, and the contact gets the children's asks), and it named an "I'm on it" button that
does not exist (the guest taps Yes, No or Maybe). The draft's closing paragraph, which repeated
the sample's delete-the-next-day line, is left out: that line is W58 on `/demo/new` and stays in
one place.
