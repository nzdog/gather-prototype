# GTC-477 - Multi-day groundwork: three faults with days, fixed first

Base: origin/main 1372817 (23 Nov 2026). The scoping, and the founder's
rulings for the whole multi-day build, are in [[GTC-476]].

## The faults

1. The host's item-create route (`src/app/api/events/[id]/teams/[teamId]/items/route.ts`,
   POST) never reads a day. The host's Add item box offers a Day list and sends
   the day it picks, and the route throws it away.
2. The coordinator page's Day list (`src/app/c/[token]/page.tsx`, line 921)
   offers only the first dish's day. The page gets its data from one call to
   `GET /api/c/[token]`, and that route does not return the event's days, only
   each dish's own day. The host's day route (`/api/events/[id]/days`) lets in
   hosts and co-hosts only.
3. `src/lib/ledger.ts` says `dayId` "never reaches a guest", but the guest page
   shows the dish's day beside it.

## The fix (as planned and approved)

1. The create route checks a sent day exactly as the item edit route does
   (GTC-403): when a day is sent, it must be a day of this event, or the route
   answers 400 "That day is not on this event" and writes nothing. No day is not
   checked. A day that passes is saved on the dish.
2. The coordinator route's reply gains `days`: every Day of the link's event, in
   date order (and in the order they were made, on the same date). The page's
   list offers "All days" first, then every day by its name.
3. `dayId` joins `ASK_FIELDS` (Q13). After the send, the plan page's Edit item
   box asks "why" when the Day is changed on a dish whose holder has answered
   yes, no or maybe, with the existing line "You're changing an ask someone's
   already answered." Nothing new is sent: the notify hooks are empty and are
   not called for an ask-field change.
