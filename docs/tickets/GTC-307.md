# GTC-307 — the carrier who is never asked about herself

---

### Signal

**Expected**
A guest who carries a child's ask and holds nothing herself is still a guest. She is asked whether
she can make it, like every other adult, and she can answer that as well as the child's question.

**Actual**
She is asked only about the child.

- **Composition.** `askSystemVoice` in `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` has two endings. The
  itemless branch — nothing of any kind, nothing carried — ends *"One tap to say whether you can
  make it"*. Every other branch, including a carrier holding nothing of her own, ends *"One tap to
  say yes, no or maybe - the details are on the page"*, which is the child's decision, not hers.
  `carriedAskSentences` in the same file records the behaviour in its docstring and calls the
  question unruled.
- **The page.** [[GTC-191]] owns what she can answer when she gets there, and the carried answer
  itself is already that ticket's under [[GTC-189]] ruling D item 3. Her own attendance answer
  beside the child's is the part ruling AA adds.

**Smallest failing case**
A household contact with no assigned items whose child holds one. Compose her ask. Nothing in it
asks whether she is coming.

---

### ⚠ A citation to correct while doing this

The docstring on `carriedAskSentences` reads:

> A carrier with nothing of their own is pointed at the child's yes / no / maybe and is NOT asked
> whether they can make it. Whether they should be is decision 8's reach, and not ruled.

*"Decision 8"* there means **[[GTC-187]] decision 8**, the itemless ask — not [[GTC-189]] decision
8, which is the proxy household reminder and is answered by ruling AE. The two are unrelated and
the sentence gives the reader no way to tell. Disambiguate it in the same change that removes it.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- `askSystemVoice` and `carriedAskSentences` in `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts`: a carrier
  holding nothing of her own is asked her own attendance question as well as the child's.
- The docstring correction above.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.
- **The register boundary, which slice 2 built and pinned.** A child's item is never named inside
  *"you bring"*, never *"your"*, and never in a sentence with the recipient's own. Adding her
  attendance question must not merge the two asks; layers B, X and T of
  `tests/carried-ask-composition-test.ts` hold that line and must stay green.
- **The one-voice rule.** *Would*, never *could*; no *please*. Founder rulings at slice 2,
  answers 1 and 5.
- The guest page. That half is [[GTC-191]]'s, and this ticket does not widen it beyond recording
  that it is widened.

### Unknowns

1. **The words.** Ruling AA rules that she is asked, not how. The existing two endings are the
   candidates and neither is obviously right for a message carrying both questions.
2. **One tap or two.** Her attendance and the child's answer are two different facts. Whether the
   message points at one link that asks both, or reads as two asks, is [[GTC-191]]'s shape and is
   not ruled.
3. **The host as carrier.** Ruling AB makes her message Gather's voice only ([[GTC-308]]), and she
   is the host — she is not asked whether she is coming to her own event. Whether ruling AA reaches
   her at all is not ruled, and A2's warning against widening applies.
