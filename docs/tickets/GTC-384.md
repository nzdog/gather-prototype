### Draft A (item 55): ruled, found untrue, replaced

Draft A was ruled before the plan ("Gather sends one message per household, to its main contact.
For your household that's you. Switch this on and I'll message you about your partner and children
too. Leave it off and I'll leave your own household to you."). Claude Code checked it against the
code and found three of its four sentences untrue, because for the host's own household the switch
changes nothing Gather sends:

- A child of the host's own household is hers whoever is picked: `resolveHouseholdContact` returns
  `HOST_HOUSEHOLD_CHILD` at `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` L260-264, before the switch is
  read at L282 (`resolveHouseholdMuted`). So her children's things always come to her list.
- Her household gets no household list (`HOST_HOUSEHOLD`, L565), and she is never messaged about
  her own event (`HOST_OWN_ASK`).
- An adult of her household with an email or mobile is asked directly either way.
- Pinned already: `tests/channel-chooser-test.ts` L541, "the host's household picked Dan and
  switched messages on: Mia is still hers", and L550, "an adult of the host's household is messaged
  individually, not through her".

Q5 above replaces the Draft A ruling: the switch goes and one true line takes its place.
