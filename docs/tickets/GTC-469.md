# GTC-469 - The "can't start a sample" message on /demo/new

Branch feat/gtc-469-sample-start-notice, made from origin/main ab463ca
(the merge of pull request 109), tracking main. The number GTC-467 is held
by a local, unpushed branch (fix/gtc-467-join-page-guest-lookups), and
GTC-468 is closed, so this ticket is GTC-469.

All dates are NZ time.

## Why (the finding)

The founder, 22 Nov 2026: "This error message needs to be more obvious."
He had pressed Create on /demo/new with a sample already open. The line
"You already have a sample event open. Go to it" showed as small grey
text (text-sm, text-gray-700) under the privacy line, below the button, and
was easy to miss.

The page (src/app/demo/new/page.tsx) draws one variable, `line`, in that
spot. It holds every refusal and the save-failed line:

- Details did not save: "Failed to update event" plus "Go to it".
- A sample is already open (W10): "You already have a sample event open."
  plus "Go to it →".
- Signed in to a real account (W9): "You're signed in to Gather. Sign out
  first to try a sample event." No link.
- Too many starts (a 429): W7, W8a or W8b, whatever the server sends in
  `body.error`.
- Anything else, including a network error (W15): "The sample event could
  not be started. Please try again."

## The founder's rulings (22 Nov 2026, his words)

Design recommended by Claude and approved by the founder by pasting it:

- The message moves above the Create button.
- It is drawn as an amber box in the style of the sample banner
  (DemoBanner), with normal-size dark text.
- "Go to it" becomes a proper button inside the box.
- When it appears it is scrolled into view and announced to a screen
  reader (role="status").
- It covers every line that spot shows today.
- No word changes. The words in src/lib/demo/start-limit.ts and the
  privacy line W58 stay exactly as they are.
