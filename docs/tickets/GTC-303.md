# GTC-303 — A kind change can leave a row no route would have created

**Status:** Open — filed, not executed. Filed 2026-09-13 | **Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [ ] High [x] Medium [ ] Low — **set by the founder, as proposed:**
*"GTC-303 and GTC-304: severity MEDIUM on both, as proposed."* Proposed at filing as Medium; the
frontmatter's `severity` records the set value, and `severity_proposed` keeps the proposal.
Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] No

**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from [[GTC-302]] *Founder answers — the build*, choice 5:

> Choice 5 — a kind change leaving an assignment in place. That is wrong and it should not ship
> quietly. A job assigned across teams and then made a dish keeps a holder the same-team rule would
> now refuse, and the row also keeps quantity state NA. Do not fix it here — file it, and say
> plainly in the ticket that the kind change can produce a row no route would have created. An
> inconsistency a route allows but would never make is the kind that surfaces months later as a
> mystery.

**Said plainly, as the founder asked: the kind change can produce a row no route would have
created.** The PATCH allows it. No route that creates or assigns a row would ever make it.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
Every row the item PATCH leaves behind is one the other routes could have made: its holder admitted
by the same-team rule for the kind it now has, and its quantity fields in the shape that kind is
written with.

**Actual**
`PATCH` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/route.ts` has accepted `kind` since [[GTC-302]],
and changes nothing else about the row when the kind changes. Three shapes follow, and no other route
creates any of them:

1. **A job made a dish keeps a holder the same-team rule refuses.** `mayHoldRow` in
   `src/lib/assignment/same-team.ts` exempts any row that is not `ITEM`, so a job may be assigned to
   a guest on any team. Change it to `ITEM` and the assignment stands — yet `mayHoldRow` refuses that
   same holder for a dish, and neither `POST` in
   `src/app/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/assign/route.ts` nor `POST` in
   `src/app/api/c/[token]/items/[itemId]/assign/route.ts` would have assigned it. The PATCH does not
   ask `mayHoldRow`.
2. **A job made a dish keeps `quantityState` NA.** `kindFields` in `src/lib/items/row-kind.ts` gave
   it NA as a job — the shape [[GTC-171]] records as what keeps task rows clear of the quantity
   gates. Back as a dish, it is a dish with a quantity state nobody chose for it, and it stays clear
   of gates a dish is meant to meet.
3. **A dish made a job keeps its quantity.** `quantityAmount`, `quantityUnit`, `quantityUnitCustom`
   and `quantityText` stay beside the new `quantityState` NA. Neither add route writes a quantity
   for a job ([[GTC-302]]); only this path makes a job that carries one.

**Why it matters, in the founder's words:** *"An inconsistency a route allows but would never make is
the kind that surfaces months later as a mystery."*

**Smallest failing case**
Assign a `TASK` row to a guest on another team, then `PATCH` it with `{ "kind": "ITEM" }`: 200, the
assignment stands, and the row is a dish its holder could never have been given.

**Reachable today through the API only.** No edit screen sends `kind` — `EditItemModal`,
`ItemEditForm` in `src/components/plan/Moment2PlanView.tsx` and the coordinator's edit do not — and
`PATCH` in `src/app/api/c/[token]/items/[itemId]/route.ts` reads none. A host reaches it the day an
edit form offers the toggle.

---

### Reproduce

**Required state**
- A HOST session on any event, two teams, a member on one of them, and a `TASK` row on the other.
- Multi-actor: [ ] Yes [x] No

**Steps**
1. Assign the `TASK` row to the member on the other team through `POST` in
   `src/app/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/assign/route.ts` — allowed, because `mayHoldRow` exempts a
   job.
2. `PATCH /api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]` with `{ "kind": "ITEM" }`.
3. Read the row's `kind`, `quantityState` and `teamId`, and its assignment's `personId`.
4. For shape 3: `PATCH` a dish that carries a quantity with `{ "kind": "TASK" }`, and read its
   quantity columns.

**Observe:** 200 each time; an `ITEM` row held across teams with `quantityState` NA; a `TASK` row with
a quantity.

**Data setup**
Not observed on real data — gather_dev holds no `TASK` row. The host PATCH's kind handling was driven
for real during [[GTC-302]], on an unassigned row of *GTC-129 Test BBQ*; the three shapes here are by
reading that route and `mayHoldRow`.

2. **A job made a dish — its quantity state.** `SPECIFIED` with no amount, `PLACEHOLDER`, or what it

4. **The coordinator's PATCH.** It reads no kind ([[GTC-302]] choice 7). If it ever does, it inherits
   all three.
