The founder's ruling at [[GTC-374]]'s plan, verbatim: *"1. The split (Q1). Asked: "Claude Code
wants to build invites only as two tickets. GTC-374 is batch 7: she can skip the plan at the start
of Moment 2, and an invites-only event can still start a plan any time before Send. GTC-375 is

The ruling it completes, from [[GTC-374]]'s scoping: *"Yes, until the invitations go
(Recommended)"*: *"An invites-only event can still start a plan, and a planned event can still go
invites-only, any time before she presses Send. After that the invitations have said what they
said, so it's fixed."*

### What is to be ruled

- Switching a plan already made to invites only: where she chooses it (the plan view, Moment 3, or
  elsewhere), and anything she must confirm first.
- What happens to the plan: its teams and items, and anyone already given one. Nothing is deleted
  without a ruling.
- `EventSetup.planApprovedAt`, which is stamped once and never cleared today ([[GTC-355]] Q4), so a
  plan approved and then put away would reopen at Moment 3.
- A plan already held: the event stays held ([[GTC-374]], C12).
- **The underlined "· not needed"** on the pre-flight's strip: Moments 2 and 3 are links there, so
  the words "· not needed" are underlined with the label (seen in [[GTC-374]]'s screenshots).
- **The guest page's footer**, "Questions? Contact your coordinator", with nobody named: it needs
  new words, for example naming the host, to be ruled here.

The two fixes above were put here by the founder at [[GTC-374]]'s commit, verbatim: *"2. The two
invites-only fixes. Asked: "Where should the two small invites-only fixes go: the underlined "· not
needed" on the pre-flight's strip, and the guest page's "Contact your coordinator" with nobody
named?" My answer: "Into GTC-375 (Recommended)": "GTC-375 comes next and works on invites only
anyway. The footer needs new words, for example naming the host, and you'd rule on those there.""*
