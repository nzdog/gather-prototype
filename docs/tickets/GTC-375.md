### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-09 at `8dfe31a`)

- **Where [[GTC-374]] leaves it.** Invites only is chosen on Moment 2's opening (`Moment2Opening`,
  W1 *"Skip the plan: invites only"*), offered only with no item and before the press.
  `POST /api/events/[id]/setup` refuses `invitesOnly: true` on an event with any item (409) and
  after the press (409); a co-host gets 403. `resolveSetupStage` answers `'invites-only'` first;
  `stripDoors` shows Moments 2 and 3 not needed; *"Let’s do this →"* on an invites-only event
  clears the flag and opens the questions.
- **Moment 2's plan** (`src/components/plan/Moment2PlanView.tsx`) ends in a sticky footer:
  *"← Back to the questions"*, *"Plan looks good →"*, and under them a small link, *"Invites,
  people and reminders →"* ([[GTC-235]]'s exit). Moment 3 (`Moment3AssignView.tsx`, *"← Back to
  the plan"*) and the pre-flight (the strip's *"What’s the plan?"* door, `?at=plan`) each lead
  back to it in one tap.
- **Gather can already save a whole plan and put it back:** `createRevision` and
  `restoreFromRevision` in `src/lib/workflow.ts` (the old dashboard's plan versions; `PlanRevision`;
  `POST /api/events/[id]/revisions` and `…/revisions/[revisionId]/restore`, the host's). A revision
  keeps the teams (with `coordinatorId`), the items with their assignments (person and response),
  the days, the conflicts and the acknowledgements. A restore deletes the current items (their
  assignments go with them), teams, days and conflicts, and rebuilds them from the revision. It does
  not put back people's team memberships or roles (`PersonEvent.teamId`, `role`: its own *"TODO
  person ID mapping"*).
- **When a team is deleted,** its items and their assignments go with it, and `PersonEvent.teamId`
  and `AccessToken.teamId` are set to null: a coordinator's link would remain, pointing at no team
  (Zone 3).
- **`EventSetup.planApprovedAt`** is stamped once and never cleared ([[GTC-355]] Q4). The entry rule
  reads `invitesOnly` before it, so a put-away plan that comes back can reopen at Moment 3, as she
  left it.
- **The AI:** an event may use it for its plan at most 10 times (`AI_CALL_LIMIT`).
- **The guest page's footer** (`src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx`): *"Questions? Contact your
  coordinator"*, then the coordinator's name only when the guest's team has one. An invites-only
  guest has no team, so nobody is named. The page already shows the host's first name (*"from
  {hostFirstName}"*).
- **The pre-flight's strip** (`MomentArc` with `notNeeded`): Moments 2 and 3 are links there, so
  *"· not needed"* is underlined with the label.
- **[[GTC-319]]:** `formatDateRange` in `src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx`, `src/app/h/[token]/page.tsx`
  and `src/app/gather/[eventId]/directory/page.tsx` takes the end date's second word, which in NZ
  English is the month (*"8 Nov-Nov"*). [[GTC-368]]'s `whenLine`
  (`src/components/shared/EventDetails.tsx`) is the ruled rule for the event's details: *"Sat 19
  Dec 2026"*; *"Fri 18 Dec to Sun 20 Dec 2026"*; across two years each date with its year; the times
  as she typed them (*"…, 12:00 to 16:00"*, *"…, from 5:30pm"*, *"…, until 11:00pm"*); NZ time;
  *"Sept"*. The guest page's payload carries the venue's name but not its times. The directory is
  unauthenticated.

1. **C1 — a restore does not put back conflicts or acknowledgements.** The revision stores them, but
   `restoreFromRevision` clears the conflicts ("re-detected on next Check Plan") and the
   acknowledgements go with them (cascade).
2. **C2 — a restore drops `Item.displayOrder`** (and `createdAt`, `aiGenerated`, `userConfirmed`,
   `generatedBatchId`): its item list is explicit and leaves them out. The plan view sorts by
   `displayOrder`, so a restored plan would not come back "as she left it" without the fix.
3. **C3 — a restore would give an item, or a coordinator's place, back to someone removed from the
   event.** Removing someone deletes the PersonEvent and keeps the Person, so nothing fails; a
   coordinator restored that way would then be minted a link by `ensureEventTokens`.
4. **C4 — "a coordinator's link pointing at no team", more exactly:** `AccessToken.teamId` is set to
   null (ON DELETE SET NULL), so `resolveToken` refuses the link and it opens nothing; the next
   `ensureEventTokens` deletes it, and (step 3b) mints a teamless COORDINATOR token for anyone whose
   role is still COORDINATOR, which is refused too and pruned again at the next call.
5. **C5 — in the Moment flow nothing writes a person's team or role.** Generated teams have no
   coordinator (`resolveGeneratedTeamCoordinatorId` returns null); only a category she adds herself
   gets her as its coordinator (`onAddCategory`, `coordinatorId: event.hostId`). Other teams, roles
   and coordinators come only from the old dashboard. gather_dev: 57 memberships on a team across the
   7 EventSetup events (old test events).
6. **C6 — `createRevision` and `restoreFromRevision` each open their own transaction,** so "one step
   that cannot half-happen" needs their bodies moved into helpers that take the transaction. Their
   callers see no change.
7. **C7 — the underline is on the setup page's strip too,** where a not-needed door is a button that
   opens, not only on the pre-flight's.
8. **C8 — the nameless footer is on every guest page with no coordinator,** not only invites-only
   ones: generated teams never have one, and most guests on planned events have no team.
9. **C9 — `hostFirstName` can be empty** (`firstNameOf('')`), hence W7.
10. **C10 — the audit trail needs a Person:** `AuditEntry.actorId` references Person, so the setup
    route resolves the acting person through `ledgerActorForUser`, as regenerate-plan does.
11. **C11 — more suites pin this than the ticket names, and the build keeps each green:**
    `test:item-kind-capture` W (every item write stays in `src/lib/workflow.ts`); `test:security`
    suite 7 (exactly one change record, last, carrying the reason); `test:task-row-revision` S1
    (whole rows in the snapshot); `test:walkthrough-batch5-words` D2 (the pre-flight route's first
    `event.findUnique` and `eventSetup.findUnique` stay first); `test:walkthrough-batch3` P2 and
