# GTC-471 - The contacts page's two "unfinished household" lines

Branch feat/gtc-471-contacts-guard-lines, made from origin/main 241fa13 (the
merge of pull request 112, GTC-470), not tracking main. The number GTC-467 is
held by a local, unpushed branch (fix/gtc-467-join-page-guest-lookups), and
GTC-468 to GTC-470 are used, so this ticket is GTC-471.

All dates are NZ time.

## Why (the finding)

The founder, on staging, 22 Nov 2026, looking at GTC-470's pages. On the
contacts page, with a household being changed, tapping a later Moment says
"Save your changes first, or press Cancel." His words: "there is no cancel
button". Save and Cancel are on the household form, not on the contacts page.
The other line, for a household half typed ("Save this household first, or
clear what you've typed."), has the same fault. The other seven items of his
browser look for GTC-470 all passed.

Cause: GTC-470's `blockedLine` (src/lib/contacts/strip-facts.ts) hands the
Moments row the setup page's own two lines, W9 and W9b of GTC-367
(STRIP_WORDS.SAVE_NEW_HOUSEHOLD and STRIP_WORDS.SAVE_CHANGES). Those are right
on Moment 1, where the form's buttons are on the same screen. On the contacts
page they send her to buttons that are not there.

## The founder's ruling (22 Nov 2026, his words)

Asked: "On the contacts page, tapping a later Moment while a household is
being changed says "Save your changes first, or press Cancel." but Save and
Cancel are on the household form, not here. The half-typed case has the same
mismatch ("Save this household first, or clear what you've typed."). What
should the contacts page say instead?"

Answered: "Add "Go back and" to each (Recommended)", which was:

- Being changed: "Go back and save your changes first, or press Cancel."
- Half typed: "Go back and save this household first, or clear what you've
  typed."

These two sentences are ruled word for word. They use the straight apostrophe
the contacts words file uses.

Also approved by pasting the build prompt: the checks GTC-470 itself wrote or
amended (contacts-back, and the GTC-470 parts of contacts-page) may be
amended to expect the two new sentences; the two sentences go into the
GTC-460 word list with a dated note; and the commit and the push of the
branch.

## What changes

W44 and W45, continuing GTC-460's W-list (its last was W43):

- W44, a later Moment tapped on the contacts page while a household is being
  changed: "Go back and save your changes first, or press Cancel."
- W45, the same while a household is half typed (a draft is kept): "Go back
  and save this household first, or clear what you've typed."
