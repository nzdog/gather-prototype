# GTC-351 — text opt-out readers disagree: flag-only and table-only

> Filed 2026-10-01 at [[GTC-288]]'s plan, on founder ruling D15, verbatim: _"GTC-351: some readers
> read only the flag (wrap-up, the manual nudge's channel choice) and some only the table
> (invite-status, invite-detail). After this ticket they can disagree about a person added after a
> STOP; for example, a thank-you recorded as failed (OPTED_OUT) where a flagged guest would read
> skipped."_ Severity blank. **Not a deploy precondition.**

**Status:** Open — a stub. Not scoped.

---

### Signal

Do-Not-Touch Zone 7 holds a text opt-out in two places: the `SmsOptOut` table (account-wide on the
phone number since [[GTC-288]]) and the `Person.smsOptedOut` flag. [[GTC-301]] gave the merge one
definition, `smsOptedOutFact` in `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview.ts` (opted out if EITHER), and the
pre-flight, the carried-answer walk, the resend door and the board's email note read through it.
Four readers do not:

- **Flag only:** the wrap-up's channel choice (`generateWrapUpLinks` in `src/lib/wrap-up.ts`) and
  the manual nudge's channel choice (`chooseManualNudgeChannel` in
  `src/lib/sms/manual-nudge-recipient.ts`). Kept flag-only at [[GTC-288]] by plan ruling D7.
- **Table only:** `invite-status` (`getOptOutStatuses`) and `invite-detail`
  (`src/app/api/events/[id]/people/[personId]/invite-detail/route.ts`).

A STOP sets the flag on every Person holding the number at that moment ([[GTC-288]]). A person a
host adds AFTER the STOP has the table's row and no flag. So:

- the wrap-up picks text for them, `sendSms` refuses it `OPTED_OUT`, and the thank-you is tallied
  failed (or falls to email), where a flagged guest would have read skipped;
- the manual nudge picks text, and the route's own table check then falls to email or refuses;
- `invite-status` and `invite-detail` read them opted out, while a person with the flag and no row
  (the seed's shape) reads textable there.

Every path still fails safe: nothing is sent to an opted-out number. What disagrees is what is
chosen, counted and shown.

### Unknowns

1. Whether all four should read `smsOptedOutFact` with the account-wide table, and what that
   changes on screen and in the counts.
2. Whether the flag should be retired in favour of the table alone.
