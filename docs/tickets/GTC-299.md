# GTC-299 — The coordinator's team message

**Status:** Open — filed from the founder rulings of 2026-09-14, not started, **not scoped** |
**Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** not set at filing — the founder's to set.

decided**: this and the household status share are both *"here is the state of a group"* rather
than *"here is your job"*, and may be one object serving two audiences. **Do not scope either alone
until the founder rules** — [[GTC-189]] decision 18.

**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from ruling W in [[GTC-189]] (*Founder rulings — third
pass*). This ticket records the ruling and what it costs. It proposes no shape.

> THE COORDINATOR IS MESSAGED even when she is bringing nothing. Running a team is a job. Her
> message outlines everyone in her team and everything they are bringing.

> ⚠ THIS IS A THIRD MESSAGE TYPE and the ask register cannot produce it. The ask is written around
> what you are bringing; this one is written around who is on your team.

### Who this reaches

A team's coordinator — `Team.coordinatorId` names their `Person`. [[GTC-189]] ruling E
([[GTC-294]]) settled the coordinator who owns an item: a guest with an item and a job, given a
`PARTICIPANT` token beside the `COORDINATOR` one. **This ruling reaches the coordinator who owns
nothing.** ~~Whom the recipient model of 2026-09-13 does not message, because it messages every
adult who owns an item.~~

⚠ **CORRECTED on the founder's instruction — [[GTC-189]], *Founder answers — the slice 1 flags*,
answer 4.** The struck sentence was wrong, and it left this ticket disagreeing with [[GTC-189]]:

> GTC-299's "not a recipient" line is wrong and predates my coordinator ruling, which says she IS
> messaged. Correct GTC-299 rather than leaving two tickets disagreeing.

An adult with no items is asked — [[GTC-187]] decision 8, as built — and an itemless coordinator is
an adult with no items. *"Every adult who owns an item is messaged directly"* names who is messaged
directly; it does not leave the itemless out. What this ticket still owns is **which** message she
is sent, not **whether** she is sent one.

### What it costs — recorded on filing, not scoped

1. **A new register, and a founder ruling to shape it.** `composeAsk` and `askSystemVoice` in
   `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` compose around the recipient's own item names (with carried
   asks, after slice 2). A roster of other people's items is not that content. `compose.ts` has one
   `VoiceProfile`, `ASK_REGISTER`, and records a new register as a founder ruling ([[GTC-187]]
   decision 7; *THE MISSING REGISTER*, still open for the thank-you). The team message's movements,
   voices and handover are unruled in the same way.
2. ~~**A third kind of recipient in the chooser.** [[GTC-189]] slice 1's ask route returns email,
   text, carried by contact, host's list, or not a recipient, and a coordinator with no items is
   *not a recipient*. Either that chooser gains a recipient whose reason is a role rather than an
   item, or a second chooser sits beside it. Slice 1 is approved and not started.~~
   **CORRECTED — not a cost** ([[GTC-189]] slice 1 answer 4). Slice 1 is built, and
   `chooseAskRoute` in `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` routes an itemless coordinator like
   any adult — by email, by text, or onto the host's list — and never as *not a recipient*. The
   chooser decides how she is reached. Which message reaches her is cost 1's.
3. **A third `kind` on the outbound record.** Slice 4's row is *ask or chase leg*. The same cost as
   [[GTC-298]], and cheaper to meet before slice 4's migration than after.
4. **⚠ The coordinator who also owns items: one message or two.** [[GTC-187]] decision 1, as
   narrowed by [[GTC-189]] ruling D, is *one message per recipient*. Two messages break that. One
   message mixes the ask register with a team register, and `compose.ts`'s closed presets exist so
   that *"an invalid authorship combination cannot be constructed"*. Neither is available as
   written. **The founder's.**
5. **A roster by text is long.** Every name and item on a team, composed into one body, runs
   several segments, and one non-GSM-7 character in a name or item (a macron) drops every segment
   to 70 characters — the cost recorded in the header of `ask-register.ts`.
6. **The roster moves.** At the press it is a snapshot. Whether the coordinator hears about later
   changes is unruled; if so, that is another trigger with its own clock.
7. **The link.** The coordinator page exists (`src/app/c/[token]`, `COORDINATOR` scope), and a
   coordinator who owns nothing needs that link and no other. [[GTC-262]] is open on unauthenticated
   endpoints handing out `COORDINATOR` tokens, and ruling E ordered [[GTC-294]] after it for that
   reason. A message that sends the link to every coordinator meets the same ordering.
8. **The preview.** Hinge §1 makes the pre-flight show the real message, so [[GTC-189]] slice 3
   would show a second message type.
9. **Whether it is chased.** Not stated.

### Collision recorded

- **"That is the only case where a message carries someone else's ask."** [[GTC-189]], THE ASK.
  The team message names other people's items without asking for them, so as a status it carries
  no ask. If it ever asks the coordinator to act on those items, it does. Recorded so the line is
  watched.
