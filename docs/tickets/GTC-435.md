# GTC-435 - An unsaved household is kept while she is away

> Filed by Claude Code on 31 Oct 2026. The fifth of the household save
> tickets (GTC-431 to GTC-435) on fix/household-save, after
> [[GTC-434]] (9ff914d). No ticket file existed; the founder's prompt
> was the ticket.

## Why

A production tester typed a household with six children, left Moment 1
and came back, and every name was gone. The form held what she typed
only in memory.

What the code does (more exact than "inside-app navigation never
triggers the warning"):

- Every way out of Moment 1 is a full page load on purpose: "← Your
  events", the menu bar, the Moments strip doors. A computer's browser
  asks "Leave site?" (GTC-368); iPhone Safari never asks, so she lost
  the lot without a word.
- Moving between Moment 1's own screens (to the summary and back) just
  swaps the form out; no browser warns for that.
- Coming back from Your Events is an in-app route change (the row's
  router.push), which builds a fresh, empty form.

The "Leave site?" warning is unchanged.

## The founder's rulings (31 Oct 2026)

The plan, approved as written, with:

1. localStorage with the 7-day limit.
2. Words as proposed, except "Discard" on both lines instead of "Clear
   it". Keep "Got it" and "Carry on".
3. Add the one new prop to the setup page. If a suite that reads that
   file goes red in the dry run, report it and wait.
4. A draft for a removed household is dropped silently.
5. A draft for a household changed elsewhere is restored anyway.

After the dry run:

1. walkthrough-batch12 K4: option 1. One line right after C7 deletes
   its own household-draft entries from the browser. K4 stays word for
   word; the product's "Leave site?" is unchanged.
2. The "households loaded" flag on the setup page is approved.
   Safeguard: if the household list fails to load, keep the draft;
   drop a draft for a removed household only after the list has loaded
   successfully and the household is not in it.
3. household-save S6: first try to leave handleEditSave untouched by
   clearing the draft where the form resets after a good save. (Done:
   S6 is unchanged and green; no re-base.)
4. The pre-existing reds stay as they are; the lockfile one is written
   up as [[GTC-439]].

## Where the draft is kept

- This browser's localStorage only. Nothing is sent to the server: no
  new route, no new field in any request, no database change.
- One entry per signed-in person, event and form:

```
gather:household-draft:v1:<userId>:<eventId>:host          her own household's step
gather:household-draft:v1:<userId>:<eventId>:new           "Save & add another household"
gather:household-draft:v1:<userId>:<eventId>:edit:<id>     "Save changes" on a saved household
```

- The value repeats its user, event and form and when it was written;
  every read checks them again, so a draft never shows under another
  event or another person even if a key were mixed up.
- Kept: what she can type or choose (the fields formSnapshot lists,
  whether the partner and littles blocks are open, each row's
  personEventId on an edit, the order of the rows). Not kept: error
  messages, and the account email on her own step.
- Who is signed in: the root layout already reads the user for the
  menu bar; it now passes only the user's id to Providers, which offers
  it through `ViewerIdContext`. No auth code changes.

## When it is cleared, and the shared device

- A good save of that form. "Save & add another household", "Save
  changes" and Cancel all end in resetForm, which now clears the draft
  of the form it resets (the form checked at this opening). Her own
  step and "Save & move on →" clear theirs after a good save.
- "Discard" on the line; the form back to exactly how it opened.
- Every page load (Providers): with nobody signed in, every household
  draft in this browser goes, so a sign-out clears them (also an
  expired session, or a sign-out elsewhere, at the next visit); with
  someone signed in, every draft that is not theirs goes, and any of
  theirs older than 7 days.
- A shared device: while she stays signed in the draft is as visible
  as the rest of her open account. Once she signs out, it is gone. If
  someone else signs in on that browser, her drafts are removed, not
  hidden. localStorage cannot delete itself: a browser she never opens
  Gather in again keeps a draft until Gather is next opened there.
- A browser that refuses storage: every touch is caught; the form
  works exactly as before.

## What she sees

- Never silently. On every restore, a quiet line above the form
  (`role="status"`):
  - a new household or her own step, the form filled: "We kept what
    you typed here earlier. It isn’t saved yet." with "Got it" (hides
    the line, keeps the form) and "Discard" (the form back to how it
    opened, the draft gone);
  - a saved household she was changing, above the empty add form:
    "You were changing <name>’s household and hadn’t saved." with
    "Carry on" (opens it for editing, with her changes over it and the
    first line) and "Discard". Pressing Edit on its card does the same.
- The line shows on every return while a draft exists; "Got it" hides
  it for this visit only.
- The column's "being typed" card (GTC-365) shows the restored
  household, as while typing.
- Ruling 4 and its safeguard: a draft for a household no longer on the
  event is dropped silently, and only once the list has loaded
  successfully without it. The next kept change, if any, is offered.
- A failed save keeps the draft; a good save clears it.
