## [GTC-474] - Moment 1 / household form: the kids-without-jobs box

**Severity:** [ ] Critical [x] High [ ] Medium [ ] Low
Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] Yes [x] No

High (ruling 6): a wrong count of kids feeds the plan's headcount, and on a
phone there is no way to correct it. Only 1 and 10 to 20 can be reached.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
The host can clear the box under "How many kids without jobs?" and type a new
number. While she types, the box shows what she typed. A whole number from 1
to 20 is taken. An empty or out-of-range box goes back to the last good number
when she leaves it.

**Actual**
On a phone the box shows 1 and cannot be changed. Deleting the 1 does nothing;
typing 2 makes the number 12.

**Smallest failing case**
On a phone, open "🧒 Kid without a job", delete the 1, type 2: the box says 12.

---

### Reproduce

**Required state**
- A signed-in host, on Moment 1 (the household form) of any event of hers.
- Event state: any in which Moment 1's form is shown.
- Multi-actor: [ ] Yes [x] No

**Steps**
1. On a phone (no up and down arrows on number boxes), open the household
   form and press "🧒 Kid without a job".
2. Delete the 1 in the box under "How many kids without jobs?".
3. Type 2.

**Observe:** the 1 cannot be deleted; after typing 2 the box shows 12.

**Data setup**
None beyond an event with Moment 1 open. Reproduced here without a browser,
by running the form's live source with its real handlers (layer 474 of
tests/decide-later-form-test.ts), because this computer has no test database.

**Stripe note**
Not applicable.

---

### The cause

`Moment1InputForm` (src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx) held the box at
its `littleCount` state, a number. The box's onChange threw away every
keystroke that did not leave a whole number from 1 to 20. Deleting the 1 left
an empty box, which is not a number, so the keystroke was thrown away and the
box drew the 1 again. The next key, 2, landed beside that 1: 12, which is from
1 to 20, so it was taken. The box had no way to be empty. On a computer the
browser's up and down arrows hide this; a phone has none.

### The fix (as ruled)

- A second state, `littleTyped`, holds what she has typed while she types, so
  the box can be empty. `littleCount` stays the only count the save, the
  browser draft and the "N people added" total read.
- `littleCountFrom` (module level, in the form) decides what is taken: a whole
  number from 1 to 20. Anything else is shown and not taken.
- Leaving the box drops `littleTyped`, so it shows the last good number
  (ruling 2: she types 2 then 5 and leaves; it shows 2).
- Every place that already set the count from outside the box (bringing back a
  kept draft, opening a saved household, the reset after a save, "× remove")
  also clears `littleTyped`.
- Ruling 1, choice A: a "−" button before the box and a "+" button after it,
  each at least 44 by 44 pixels, named for a screen reader "One fewer kid
  without a job" and "One more kid without a job". "−" is off at 1 and "+" at
  20. A tap starts from the last good number. The question's label is tied to
  the box (id `m1-little-count`).
- The box is still a number box, 1 to 20, focused when it opens, with the same
  look. The form has no words file of its own, so the two names are written in
  the form beside the box.
- Pattern followed: `InlineQuantityEdit` in src/components/plan/Moment2PlanView.tsx
  already keeps typed text apart and decides on leaving.
