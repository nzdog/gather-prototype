## What was decided (per scope question 1–8)

**1. Disposition of `EventStatus.FROZEN` — collapse into `Event.sentAt`.**
Rejected: renaming the enum value to SENT (keeps sent-ness as an authored state and loses the
timestamp every downstream consumer needs), and keeping FROZEN permanently for read-compat
(reintroduces the `event.setup`-truthiness anti-pattern). The decisive finding is that the send
already exists in the code as `Event.inviteSendConfirmedAt` — it anchors the nudge machinery and is
set **before** freezing, so FROZEN is the redundant second ceremony, not the thing to rename.
`sentAt` is therefore a rename-with-backfill, not a new field. `checkFreezeReadiness` survives as
`checkSendReadiness` (its warnings are the Hinge §1 pre-flight's hunt for absence); its <80%
compliance reason requirement is deleted as a forbidden demand for justification.

**2. Disposition of `COMPLETE` — derived at read time from `endDate`; never written.**
A cron sweep that *sets* COMPLETE can drift; a derived predicate cannot, and §10.1 forbids nudges
firing after the date. A sweep is still needed for edge-triggered effects (the thank-you offer), but
the rule is "the sweep may create work; it may never set the phase." `wrap-up` migrates from
`status === 'FROZEN'` to `isComplete(event)`, loses its `confirmEarly` bypass, and stops writing
`status` — it writes `wrappedAt` only. COMPLETE also stops blocking mutations, because §8.8's
paper-then-system workflow requires post-event edits to be accepted.

**3. Consumer migration sequence — additive, then flip, then delete.**
Full site inventory by surface in the plan §4.2, sequenced Layer 0 (A2 foundation) → Layer 1 (server
gating) → Layer 2 (ledger wiring) → Layer 3 (host UI) → Layer 4 (token-route UI). Sites larger than a
predicate swap flagged individually (plan §4.4): `frozen-edit` (deleted and redistributed),
`guards.ts` (rewrites the security suite), the SMS eligibility predicates (invert), `p/ack` (semantic
inversion), `wrap-up`, `h/[token]/status`, `events/[id]` PATCH, and the god file.

**4. Ledger unification — `AuditEntry` is canonical; `InviteEvent` stays the channel log.**
They answer different questions; merging would put `SMS_BLOCKED_INVALID` beside "reassigned the beef,"
which Hinge §2 explicitly guards against. `frozen-edit`'s `InviteEvent`+`metadata.auditType` writes are
an expedient, corrected by giving the ledger a `reason` column. **Versions ARE ledger entries** —
`AuditEntry.sequence` is the version number, which is what makes storage tractable (est. 0.3–0.8 KB
per scoped entry vs ~60 KB per full `PlanRevision` snapshot; A2 measures both). Complete history is
reachable forward from the CONFIRMING snapshot or backward by inverting `before`. No deduplication:
the unit is a persisted mutation, not a keystroke. `PlanRevision` demoted to coarse checkpoints
(phase boundary, the press, AI regenerate) with the 5-item GET cap removed.

**5. Why-scope rule — five enumerated triggers, post-send only.**
T1 assignment created/moved/deleted (any response state); T2 person removed while holding assignments;
T3 item deleted while assigned; T4 an `ASK_FIELDS` change where `response != PENDING`; T5 date/venue
change. `ASK_FIELDS` = name, quantity family, dropOff family — exactly the Hinge §3 message contents.
Explicitly not touching: criticality toggles (§8.3: "touches nothing else"), description/notes,
team/day/order, unassigned items, additions without assignments, guest responses, and **any
`ASK_FIELDS` change while the assignment is still `PENDING` — which is the typo-fix case**. Rule is
actor-agnostic.

**6. Removal of the unfreeze path — deleted outright.**
The whole `status` branch of `PATCH /api/h/[token]/status` (including `unfreezeReason` and
`OVERRIDE_UNFREEZE`), `UnfreezeSection.tsx`, and the god-file/token-page gates. Replacement per Hinge
§2 is the material-change machinery (Epic F / GTC-183), and the handoff point is concrete:
`PATCH /api/events/[id]` is the only route writing date/venue, and it currently has no gating and no
audit logging at all. A3b adds a no-op `onMaterialChange()` hook there for F1 to fill. The gap window
between A3 and F1 is stated in the plan (§7.3), and F1 is promoted from a feature dependency to a
correctness dependency.

**7. Mini-sends — a per-person send timestamp is required, and it already exists in the wrong place.**
It cannot be derived (`PersonEvent` has no `createdAt`, and added ≠ sent-to). `Person.inviteAnchorAt`
already implements the semantic in five call sites — but lives on `Person`, so a person in two events
shares one anchor and the second event's clocks are wrong. A2 moves it to `PersonEvent.sentAt`.
`nudge24hSentAt`/`nudge48hSentAt` have the identical leak but move under E1 (zone-7-adjacent).
"Was this a mini-send?" is then `PersonEvent.sentAt > Event.sentAt` — no extra field; and truncation
by event date falls out of `Item.dropOffAt ?? Event.endDate` arithmetic with no special case.

**8. Migration and rollback — Epic A never destroys anything.**
Five steps; Epic A lands at step 3. Enum values `FROZEN`/`COMPLETE` are retained-but-unwritten for the
whole epic behind a compat shim in one function, so every commit is revertible by `git revert` alone
with no down-migration. The one-way steps (data migration + enum drop) are held back to a separate
later ticket (A4, unfiled) sequenced after Epic J. Backfill rules and the one live behaviour change on
deploy (existing CONFIRMING events with invites out are promoted to SENT) are documented in plan §9.

## Sub-decisions escalated to Nigel

**1. The security suite's FROZEN assertions must be rewritten — Do-Not-Touch zone 6 (Stop Condition 3).**
Four assertions in `tests/security-validation.ts` (1.2, 1.4, 3.4, 3.5) plus the FROZEN fixture in
`tests/security-fixtures.ts` assert the contract this change supersedes. They are not wrong, they are
obsolete — and zone 6 forbids weakening them without instruction. Replacement assertions proposed in
plan §12.1. **Neither file was touched.** A3a cannot land without approval, because deleting
`requireNotFrozen` breaks the preflight gate.

**2. Coordinator authority after the send.** Both specs speak only about Kate. Today coordinators are
hard-blocked from all item mutations when FROZEN (7 call sites). (a) same always-allow + ledger as the
host, or (b) the lock stays a wall for coordinators. **Recommend (a)** — the lock's stated reason
("drift under their feet must carry a story") applies identically — but this is a security-relevant
authority widening on token routes and is not mine to infer.

**3. Post-send AI regeneration.** No spec covers it; `regenerate` replaces the whole plan and would
orphan every assignment. (a) allow with checkpoint + per-change ledger + one why, (b) refuse (a hard
block, forbidden by §7), (c) allow and fire F1's re-ask for everyone. **Recommend (a) now, (c) once F1
exists.** Blocks A3b's treatment of the generation routes only.

Seven further interpretations — taken from the specs but worth review — are listed in plan §13,
including the load-bearing one: **a required reason never blocks the change** (Hinge §2's "required"
reconciled with Moment 4 §7's "never demands justification" — the UI always asks, the server always
accepts, and `reasonRequired: true, reason: null` records the gap honestly).
