# GTC-451 - A moved item shows on the new holder's card

> Opened by Claude Code on 4 Nov 2026 from Cowork's brief, on branch
> fix/glance-card-refresh made from origin/main f0b799a (the merge of
> pull request 85; same tree as 929a3da). Planned and stopped at step 4;

## Why

The founder, 4 Nov 2026, on the sample's host board: moved an item to
Aroha Ngata, opened her card, and the item was not there. After a
reload it was. Every card on the board is built from the board as the
page loaded it, and the live island (GlanceLive) repaints values only,
so after "Move" or "I'll do it" the new holder's card lacks the item
and the old holder's card still lists it until a reload. A bug for
every host, not only the sample.

The founder's word (4 Nov 2026), for this ticket only: GlanceLive's
header rule "values, never structure" and its three ceilings are
relaxed narrowly, for reassign and take over only, and only as far as
a person's card listing the items they now hold. Remind, resend,
replies and the poll keep every rule they have.
