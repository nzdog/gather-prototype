## ⚠ THE GENERAL CASE, FOUND WHILE MEASURING THE PRESS FOR SLICE 5f — AND IT IS THE WHOLE TICKET

> ⚠ **RULING M's `UNREACHABLE` RED DOES NOT FIRE FOR ANYBODY ON THE HOST LIST, WHICH IS EXACTLY WHO
> RULING M IS ABOUT.**

Ruling M's words are *"a line on the host's list reads red on the board, from the press."* [[GTC-189]]
slice 7a built that red by reading the fact off the `OutboundMessage` row the press wrote — a founder
ruling, and the right shape, because it means the board and the press cannot disagree.

**But the press writes no row for a host-list person.** `pressSend` creates one row per `addressed`
recipient, and `addressed` comes from `preview.recipients`; the chooser puts an unreachable person on
`preview.hostList` instead, which `pressSend` never iterates. **So there is no row to carry the
withheld code, and `UNREACHABLE` can only fire in the narrow case where the chooser's answer CHANGES
between the press and the drain** — a person whose email is deleted inside the press window.

**Measured on the one pressable board in `gather_dev`, 2026-09-19** ("Henderson Family Christmas
2025", 43 memberships): **38 recipients and 4 on the host list, all four `NO_CHANNEL`.** After a press
those four would read **AMBER** — *"with Gather"* — about people Gather has already decided it cannot
reach. **That is live, not latent**, and it is why this ticket was raised to High before it was finished.

⚠ **AND IT IS NOT A DEFECT IN SLICE 7a's MECHANISM** — the mechanism does what it was ruled to do. It
is that ruling M's population and the press's row-writing population are different sets, and nobody
noticed until the press was measured. Case 1 below is the sharpest instance; **the general case is
every host-list person.**

---

## ⚠ THE FALSEHOOD, WHICH IS THE ONE RULING J ALREADY NAMED

**AMBER MEANS GATHER HAS A NEXT MOVE.** That is the whole of it — the summary sentence counts amber as
*"with Gather"*, and the strip carries no why because there is nothing for the host to do.

**In both cases below, Gather has no next move and never had one.** Nobody was asked, nothing is on
its way, and the board says *with Gather*. [[GTC-189]] ruling J called that exact reading wrong — *"the
amber is wrong"* — and ruling M answered it with the red that slice 7a built. **These two are the cases
that red cannot reach.**

---

## Case 1 — a child whose household has no messageable contact (the sharpest instance)

**Their ask reached nobody.** The chooser refuses the carrier route — `NO_CARRIER` when the household
has no messageable contact, or `HOUSEHOLD_MUTED` when it is switched off — and slice 1 answer 5 sends
the item to the host's list. **No message is sent and no row is written.**

**So their rows read AMBER**, and the host reads *"with Gather"* about a child nobody was asked about.

⚠ **AND SLICE 7a's MECHANISM STRUCTURALLY CANNOT SEE IT.** The founder's ruling for 7a was that the
delivery fact is READ BACK off the row the press wrote, so the board and the press cannot disagree.
Ruling S then gives a child their CARRIER's row. **A child with no carrier has no row to inherit** — and
children never have rows of their own, because `pressWillMessage` excludes them. The fact is missing
rather than wrong.

---

## Case 2 — `NO_LINK`: reachable, with nothing to send

`withheldWhy = 'NO_LINK'` means the press promised a link and issuance did not produce one, or a token
was revoked between the press and the drain. **The person is perfectly reachable and Gather has nothing
to send them.**

Slice 7a read it as **not** a red, deliberately: ruling M's red is about a person *nobody can reach*,
and this person can be reached. `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts` says so at the entry rather than
leaving it to be found. **But nothing was sent, so the amber is false here too** — and unlike case 1 the
row exists and carries the reason, so this one is a mapping decision rather than a missing fact.

⚠ **The pre-flight already has words for it** — slice 5b's *"I can't give them a link, so I won't
message them at all"* — so the host is told BEFORE the press. What is unruled is whether the board says
anything AFTER it.

---

## Measured in `gather_dev`, 2026-09-19

- **60 CHILD memberships, 36 of them holding an item** — so 36 children are carried today.
- ⚠ **ZERO of them lack a carrier.** Every household holding a child with an item has a messageable
  contact, so **case 1 has no instance in this database.** The falsehood is latent rather than live.
- `OutboundMessage` holds **0 rows**, so case 2 has no instance either — nothing has been pressed since
  slice 5 shipped.

**Both are therefore real in shape and unobserved in fact**, which is the honest reason this is Medium
rather than High and the reason it does not block anything.

---

## ⚠ Why it is a question and not a fix

**Because answering either one reopens a choice the founder just made.**

Ruling M's own aside said `read.ts` would consume slice 1's chooser to know who is unreachable. Slice
7a **superseded that**: the press records the fact and the board reads it back, *"so the board and the
press cannot disagree by construction."*

**Case 1 cannot be answered that way.** There is no row, so the only source is the chooser, re-run at
read time — which is the path that was just closed, and which costs what slice 7a measured:
`resolveCarriedSubjects` runs five queries per carrier. The cheap version calls
`resolveHouseholdChannel` and asks *"did this child get a carrier at all"*, which is **one pure call and
no query** — but it is still the board deriving a send decision rather than reading one, and that is the
line the ruling drew.

**So the question is: may the board derive an unreachability the press did not record, in the one case
where the press writes nothing at all?** That is a ruling, not an implementation.

---

## The three shapes

1. **Leave them.** The board understates: three populations read amber when nobody was asked. ⚠ The
   general case has **four instances on the one pressable board today**, so this shape is not the
   no-op it looks like.
2. **Derive case 1 at read time** with the pure household rule — no query, no chooser ladder — and give
   the child `UNREACHABLE`. ⚠ The board then holds one fact it derived and one it read, and the next
   reader has to know which is which.
3. **Make the press record it.** If the press wrote a row for a carried child it could not route — a
   withholding with `NO_CARRIER`, on the CHILD's membership — then 7a's mechanism sees it with no change
   at all. ⚠ That is a change to the press, on an event ruled ONE ACT NO RECALL, and it means children
   have `OutboundMessage` rows for the first time, which several fences currently assert they do not.

**Shape 3 is the executor's lean and it is NOT a recommendation**, because it puts a row on a
membership the whole outbound model excludes, and the fences that exclude it were written deliberately.

  is about, and both are keyed on closed unions so a change is visible.

  no message and holds no token; that is ruled and is not what this ticket questions.

## Unknowns

1. **Whether a household with a child and no messageable contact can exist through the real capture
   flow at all**, or only by direct writes. Zero instances in `gather_dev` may mean the capture surface
   prevents it — in which case case 1 is unreachable code and the answer is shape 1.
2. **Whether the host's list already tells her.** Slice 1 answer 5 sends a carrierless child's item to
   the host, and the pre-flight shows the host list. If she is already told there, the board's amber is
   a duplicate omission rather than the only one.
3. **Whether `NO_LINK` can occur after [[GTC-316]]**. It needs issuance to fail or a token to be
   revoked inside the press window; nothing in the tree produces it today.
4. ⚠ **Whether shape 3 answers the general case as well as case 1.** If the press wrote a withholding
   row for every host-list person — not only carried children — slice 7a's mechanism would see all of
   them with no change at all, and the board would read the code the chooser actually produced. **That
   is the single edit that would close this whole ticket**, and it is a change to the press, on an act
   ruled ONE ACT NO RECALL, so it is not the executor's.
