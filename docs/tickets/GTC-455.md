# GTC-455 - Listening follow-ups

**Status: built and committed (15e86af), approved by the founder 6 Nov 2026 (NZ time).** No deploy needed.

## What and why

An independent review of GTC-453 and GTC-454, run by a separate session on the founder's Mac
against `gather_demo` (5 Nov 2026), found no defect in what the report or the tally does, and
seven things worth fixing in the tests and the words. The founder approved this ticket and chose
option A for the single-sample tally line (5 Nov 2026: "A, and GTC-455 approved to build").

## What changed

- `tests/listening-report-test.ts`
  - **C2 matches whole words.** It matched any seeded word of 3 or more letters anywhere in
    the report, so on the founder's `gather_demo` (582 seeded values) a short person name
    sitting inside one of the report's own label words failed it: 37 passed, 1 failed, a false
    alarm, not a leak. Now a seeded word counts only when no letter or digit touches it.
  - **W1-W6 pin the matcher** on invented words: a short name inside a label word is not a
    leak; the same name as a whole word is; case does not hide one; an email, a phone number
    with its plus sign, and a multi-word title are caught.
  - **C5 and C6 skip, said aloud, on a database with no ordinary gathering** (a local
    `gather_demo` can be all samples). They crashed there before. A skip is printed and never
    counted as a pass.
- `tests/demo-cleanup-test.ts`
  - **V3b checks "types", not assumes it.** `importsOf` reads only single-quoted
    `import ... from` lines, and V3b compared only the list of names. V3b now also requires the
    client import in `tally.ts` to be `import type`, and that neither `tally.ts` nor
    `entry-stage.ts` uses a form `importsOf` cannot see (double quotes, `export ... from`,
    `require(`, a dynamic `import(`). The shared `importsOf` helper is unchanged.
- `src/lib/demo/tally.ts` - **comment only.** It promised "never a value from one sample". A run
  that deletes one sample prints that sample's own counts, with no id, name or address. Kept,
  per the founder's option A; the comment now says so.
- `docs/tickets/GTC-453.md`, `docs/tickets/GTC-454.md` - corrections, each listed in a
  "Corrections (GTC-455)" section: "merge to master" is now main; "four files" is five; why
  Zones 2 and 3 are untouched is now said; B3's cause is traced.
- `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` - the two new tests and what each needs; the `[demo-tally]` line
  on the demo clean-up's cron row; B3 and D8 recorded as the seed-name drift; and a dated note
  that `main` is the integration branch, kept separate from the older `master` text, which
  stays for history. Its Railway paragraph records the founder's statement of 6 Nov 2026 (NZ
  time), labelled as the founder's and not recorded by the repo: production's automatic build is
  off; staging deploys automatically from `main`.
- `docs/tickets/GTC-454.md` Shipping, corrected to match: merging to `main` is the staging
  deploy, a push to this branch reaches neither environment, and production is deployed by hand
  once staging looks right.

No app behaviour changed, no schema change, no migration, no zone touched, nothing deployed.
