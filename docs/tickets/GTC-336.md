### Signal — two defects, one cause

**1. The door refuses a carried child with a false sentence.** A carried child can read red
`NOT_DELIVERED` through ruling S (*"Ollie's strip goes red when the message carrying his ask
bounced"*). `doorOffered` in `src/lib/glance/actions.ts` keys on reasons, so the child's panel
offers the door. The door's GET — `resolveSubject` in `src/lib/press/resend.ts` — reads ASK rows for
the CHILD's own membership (`personEventId: membership.id`). A child never has one, so
`deliveryFactFrom` finds no failure and it refuses 409 `NOTHING_FAILED`:

> Their last message did not fail, so Gather has nothing to try again.
> (`RESEND_REFUSAL_WORDS.NOTHING_FAILED` in `src/lib/press/resend-door.ts`)

That is false for that child: the message carrying their ask did fail. Confirmed by reading the
code at [[GTC-305]]; the runtime check is recorded in GTC-305's Evidence.

**2. Ruling S's lookup reaches children the message never carried.** `readEventGlance` finds a
child's carrier with `carrierMembershipFor` in `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts`, which follows the
household's CONTACT (`contactPersonEventId`, else PRIMARY_CONTACT) — not the chooser's ladder. Its
docstring names that limit. So three children inherit a bounced ASK row their item was never in,
and read red `NOT_DELIVERED`:

- a host's-household child whose household contact is another adult (under ruling A the child's
  item went to the host's list, not into that adult's message);
- a host's-household child whose contact is the host, when the host holds an A2 carried row that
  bounced;
- a child with no items, in a household whose contact's ASK row bounced (nothing was ever asked of
  them).

Each is pinned AS BUILT — labelled as the built behaviour, not the wanted one — in
`tests/board-not-chased-test.ts` layer D (the three *"AS BUILT (point 3)"* assertions). They flip
when this is fixed, and they should. The door is then offered to these children too, and refuses
them with the same false sentence.

> ⚠ **2026-09-30, checked against HEAD (e4414a0) at the plan — two corrections to the stub.**

> - **The second child is latent, not live.** The press writes the host-as-carrier no ASK row: the

>   `askRowPopulation` leaves the host out, and [[GTC-297]] owns that link. The case exists only as a test

>   `chooseAskRoute` answers `HOST_LIST` with `carrierId` for a carrier who holds no usable mobile —
>   the same carrier, no longer `CARRIED`. So the board's lookup follows the chooser's carrier on
>   both routes.

does, so only children whose ask really rode in the failed message inherit its failure), or the
door is not offered on a child's strip at all and the carrier's own strip carries it.

## Evidence (Executor-Completed)

**Root cause confirmed**

Two defects, one cause. `readEventGlance` in `src/lib/glance/read.ts` found a child's carrier with
`carrierMembershipFor` in `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts`, which followed the household's contact
(`resolveHouseholdChannel`), not the chooser. So a child inherited the contact's ASK row whether or
not their ask was in it: the host's-household child, the child holding nothing, and (found at the
plan) the child of a switched-off household. And `doorOffered` in `src/lib/glance/actions.ts` keyed
on reasons only, so a red child was offered ruling U's door, whose `resolveSubject` in
`src/lib/press/resend.ts` read the child's own ASK rows. A child never has one, so it answered
`NOTHING_FAILED`: *"Their last message did not fail"*, false of that child.

**The fix, as ruled**

- The carrier is the chooser's (Q2). `readAskPreview` in `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview.ts` records
  `askRoutes`, the `chooseAskRoute` answer its walk already computes, for every membership.
  `carrierOfAsk` in `delivery-fact.ts` reads it: a `CARRIED` route's recipient, or a `HOST_LIST`
  route's `carrierId` (the carrier who cannot be reached, or whose address was blocked after the
  bounce), else no carrier. `carrierMembershipFor` is deleted.
- No door on a child's card (Q1). `doorOffered` reads `householdRole` and answers false for a child
  (`isChildMembership`). The remind stays withdrawn on those reds; reassign and take-over stay.
- The words (Q4). `carriedChildNoteFor` and `CARRIED_CHILD_WORDS` in `delivery-fact.ts` compose W1
  to W4; `read.ts` sets `GlancePerson.carrierNote` for a red child with a carrier;
  `src/components/glance/PersonSurface.tsx` shows it grey under the why-line.
- The route (Q1). `resolveSubject` refuses a child 409 `CHILD_NOT_MESSAGED` right after the
  membership is found, before `NOT_PRESSED`, on GET and POST. W5 is its sentence in
  `RESEND_REFUSAL_WORDS` in `src/lib/press/resend-door.ts`.

**What each flipped child reads now (Q2)**

- The host's-household child whose contact is another adult: NOT_CHASED [HOST_HOUSEHOLD_CHILD].
- The host's-household child whose contact is the host, with the A2 row bounced: the same.
- The child with no items: NOT_CHASED [CHILD_WITHOUT_ITEM].
- The child of a switched-off household (plan question 1): exactly what a like child reads when
  the contact's message arrived (the suite compares the two).
