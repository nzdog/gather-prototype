# GTC-332 — the Resend webhook, for the complaint the poll stops listening for

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-09-27 at [[GTC-189]] slice 8a's close. Not scoped.
**Severity:** proposed Medium.

---

### Signal

Slice 8a learns a spam complaint from the delivery poll (`pollOne` in
`src/lib/email-delivery/delivery-poll.ts`), which stops asking about a message **24 hours after
acceptance** (`POLL_STOP_AFTER_MS`, a founder ruling of 2026-09-19). Resend's `email.complained` is
documented as arriving after `delivered`, and nothing bounds how long after. So a complaint made on
day three is never read from the message that drew it.

The backstop, built at 8a: the next email Gather sends to that address — a reminder (slice 8b), a
door resend, a late arrival's invitation, another host's invitation — comes back `suppressed`,
which the poll reads and records as a block (neutral words only, and no per-event opt-out, because a
suppression does not say whether it was a complaint).

**What the backstop costs:** one more message into the address, which Resend accepts and does not
deliver, and the host whose invitation was reported never sees ruling 3's middle sentence — the
block's reason reads `SUPPRESSED`, not `COMPLAINED`.

### The fix, named and not designed

Resend's webhook (`email.complained`, `email.bounced`, `email.suppressed`), verified by its
signature scheme. It needs a public URL, a new public route (the route-scan pins move), a shared
secret, and the webhook payload's shape observed — [[GTC-323]]'s open question.

### Unknowns

1. Whether `last_event` on a polled message moves from `delivered` to `complained` at all
   ([[GTC-323]]).
2. The webhook payload's real shape, which is not in this repo.
