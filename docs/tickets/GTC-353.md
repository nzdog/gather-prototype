# GTC-353 — three email paths write no `OutboundMessage`, so their bounces are never learned

> Filed 2026-10-02 at [[GTC-258]]'s plan, on founder ruling Q14 (*"yes, as you default them"*), the
> plan's wording: _"the follow-up's, the thank-you's and the host's nudge's own emails still write no
> `OutboundMessage`, so their bounces are never learned."_ Severity blank. **Not a deploy
> precondition.**

**Status:** Open — a stub. Not scoped.

---

### Signal

[[GTC-258]] gave the TEXT sends of the "please decide" follow-up (`src/lib/sms/decide-by-sender.ts`),
the thank-you (`src/lib/wrap-up.ts`) and the host's own nudge
(`src/app/api/events/[id]/people/[personId]/nudge/route.ts`) an `OutboundMessage` each, so TNZ's
delivery report can reach them. Their EMAIL sends were left as they were: `sendDecideByEmail` and
`sendNudgeEmail` are called with no row written.

The email delivery poll (`src/lib/email-delivery/delivery-poll.ts`, [[GTC-289]]) reads only
`OutboundMessage` rows. So a bounce, a suppression or a spam complaint on one of those emails is never
read, and never writes the `EmailBlock` ([[GTC-324]]) that would stop every other host emailing that
address. (The one exception since [[GTC-258]]: the single email RETRY of a failed follow-up or
thank-you text is an `OutboundMessage`, and is polled.)

### Not decided here

Whether each of these emails gets a row (and so a poll, and so a block), and what the host is shown
when one bounces.
