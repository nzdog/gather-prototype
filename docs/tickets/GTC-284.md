# GTC-284 — regenerate-plan under the shared predicate

**Status:** Open — needs scoping when pulled | **Effort:** TBD

*Filed by [[GTC-237]], 2026-09-12.*

## What happened, and why this is "prove" rather than "fix"

[[GTC-236]] shipped `regenerate-plan` with a preserve predicate reading `source` and
`isProtected` — and **not** `assignment.response`. A `GENERATED` item a guest had already
accepted was disposable to it. That is the same gap [[GTC-237]] closed in `finalize-plan`, in
the route that was otherwise the careful one.

The founder's Ruling 1 on 2026-09-12 said file-don't-fix: widen the preserve set in
`finalize-plan`, leave `regenerate-plan` to its own ticket. **The extraction overtook that
ruling.** Once both routes call `disposableItemWhere`, the helper carries one predicate and
`regenerate-plan` inherits the wider preserve set whether or not the ticket intends it; the
alternative was to parameterise the one module whose purpose is that there is only one answer,
so that a caller could keep the wrong one. Founder ruling, same day: **inherit.** The ruling was
right about not widening scope and wrong about what was still possible once both routes shared
a definition — recorded here rather than quietly reversed.

So the behaviour change has already landed. What has not landed is the evidence for it.

## What this ticket owes

- **Assertions on `regenerate-plan` itself.** `tests/plan-write-preserve-test.ts` exercises
  `applyPlanSections` directly and proves the predicate. Nothing yet proves the route around it:
  scope filtering to one team, the `noop` short-circuit's count now that fewer rows are
  regenerable, the 422 on no engaged categories, and the checkpoint ordering.
- **The `noop` path specifically.** `regenerableCount === 0` short-circuits before the
  checkpoint and the AI call, and its copy says "every item in this plan was added or edited by
  you." That sentence is now wrong in one case: a plan of untouched generated items that guests
  have all answered is also a no-op, and she did not add or edit any of it. The copy has to grow
  a second reason or lose its specificity.
- **The comment that explains the predicate to the next reader.** `disposableItemWhere` carries
  the reasoning; `regenerate-plan` carries a pointer to it and should carry the "this route now
  deletes strictly fewer rows" note in its own voice.

## Adjacent, and deliberately not folded in

`aiCallsUsed` is incremented INSIDE `regenerate-plan`'s transaction and OUTSIDE
`finalize-plan`'s. [[GTC-237]] recorded the difference at both sites and changed neither.
Settling it belongs in one ticket covering both routes, and it is not this one unless the
scoping says so.

All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. Do not narrow `disposableItemWhere` to
`ACCEPTED` — see the warning at that symbol. `DECLINED` and `MAYBE` preserve the row on purpose.
