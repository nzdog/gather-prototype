## SCOPED 2026-10-03 — two founder rulings (in Cowork)

*Asked in Cowork on 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03. Verbatim.*

Context given, in summary: the founder asked for the Moment flow to run from Moment 1 to 4 through
the Hinge. There is no Moment 3 screen: the V2 stages end at Moment 2's plan view, whose only way
on is "Go to dashboard" ([[GTC-235]]), the V1 dashboard, where assignment happens on the old
People / teams board.

**Ruling 1.** The founder, verbatim: **"I want to build moment 3"**.

**Ruling 2.** *"One question before I set Moment 3 up for Claude Code. Your document lets one item
have several people ("Setup crew — 3 people"). Today Gather gives each item one person, and the
send, the reminders and the board all rely on that. For this first version of Moment 3, which
should it be?"* Options: *One person per item (Recommended)*; *Several people per item now*. The
founder chose, verbatim: **"One person per item (Recommended)"** — *"Each item has one person, as
Gather works today, so the send, the reminders and the board need no changes. Anything that needs
three people becomes three items ("Setup crew" three times), each with its own person. Several
people per item can come later, with teams."*

### What the rulings settle

- **Scope: the document's Phase 1.** The opening screen (the arc and "Now. Who's on what."), the
  two-panel direct assignment, tap-to-assign in both directions, unassigning, the counter, the
  unassigned and unplaced indicators, "Just attending", kids with jobs, Gather's opt-in
  suggestions in direct mode, the behaviour details, and the completion. **Not** the team builder
  (Phase 2), and **not** the document's "Multiple people per item" section (Ruling 2).
- **Data: one `Assignment` per item, unchanged.** `Assignment.itemId` is unique and the row already
  joins to `Person`, not `PersonEvent`, as the document's technical note asks. The document's
  "Likely schema additions" for direct mode are not needed for one person per item.

### For the plan (Cowork's notes, not rulings)

1. **Reuse before building.** `POST /api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/assign` already assigns an item
   to a person ([[GTC-196]]: a reason only after the send, never blocking). Inspect it and the V1
   board before adding endpoints; the document's endpoint list is a suggestion, as it says itself.
2. **Where it sits.** The V2 stages are `opening | moment1 | moment2-step1 | plan`
   (`src/lib/setup/entry-stage.ts`). Moment 3 follows Moment 2's approval in the same flow, and a
   returning host lands back where she was ([[GTC-235]]'s rule). Propose the stage and the routing.
3. **Where "All sorted →" goes.** The document says the arc then shows Moment 4; the Hinge spec puts
   the pre-flight and the press between 3 and 4. Default: "All sorted" leads to the pre-flight.
   Propose; the founder rules.
4. **The arc's words.** `MomentArc` labels 3 "Who's bringing what?"; the document says "Who's on
   what?". Propose the change for the founder's ruling; it shows on Moments 1 and 2 too.
5. **Words.** Every host-visible sentence in the document is the founder's: "Now. Who's on what.",
   "[X] of [Y] items assigned.", "Just attending", "Want me to suggest?", "All sorted →", the three
   completion dialogs, "No plan items yet." and "← Back to the plan". Use them as written. Anything
   new is proposed for ruling.
6. **"Just attending".** Nothing in the code has it. Say where it is stored (on `Person`, not
   `PersonEvent`: [[GTC-104]]) and what the send does today for an adult with no item. Default: it
   changes only Moment 3's count, never who the press sends to. If that cannot hold, ask.
7. **Kids.** Kids without jobs don't appear; kids with jobs are assignable ([[GTC-112]]). A child's
   ask is still carried in the grown-up's message (the recipient rule): Moment 3 changes nothing
   about who is messaged.
8. **Suggestions.** Structural only, no AI call. For each unassigned item, the person with the
   fewest assignments and no dietary conflict, shown as ghost assignments with ✓ and ×. Say how a
   dietary conflict is decided from the data that exists.
9. **The conflict system's auto-recheck** fires after assignments change, as the document asks.
   Say how.
10. **The old dashboard** is out of scope except where it must change for Moment 3 to work; its
    assigning stays. Joining the Hinge to the board, and giving the board a way in, are separate.
11. **Multi-person items (Ruling 2).** No editor for "Setup crew — 3 people". If Moment 2 can
    produce an item that needs several people, say what Moment 3 shows for it.
12. **The RED list** names every check that can only pass once committed: carried-child-door F
    (zone files), and if `prisma/` changes, board-not-chased G and the zone checks in gone-quiet
    and decide-by-email.

### Acceptance (assertions to hold, not a design)

*Written at scoping, 2026-10-03, from the document and the two rulings.*

1. After Moment 2's plan is approved, the host's next screen is Moment 3: the arc, "Now. Who's on
   what.", and the assignment interface in direct mode, with no button in between.
2. Tapping an unassigned item then a person, or a person then an item, gives that item to that
   person: the item shows the name and the person's count rises. No toast, no dialog.
3. Tapping an assigned item's × takes the person off it; it reads unassigned again. No dialog.
4. An item has at most one person (Ruling 2).
5. The counter reads "[X] of [Y] items assigned."; unassigned items and people with nothing are
   marked, subtly.
6. "Just attending" takes a person out of the unplaced count; kids without jobs never appear; kids
   with jobs can be given items.
7. "Want me to suggest?" shows structural ghost assignments that the host accepts (✓) or dismisses
   (×). Nothing is assigned without her.
8. "All sorted →" is always visible, and the three completion dialogs read as the document writes
   them.
9. An item given in Moment 3 is the same `Assignment` the pre-flight and the press read, so the send
   works unchanged.
10. Nothing sends for real.

### Note, 2026-10-03 — team leads

The founder chose a middle layer for team leads: the household is the team and its contact is the
lead, told (not asked) what her household has been asked to bring. Filed as [[GTC-356]], a change
to the send. **Moment 3 Phase 1 needs no team or lead step for it**; the team builder stays in
Phase 2, after launch.

**What Q1 and Q2 settle, as the plan put them:**

- **Q1 (a)** — the assign route refused every Moment 1 guest a dish: Moment 1 creates every guest's
  membership with no team, and `mayHoldRow` (`src/lib/assignment/same-team.ts`) allowed an ITEM row
  only to someone on its own team. A third exception: a person on no team may hold any row when the
  actor holds host authority (HOST or COHOST). A teamed guest stays fenced to their team, and a
  coordinator gains nothing. The route file is unedited. Consequence, accepted with the ruling: the
  board's reassign picker reads the same rule, so it now offers a host or co-host every guest on no
  team. Its code does not change; the names it offers do.
- **Q2** — "Just attending" is `PersonEvent.justAttending Boolean @default(false)`, per event.
  GTC-159 (`reconcileMembers`, b73f140) keeps membership ids across a household edit, and `Person`
  is global across events.

**A further ruling at GREEN (in chat, 2026-10-03) — test:host-holds-items.** The first GREEN gate
