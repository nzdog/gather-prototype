## Scope

(A); QB2 stands). In the pre-flight, right after the pretend panel, a
new `SampleBoardLink` in its own file draws W42 to /plan/[id]/glance,
beside the panel, not in it. On the board, a sample note W43 says what

Host / Guest toggle (W44 to W47). The fixed guest is Aroha Ngata when

copy, so the visitor sees the guest's own page and answers through the
real routes. On a copy the guest page gains W48 back to the board.
