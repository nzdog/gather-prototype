## Scope

The demo's two development-only guards read the switch alone. In
src/app/demo/page.tsx, `process.env.NODE_ENV === 'development' &&
isDemoStartOn()` becomes `isDemoStartOn()`, and `await connection()`
makes the page dynamic by its own word. src/app/demo/new/layout.tsx
gives `notFound()` unless `isDemoStartOn()`, with the same
`connection()`. GTC-430's "Under construction" stays as the off state
(Q2). The privacy line W58 goes on /demo/new under its start button.
The reset route keeps its production 404, and "Go to it →" is kept.
The switch's row in GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS says it now opens the page,
the sample page and the route. The turn-on and fast turn-off steps are
the founder's, in the order the plan gives. New suite test:demo-public
(about 25).
