## The state, measured 2026-09-19

- **`prisma/seed.ts` creates ZERO households.** The Henderson board carries **43 memberships,
  every one with `householdRole: null` and `householdId: null`.**
- Across the WHOLE database: **0 households have a picked `contactPersonEventId`, and 0 have
  `messagesMuted` set.** Not zero on the pressable board — zero anywhere, seeded or accumulated.
- 96 households exist, all on GTC-133 and GTC-192 boards and one hand-made DRAFT, created by
  `household.create` with nothing else set.
- 60 `CHILD` memberships exist, none of them on the pressable board.

---

## ⚠ What that means, and it is more than a coverage gap

**The ask model has two halves.** An adult is messaged directly; a child's item is carried in
their household contact's message — *"the route by which a child's job reaches an adult"*, which is
[[GTC-189]]'s founder ruling of 2026-09-13 and the thing the household exists for at the ask.

**The second half has never run against data.** On the only board the press can run against, the
chooser's child branch is unreachable, because `isMessageableRole(null)` admits every membership as
an adult. So every one of these is exercised only by a suite's own fixture:

- **Ruling A as corrected** — a child of the host's OWN household is hers; a child of another
  household whose contact is the host is carried to her.
- **Ruling A2** — the host as carrier, *"the one case in the model where the host receives an
  ask"*, and the single exception to [[GTC-256]] Ruling 8.
- **Ruling S** — a carrier's bounce turns the carried child's rows red, which overturned Moment 4
  §8.7 and is the reason `carrierMembershipFor` exists in `delivery-fact.ts`.
- **The household mute switch** (`resolveHouseholdMuted`), and slice 1 answer 5's ruling that a
  muted household's child comes to the host's list.
- **Four why-codes**: `NO_CARRIER`, `HOUSEHOLD_MUTED`, `HOST_HOUSEHOLD_CHILD`,
  `CHILD_WITHOUT_ITEM` — each of which has words on the pre-flight and a branch in the chooser.
- ⚠ **And `resolveHouseholdChannel`'s PICKED-CONTACT branch specifically.** With
  `contactPersonEventId` null everywhere, every carrier resolution in this repo has gone through the
  FALLBACK. The branch the capture surface writes has never been read back.

**So every press measurement recorded in [[GTC-189]] — 38 recipients, 4 on the host list, 37 email
to 1 text, the channel split, the withheld codes — was taken on a board that cannot produce a
carried ask.** The numbers are true and they describe half the model.

---

## ⚠ What fixing it costs, which is why this is a ticket and not an edit

**Seeding households moves the board every recent measurement is anchored on.** That is the whole
of the cost and it is not small:

- `38 recipients / 4 host list` becomes some other pair, because children stop being counted as
  adults and their items start being carried.
- [[GTC-322]]'s backfill population (86 rows over 8 events) was computed from the current shape.
- [[GTC-325]]'s own evidence, `tests/host-list-row-test.ts`'s expectations, slice 5b's withholding
  arithmetic, slice 5f's *"38 recipients, 37 rejected, 1 withheld"* — all of them describe a board
  with no households on it.
- ⚠ **And a re-seed is the only way to apply it**, because a household is not a column you set: it
  is a membership graph with roles, a picked contact and a mute flag. Retrofitting one onto the
  existing board by hand is inventing data, which is the thing [[GTC-292]] and the standing warning
  both exist about.

**So the change is: edit the seed, reseed, and re-baseline every press measurement in GTC-189
against the new board.** The re-baselining is the work, not the seed edit.

---

## The shapes, and none is obviously right

1. **Leave it.** The carrier half stays fixture-only. Cheapest, and every future press measurement
   goes on describing half the model without saying so.
2. **Seed households on a SECOND board**, leaving Henderson exactly as it is. No measurement moves,
   and the carrier half gets data. ⚠ But the press can only run on one board at a time and the new
   one would have to be `CONFIRMING` and unpressed to be pressable, so there would be two pressable
   boards and a question about which one the measurements mean.
3. **Seed households on Henderson** and re-baseline. The board becomes the realistic one it is
   supposed to be, and every number in GTC-189's evidence needs a note saying which board it
   describes.

⚠ **Shape 2 is the executor's lean and it is NOT a recommendation.** It gets the coverage without
invalidating a week of measurements, and it costs a second board somebody has to remember exists.
