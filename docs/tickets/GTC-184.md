# GTC-184 — G1: Reconcile dietary models; add per-person capture at Moment 1

**Status:** Open | **Branch:** TBD | **Commit:** [pending] (filed `e5f6e82`) | **Effort:** max, schema + AI prompt + conflict-detection

*Effort level is a proposal from the discovery-report ticket breakdown — run `/effort` manually
before execution.*

### Context

Moment 4 spec §10.5: per-person dietary is captured at Moment 1. Dietary needs are facts about
people, and Moment 1 is where Kate tells Gather about her people — an optional per-person note at
household capture, costing seconds, only for those with needs. Moment 2 **consumes** rather than
asks — generation reads per-person needs plus the event-level answer; the dietary three-state
remains the safety gate. The pre-flight's by-name re-verify then shows her what she entered,
against the plan.

Today: **two contradicting dietary models coexist.**
- GTC-150's three-state model (`unanswered|confirmed_none|confirmed_needs`) on
  `EventSetup.dietaryData` — well-built, but **event-scoped**, not per-person.
- A legacy model on `Event` itself: `Event.dietaryStatus`
  (`UNSPECIFIED|NONE|SPECIFIED` — different enum, different names) plus
  `Event.dietaryVegetarian/Vegan/GlutenFree/DairyFree` int counts.

Critically, `src/lib/ai/check.ts`'s `detectDietaryGaps()` reads the **legacy** counts, not
GTC-150's model — a host's real Moment 2 dietary answer never reaches conflict detection today.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- Add an optional per-person dietary note field at Moment 1 household capture.
- Wire Moment 2's generation prompt (`finalize-plan`) to consume per-person + event-level dietary
  data together.
- Repoint `check.ts`'s `detectDietaryGaps()` at the GTC-150 model (`EventSetup.dietaryData` via
  `readDietaryData()`) instead of the legacy `Event.dietary*` counts.
- Decide and document the legacy model's fate (deprecate in place vs. remove) — if removal
  touches live data in a way that isn't a clean empty-column drop, treat that as its own
  migration decision requiring sign-off, not a silent inclusion in this ticket.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.
- The dietary three-state values themselves (`unanswered`/`confirmed_none`/`confirmed_needs`) —
  proven and working; this ticket extends their scope to per-person, it doesn't redesign them.
