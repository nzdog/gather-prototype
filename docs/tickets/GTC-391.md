# GTC-391 - No reminders on the day of the event

> Stub, filed 17 Oct 2026 on Nigel's ruling at GTC-390 Q3: "All as
> recommended (Recommended)", which made "no reminders on the day of the
> event" its own ticket. Not scoped, no severity, not a precondition of
> GTC-390 or anything else.

## The gap (found planning GTC-390, read at `7837309`)

- Reminders stop when an event leaves `SENT_AND_LIVE`
  (`src/lib/lifecycle.ts`), which is at its STORED end. A form-made event
  stores its last day as 00:00 UTC, which is 1pm NZ in summer and noon in
  winter. So a guest can be reminded on the morning of the event, up to
  lunchtime. GTC-390 Q2 kept that as it is ("Leave them as they are").
- Cowork's lean, recorded at GTC-390: chasing people on the day of the
  event is wrong whatever the end rule says.

## What the GTC-390 plan found (its correction 8 and flagged item 1)

- "Quiet hours still hold 9pm to 8am" is true only of TEXTS, and only at
  the drain. The reminder finder (`findNudgeCandidates`) queues at any
  hour; `drainOnce` holds a text in quiet hours (`quietHoursDefers`,
  `src/lib/press/dispatch.ts`) and sends an email at once.
- The sending job never asks whether the event is still on
  (`findNeverAttempted`, `findDueForRetry`, `drainChaseRow`). A text
  reminder queued at 11pm is held and goes at 8am the next morning,
  whatever has happened to the event in between. Harmless today, because
  the finder stops at the stored end (1pm) and texts are held only
  overnight; it matters the moment the stop moves.

## The option this ticket would weigh (GTC-390 plan, Q2 c)

Stop reminders earlier, at the start of the event's first NZ day: no
chasing on the day. A real product change with its own tests:
- a new where-fragment in `src/lib/lifecycle.ts` (sent, and the event's
  first NZ day not yet begun), worked out from the same NZ-midnight
  instant GTC-390 adds;
- both finder files would use it in place of `SENT_AND_LIVE`:
  `src/lib/sms/nudge-eligibility.ts` (reminders) and
  `src/lib/sms/decide-by-eligibility.ts` (the decide-by follow-up);
- the sending job needs its own check (`src/lib/press/dispatch.ts`), or a
  text queued between 9pm and midnight the night before is still sent at
  8am on the day;
- it stops chasing on day two of a multi-day event, which today is chased
  until lunchtime on the last day;
- the suites that build a "live" event, and the security suite's and the
  cron-health suite's preconditions (which count `SENT_AND_LIVE` rows),
  need reading for it.

## Not decided here

Whether to stop on the day at all, from when (midnight, or the evening
before so nothing is held overnight), whether the decide-by follow-up
takes the same stop, and what the board says about a guest no longer
being chased. Any answer is a send change and needs its own plan.
