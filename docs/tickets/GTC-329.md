# GTC-329 — after the press, the host has no way back to her chase decisions

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-09-27 at the close of [[GTC-311]], on its SCOPED ruling 3.
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [x] High [ ] Medium [ ] Low — **SET HIGH 2026-09-27 by the founder**,
as a **candidate precondition of the GTC-311 + [[GTC-189]] slice 8 deploy**. Not yet ruled a
precondition; ruled High so that question is asked before the deploy, not after it.

---

### Signal

GTC-311's SCOPED ruling 3 asked: after the press, is the pre-flight, and the don't-chase mark on
it, reachable? **Only by typing the address. The product has no path there.** Read from the code
at GTC-311's close; not walked through a real press, because a press cannot be undone.

- **Nothing on the server stops it.** The page, `GET /api/events/[id]/pre-flight`, the cadence
  `PATCH` and GTC-311's chase `PATCH` have no `Event.sentAt` gate. A typed URL loads, and the mark
  and the named exception still write.
- **Nothing in the product links to it.** The host-token page hides "Review and send" once
  `inviteStatus.sentAt` is set (`src/app/h/[token]/page.tsx`). `InviteStatusSection` shows its
  "Review and send" button only while `hasUnsentPeople` is true — someone is NOT_SENT — so the
  link comes back only while a late arrival is waiting.
- **And the page does not know it is after the press.** Its step 5 still offers Send, which the
  press refuses `ALREADY_SENT`.

**Why it matters under ruling AH.** Most guests with no usable mobile are emailed by the chase by
default. A host who changes her mind on day two about one of them — *hand this one to me* — has
nowhere to say so. That is the case GTC-311's SCOPED ruling 3 named as the one to avoid.

### ⚠ In scope, by founder ruling: a dead end that already exists

`DONT_CHASE_NOT_ADDRESSABLE_MESSAGE` in `src/lib/eligibility/nudge-mark.ts` is shown to the host
when she presses nudge on a marked person:

> *"This person is marked don't-chase and will not be nudged. Change the mark in the pre-flight
> first."*

After the press she cannot reach the pre-flight, so that sentence sends her somewhere she cannot
go. GTC-192 Ruling 17 sealed the board's grey strips on the same ground (*"the don't-chase mark is
revisited where it is set (the pre-flight)"*), so the board points there too. **This predates
GTC-311** and is not caused by it; it is the same missing path.

### Scope (to be designed — nothing below is ruled)

- A path from the post-press host surfaces (the host-token page, `InviteStatusSection`, the board)
  to the two decisions that are read when the chase runs: the don't-chase mark and the named
  exception, plus the event switch.
- The pre-flight after the press must not present itself as before the press. Whether that is the
  same page in a post-press mode or a smaller surface is this ticket's design question.
- `DONT_CHASE_NOT_ADDRESSABLE_MESSAGE` names a place the host can reach — or the words change.
- **No change is needed in GTC-311's storage or resolver.** The chase reads the stored values at
  the moment it runs, so a later surface costs no rework there (GTC-311 SCOPED ruling 3).

### Unknowns

1. Should a change after the press apply only to chase legs not yet sent? The resolver answers at
   send time, so that is the natural reading. Confirm at scoping.
2. Does a change after the press need recording — who took whom off the chase, and when?
