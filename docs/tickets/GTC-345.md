# GTC-345 — catch common typos in email addresses when they are entered

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-01 at [[GTC-335]] on founder instruction (*"catch common
typos in email addresses when they are entered, because Resend may shut an account whose bounce
rate passes 4%"*). Not scoped.
**Severity:** not set at filing — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

Gather sends every guest email from one Resend account. Resend may shut an account whose bounce rate
passes 4%. That puts a whole platform's email at the mercy of the addresses hosts type in, and a
typo'd domain (`gmial.com`, `hotmial.com`, `gmail.con`) is the commonest hard bounce there is.

What Gather does after a bounce is already built: the delivery poll records it, the address is
blocked for every host (`recordEmailBlock` in `src/lib/eligibility/email-block.ts`, [[GTC-324]]
ruling 2), and the board shows the red once ([[GTC-335]]). Nothing catches the address before
the first send, which is the one bounce that still counts.

### Unknowns

1. Every place a guest's address is entered or imported (typed on setup, the household edit, the
   VCF and batch imports). Scoping lists them.
2. Whether a likely typo is refused, corrected, or only flagged for the host to confirm.
3. Which typos count as common, and whether the list is Gather's own or a library's.
