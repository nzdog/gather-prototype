# GTC-309 — refuse, and say what to fix

**Status:** Open — **filed, not scoped.** Filed 2026-09-15 | **Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [ ] High [x] Medium [ ] Low — proposed at filing; the founder's to set.
`severity_proposed` carries the proposal and `severity` stays empty until it is set.
Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] No — no paid event is in this state today (measured below), and
the ruling is that nothing is sent rather than something being sent wrongly.

**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from [[GTC-189]] *Founder rulings — fourth pass (Nigel,
2026-09-15)*, ruling AC, which answered decision 12:

> DECISION 12 — REFUSE TO SEND. An event whose host has no account is fixed before it sends, not
> sent with a hole in it.

and, with it, the founder's condition on the ruling:

> SAY WHAT SHE SEES. Refusing is only right if she is told what to fix and can fix it.

Tree read at `99cb852`. gather_dev measured read-only on 2026-09-15.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
The press either has a reply-to address for the host, or refuses and tells her how to get one.

**Actual**
`readAskPreview` in `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview.ts` returns `replyTo` as
`event.host?.user?.email ?? null` and its own comment calls the null case *"decision 12,
unanswered"*. The pre-flight renders whatever it gets. The press does not exist yet, so nothing
refuses, and nothing names the problem.

**Smallest failing case**
An event whose `Event.hostId` points at a `Person` with `userId: null`, opened by a signed-in user
who holds a HOST `EventRole` for it. The pre-flight loads and the reply-to line has no address in
it.

---

### The measurement, and why the ruling stands

**Measured read-only against gather_dev, 2026-09-15.** 29 events; **2 have a host `Person` with no
`User`** — unchanged from ruling F's count of the same thing on 2026-09-13. **0 of the 2 carry a
`stripePaymentIntentId`.**

- **`POST` in `src/app/api/events/route.ts` cannot produce this state.** It creates the `User`, the
  host `Person` and the `EventRole` in one transaction, so every event a host pays for has a
  `User.email`. Both affected events are seed or script events.
- **The refusal is reachable on one of the two.** *Kate test event* has one HOST/COHOST `EventRole`,
  so a signed-in user can open the pre-flight on it while the host `Person` has no `User`. *Summer
  BBQ Party* has no HOST/COHOST `EventRole` at all, so nobody can sign in to it and the refusal is
  unreachable there.
- **Why the reachable case exists.** `requireEventRole` in `src/lib/auth/guards.ts` keys on the
  **session user's** `EventRole` for the event. `replyTo` reads a different identity — `Event.hostId`
  to `Person.userId` to `User.email`. A co-host, or an event whose host `Person` was made by a
  script, passes the guard with no host account behind it.

**She can perform the fix, so the ruling stands.** The claim flow:

1. `GET` in `src/app/api/h/[token]/route.ts` sets `needsClaim` when `!person.userId`.
2. `POST` in `src/app/api/auth/claim/route.ts` takes the address **from the request body**, so she
   types it. The host `Person` does not need a stored email first — *Kate test event*'s host
   `Person` has `email: null` and is still fixable.
3. `POST` in `src/app/api/auth/verify/route.ts`, given a `personId`, sets `person.userId` and
   creates the HOST `EventRole` in one transaction.

---

### ⚠ Three things stand between the ruling and a host — and they are the work

1. **Nothing on the pre-flight names the problem or the fix.** A refusal with no route out is the
   failure the founder's condition was guarding against.
2. **The fix lives behind her host magic link**, a surface the pre-flight does not link to and may
   not be able to link to safely. Handing a `/h/{token}` URL out of a signed-in screen is Zone 3
   territory and is not decided here.
3. **The claim email goes through Resend, which does not authenticate in this environment**
   ([[GTC-247]]). The fix is performable in principle and **unverifiable here** until a key works.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- What the host is shown at the pre-flight when the host `Person` has no `User`: the problem named,
  and a route to the claim flow.
- The refusal itself belongs to **slice 5** of [[GTC-189]], which owns the press route. It is named
  here so the two halves are not built by two people with two different messages, not to move the
  boundary.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. **Zone 2 is live** — the claim flow is the
  magic-link auth path. Nothing here changes token generation, expiry or consumption.
- **Zone 3.** No token is minted, exposed or re-scoped by this ticket.
- The reply-to rule itself. Ruling F: `User.email`, no custom column — *"building it now is
  building for a want nobody has expressed."*

---

### Unknowns

1. **How she reaches the claim flow from a signed-in screen.** She is signed in as one identity and
   the thing needing repair is another. Whether the pre-flight can safely offer that route, or
   whether this is a support action rather than a self-serve one, is not decided.
2. **Whether the two identities should be reconciled instead.** A signed-in HOST `EventRole` holder
   whose `Event.hostId` is a different `Person` may be a data defect rather than a missing account.
   That is upstream of this ticket and is not scoped here.
3. **Whether the refusal is the press alone or the whole pre-flight.** Ruling AC says the press
   refuses. Whether step 4 should also decline to preview a message with no reply-to is not ruled.
4. **Verification.** Unprovable end to end in this environment until [[GTC-247]] is resolved.
