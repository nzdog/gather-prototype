### Scope (Nigel's words quoted; Cowork's findings after each)

**Item 53 - the event date.** His words: "Event Date needs sorting - end can be before start. Should
only be one day (ask Claude if this is the right decision about only one day.)" RULED (14 Oct): "One
date, with a link for more days (Recommended)". The form asks for one date. A small link, "It runs
over more than one day", opens an end date that cannot be before the start. Weekend events still
work. Reason: multi-day is used by the details card (`whenLine`), the guest pages' dates, "Answers
by" (the event's end date) and the AI prompt. Found at `ab142d7`:
- New Event, `src/app/plan/new/page.tsx`: Start Date L514-526 and End Date L530-542, both type date,
  both required; the only check is that both are filled (L251-252). No end-before-start check
  anywhere.
- Edit Event, `src/components/plan/EditEventModal.tsx`: both dates (L329-344), PATCH to
  `/api/events/[id]` (route L127-128 writes both with no check).
- Clone a template, `src/components/templates/CloneTemplateModal.tsx`: both dates (L188-198), POST
  to `/api/templates/[id]/clone` (route L33-47 checks only that both exist and parse).
- A New Event's dates ride through Stripe session metadata and are read back AFTER payment in
  `src/app/api/events/route.ts` (L124-164).

Plan: when one date is given, the end date is stored equal to the start. On Edit Event, an event
that already runs over more days opens with both dates showing. A browser cannot then send end
before start; say whether the PATCH and clone routes should refuse it too (Cowork thinks yes). DO NOT
add a refusal in the post-payment route (`events/route.ts`): she has already paid, and refusing
would strand her. A check before payment would be in `src/app/api/billing/checkout/route.ts`, which
is Zone 4: Cowork's recommendation is to leave Zone 4 alone (the form cannot send a bad pair) unless
Claude Code sees a real way in; say what is found. The labels and the link's exact placement are
Claude Code's to propose; look at the screen first.

**Item 58 - a progress bar for Generate plan.** His words: "Progress bar for generate plan". Found:
"Generate plan →" is the sticky button in `src/components/plan/Moment2Step1Modal.tsx` (L1007-1019).
Its handler (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/setup/page.tsx` L1520-1545) hides the questions, shows
`Moment2Step2Skeleton` and awaits ONE POST to `/api/events/[id]/finalize-plan`, which makes one AI
call (`lib/ai/claude.ts`, not streamed). So there is no true progress to show: the bar can only be
an honest estimate. Rules for it: it never reaches the end until the plan has really arrived; it
never goes backwards; if the call fails or is refused (429, the 10-call limit), the existing toasts
and the way back stay as they are. Plan: measure how long a real generation takes (from the dev log
of earlier runs; do NOT make a live call) and say what the estimate should be based on; where the
bar sits on the skeleton screen (`src/components/plan/Moment2Step2Skeleton.tsx`); whether stage
words go with it, and who writes them (do not invent copy: propose words and put them to Nigel).
The test must be able to show the bar moving without an AI call: say how (a delayed stand-in
response inside the browser, behind the safeguards, and fake timers for the render checks).

**Item 59 - V and GF marks on dishes.** His words: "V and GF notes for dishes". Found: the AI
already tags each dish (VEGETARIAN, VEGAN, GLUTEN_FREE, DAIRY_FREE, NUT_FREE; `lib/ai/prompts.ts`
L150, L226, L265, L751) into `Item.dietaryTags`, with `Item.glutenFree`, `dairyFree` and
`vegetarian` booleans beside it (`prisma/schema.prisma` L608-611). The setup page maps them to
`dietaryFlags` (`setup/page.tsx` L214-224) and `Moment2PlanView.tsx` receives them (L23) but never
draws them (the dish name is at L630). The older pages do draw them (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx`
L2770, /h team page, /c page). Moment 3's view draws none. His item says V and GF; the AI also sets
VEGAN, DAIRY_FREE and NUT_FREE. SAFETY: these marks are the AI's guess, and a host could rely on
"GF" for a guest with coeliac disease. Plan: which marks show (V and GF only, or all five), where
(the plan, Moment 3, the printed list, a guest's own asks: Cowork's proposal is the host's screens
and the print only, not guests' asks), how a host corrects a wrong mark (what can she edit today in
the item editor at `Moment2PlanView` L759-763), and what honest words go with them (for example that
they are suggestions). Check hand-made items and regenerated items: do they get tags at all? Put the
recommendation as a question.

**How the words are written:** in the app, apostrophes in these words are curly, as the existing
lines are. Anything that goes out as a text or email stays in plain GSM-7 characters.
