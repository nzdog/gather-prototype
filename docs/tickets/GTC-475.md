## The Broken Promise

Gather said "I'll handle it from here" and then showed her four small
dead ends. A button that promises the new flow and opens the old screen. Two
buttons that look and read alike. Boxes that save by themselves and never say
so. A warning that names a gap and offers no way to close it.

## The Cost

She carries the doubt: did that save, which button was that, where do I put
the venue. Each is a small stall at the moment she is trying to hand the work
over.

## The Fix

Five small changes, screens, words and tests only. No route, no schema, no
migration, nothing in the send, the nudges, the decide-by clock or the board.

1. Your Events: the "Invites & people" button is gone. The row opens the event's
   Moment home, as it does now; the old dashboard stays reachable from the
   board, "Change these details" and Moment 2's plan view.
2. Moment 1: "Save & add another household" keeps its words and becomes a
   solid, full-strength outline with a plus drawn before the words by the
   stylesheet (no text added).
3. Moment 1: under both columns, the line "Finished with people for now?" and,
   beneath it, the button "Save & move on to items →". The line is tied to the
   button as its description.
4. Moment 2's questions: a button "Add" under every free-text box (the kind of
   event "Other", every food and drink "Other", dietary "Other dietary needs",
   Set up, Clean up, Other, Notes). Pressing it saves what is typed right now
   and says "Added" until she types again. It makes no plan and calls no AI.
5. The drop-off line with no venue: one door after it, "Add the venue", to the
   old dashboard with the Edit event box open at its venue step.
