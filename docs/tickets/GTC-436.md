# GTC-436 - Hidden household boxes are not checked when she hosts alone

> Filed by Claude Code on 31 Oct 2026. Found while building
> [[GTC-431]] (commit 02c3791); the founder ruled it a fix the same day.
> Not built yet.

## The founder's ruling (31 Oct 2026)

- "The 'hosting alone' finding is ruled a fix: hidden household boxes
  must not be checked."

## What happens today

On her own household's step (the form in `hostMode`), ticking "I'm
hosting alone" hides the household section with a `hidden` class
(src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx, the "Household members
section"). Its partner, kid and guest boxes are still in the form and
still checked by `hasValidationErrors`:

- A bad email or phone, or a nameless kid row, typed before she ticked
  the box, still stops "Save and add everyone else →".
- Since GTC-431 the words beside Save count that box ("Please fix 1
  thing above"), but no box she can see is marked, and nothing takes
  focus: `checksFail` focuses only a visible box.
- What she typed there is not sent anyway: handleSaveHostHousehold
  already drops the partner, kids and guests when `hostingAlone` is on.

So she can be stopped by a box she cannot see, for data that would
never be saved.

## The fix (proposed)

- In `hasValidationErrors`: when `hostingAlone` is on, check only her
  own boxes (name and phone). Clear the partner, kid and guest errors
  rather than set them, so the count and the markers match what she
  sees.
- In `errorCount`: count no partner, kid or guest error while
  `hostingAlone` is on.
- If she unticks "I'm hosting alone", the boxes show again with what
  she typed. They are checked again on the next Save, as before.
- Nothing else moves: the ordinary add and edit forms never hide those
  boxes, and the payload rules are unchanged.

## Tests (proposed, test:household-save)

- Source: the hosting-alone branch skips the member checks and the
  member error count.
- Chrome, at 390, on an event without her household: add a kid with
  phone "+61 412 345 678", tick "I'm hosting alone", press Save: no
  "Please fix" words, one POST to host-household, a household of one
  stored. Untick before Save instead: the stop and the words as in
  GTC-431's C10.
- Red before: on the current form the first case stops with "Please
  fix 1 thing above" and nothing is sent.
