# GTC-327 — An email unsubscribe reads AMBER on the board, with nothing saying why

**Status:** Open — filed 2026-09-22 by [[GTC-296]]'s executor, on the founder's instruction,
rather than folded into that ticket.
**Severity:** proposed Medium — the vocabulary and the words already exist and nothing is
false on any screen; what is missing is a sentence the host would find useful.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
A person the press decided against because they unsubscribed from email for that event shows
the host *something* on the board about why no message went.

**Actual**
Their rows read AMBER — *waiting on them* — with no why-line. Nothing on the strip
distinguishes them from somebody who simply has not answered yet.

**Smallest failing case**
Unsubscribe a recipient after the press (a mini-send, or the window between the press and the
drain), then open the board.

---

### Why it is open rather than fixed

[[GTC-296]] correction **R6**, 2026-09-20. The Scope carried two bullets that cannot both
hold — *"no new surface"* and *"the board reads through the same path as other
suppressions"* — and the founder ruled the smaller half:

- `WITHHELD_MEANS_UNREACHABLE` in `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts` maps `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` to
  `null`, so no red is derived;
- `'UNREACHABLE'` was refused because it renders *"nowhere to send"*, which is **false** of
  somebody holding a live address they have just used;
- a sixth red FAMILY was refused because slice 7b's door opens on a red, and its actions
  include *"send it again"* and *"send to the phone instead"* — offered about the person who
  just unsubscribed.

**The row itself is not lost.** `OutboundMessage.withheldWhy` carries `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT`, and
the host's pre-flight list carries the full sentence from
`ADULT_WHY.EMAIL_OPTED_OUT`. What is missing is only the strip.

---

### Scope

**Fix here:**

- `WITHHELD_MEANS_UNREACHABLE` in `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts` — give
  `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` a producer.

⚠ **IT IS ONE EDIT, AND [[GTC-296]] BUILT EVERYTHING ELSE FOR IT ALREADY.** The reason is in
`RED_REASONS` in `src/lib/glance/state.ts`, declared with no producer the way
`EXHAUSTED_SILENCE` has been since [[GTC-192]]; it is in `WHY_PRECEDENCE`; and
`WHY_LINES.EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` in `src/components/glance/strip.ts` already reads
**"opted out"** (9 characters, inside the ≤16 pin). Nothing needs wording.

**Do not touch:**

All Do-Not-Touch Zones. In particular, do NOT close this by mapping the code to
`'UNREACHABLE'` — see *Why it is open* above.

---

### Unknowns

1. **Does the door open on this red, and should it?** `DOOR_REASONS` in
   `src/lib/glance/actions.ts` is an explicit two-member array, so the door does **not**
   open on a new reason by accident. Whether it should offer anything here is the founder's:
   the honest answer may be an action-less red, which is what ruling M's `UNREACHABLE`
   already is.
2. **Is red the right colour at all**, or does this want a grey of the Ruling 14 family — a
   person deliberately not being chased, rather than a person Gather failed to reach? The
   strip's two greys exist for *expected, just unbothered* and *absent*, and an unsubscribed
   guest is arguably the first.
