## What this ticket does, in plain English

Six small changes a host sees first:

- The top-right button reads "Host Sign In" (tweak 2).
- The sign-in page's "we've sent you a link" sentence is the founder's own
  (tweak 3).
- The home page loses its "Browse event templates" line (tweak 13); the
  templates page stays.
- The signed-in account menu gains "Your events", "Help and support" and
  "Send feedback" (tweak 17).
- One small line, "Need help? Email hello@gatheringtogether.co.nz", on the
  new error and not-found pages, the sign-in page, the sign-in link's
  error page and the paying page's cancelled screen (tweak 18).
- The privacy page's address becomes the same support address.

Before any of that, Part 0: the guard suite (test:tweaks-guard) holds four
more things, by the founder's ruling.

Nothing is stored, sent or read differently. No route, no database
column, no email changes.
