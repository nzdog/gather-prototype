# GTC-066 — Plan Status modal closes automatically after freezing — CLOSED

**Status:** Complete
**Branch:** gtc-042-part1-nudge-schema
**Commit:** 5481ec9

### What was found
The freeze action inside the Plan Status expansion modal is handled by the `FreezeCheck` component rendered at `src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx:1951`. Its `onFreezeComplete` callback (line 1955) called `loadEvent()` and `loadTeams()` to refresh data, but did **not** call `handleCloseExpansion()` to dismiss the `SectionExpandModal`. This left the modal open showing stale "no conflicts" messaging while the FROZEN dashboard sat behind it.

The hidden-section `FreezeCheck` (line 1754) and the `UnfreezeSection` callbacks were not affected — this was specific to the freeze action triggered from within the Plan Status expansion modal.

### What was changed

**`src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx`**
- Added `handleCloseExpansion()` call to the `onFreezeComplete` callback inside the Plan Status `SectionExpandModal`. This closes the modal immediately after a successful freeze, revealing the FROZEN dashboard. `loadEvent()` and `loadTeams()` still fire to refresh state. `handleCloseExpansion()` also refreshes items, teams, and people data and clears the breadcrumb trail.
