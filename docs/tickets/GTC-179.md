# GTC-179 — E2: Cadence controls — pre-flight pace + per-person go-gentle

**Status:** Open | **Branch:** TBD | **Commit:** [pending] (filed `e5f6e82`) | **Effort:** high

*Effort level is a proposal from the discovery-report ticket breakdown — run `/effort` manually
before execution.*

### Context

Moment 4 spec §10.3: cadence controls live where the people-decisions live. **Per-event pace**: at
the pre-flight (*nudge pace: standard / relaxed / off*) — a sending decision made where Kate
reviews how everyone is contacted. **Per-person**: a mark alongside the recipient picker (*go
gentle / don't chase*), set at Moment 1 or any time — a hosting judgement the system stores and
obeys. **Don't-chase is the far end** — the per-person off-switch (the mother, the bereaved, the
feuding cousin). Gentle means fewer/softer. **Not a settings page.**

Today: no cadence config field anywhere. The only "gentle" concept is `HostNudgeVariant`
(`warm|casual|gentle|direct`), a message *tone* choice for one-off manual nudges via
`NudgeComposer.tsx` — unrelated to adjusting automated cadence.

### Founder Rulings (2026-08-27) — Phase 0 gate

*Four rulings closing the Phase 0 gate raised by the audit pass. Every finding the audit
reported was checked against the tree at `aa64a5f`, not against ticket text. Rulings 1-4
unblock execution; the audit items recorded after them are carried into scope, and the five
decisions the founder deliberately left open are listed last so a later session does not
mistake silence for settlement.*

**Ruling 1 — GENTLE means one nudge at day 5. Offsets `[5]`. After that the system stops.**
Moment 4 §10.3 defines gentle only as "fewer/softer" — no day count exists anywhere in the
spec, the Hinge spec, or the discovery report (grep-verified across `docs/03_specs`,
`docs/04_roadmap` and `docs/tickets`). This supplies the number.

**Gentle does not hand back to the host any differently from anyone else.** Whatever handback
exists once [[GTC-251]] (E6) lands applies to gentle exactly as it applies to a spent standard
cadence. **Gentle is a volume control, not a handback control** — it changes how many messages
a person receives, and nothing about what the system does when it runs out of them. Recorded
explicitly because the opposite reading is available and wrong: "the system stops after day 5"
describes the cadence ending, not a different ending.

**Ruling 2 — RELAXED means two nudges at days 6 and 12. Offsets `[6, 12]`.** Same absence in
the spec, same remedy. Two legs, as standard has, spaced wider.

**Ruling 3 — don't-chase suppresses the proxy path. A person marked `DONT_CHASE` must receive
no automated message on EITHER path.**

**Implement as SUPPRESSION ONLY** — a boolean read in `findProxyNudgeCandidates`
(`src/lib/sms/proxy-nudge-eligibility.ts`), gated **after** the child rule (`isMessageableRole`)
and **after** opt-out (`isOptedOut`), never through either. **Do NOT retime the proxy path.**
Retiming needs a decision about which clock a household nudge counts from, which is
[[GTC-252]]'s and is undecided; suppression needs no clock at all, which is exactly why the two
separate cleanly here.

This is **the paradigm case from §10.3**. The spec names "the mother" as the archetype for
don't-chase, and the mother is the person most likely to be her household's picked channel
(`Household.contactPersonEventId`, C1 / [[GTC-172]]). Without this ruling the direct path goes
silent while `findProxyNudgeCandidates` keeps messaging her on every cron tick — **the control
would fail precisely where it matters most.** Audit finding, ruled in.

Note what this does NOT do: it does not fix [[GTC-252]]'s unbounded re-nudge, which affects
every household regardless of any mark. That ticket is untouched and still open.

**Ruling 4 — composition of the two axes: QUIETER WINS.**

**The strict override ladder is wrong and must be corrected.** `resolveNudgeOffsetDays` in
`src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts` documents its intended precedence as "per-PERSON override, else
per-EVENT default, else the system default". Under that ladder a person marked `GENTLE` on an
`OFF` event resolves to `[5]` — **more nudges than an unmarked person on the same event, who
gets none.** A control whose only purpose is to reduce contact would increase it. The ladder's
docstring is corrected as part of Phase 1.

**The rule:** resolve each layer to a candidate offset array, then reduce by comparing them and
taking the quieter. **Quieter means FEWER NUDGES FIRST, THEN LATER** — count dominates; timing
breaks ties.

- `GENTLE [5]` on a `RELAXED [6, 12]` event resolves to **`[5]`**. One message beats two, even
  though it lands earlier. Stated by the founder as the worked example.
- `OFF` or `DONT_CHASE` resolves to `[]` and beats everything — zero is the fewest.
- A layer that is unset has **no opinion** and contributes no candidate. It is not the system
  default wearing a disguise; with both layers unset the default `[4, 7]` applies, which is what
  keeps `resolveNudgeOffsetDays({})` and `({ person: null, event: null })` at `[4, 7]`.

**The full composition matrix.** Rows are the per-person mark, columns the per-event pace.

| | pace unset | `STANDARD` `[4,7]` | `RELAXED` `[6,12]` | `OFF` `[]` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **mark unset** | `[4,7]` *(system default)* | `[4,7]` | `[6,12]` | `[]` |
| **`GENTLE`** `[5]` | `[5]` | `[5]` *(1 < 2)* | `[5]` *(1 < 2)* | `[]` *(0 < 1)* |
| **`DONT_CHASE`** `[]` | `[]` | `[]` | `[]` | `[]` |

The equal-length tie-break ("then later") is **unreachable with today's vocabulary** — no two
distinct values in the matrix share a length above zero, and a single-opinion cell has nothing
to compare against. It is implemented and tested directly against the comparator anyway, because
it is the ruled rule and the next pace value added would reach it silently.

### Recorded from the Phase 1 audit (2026-08-27)

Four findings the audit surfaced, carried into this ticket rather than left in a report.

**(a) The `nextNudgeAt` collision with [[GTC-251]] — this ticket lands first, so GTC-251 must
absorb it.** `nextNudgeAt` in `src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts` returns null when the cadence is spent,
and its own docstring already insists "NULL DOES NOT MEAN RED". Don't-chase makes it return null
**from moment zero**, a state GTC-251's exhaustion model has not been designed against. An
exhaustion predicate written as "null means spent" would **turn every don't-chase person red the
instant they are marked — inverting the control**: Kate says *stop chasing my mother* and the
system escalates her mother. **GTC-251 must distinguish no-cadence from spent-cadence.** A note
to that effect is added to [[GTC-251]] by this pass.

**(b) A FOURTH label site, which [[GTC-178]] missed, and it is a correctness bug rather than
copy.** GTC-178's closing follow-up flags three host-facing strings. The fourth is
`nudgeSummary`'s `pendingFirst` / `pendingSecond` terms in the GET handler of
`src/app/api/events/[id]/invite-status/route.ts`. They mirror the stamp and response gates but
not the cadence gate, so **a don't-chase person counts as pending** and the dashboard reports a
pending reminder for someone the system will never nudge. The block's own comment claims "both
survive GTC-179 unchanged" — true for a *pace* change, false for a *mark*. **Fixed in phase 3**,
in the same commit as the eligibility cutover, because that comment also demands the count and
the sweep never disagree.

**(c) Stop Condition 7 now applies to this ticket.** It was not declared. This ticket needs a
Prisma migration: `PersonEvent` has no JSON column, so the per-person mark requires DDL under
every storage option. See Stop Conditions below. [[GTC-178]] was scoped read-side and grew a
migration mid-flight; this one declares it up front instead.

**(d) [[GTC-172]]'s stale body header, fixed by this pass.** Its frontmatter reads
`status: closed` / `commit: 63764d1` (confirmed by `git log`), while its body header still read
`**Status:** Open | **Branch:** TBD | **Commit:** [pending]`. Corrected in place — a leftover,
not a reopening.

### Still OPEN — deliberately not ruled at this gate

Five decisions the audit surfaced that the founder left open on 2026-08-27. **They are needed
before their own phases, not before Phase 1.** Do not settle them by executor inference.

1. **Whether "softer" is in scope at all.** §10.3 defines gentle as "fewer/**softer**"; this
   ticket's Do-Not-Touch excludes `HostNudgeVariant` in `src/lib/sms/nudge-templates.ts` and
   `NudgeComposer.tsx` as message-tone. Ruling 1 supplies "fewer". If softer stays out, this
   ticket delivers half the spec's definition and must say so at close.
2. **Whether don't-chase records a skip reason** in `EligibilityResult.skipped`. Every other
   reason a person is not nudged calls `addSkip`; the cadence gate does not, so a suppressed
   person is invisible in the run result. Needed before phase 3.
3. **What `getNudgeStatus` returns for a don't-chase person.** Today it would return `'pending'`
   forever. Any new value is an API contract change. Needed before phase 5.
4. **Where the per-person mark renders.** §10.7 says "beside the same picker" and "One decision
   per household, not a matrix"; the picker is per-household and the mark is per-person. Needed
   before phase 4.
5. **Whether a mid-flight pace change re-opens already-stamped legs.** `OFF` → `STANDARD` on
   day 9 makes both legs instantly due — a two-message burst. Needed before phase 3.

**One more, not in the founder's list of five and recorded so it is not lost:** the audit's
decision 10 (whether the per-event pace lives in an `Event` column or in `EventSetup`'s JSONB)
was not named as open, and the founder's instruction to add Stop Condition 7 forecloses the
no-migration reading. This ticket treats the column layout itself as governed by Stop Condition
7's sign-off gate at phase 2, **not** as ruled here.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- Per-event pace field (`STANDARD` `[4,7]` / `RELAXED` `[6,12]` / `OFF` `[]`), read by E1's
  (GTC-178) rebuilt cadence logic. Offsets per Ruling 2.
- Per-person mark field (`GENTLE` `[5]` / `DONT_CHASE` `[]`), placed beside C1's (GTC-172)
  household contact picker. Offsets per Ruling 1.
- Resolve the two by **quieter-wins**, not by an override ladder (Ruling 4) — and correct the
  ladder documented on `resolveNudgeOffsetDays` in `src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts`, which is wrong.
- Wire E1's nudge scheduler to respect both — don't-chase means zero automated nudges for that
  person; gentle means fewer per §10.3 and Ruling 1.
- **Suppress `DONT_CHASE` on the proxy path too** (Ruling 3): a boolean read in
  `findProxyNudgeCandidates` (`src/lib/sms/proxy-nudge-eligibility.ts`), gated after the child
  rule and after opt-out. Suppression only — no retiming.
- Fix `nudgeSummary`'s `pendingFirst`/`pendingSecond` terms in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/invite-status/route.ts` so they exclude don't-chase — audit finding
  (b), a correctness bug, not copy.

- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`, especially SMS opt-out (Zone 7) — this is

- `HostNudgeVariant`/`NudgeComposer.tsx` — a separate, message-tone concept, not cadence.
  (Whether the "softer" half of §10.3 is in scope at all is OPEN — see Still OPEN, item 1.)
- **Retiming the proxy path** (Ruling 3). Suppression only. Which clock a household nudge counts

storage to read.
