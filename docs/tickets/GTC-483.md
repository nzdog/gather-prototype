# GTC-483 - Multi-day: the release

Base: origin/main 2c9771b (26 Nov 2026, the merge of pull request #124,
GTC-481). The founder's multi-day rulings are in [[GTC-476]], with the
founder's ruling on the order of 26 Nov 2026 (this release before
[[GTC-482]]). In the code a session is a `Day` row; the screens say
"session" (Q1). All dates and times are NZ time.

Everything multi-day is behind `GATHER_SESSIONS` (GTC-478). This ticket
fixes the dish-list words, proves what switching on changes for events
that already exist, gives the founder a read-only count to run on
production, looks at the whole journey on screen, and writes the release
runbook. Turning the switch on is the founder's, never Claude's.

  capitalise on display", not built). Left as they are (ruling 6).

  at the press, and nothing stores the drafted line. So with the switch on
  the range line (GTC-485 G) also reaches anyone asked later on an event

  `src/lib/workflow.ts`). The runbook says so.
- No database was read or written while planning.

## The plan (as approved, 26 Nov 2026)

The plan of record is the one given to the founder; what follows is its
substance.

### A. The dish words: option 1

Inside `dishList` only, name by name:

- A name starting with a possessive name ("Mum's", "Nana's", "Kate's")
  gets no "the" and is shown as typed.
- A name starting with a number or with a, an, the, some, my, our, your,
  their, his, her, extra gets no "the"; that first word is lowered.
- Otherwise "the" and the name. Its first letter is lowered only when the
  first word, lowered, is on the everyday list below; it is written with
  one capital and the rest small; and the next word, if any, starts small.
- Anything else is shown exactly as typed: a word not on the list keeps
  its capital, as today.

### B. Switching on with no sessions added

What a host or guest sees or receives that changes:

- Moment 1's summary, every event: the Sessions card, empty, with its
  one-day or many-dates line, "+ Add a session", and "Add one session for
  each date" on events over 2 to 14 dates.
- The invitation's first line, on an event over more than one NZ date
  whose host saved no line: "from <first day> to <last day>" where it said
  "on <first day>". In the pre-flight's step 4, the host's test email, the
  demo's pretend send, and in the invitation, a resend and the invitation
  to a guest added after the send (so also on events already sent, for
  anyone asked after the switch goes on). One date, or a saved line:
  unchanged.

Behind the screens, nothing visible: Moment 1's card, Moments 2 and 3 and
the guest page each ask for the event's sessions once and draw nothing
more; the board reads the plan's dishes once more for "Answers by" (the
same day); the session addresses answer instead of refusing.

What does not change: Moments 2 and 3 (no tabs, Session list or
headcounts); Moment 3's warning and suggestions; the guest page and the
host's link preview; the board (no tag, the same "Answers by", amber and
red at the same moments); every dish's decide-by; the maybe follow-up
(who, when, its words, its email retry); the nudges and reminders (they
never read the switch); the opt-outs and blocks; the coordinator page;
"Invites only" and bringing a plan back; sharing a dish; the why box, the
text exports, the drafting, the thank-you and sign-in mail.

Events that already hold sessions change as if the host had added them:
tabs and the Session list, "name, date" on the guest's dish, the board's
tag, and each such dish's decide-by from its session's date (the maybe
follow-up may come sooner).

2, `sent_multi_date_still_ahead_without_own_line`. The founder runs it;
Claude runs it once on gather_dev and gather_demo only (ruling 4).
