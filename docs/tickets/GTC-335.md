## Evidence (Executor-Completed)

**Root cause confirmed**

`deriveReplay` in `src/lib/glance/replay.ts` built each person's past `derivePersonState` input with
no `delivery` key at all, and with `chase: person.chase`, today's value. So a failed ask derived no
failure in the past and a failure now, and replayed a red on every visit; and a guest who opted out
or was blocked while the host was away derived the same chase fact at both ends, and played nothing.

**The fix, as ruled**

- The times are read in one place: `rewindGuestFacts` in `src/lib/glance/rewind.ts`. It returns
  each membership's latest ASK row as at `since` (rows made after `since` dropped, then
  `deliveryRowAsAt`), the guest facts recorded after `since` as four sets (`LaterFacts`), and each
  membership's ordering key (`movedSince`). Reads only.
- `deliveryRowAsAt` in `src/lib/glance/delivery-fact.ts` drops each door recorded after `since`:
  `rejectedAt`, `withheldAt` (with `withheldWhy`), and `deliveryState` by `deliveryCheckedAt`. A
  state with no time is held. `deliveryFailureRecordedAt` gives a failed row's time for the order.
- `readAskPreview` in `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview.ts` takes an optional `{ discount }` and
  subtracts the four sets where the chooser person is built. Without it, nothing changes; the
  press, the drain, the chase and the pre-flight never pass it. No time enters the module.
- `readGlanceReplay` in `src/lib/glance/replay-entry.ts` reads the PAST preview (the discount
  applied) and builds, per person, the past delivery fact (a child's through `carrierOfAsk` on the
  past ask route), the past chase fact (`chaseFactFrom` with today's marks and switch), exhaustion
  from the past route (plan Q1), and the ordering key (a child's is its carriers').
- `deriveReplay` reads them through three optional `GlanceRewind` maps (`deliveryAt`, `chaseAt`,
  `factChangedSince`). No `chaseAt` entry holds today's chase fact; no `deliveryAt` keeps the old
  meaning. The order is the larger of the rows' key and the facts' key; the reversal still sorts
  last. No timestamp reaches a step.
- Held as it is now (Ruling 22 and ruling point 3): the mark, the reminders switch, the exceptions
  and the event default, households, addresses and phones, the rows held, and any fact with no
  recorded time.

**The recorded times, as their writers keep them** (checked at planning; the plan has the detail)

  terminal), `withheldAt` (written once), `deliveryCheckedAt` (the failing read is a row's last,
