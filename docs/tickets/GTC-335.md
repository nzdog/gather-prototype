# GTC-335 — the replay rewinds rows and attendance, and nothing else

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-09-28 at [[GTC-305]] on founder ruling (Finding 2): *"File
one follow-up on how the replay rewinds facts, covering the chase fact and the delivery case."* Not
scoped.

> ⚠ **CANDIDATE PRECONDITION OF THE [[GTC-311]] + [[GTC-189]] SLICE 8 DEPLOY — founder ruling,
> 2026-09-28, beside [[GTC-251]] and [[GTC-329]].** Severity set by the founder the same day:
> MEDIUM, not low: it is the same class as [[GTC-305]] — the board tells the host something false,
> here that a bounced person has just gone red, every time she opens it.

---

### Signal

`deriveReplay` in `src/lib/glance/replay.ts` computes each person's PAST state by handing
`derivePersonState` the rows and attendance as `rewind.ts` reconstructs them at `since`. Two facts
the present state is derived from are NOT rewound:

1. **The delivery fact ([[GTC-189]] slice 7a) is not passed at all.** So the past derives with no
   failure while the present carries one, and a person whose ask failed replays a step on EVERY
   visit, however long ago it failed. **Measured at GTC-305 (`test:board-not-chased` layer H), with
   nothing changed in the window: 4 of 4 `NOT_DELIVERED` people replay a step** — two
   `AMBER→RED`, and two `NOT_CHASED→RED` (a handed-over guest and a text-opted-out guest, whose past
   is now GTC-305's grey). Ruling 26(b) plays reds, so each plays every time she opens the board.
2. **The chase fact ([[GTC-305]]) is passed UNREWOUND**, as the mark is — so nothing replays
   spuriously, and the cost goes the other way: **a guest who opts out of email while the host is
   away turns red with no step played**, though Ruling 26(b) plays reds.

### What a fix has to decide

Whether each fact is rewound from its own timestamps (`OutboundMessage` delivery state and
`rejectedAt`; `EmailOptOut.optedOutAt`; `EmailBlock.firstSeenAt`; the host's exception and switch
have no history at all) or held constant, and which of the two failures above is worse for each.
