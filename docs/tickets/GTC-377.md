### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-09 at `0a689ca`)

- The pre-flight (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx`) has a header (*"Before you send"*,
  *"Five things to go through. Nothing goes out until you press at the end."*, and [[GTC-374]]'s
  invites-only box) and five steps, each a `Step` section, titled from `PREFLIGHT_STEP_TITLES`
  (`src/lib/preflight/next-check.ts`): *"What is still loose"*, *"Dietary needs"*, *"Who Gather
  talks to"*, *"The message, shown"*, *"Ready"*. Every step is always open.
- A `Step`'s title row holds *"Step N of 5"*, the title, a one-line blurb and the tick box
  (*"Checked"*; step 4's reads *"Settled"*, [[GTC-311]]). Its content sits under the row. On an
  invites-only event [[GTC-374]] settles steps 1 and 2: ticked, greyed, *"Not needed: this event is
  invites only."* in place of the blurb, the content left out.
- Send is inside step 5, Ready, greyed until all five are ticked, with [[GTC-366]]'s line under it
  (*"Go to step N: {title} ↑"*), which scrolls to that step, focuses its tick box and rings it for
  two seconds (`goToStep`).
- Step 3 already folds its households with `AccordionShell` (`src/components/plan/AccordionShell.tsx`),
  the fold-out row Moment 2's sections use ([[GTC-188]], [[GTC-364]]: the parent decides what is open,
  several rows can be open at once, and opening one never shuts another). Step 1 shows its warnings
  in a `<details>`.
- At least nineteen suites read the pre-flight page: walkthrough batches 1, 2, 4, 5, 5-words, 7 and
  8 (batch 4 pins the steps' markup and the step line; batch5-words pins Send byte for byte),
  `test:hold-the-plan`, `test:moment-three`, `test:after-the-press`, `test:phone-menu`,
  `test:preflight-withholding`, `test:press-route`, `test:ask-preview`, `test:board-door`,
  `test:chase-channel`, `test:household-list`, `test:texts` and `test:sign-in-hole`.

### For the plan

- **The fold:** steps 1 to 4 start closed on every visit; Ready stays open, with Send and the step
  line. `AccordionShell`, or the plan says why not, with Moment 2's rule: several open at once,
  opening one never shuts another.
- **The closed row:** *"Step N of 5"*, the title, the tick box (ruled: it works with the step
  closed), and whatever else the plan proposes to keep there (the blurb, or a short line on what is
  inside). Any word added or changed is a W-line.
- **The step line:** *"Go to step N: … ↑"* opens the step it names as well as taking her there; its
  words unchanged.
- **Invites-only:** steps 1 and 2 stay settled as [[GTC-374]] ruled (ticked, greyed, *"Not needed:
  this event is invites only."*), with nothing inside to open.
- **Send:** unchanged, byte for byte, where it is, inside Ready.
- **Inside the steps:** the households already folding inside step 3, the warnings in step 1, and
  anything else the fold changes, said plainly.
- **On a phone:** the closed rows and the open Ready at 390.
- **Size:** if it is more than one ticket carries well, say so and propose a split before building.

  AccordionShell's ▾ and fold, which AccordionShell itself uses (its markup byte for byte as now);

  a suite could render. Recommended.
- Q2. A short line on the closed rows: (b) W1 to W3, each only when something needs her except
  W2, which repeats Moment 2's words; (a) no new words. Recommended: (b).
- Q3. Ready: no fold and no ▾, as today. Recommended.
- Q4. The invites-only steps 1 and 2: no fold, as today. Recommended.
- Q5. Ticking and folding are separate: a tick never opens or closes a step, opening never ticks,
  and nothing opens the next step by itself. Recommended.
- Q6. The step line opens its step, then scrolls once the fold has opened (about a fifth of a
  second); it never closes a step; focus and ring as today. Recommended.
- Q7. The spacing between steps unchanged (the tighter gap put step 2 at 68px, near batch 4's
  96px). Recommended.
- Q8. A closed step keeps what is inside it loaded, as on Moment 2: step 4's preview loads on
  arrival, an unsaved line or dietary change survives closing, and closed controls stay reachable
  by Tab, as on Moment 2 today. Recommended: leave it as Moment 2 has it.
- Q9. Step 1's "The sweep’s own wording" stays a native fold inside step 1. Recommended.
- Q10. Tests: a new suite, `test:preflight-fold`, and one edit to walkthrough-batch2 (a guarded
  tap opening step 3 before C6 to C8 look inside it). Recommended.
- Q11. One ticket, no split. Recommended.
