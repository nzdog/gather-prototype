# GTC-419 - Ticket E, guest buttons: a guest's answer stays as buttons

## What this ticket does, in plain English

When a guest has answered, the guest page used to show the answer as a
coloured bar (or a heading) with small "Change to ..." or "Changed your
mind?" links under it. Now the answer buttons simply stay on the page,
with the one she chose clearly marked, and she can tap a different one to
change her mind. Each tap sends exactly what the old link sent. One short
note in the attendance box tells her she can change it.

## The founder's rulings (28 Oct 2026, in Cowork)

- Page only: src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx. The answer calls (what the page
  sends, to which route, with which values) do NOT change, not by a
  character; no route, no token, no access scope (zone 3) is touched.
- Attendance: after the guest has answered, the page keeps showing two
  buttons, "Yes" and "No", with the chosen one highlighted (a clearly
  different look, and aria-pressed set), instead of the "Changed your
  mind?" link. Pressing the other one does what the link did. Keep the
  words the page uses today for the first-time buttons and for the
  confirmations ("Yes, still coming" must stay: batch7 R7). There is NO
  Maybe for attendance (the schema rules it out; zone 5).
- Items: on each item card, after an answer, three buttons "Yes", "No"
  and "Maybe" stay showing with the chosen one highlighted, instead of
  the coloured bar with "Change to Yes" and "Change to No" links. Use the
  same words the item buttons use today before an answer. Pressing one
  makes the same call the old control made.
- The note, once on the page after the guest's first answer, in the
  attendance block: "Changed your mind? Tap a different answer. You can
  come back to this page any time."
- Say every state of the page the change touches and what a guest sees
  in each, before and after. Any state where a highlighted button would
  be wrong or the call would differ from today: HARD STOP for ticket E:
  SKIP it, revert, report.

## How the rulings are read here

- "Yes" and "No" (attendance) and "Yes", "No", "Maybe" (items) are the
  words on the buttons that stay after an answer, exactly as quoted. The
  first-time buttons ("Yes, still coming" or, on an invites-only event,
  "Yes, I’ll be there"; "Can't make it"; the items' "Yes", "No",
  "Maybe") and the confirmations ("Great — we'll see you there." / "Your
  host knows to expect you."; "Thanks for letting us know" / "We'll miss
  you at the event!") are kept as they are.
- The marked button does nothing when pressed (it had no control
  before: there was never a link to give the same answer again). So no
  new call can be made.
- The coloured bar goes, with its words ("Yes — you're bringing this",
  "Maybe", "No — you're not bringing this", and for a child's row "Yes —
  on Mia’s behalf" / "No — on Mia’s behalf"). The one-line note under it
  ("Your host can see you've confirmed ✓", "It's still yours — we'll
  check back ...", "Your host has been notified") is kept, under the
  buttons: the ruling names only the bar and the links.
- The note "Changed your mind? Tap a different answer. ..." sits in the
  attendance box, shown once she has answered attendance. A guest asked
  only about dishes never sees the attendance box (the server never asks
  her attendance: Hinge §3), so she never sees this note; her "You're
  all set. See you there." box keeps "You can change any answer below."
  (A question for the founder, recorded in the report.)

## The words, old and new

```
attendance, after an answer
old  Great — we'll see you there. / Your host knows to expect you.
     [Changed your mind?]                       (a link; after a no:
                                                 [Changed your mind? Click here])
new  Great — we'll see you there. / Your host knows to expect you.
     [Yes*] [No]                                (* = marked; after a no [Yes] [No*])
     Changed your mind? Tap a different answer. You can come back to
     this page any time.

a dish card, after an answer (Glazed ham, a yes)
old  Yes — you're bringing this                 (a green bar)
     Your host can see you've confirmed ✓
     [Change to No] [Change to Maybe]
new  [Yes*] [No] [Maybe]
     Your host can see you've confirmed ✓
```

The marked button is solid in her answer's colour with white words
(Yes sage, No grey, Maybe amber) and has aria-pressed="true"; the others
are white with a grey edge and aria-pressed="false". The marked one sends
nothing when pressed.
