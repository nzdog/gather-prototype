# GTC-259 — `Event.askAuthorLine` — CLOSED

**Status:** Complete | **Branch:** `feat/moment-one-redesign` | **Commit:** `e6952e5` | **Effort:** low

⚠ **Stop Condition 7 governed this ticket and was declared up front, not discovered mid-flight.**
**DISCHARGED:** the SQL below was surfaced and approved by the founder (Nigel, 2026-08-29) before
`prisma migrate dev` ran. Generated with `--create-only`, diffed byte-for-byte against the
approved text, then applied.

### Context

[[GTC-187]] built the three-movement ask and shipped it to the pre-flight screen, and stopped one
field short of done. Its decision-2 ruling says the host's movement 1 is **stored, not
ephemeral**, and flags in the same breath that no column holds it. Two `ANCHOR(GTC-187)` comments
mark the sites:

- `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` — `ComposeAskInput.storedAuthorLine`, which `composeAsk`
  reads and falls through from.
- `src/app/api/events/[id]/pre-flight/message/route.ts` — the response field, hardcoded `null`.

Today the pre-flight's movement-1 box is editable and **says in as many words that it is not
saved** (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx` — "NOT PERSISTED — see the notice rendered
below"). Kate writes her line, it goes out, it is gone.

**The case that makes it matter is the mini-send.** A cousin confirms two weeks after the
invitations went out, and Kate sends to that one person. With no stored line she writes movement
1 again from scratch, and the late guest gets different words from everyone else — the one thing
storage exists to prevent.

### Ruled before this ticket was filed

- **[[GTC-187]] decision 2 (2026-08-23):** the line is stored, presented pre-filled at mini-send,
  editing is one gesture and not required.
- **[[GTC-187]], 2026-08-29:** a mini-send edit **overwrites**. One value, last write wins; no
  per-send version on the field. That ruling also carries a premise correction — there is no
  record of sent message bodies today — which does not change it. See the ruling for why.

### Scope

**Fix here — the column, and nothing else:**
- One nullable column on `Event`, plus its migration.
- The doc comment recording the NULL convention and the empty-string rule below.

**Deliberately NOT here:**
- **No wiring.** `storedAuthorLine` stays hardcoded `null` in the pre-flight route; `composeAsk`
  keeps falling through to `draftAuthorLine`. Behaviour after this ticket is byte-identical to
  behaviour before it.
- **No write path.** No route accepts the line, no UI saves it, and the pre-flight's
  "not saved" notice stays true and stays on screen.
- **No backfill.** Nullable, and NULL is exactly today's behaviour.
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.

The split is deliberate: add the column, verify it, then wire it. If something breaks, which half
broke is not a question.

### The placement check — `Event`, not `EventSetup`. Confirmed.

The founder's instinct was that the line belongs on `Event`, and named `EventSetup` as the rival
candidate to be checked rather than assumed. Checked. **`Event` is right**, on four grounds, one
of which is decisive on its own.

**1. The identical question was ruled two days ago.** [[GTC-179]] Ruling 5 (2026-08-27) settled
`Event` column vs `EventSetup` JSONB for the nudge pace, and its third bullet is this ticket's
argument verbatim: *"`EventSetup`'s JSON tenants are Moment-2 form state — `mainsData`,
`dietaryData`, `extendedCategoriesData` and the rest. An unrelated concern."* The ask's movement 1
is a Moment-4 pre-flight artefact. Same shelf, same reason.

**2. DECISIVE — `EventSetup` does not exist for the events that reach the pre-flight.** The row
is created by exactly one site: the Moment-2 Step-1 autosave upsert in
`src/app/api/events/[id]/setup/route.ts`. An event that never went through that flow has no row.
Measured against the development database, 2026-08-29:

```
 events | setups | events_without_setup
--------+--------+----------------------
      9 |      2 |                    7
```

**`Henderson Family Christmas 2026` — the CONFIRMING event [[GTC-187]]'s entire evidence package
was measured on, the one that reaches this screen — has no `EventSetup` row.** Neither does
either CONFIRMING security-test event. Storing the line there means saving a Moment-4 message
creates a Moment-2 brief row as a side effect, for the majority of events. That is not a style
preference; it is a coupling between two moments that have nothing to do with each other.

**3. `Event` already carries the precedent, with the exact NULL semantics wanted.**
`Event.decideByOffsetHours` and `Event.nudgePace` are both nullable typed columns whose doc
comments say NULL means *not set*, not zero and not a stored default. This field wants the same
sentence.

**4. The reader is already there.** The pre-flight message route selects from `Event` and its
relations. `EventSetup` would be a second `include` for one string.

**One argument that does NOT carry over, stated so it is not cited later:** Ruling 5's lead
bullet was "the nudge scheduler reads the pace on every 15-minute cron tick — hot scheduler
input". Nothing reads this field on a cron. It is read once, when the pre-flight screen opens.
Grounds 1–4 stand without it.

### The exact schema change

One addition to `model Event` in `prisma/schema.prisma`. Placed immediately after `nudgePace`,
which is where the other Moment-4 nullable tuning columns already sit.

```prisma
  /// GTC-259: the host's movement 1 for the ask — Kate's own words, the first of the three
  /// movements Hinge §5 makes seam-visible ([[GTC-187]] decision 2, "stored, not ephemeral").
  ///
  /// NULL MEANS SHE NEVER WROTE ONE, not "she wrote nothing". `composeAsk` falls through to
  /// `draftAuthorLine(event)` — today's behaviour, always — via `storedAuthorLine ?? draft` in
  /// src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts. Same convention as `decideByOffsetHours` and `nudgePace`
  /// directly above: NULL is not-set, and not-set is not a stored value.
  ///
  /// ⚠ THE WRITE SITE MUST NORMALISE EMPTY TO NULL. The read is `??`, which falls through on
  /// null and undefined but NOT on `''`. A host who clears the box and saves would otherwise
  /// store an empty string and compose an empty movement 1 — the seam Hinge §5 exists to
  /// protect, silently deleted. No write site exists yet; this is the rule the one that does
  /// must follow.
  ///
  /// ONE VALUE, LAST WRITE WINS ([[GTC-187]], 2026-08-29). A mini-send edit overwrites; no
  /// per-send version lives here. What each recipient actually received is [[GTC-189]]'s to
  /// decide and does not belong in this column.
  ///
  /// NOT LENGTH-CAPPED, on purpose — [[GTC-187]] decision 6: "no enforced cap ... do not
  /// truncate, do not block". Prisma maps `String` to Postgres `TEXT`.
  ///
  /// NOT `EventSetup` JSONB — see this ticket's placement check, and [[GTC-179]] Ruling 5 for
  /// the same call on the same question.
  ///
  /// NO READER AND NO WRITER AS OF THIS TICKET. The pre-flight route still returns
  /// `storedAuthorLine: null` hardcoded and the screen still says the box is not saved. Wiring
  /// is deliberately a separate change.
  askAuthorLine              String?
```

**On the name.** `authorLine` is the spec's own slot name for movement 1 and is the field name in
`MovementContent` / `VoiceProfile` in `src/lib/messages/compose.ts`; `ask` matches the register it
serves (`ASK_REGISTER`, `composeAsk`, `askSubject`, `askHandover`, `askSystemVoice`). Qualified by
register rather than left bare because `THANK_YOU_REGISTER` is explicitly unruled in [[GTC-187]],
and §8.4 gives the thank-you a Kate line too — a bare `hostAuthorLine` would invite someone to
overload one column with two different sentences. The alternative considered and rejected was
`hostAuthorLine`; recorded here so the choice is legible, not re-litigated.

### ⚠ Correction — the empty-string mechanism, measured (GTC-260, 2026-08-29)

This ticket's schema comment and its prose both said a stored empty string would "compose an
empty movement 1", and the [[GTC-260]] wiring pass measured it instead of repeating it. **That
description was wrong.** `authorLineFor` in `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` already guards the
blank case:

```ts
const written = line?.trim();
return written ? `Hi ${recipientFirstName} - ${written}` : `Hi ${recipientFirstName},`;
```

Composed three ways against `Henderson Family Christmas 2026`, all three movements survive in
every case:

| `storedAuthorLine` | movements | movement 1 |
|---|---|---|
| `null` | 3 | `Hi Finn - We're doing Henderson Family Christmas 2026 on Wednesday, 23 December, at the Henderson place. Would love to have you there.` |
| `''` | 3 | `Hi Finn,` |
| `'   '` | 3 | `Hi Finn,` |

**The real consequence is narrower and still worth preventing:** an empty string does not blank
or drop movement 1, it **suppresses the fall-through to the draft**. The host clears the box, and
instead of the generated draft returning, every guest gets a bare greeting and no line from her.
The rule this ticket recorded — normalise empty to NULL at the write site — is unchanged and
correct; only the mechanism behind it is restated.

The schema comment is corrected in place by [[GTC-260]]'s commit. The quoted block above is left
as written, as the record of what this ticket actually said at sign-off.

### ⚠ SUPERSEDED — the normalise-empty-to-NULL rule (founder ruling, 2026-08-29)

**The rule this ticket recorded and shipped — "THE WRITE SITE MUST NORMALISE EMPTY TO NULL" —
is superseded.** It is kept above, and in `prisma/schema.prisma`, rather than deleted: the
reasoning was sound on the facts available, and the record of why it was replaced is worth more
than a clean comment.

**What replaced it.** `Event.askAuthorLine` has **three** states, not two:

| stored | means | composes to |
|---|---|---|
| `NULL` | never authored | the generated draft — today's behaviour, unchanged |
| `''` | **deliberately no line from the host** | the bare greeting, `Hi Rob,` — and **not** the draft |
| a value | her words | `Hi Rob - <value>` |

**Why.** The old rule's premise was that a stored `''` suppresses the draft, and that this was a
defect. The suppression is real — the correction above measured it — but it turned out to be the
**feature**. Two reasons, recorded:

1. `authorLineFor`'s own doc comment already treated the blank case as deliberate: movement 1 is
   *"hers to cut to nothing"*. The branch was written for this state before there was a way to
   reach it; the ruling gave it the way in rather than changing what it does.
2. Hinge §5 makes the **handover** the load-bearing beat, not movement 1's length. A host sending
   to sixty people, or re-sending after she has already said her piece, must be able to decline
   to speak without Gather's words standing in for hers.

**What normalisation survives:** whitespace-only still normalises — to `''`, **not** to `NULL`,
because `authorLineFor` trims before deciding and storing `'   '` would be a fourth spelling of
the third state. Values are stored trimmed. The column holds exactly `NULL`, `''`, or a trimmed
non-empty string.

Implemented and proven in [[GTC-260]], which also carries the UI consequence: two clearing
actions that must read differently, because both empty the box and they mean opposite things.
