## The state

**`Event.sentAt` has been written since [[GTC-169]] (A3a) by a press that sent nothing.** That press
locked the plan, stamped every `PersonEvent.sentAt` ([[GTC-196]] / A3b), set the invite anchors and
wrote `SEND_PRESSED`. It dispatched no message, because no dispatcher existed — [[GTC-189]] slice 5
is what built one.

So an event pressed before slice 5 is in a state the new model has no name for:

- `Event.sentAt` **set** — the plan is locked and the board treats it as sent.
- `PersonEvent.sentAt` **set for everyone**, by the `updateMany` slice 5a deleted.
- `OutboundMessage` rows: **none.** The table did not exist until slice 4a.
- And **nothing will ever create them.** Slice 5e's `enrolMiniSends` deliberately requires an event
  the new press has written ask rows for, because *a mini-send is a send after a send, and a legacy
  pressed event never sent — so there is nothing for a late arrival to be late to.*

**Measured in `gather_dev`, 2026-09-19:** 8 events carry `sentAt`. All 8 have zero ask rows. 109
`PersonEvent` rows carry a `sentAt` that no provider acceptance ever produced.

---

## ⚠ Why it is a question and not a fix

**Because the obvious fix is the defect slice 5e just closed.**

Slice 5e's sweep, keyed on `sentAt` alone, treated every recipient of every such event as a late
arrival and enrolled them. On its first run that created **20 rows across four real boards** — three
security-test events and `GTC-192 replay — arrival`, a seeded demo board — and the dispatcher claimed
and attempted a send for all 20. Nothing was delivered, and the two reasons were **a broken Resend key
and reserved email domains. Both luck.**

**So "just enrol them" is exactly what was tried by accident, and it was wrong.** On a real deployment
it is every guest of every previously-pressed event invited a second time, from a lock they were told
was final — against a press ruled ONE ACT, NO RECALL (Hinge §2, ruled gap #1).

---

## The three shapes, and none is obviously right

1. **Leave them.** Their guests are never messaged by Gather. The host either already told them by
   hand — which is what the old press's two buttons said she had done, and which [[GTC-321]] is about
   — or they were never told at all. Nothing sends, nothing surprises anybody, and the board goes on
   claiming they were sent to.
2. **Re-press them deliberately**, as a one-off, per event, with the host's hand on it. Honest about
   what is happening and it needs a surface — she is pressing a second time on an event whose lock
   says once.
3. **Backfill the rows as already-finished**, so the model is consistent and nothing sends: an
   `OutboundMessage` per recipient, `withheldAt` set with a why naming this ticket. The board stops
   lying about a send that never happened, the chase stays off them, and **nothing is delivered.**

⚠ **Shape 3 is the executor's lean and it is NOT a recommendation**, because it writes a row asserting
a withholding that no gate produced, and `withheldWhy` is documented as carrying OUR reason for not
sending — which here would be *"this event predates the sender"*. That may be exactly right or it may
be putting a false sentence in a column separated from `providerError` specifically to keep it true
(ruling AN). **That is the founder's call and it is the whole of this ticket.**

---

## ⚠ What makes it more than tidiness — AND WHY IT IS HIGH

**`PersonEvent.sentAt` is set for 109 memberships that no provider ever accepted**, and ruling G makes
that column mean *"this person's provider accepted their message."* So on every legacy event the column
means something it was never true of.

The consequence is not hypothetical: `findNudgeCandidates` filters `sentAt: { not: null }` and anchors
the whole cadence on it. **So the chase is live on those events, counting from a send that never
happened** — and slice 8 will chase those guests about an ask they were never sent, unless this is
ruled first.

⚠ **THAT IS WHY IT IS HIGH, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF THE SEVERITY ARGUMENT.** Nothing is wrong for a
guest today, because nothing chases anybody — slice 8 is unbuilt. **The defect is fully formed and
waiting.** On the day slice 8 lands, `findNudgeCandidates` will read 109 memberships whose `sentAt`
says a provider accepted a message, and chase those people — by text or email, twice — about an ask
that was never composed, never sent, and whose contents Gather does not have.

**So this must be ruled BEFORE slice 8, not before a deploy.** A ticket that reads as housekeeping
gets scheduled after the thing it blocks, which is exactly the failure this note exists to prevent.
⚠ **`type` was changed from `chore` to `bug` for the same reason** — the word at the top of a ticket
is the first thing that decides when it is read.

⚠ **WHETHER IT IS A FORMAL PRECONDITION OF SLICE 8 IS NOT CLAIMED HERE**, and no `blocks` edge is
asserted. [[GTC-296]] carries one for that slice by an explicit ruling; this ticket has been raised to
High and re-framed but not ruled, and adding the edge would be the executor deciding an ordering. It
is named as reaching slice 8 so the question is unavoidable when slice 8 is scoped.

⚠ **AND THE POPULATION IS NOT SMALL RELATIVE TO THE DATABASE:** 109 of 335 memberships — roughly a
third of every membership in `gather_dev` — carry a `sentAt` that ruling G's definition makes false of
them.

## Scope

**Decide here:**
- Which of the three shapes, per event or globally.
- What `PersonEvent.sentAt` means on a legacy event, and whether slice 8 may chase from it.
- If shape 3: whether a withholding Gather did not decide may be written into `withheldWhy`.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.
- `enrolMiniSends`'s predicate. It is the definition of the mini-send population and not a guard over
  a wider one — see [[GTC-189]] *Evidence (slice 5e)*.
- `Event.sentAt` on any legacy event. Clearing it would unlock a plan the host was told was final.

## Unknowns

1. **Whether any legacy pressed event has a real host who believes her guests were invited.** In
   `gather_dev` all 8 are seed or test boards. On a deployment the answer is unknown and it decides
   whether shape 1 is honest or negligent.
2. **Whether the board should stop claiming they were sent.** That is a glance question and
   [[GTC-192]]'s territory, not this ticket's to answer alone.
3. **Whether the two `stripePaymentIntentId` measurements matter here** — [[GTC-256]] Ruling 12's
   lapse recorded 10 of 29 events paid. If any paid event is in this population, shape 1 costs more
   than it does on a seed board.
