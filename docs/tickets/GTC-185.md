# GTC-185 — G2: Event-level dietary re-verify screen at the pre-flight

**Status:** Open | **Branch:** TBD | **Commit:** [pending] (filed `e5f6e82`; rescoped 2026-08-23 —
see founder ruling below) | **Effort:** high

*Effort level is a proposal from the discovery-report ticket breakdown, carried over from the
original per-person scope — re-run `/effort` manually before execution given the smaller,
rescoped surface.*

### Context

Originally scoped around Hinge spec §1 ("dietaries — re-verified by name") and Moment 4 spec
§10.5 (per-person capture at Moment 1, consumed by generation and re-verified by name at the
pre-flight). **Both are superseded by the founder ruling below (2026-08-23): per-person dietary
capture is not being built.** [[GTC-184]]'s 2026-08-15 ruling already settled this for G1
("per-person dietary capture at Moment 1 — ruled out, not deferred... a different product"), but
this ticket was never updated to match and still assumed by-name data G1 does not, and will not,
produce.

What actually exists after [[GTC-184]]: `EventSetup.dietaryData` — one JSON blob per **event**
(`{ status, requirements: string[], other? }`, read via `readDietaryData()` in `src/lib/dietary.ts`).
No per-person or per-household dietary attribution exists anywhere in the schema — `Person`,
`PersonEvent`, and `Household` were all checked; none carries a dietary field.

Today: no route or component re-displays this data anywhere. The pre-flight screen itself (I1,
[[GTC-188]]) is also unbuilt — this ticket builds one piece of a screen that doesn't exist yet.

### Founder ruling — per-person dietary capture confirmed out of scope (Nigel, 2026-08-23)

- Counts are sufficient. The [[GTC-184]] 2026-08-15 ruling stands and is extended explicitly to
  this ticket: per-person dietary capture is not being built, for G1 or G2.
- Reasoning (unchanged from the GTC-184 ruling): dietary needs are event-level counts-of-kind;
  counts are sufficient to verify coverage. Per-person identity would only be required for
  portioning or for telling an individual guest which dish is theirs — a different product.
- **This supersedes the original G1 scope in the discovery report**
  (`docs/04_roadmap/moment4-hinge-discovery-report.md:154`), which had G1 itself include
  "add per-person dietary capture at Moment 1 household capture per §10.5" — that line no longer
  reflects what will be built.

### Spec lines superseded by this ruling (specs not edited)

Both of the following remain in the spec documents as written, unmodified, and both now describe
something that will not be built. Stated here so a future session reading the specs fresh does
not re-derive the per-person requirement and re-open this question:

- `docs/03_specs/gather-hinge-spec-v1.md:20` (§1) — "**Dietaries** — re-verified by name. Dietary
  needs are a safety condition in this product, not a preference; the last check before exposure
  re-confirms the one thing that must never be wrong." **The "by name" clause is superseded; the
  re-verification this ticket builds is event-level, not by name.**
- `docs/03_specs/gather-moment-4-spec-v1.md:144` (§10.5) — "Per-person dietary is captured at
  Moment 1... The pre-flight's by-name re-verify then shows her what she entered, against the
  plan." **Superseded in full — no per-person capture point exists or will exist under this
  ruling.**

Whether a dedicated ticket should reconcile the spec documents themselves (rather than leaving
this contradiction standing only here) is an open question for the founder — not decided or
filed by this ticket.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- A pre-flight sub-screen displaying the event-level three-state dietary answer
  (`EventSetup.dietaryData` — `status`, `requirements`, free-text `other`) for Kate to confirm or
  correct before the send. Not by name. No per-person data of any kind.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.
- The rest of the pre-flight (coverage sweep, message-shown, recipients-confirmed) — this ticket
  builds one piece of I1 (GTC-188), not the whole screen.

**Out of scope:**
- Per-person dietary capture, storage, or display — ruled out above, not deferred. Reopening this
  requires a new founder decision; it is not this ticket's concern.
