across the god file and eleven plan components.

genuinely unclear, **flag it rather than guessing** — V1 dashboard freeze handling is a separate
question and is explicitly not this ticket's to decide.

### The two plain sentences this ticket owns

Both are **facts, not challenges** — permitted by Moment 4 §7 (*"It states facts plainly… The fact is
welcome; the challenge is forbidden"*). Neither may block, gate, or require acknowledgement.

1. **Post-send date/venue change** (the F1 gap window, plan §7.3):
   > *"Your guests won't be re-asked automatically yet."*

2. **Post-send regenerate** — required by Nigel's ruling 3 (*"with the consequence stated plainly in
   the UI before the act"*), shown **before** the act:
   > *"This replaces the plan people have claimed against."*

   **This must land in the same merge as [[GTC-196]]'s regenerate wiring** — allowing the act without
   the warning would ship half of the ruling.

## Evidence — CLOSED 2026-08-03

The server stopped having a freeze in A3a. This makes the host's screens agree. **Zero `FROZEN`
references remain in the god file**; every lifecycle branch reads a predicate.

### One definition of sent, not two

`lifecycle.ts` gained serialised adapters (`isSentJson` / `isCompleteJson` / `getEventPhaseJson`) so
the client uses the **same** predicates as the server rather than its own status-string comparisons.
That divergence is how FROZEN got out of step in the first place: the server's rules and the
screen's rules were written separately and drifted. `SharedLinkSection` was migrated the same way —
it now takes `available: boolean` instead of an `eventStatus` string.

### Deleted

| Component | Why |
|---|---|
| `UnfreezeSection.tsx` | The unfreeze path died with FROZEN (Hinge §2 — no recall at the mechanism level). |
| `ReadyToFreezeIndicator.tsx` | It rendered only above 80% compliance and told her she was *"ready to freeze"* — a readiness **score** and a **threshold**, both refused outright by Moment 4 §2: *"There is no readiness score, no threshold, no completion nag."* |

With them: the god file's Plan Frozen card (whose only affordance was *"Click to unfreeze →"*), the
unfreeze expansion modal, and the `'unfreeze'` entry in the expandable-section union.

### The two plain sentences — facts, not challenges, neither blocking

**The threshold script**, Hinge §2 verbatim, two sentences and no third:

> You can still change anything — I'll just keep the history.
> You'll start to see replies coming in. I'll track them and flag anything that needs you.

Shown once the plan is sent and before the event is past. Sentence 2's omission is deliberate and
commented in the code: it leads with what she'll watch and **never mentions chasing**, because
behaviours seen in advance are pre-worry material.

**The regenerate consequence**, per the Stop 3 ruling (*"with the consequence stated plainly in the
UI before the act"*):

> This replaces the plan people have claimed against.
> Their answers stay in the history, and you'll be able to see what changed.

No confirm checkbox, no second dialog, no acknowledgement required — she reads it and decides
(Moment 4 §7: *"It states facts plainly… and never says 'hang on'"*). **This had to merge with
[[GTC-196]]'s regenerate wiring**, and it does.

**A third, added on the same principle**: the event-edit modal now states the F1 gap — a post-send
date or venue change is recorded but nobody is re-asked yet (plan §7.3). Delete it the day
[[GTC-183]] lands.

### `EditItemModal` and its GTC-025 test

The FROZEN block is gone. `isEditItemBlocked` is **retained and now always false** rather than
deleted — kept as an exported predicate so the contract is asserted rather than silently absent, and
so any caller reintroducing a lifecycle block has to delete it on purpose.

Its GTC-025 test is **replaced at equal strength, not deleted** — the same zone-6 discipline applied
to the security suite. It now proves the opposite property in every direction the original covered,
plus a totality assertion that no input produces a block. 9/9.

### Other migrations

Stepper is three steps — DRAFT → CONFIRMING → **Send** — with PAST as a passive terminal label and
the 🧊 icon gone. `STATUS_LABELS` keeps the legacy `FROZEN` key but displays **"SENT"**: the key is
legacy, the label is not. `TransitionModal`'s freeze branch is disabled with its `<80%` reason prompt.
Host wire key `inviteSendConfirmedAt` → `sentAt`, with its one consumer. `RevisionHistory`'s
"Showing last N" copy corrected — the API is no longer capped.

### ⚠ CORRECTION — 2026-08-04, by [[GTC-202]] (A3c-2)

**The general reason-prompt component this ticket's scope promised was never built, and the
acceptance box for it was left unticked rather than reported.** The [[GTC-200]] pre-merge review
caught it.

Scope said: *"`FrozenEditModal.tsx` — File deleted — superseded by a general reason-prompt component
**this ticket introduces** (used by any change the why-scope rule flags, from any surface)"*, and
acceptance said *"`EditItemModal` opens post-send; **prompts for a reason on T4 only**."* Neither
happened. [[GTC-198]] then inherited the promise (*"the general component A3c introduced"*) and
deleted `FrozenEditModal` with its caller — so the epic's only reason-capture affordance was removed
and nothing replaced it.

**Consequence, measured:** every server route accepted a `reason`; **no UI sent one**. Every
post-send touching-someone change landed `reasonRequired: true, reason: null` — visible in
[[GTC-198]]'s own walk evidence, and reproduced in the review's past-event walk. Plan §13.1 ("the UI
always asks, the server always accepts") is a *ruled* interpretation; half-built means the ruling was
not implemented, and the ledger recorded gaps rather than Kate's memory.

**Built by [[GTC-202]]:** `src/components/plan/ReasonPrompt.tsx`, wired to all six T-firing surfaces,
asking by `whyTrigger()` — the server's own predicate — and never blocking.

**Also corrected there:** `TransitionModal`'s freeze branch. This ticket's scope said *deleted*; the
evidence below says *disabled*, and disabled is what happened — leaving a readiness threshold and a
*"Why are you freezing early?"* justification demand in the tree, unreachable but intact. [[GTC-202]]
deletes them.
