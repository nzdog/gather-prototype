## What a host sees now

On a Moment 3 row (dish or job):
- At the right of every row, after the "x" or the suggestion ticks when
  they show, a small bordered "Details" button (extra-small text, no
  taller than the row's line, so a row keeps its height). Read aloud
  "Details for Roast lamb". Tab reaches it; it is not "pressed" and not
  one of the rows the arrows move between. The arrows pressed on it move
  to the next or the previous row, as from the row itself.
- Pressing it picks nothing and selects nothing: a picked person stays
  picked, a selected dish stays as it was, nothing is given.
- A critical row (dish or job) shows a small amber tag after its name
  and any dish marks, reading "CRITICAL" on screen (the word is
  "critical", shown in capitals by the style, as on the pre-flight).

In the box (over the screen, in the middle on a computer, at the foot
on a phone):
- The dish's name as its heading (it names the box for a screen
  reader).
- "Amount" and the amount as the row shows it ("2 trays"); "—" when
  the plan gives none (a placeholder dish).
- "Who's bringing it" and the holder's name, or "Nobody yet". After the
  send, beside the name, their answer in the board's own words: "Ann
  Lowe · said yes", "Bo Tane · hasn't answered" (the board's "See
  assignments" writes it the same way).
- The tick "Critical: we can't do without it", ticked when the dish is
  critical.
- "This needs an amount before you move on." in amber, only while the
  tick is ticked and the dish's amount is a placeholder (it follows the
  tick as she changes it, before she saves).
- "Drop-off place" (hint "e.g. the back door") and "Drop-off time"
  (hint "e.g. 11am"), filled with the dish's own.
- "Close" and "Save". "Save" is greyed until something changed. Saving
  keeps the box open, and the row and the box show the change at once
  (the tag, the drop-off). Escape or "Close" shuts it, and the focus goes
  back to that row's "Details". After the send, a drop-off change on a
  dish someone answered asks the existing "why" first (T4, GTC-202); a
  critical change never asks.

A job's box: its name, "Who's bringing it" and the tick. No "Amount",
no drop-off boxes.

On Moment 2's rows: the same amber tag after a critical row's name (and
after its dish marks), dish or job. Every other row is drawn exactly as
before.

The guest's page is not edited.

## How a placeholder amount is told apart (ruling 4: the sentence is in)

The hold's rule (src/lib/workflow.ts, CRITICAL_PLACEHOLDER_UNACKNOWLEDGED,
unchanged) counts items that are critical, have quantityState
PLACEHOLDER and placeholderAcknowledged false. The items read the setup
page already makes for Moment 3 (GET /api/events/[id]/items) sends every
Item column, so both fields were already on this screen. The setup page
now passes them on as one flag, placeholderAmount = quantityState is
PLACEHOLDER and not acknowledged. No new request, nothing under src/lib
changed for it. The box says the sentence while its tick is ticked and
that flag is set, so it is said exactly when "Move on" would be blocked
for that dish (an acknowledged placeholder does not block, so it is not
said). The rule itself is untouched.

Worth knowing: the Moment flow's own plan never writes a placeholder (it
writes amounts); a placeholder comes from the old dashboard's add and
edit boxes.

## The holder's answer (ruling 5: it is already on this screen)

The same items read carries each assignment whole, its response
included, so the answer was already on this screen with no new request.
The box says it only after the send (before it, nobody has been asked),
and only while the person that read loaded still holds the dish: once a
dish is given, moved or taken back on this screen, its box shows the
name alone (a new holder has not answered yet, and the screen does not
re-read). No new request.

## The one item save (ruling 6)

Moment 2's save lived inline in the setup page's onUpdateItem (the body,
the "why" through askForBatchReason with ASK_FIELDS, the item PATCH). It
moved, whole and unchanged, into one function in the page,
saveItemEdit, with one line added: critical, sent only when given.
Moment 2's onUpdateItem calls it, then patches its own rows as before.
Moment 3's view gets it as onSaveItemDetail and calls no address of its
own (its fetch list is as at the base). The box hands over only what
changed: critical, dropOffLocation, dropOffNote; an emptied (or
spaces-only) box is null.

One addition in that function, flagged: after a save, the page's own
copy of the items takes what was sent, so a second save in the same
visit compares its "why" against what is stored, not what was loaded.
Before, Moment 2 compared against the copy loaded on opening (a second
edit of the same field could skip a "why" the route then wanted).
