### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-13 at `d45294e`)

- Moment 2's opening is `src/components/plan/Moment2Opening.tsx` (123 lines): the arc, "What this
  does" and "When it's done" (`MomentWords`), the solid "Let's do this →" (`onStart`), W2 of
  GTC-375 when a plan is put away, the quiet invites-only link with its explanation
  (`INVITES_ONLY_WORDS` in `src/lib/setup/invites-only.ts`), and the way back to Moment 1. The page
  wires it at `src/app/plan/[eventId]/setup/page.tsx` L1492-1550. `start()` opens the questions
  (`showMoment2Step1`); with invites only chosen it first clears the flag.
- The questions (`Moment2Step1Modal`) write the answers to `EventSetup` through
  `POST /api/events/[id]/setup`; "Generate plan →" calls
  `POST /api/events/[id]/finalize-plan`, which refuses a record with no `eventType` (GTC-381 Q3, the
  same 404 as no record) and spends one of the
  event's ten AI calls (`aiCallsUsed`). `regenerate-plan` refuses the same way.
- The plan view is `src/components/plan/Moment2PlanView.tsx` (1125 lines): the heading "Here's what
  I'd suggest for {eventName}.", "N items across M categories, based on G guests" (the guests
  button goes back to Moment 1), "Not quite right? Regenerate the plan" (`onRegeneratePlan`), the
  category sections with "+ Add item" and "↻ Regenerate this category" (`onRegenerateCategory`),
  "+ Add category" at the foot, the bulk-remove button, and "Invites, people and reminders →".
  Adding an item or a category already works by hand through `onAddItem` and `onAddCategory`.
- Teams and items: `Team.source` and `Item.source` are `ItemSource` (`GENERATED`, `TEMPLATE`,
  `MANUAL`, `HOST_EDITED`), `MANUAL` by default on a Team. `disposableItemWhere`
  (`src/lib/ai/plan-write.ts`) lets a regeneration delete only `GENERATED`, unprotected, unanswered
  items, so a hand-written item survives a redraft.
- **What counts as "a plan".** `hasGeneratedPlan(items)` in `src/lib/setup/entry-stage.ts` is true
  only for items whose source is `GENERATED` or `HOST_EDITED`. It is read by `resolveSetupStage`
  (opens the plan view), by the strip's doors (`src/lib/moments/strip.ts` L75, wired at
  `setup/page.tsx` L825), by the strip's Moment 2 tap (`goToMoment`, L811), by the asked-for stage
  (L519) and by the way-back band (`src/lib/moments/way-back.ts` L63). Its own comment says teams
  are "the cheap test and the wrong one: V1 events have teams, and so does a plan built by hand".
  So today an event with only hand-written items, or only empty sections, reads as having no plan:
  a reload puts it back at the opening or the questions, and the strip says there is no plan.
- **What counts as "Moment 2 has begun".** `momentTwoBegun(setup)` (GTC-381 Q1): a kind chosen,
  invites only, or the plan approved. A hand-made plan chooses no kind, so a reload before approval
  reads as "not begun" unless something else says so.
- Where `eventType` is read besides those routes: `setup/page.tsx` L447 and L855 (the details on
  screen), `pre-flight/page.tsx` L121 and its route L213, `glance/page.tsx` L215, the old
  dashboard `plan/[eventId]/page.tsx` L115. Each already reads it as possibly null.
- An approved plan opens at Moment 3 with or without items (`entry-stage.ts`), so an empty plan can
  be approved today and Moment 3 has its own empty state.
