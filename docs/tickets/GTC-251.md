### ✅ SCOPED 2026-09-29 — FOUNDER RULINGS (in Cowork, one question at a time)

**Q1 — red only, or red plus "more reminders", for launch?** Asked with the reminders' current

**So the full door of Moment 4 §4 is in scope for launch:** the red, and from it a hand-back to

**Q2 — when does a guest turn red?** Asked with the cadence stated (reminders on days 4 and 7
after the invitation; one on day 5 for "go gentle"). The founder chose, verbatim: *"Three days"* —
*"The last reminder gets a fair chance: the same gap Gather leaves between its two reminders. They

*"Match my reminder pace"* (three days at the normal pace, six at the relaxed pace). **So a guest
turns red three days after the last reminder of their cadence, whatever the pace, and three days
after the last of any further reminders the host gives them.** This refines [[GTC-305]]'s
*"amber is false from then on"*: for those three days the last reminder is still working.

**Q3 — how does the hand-back work?** The founder chose, verbatim: *"Pick 1, 2 or 3 more"* —

### ⚠ Added 2026-08-27 by [[GTC-179]] (E2) — don't-chase makes `nextNudgeAt` null from moment zero

**This ticket must distinguish NO cadence from a SPENT cadence.** GTC-179's Phase 0 gate ruled
that a person marked `DONT_CHASE` resolves to an empty offset array, and an empty cadence makes
`nextNudgeAt` in `src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts` return null **immediately** — not after two nudges,
but from the instant the mark is set. A `GENTLE` person reaches null after one nudge rather than
two, on a different day from everyone else.

`nextNudgeAt`'s own docstring already warns "NULL DOES NOT MEAN RED... whether that is
exhaustion, and what colour it earns, is GTC-251's (E6) to decide." That warning was written
against a world with one cadence. **An exhausted predicate written as "`nextNudgeAt === null`"
would turn every don't-chase person red the instant they are marked — inverting the control.**
Kate says *stop chasing my mother*, and the system escalates her mother to the host. That is the
exact opposite of what §10.3's off-switch is for.

Ordering: **GTC-179 lands first** (it is unblocked; this ticket additionally waits on
[[GTC-180]]), so the reconciliation is this ticket's to absorb, not GTC-179's to pre-empt.

Two further consequences worth checking when this ticket is scoped:
- A **retry count** (§4's "with a retry count she specifies") applied to a don't-chase person
  would re-open nudging for someone the host switched off. Retry must not override the mark.
- The **exhaustion count** this ticket derives is a count against a cadence whose length is now
  variable — 0, 1 or 2 legs depending on pace and mark. "Exhausted" cannot be hard-coded to two.

GTC-179 does not settle any of this; it records the collision so this ticket is not surprised by
it. See GTC-179's "Recorded from the Phase 1 audit (2026-08-27)", item (a), and its Ruling 1 on
gentle being a volume control and not a handback control.
