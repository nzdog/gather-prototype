# GTC-348 — the door's route opens for an opted-out guest, and one refusal is false

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-01 at [[GTC-340]] on founder instruction (plan ruling Q6:
*"file all three (findings 1, 3 and 4) as stubs, numbered from GTC-347, severity blank, none a
precondition"*). Not scoped. **Not a deploy precondition.**
**Severity:** not set at filing — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

On the board, a guest who opted out of email for the event, or whose email reported it, reads the
opted-out red with no door ([[GTC-305]] ruling 3: the chase standing sits above the delivery
failure, so `doorOffered` is false). The board is right.

The door's route is not. `resolveSubject` in `src/lib/press/resend.ts` asks only whether the latest
ASK row failed (`deliveryFactFrom`); it never consults the chase standing. So a direct GET or POST,
or a board opened before the opt-out, opens the door for that guest:

- AGAIN or EDIT queues an EMAIL ask row. The drain re-runs the chooser, which withholds it
  `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` (or `EMAIL_REPORTED`), so nothing is sent — but the host was offered, and
  could press, "send it again" about somebody who said stop.
- PHONE is refused `NO_PHONE`: *"They have no mobile number, so there is nothing to text."* That is
  false of a guest who holds one. `textAskReachOf` passes the chooser's `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` refusal
  through unchanged ([[GTC-296]]), and `resendToPerson` maps every refusal other than
  `SMS_OPTED_OUT` and `PHONE_UNUSABLE` to `NO_PHONE`.

This was already true of an email guest whose ask was rejected and who later opted out. [[GTC-340]]
adds the guest whose text Gather's own setup stopped (now red `NOT_DELIVERED`).
