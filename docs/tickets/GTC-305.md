## ✅ SCOPED 2026-09-28 — THE BOARD ONLY; THREE FOUNDER RULINGS

The pre-flight half of this ticket was built by [[GTC-311]] (`0067be3`), which lists the host's
"asked but not chased" people at the pre-flight. **What remains is the board.** Three rulings, asked
one at a time, govern it; they overturn the red this ticket was filed on.

### The principle — AMBER MEANS GATHER IS CHASING SOMEONE, AND NOBODY ELSE IS AMBER

Amber says Gather is following this person up. Since [[GTC-311]] and [[GTC-189]] slice 8b there are
people Gather invites and will never remind, and today they read amber: a false sentence on the main
screen.

### Ruling 1 — a person the host has handed to herself reads GREY

*"Hand this one to me"*, or the event default set to leave them to her, reads **grey: she has taken
it.** Ruling T's own ground — *"grey means she has taken it"* — and red would be Gather telling her
something she told it first. **This overturns ruling Y's red and the red the AG and AH banners above
assume.** Unknown 1 is answered: this population needs **no new red reason**.

### Ruling 2 — this grey replaces "waiting to hear back", and nothing else

Unlike the don't-chase mark ([[GTC-192]] Ruling 14, grey over every red, **unchanged** — `NOT_CHASED`
in `derivePersonState` in `src/lib/glance/state.ts`), this grey takes the place of AMBER only:

- a row the guest has answered shows the answer, as for anyone;
- a delivery failure still reads red — a bounced invitation is *"never got it"*, with ruling U's door
  to fix the address or send it as a text.

The ground: *"hand this one to me"* means *"I'll do the follow-up"*, not *"leave everything about them
to me"*, which is what don't-chase is for. Without the red the host would chase someone about an
invitation they never got, and these are mostly people without a mobile, so a dead address leaves no
way of reaching them at all. Unknown 2 is answered: grey while she waits, the answer when it comes.

### Ruling 3 — the two other groups Gather never reminds

- **Opted out of texts** (ruling O: asked by email, never chased on any channel) — **GREY**, under
  ruling 2's rule. The host was told at the pre-flight before she pressed Send (ruling AM's line).
- **Unsubscribed from, or reported, the invitation after it went out** ([[GTC-296]]'s per-event
  opt-out; [[GTC-324]] ruling 1) — **RED, "opted out"**: it is news she has not been told. The reason
  and its strip line already exist, declared with no producer at GTC-296 (`EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` in
  `RED_REASONS`, *"opted out"* in `WHY_LINES`); **this ticket gives it a producer.** Like the grey,
  it takes the place of "waiting to hear back": a guest who has answered everything shows their
  answers.
  - **No "send it again" door** — the worst thing to offer someone who has just said stop.
    `EMAIL_OPTED_OUT` stays out of `DOOR_REASONS` in `src/lib/glance/actions.ts`.
  - The by-hand nudge stays as [[GTC-296]] ruling 4 and [[GTC-324]] ruling 1 have it: by text, with
    its notice.
- **This absorbs [[GTC-327]]**, filed at GTC-296 for exactly this gap. It closes with this ticket.

**A bounce still reads red for any of these people** (ruling 2).

### What the principle implies — [[GTC-251]]
