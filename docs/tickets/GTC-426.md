## Why

GTC-425 closed the demo's copy-start route until launch and left one
thing open: a real event can reuse a Person that was made inside a
sample (a demo copy), because nothing on Person says it was made for a
sample. The founder's leaning then was a marker column on Person
(option B). This ticket is T1 of the sample clean-up work: the column
only. T2 changes the identity rule (Q2) and writes the marker.
