### Context

Gather does with Kate, and as she moves through it, the weight lifts. The pre-flight explicitly
includes: **coverage** (everyone/everything accounted for, nothing unassigned she doesn't already
know about), **dietaries** (event-level re-verify, not by name — G2, GTC-185, rescoped per the
2026-08-23 founder ruling: per-person dietary capture is not being built), **the message, shown** (she reads
exactly what each person will receive before it goes), **recipients confirmed** (per household,
per the Moment 1 toggles — C1, GTC-172). Now also the per-event cadence-pace selector (§10.3 —
E2, GTC-179).

Today: no coverage sweep, no message-preview, no recipient-confirmation screen anywhere. The
closest existing artifact is `checkFreezeReadiness`'s warnings (`UNASSIGNED_ITEMS`,
`LOW_COMPLIANCE`, `CRITICAL_GAPS`), which A1's plan (GTC-167) may or may not repurpose for the
coverage-sweep piece — check A1's decision before assuming this ticket rebuilds coverage-checking
from scratch.

### Founder rulings — auto-assign (Nigel, 2026-08-29)

*Four rulings made while deciding whether the pre-flight could offer item assignment. They
change `POST /api/events/[id]/people/auto-assign`, which this ticket did not originally name;
recorded here because this is the ticket that executed them.*

**Ruling 1 — auto-assign places people only.** The item-distribution half (step 5b) is removed.
Auto-assign puts participants on teams and stops; it creates no `Assignment` rows. Rationale, on
the record: **dealing out who brings what is a decision, not a distribution.** Two harms were
measured on the GTC-133 fixture, not hypothesised: the round-robin dealt 8 of 27 items to three
CHILD-role people who are guaranteed never to be messaged about them (§10.6 is absolute;
[[GTC-207]] makes them assignable but never messageable), and it treated the host as a guest.
**Auto-assigning items is not deleted from the roadmap, only from this button** — it may return
later as an explicit, separate option.

**Ruling 2 — people-only everywhere, including the V1 dashboard button.** The old behaviour must
not survive anywhere. Two behaviours behind one name is how this gets misremembered. Satisfied
server-side: `PeopleSection.tsx`'s button is the only caller, so removing the half in the route
removes it everywhere. No flag reinstates it.

**Ruling 3 — the host exclusion. ⚠ THE RULING'S PREMISE WAS WRONG, AND IS CORRECTED HERE at the
founder's instruction (2026-08-29) rather than built past.**

The ruling stated that the exclusion "compares `event.hostId` (a User id) against
`personEvent.personId` (a Person id)". **It does not.** `Event.hostId` is a **`Person`** FK
(`host Person @relation("EventHost")` in `prisma/schema.prisma`), so the original comparison was
type-correct. The defect the ruling named does not exist. The error originated in this ticket's
own earlier reporting and was inherited by the ruling.

**The hardening was kept anyway, and the founder confirmed keeping it.** The exclusion now
resolves the host by three identity paths — `personId === Event.hostId`, `role === 'HOST'`, and
a shared non-null `Person.userId` — so it is resilient to whichever path a given event has. It is
resolved POSITIVELY and then excluded by primary key, never as `NOT { OR: [...] }`: the negated
form was written first and **emptied the participant list on a real 14-person event**, because
`NOT (person.userId = 'x')` is NULL rather than TRUE for every guest whose `userId` is null —
the same SQL three-valued-logic trap `src/lib/eligibility/child-exclusion.ts` records and refuses
an allowlist to avoid. That regression is pinned by a test that was verified to fail against the
buggy form.

**The real gap is [[GTC-256]], filed.** On a Moment-flow event the host has no `PersonEvent` at
all, so no exclusion of any shape can reach them. That is a Moment 1 capture decision and is not
this ticket's.

**Ruling 4 — children are placed on teams.** Confirmed as already the behaviour and left
unchanged: no `householdRole` filter exists in the participant query, and adding one would
contradict [[GTC-207]], which pins the kid-with-a-job as assignment-eligible and forbids
importing the message-exclusion gate into any assignment path. A child on a team is fine; a
child holding an item they can never be told about was the problem, and Ruling 1 removed it.

**Filed, not fixed, by founder instruction:** [[GTC-255]] (team move silently deletes every
assignment a person holds, unlogged and untransacted) and [[GTC-256]] (above).

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- The pre-flight screen assembling: coverage-sweep, G2's dietary re-verify, a message preview
  (what each recipient will actually receive), C1's recipient confirmation, and **BOTH of E2's
  cadence controls** — into one guided, has-an-end flow.
- **E2's controls, both of them (updated 2026-08-27 by [[GTC-179]]'s Ruling 8).** This bullet
  previously named only the per-event pace selector. It now covers:
  - the **per-event pace** — `Event.nudgePace`: standard `[4, 7]` / relaxed `[6, 12]` / off `[]`;
  - the **per-person mark** — `PersonEvent.nudgeMark`: go gentle `[5]` / don't chase `[]`.

  **The mark moved here from Moment 1.** Moment 4 §10.3 and §10.7 place it "alongside the
  recipient picker", and Ruling 8 supersedes both: the picker is ONE decision per household and
  the mark is PER PERSON, so putting marks beside it produces four controls where §10.7
  deliberately put one — the matrix §10.7 itself refuses. The pre-flight is a per-person review
  by nature and already hosts the pace, so both cadence controls belong on this screen.
  ⚠ The spec passages still read the old way and are not edited by that ticket; do not re-derive
  the Moment 1 placement from them.

  **The machinery is already built and needs nothing from this ticket but a surface.** [[GTC-179]]
  stores both settings, obeys them on the direct AND proxy send paths, composes them by
  quieter-wins (its Ruling 4 — a GENTLE person on an OFF event gets nothing, not one nudge), and
  records a distinct skip reason for each suppression. Read and write the two columns; do not
  re-implement the resolution. `resolveNudgeOffsetDays` in `src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts` is pure and
  client-safe precisely so this screen can render the same clock the sweep enforces.

  **Until this ticket lands, neither control is reachable by any host** — GTC-179 closed as
  machinery, deliberately and on the record.
