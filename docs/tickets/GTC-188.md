### Context

Hinge spec §1: the threshold is not a summary screen before a button — it is a guided check
Gather does with Kate, and as she moves through it, the weight lifts. The pre-flight explicitly
includes: **coverage** (everyone/everything accounted for, nothing unassigned she doesn't already
know about), **dietaries** (event-level re-verify, not by name — G2, GTC-185, rescoped per the
2026-08-23 founder ruling: per-person dietary capture is not being built), **the message, shown** (she reads
exactly what each person will receive before it goes), **recipients confirmed** (per household,
per the Moment 1 toggles — C1, GTC-172). Now also the per-event cadence-pace selector (§10.3 —
E2, GTC-179).

Today: no coverage sweep, no message-preview, no recipient-confirmation screen anywhere. The
closest existing artifact is `checkFreezeReadiness`'s warnings (`UNASSIGNED_ITEMS`,
`LOW_COMPLIANCE`, `CRITICAL_GAPS`), which A1's plan (GTC-167) may or may not repurpose for the
coverage-sweep piece — check A1's decision before assuming this ticket rebuilds coverage-checking
from scratch.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- The pre-flight screen assembling: coverage-sweep, G2's dietary re-verify, a message preview
  (what each recipient will actually receive), C1's recipient confirmation, and **BOTH of E2's
  cadence controls** — into one guided, has-an-end flow.
- **E2's controls, both of them (updated 2026-08-27 by [[GTC-179]]'s Ruling 8).** This bullet
  previously named only the per-event pace selector. It now covers:
  - the **per-event pace** — `Event.nudgePace`: standard `[4, 7]` / relaxed `[6, 12]` / off `[]`;
  - the **per-person mark** — `PersonEvent.nudgeMark`: go gentle `[5]` / don't chase `[]`.

  **The mark moved here from Moment 1.** Moment 4 §10.3 and §10.7 place it "alongside the
  recipient picker", and Ruling 8 supersedes both: the picker is ONE decision per household and
  the mark is PER PERSON, so putting marks beside it produces four controls where §10.7
  deliberately put one — the matrix §10.7 itself refuses. The pre-flight is a per-person review
  by nature and already hosts the pace, so both cadence controls belong on this screen.
  ⚠ The spec passages still read the old way and are not edited by that ticket; do not re-derive
  the Moment 1 placement from them.

  **The machinery is already built and needs nothing from this ticket but a surface.** [[GTC-179]]
  stores both settings, obeys them on the direct AND proxy send paths, composes them by
  quieter-wins (its Ruling 4 — a GENTLE person on an OFF event gets nothing, not one nudge), and
  records a distinct skip reason for each suppression. Read and write the two columns; do not
  re-implement the resolution. `resolveNudgeOffsetDays` in `src/lib/nudge-cadence.ts` is pure and
  client-safe precisely so this screen can render the same clock the sweep enforces.

  **Until this ticket lands, neither control is reachable by any host** — GTC-179 closed as
  machinery, deliberately and on the record.
