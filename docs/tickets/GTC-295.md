# GTC-295 — Drop `PersonEvent.contactMethod`

**Status:** Open — filed 2026-09-13, not started | **Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** [ ] Critical [ ] High [ ] Medium [x] Low
Escalated (brand/trust risk): [ ] No

⚠ **DO NOT START BEFORE [[GTC-189]] SLICE 8 LANDS.** That slice removes the column's last
reader. Until then the column is load-bearing, wrong, and read.

**Filed on the founder's instruction, 2026-09-13**, from ruling C in [[GTC-189]]. The reason, in
the words it was filed with:

> A column five places write and nothing reads is the drift we just measured.

---

### What this retires

`PersonEvent.contactMethod` is a stored `ContactMethod` (`EMAIL | SMS | NONE`) derived
phone-first at capture. **In gather_dev, 184 of 269 adult non-host memberships carry a value
their own `Person` fields contradict** (measured 2026-09-13): 35 say `SMS` with no
`phoneNumber`, 125 say `NONE` with an email, 24 say `NONE` with both. Every one of those rows is
on a fixture or test event, so the number measures drift in this database rather than in real
capture — but the mechanisms that produce it are in the tree, and they are why the column is
being dropped rather than corrected:

- **A per-event snapshot of a global record.** `Person` is shared across events; the column
  lives on each `PersonEvent` and is recomputed only on the event being edited
  (`reconcileHouseholdMembers` in `src/lib/households/reconcileMembers.ts`, `PATCH` in
  `src/app/api/p/[token]/contact-info/route.ts`).
- **An edit that never recomputes.** `PATCH` in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/[personId]/route.ts` writes `email` and `phoneNumber` and
  leaves the column alone.
- **Two phone columns.** `POST` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/batch-import/route.ts` stores
  the legacy `Person.phone`, and it and `createMember` count that as a phone, while every sender
  reads `phoneNumber`. Such a row says `SMS` and can never be texted.
- **Fixtures write it directly.**

It went unnoticed because exactly one path ever read it: `findProxyNudgeCandidates` in
`src/lib/sms/proxy-nudge-eligibility.ts`, which skips a household channel whose stored value is
not `SMS`.

**What replaces it:** the two pure choosers of [[GTC-189]] slice 1 — the ask's and the chase's —
which read `email`, `phoneNumber` and `smsOptedOut` at the moment of sending, so there is nothing
stored to drift.

---

### Sequence — three steps, and this ticket owns the last two

1. **[[GTC-189]] slice 8 stops the read.** `findProxyNudgeCandidates` moves onto the chase
   chooser. Not this ticket's.
2. **Phase 1 here — stop writing.** Remove the derivation from the five write sites; mark the
   column retained-but-unwritten in `prisma/schema.prisma` with a `DO NOT READ` docstring naming
   this ticket as its end condition, in the shape [[GTC-174]] and [[GTC-178]] used. No migration.
3. **Phase 2 here — drop it.** After at least one further ticket has landed on top of phase 1, so
   the change reverts cleanly and a stray value stays auditable: drop the column and the
   `ContactMethod` enum in a migration that carries nothing else, rehearsed against a copy of the
   production database per the Deploy caveat in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`.

### Inventory — verify at execution rather than trusting this list

**The five writers:** `createMember` inside `POST` in
`src/app/api/events/[id]/households/route.ts`; `reachabilityFor` in
`src/lib/households/reconcileMembers.ts`; `POST` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/route.ts`;

`src/app/api/p/[token]/contact-info/route.ts`.

**The one reader:** `findProxyNudgeCandidates` — removed by [[GTC-189]] slice 8.

**Fixtures that set it:** `prisma/seed.ts`; `scripts/create-gtc-test-event.ts`,
`create-test-fixtures.ts`, `gtc-test-event-add-participants.ts`, `repopulate-fixture-people.ts`,
`seed-rsvp-test.ts`; and `tests/` — `child-message-exclusion-test.ts`,
`decide-by-followup-test.ts`, `glance-actions-test.ts`, `host-never-messaged-test.ts`,
`nudge-cadence-test.ts`, `nudge-cadence-controls-test.ts`, `nudge-clock-origin-test.ts`,
`nudge-dedup-scope-test.ts`, `wrap-up-double-send-test.ts`.

**Same name, different thing — leave them alone.** `NudgeComposer` and
`PersonInviteDetailModal` carry a local `'sms' | 'email'` prop called `contactMethod`; the manual
nudge route has a local variable of the same name; `tests/host-nudge-test.ts` has its own
`resolveContactMethod`. None reads the column.

### Not in scope, and named so it is not swept in

`PersonEvent.reachabilityTier` is written at the same five sites from the same fields and has the
same drift shape. It is **read for display** by `src/app/api/events/[id]/invite-status/route.ts`
and `src/components/plan/ReachabilityBar.tsx`, and [[GTC-172]] deliberately kept it as a
signal-quality assessment rather than a recipient decision. Dropping it is a different question
and needs its own ticket.
