# GTC-485 - Multi-day: the deadline from the dish's session, and what guests and the board show

Split from [[GTC-480]] at its plan, 24 Nov 2026 (the founder's ruling 1:
"GTC-485 (D, E, F, G) is written into this ticket's docs commit as a scoped
ticket and built after GTC-480 merges"). Parts D to G of GTC-480's brief, with
their letters kept. Everything behind `GATHER_SESSIONS`: with it off, the send,
the nudges, the decide-by, the board and every screen behave exactly as today.

**Status: scoped.** Built after GTC-480 merges. Its own plan must show the
founder the exact changed lines in the two Zone 7 and Zone 9 files and in the
decide-by-clock check before the founder's final word (ruling 5).

## The scope, from GTC-480's brief

- **D. The deadline:** a dish with a session counts back from that session's
  date, through the date helper, in ONE place (`lifecycle.ts` `neededBy`).
  With no session, or the switch off, exactly as today.
- **E.** The guest page shows a dish's session name AND its date.
- **F.** The board shows a small session tag on each dish line.
- **G.** The pre-flight's draft first line names the event's whole date range,
  not just the first day, when the event spans more than one date.

## Every reader of neededBy, and what each does with the change

- The board's colour for a maybe (`src/lib/glance/state.ts` `deriveItemState`,
  through `isDecideByExpired`): amber while there is time, red "maybe timed
  out" after. A maybe on an earlier session turns red sooner. Load:
  `src/lib/glance/read.ts`.
- The board's replay (`src/lib/glance/rewind.ts`, `replay.ts`): the amber to
  red steps since the host's last look. The same change. Load: `rewind.ts`.
- The reply facts (`src/lib/chase-reply-read.ts`): whether a maybe's
  follow-up is still owed and the "follow-up already sent" sentence. The same.
- The deadline carried on the board's data (`state.ts` `decideByAtFor`):
  carried, never shown.
- "Answers by" on the board's top line (`src/lib/events/event-dates.ts`
  `answersByDay`): event-level, no dish (ruled below).
- The maybe follow-up's finder (`decide-by-eligibility.ts`, the
  decide-by-followups cron every 15 minutes): who is due the one follow-up
  now. A maybe on an earlier session is followed up sooner. Zone 7.
- The follow-up's words (`decide-by-sender.ts`, `nudge-templates.ts`): "Still
  good for the X? Kate needs to know by <day>." The day comes from the finder;
  the words do not change.
- The follow-up's email retry (`decide-by-sender.ts`): names the earliest open
  maybe. Zone 9.
- Not readers: the nudges (timed from the send, they stop at the event's end),
  the guest page (no deadline date), the ask composer (start date only).

## Ruled at GTC-480's plan (24 Nov 2026)

- **Decision 3, the deadline after the send:** it moves at once (ruling 3). A
  move after the send asks "why" first (a maybe is an answer: T4); nothing is
  sent by the move; the one follow-up may come sooner; a deadline already
  passed gets no text and shows "maybe timed out". A session's own date change
  after the send is left as it is and recorded for [[GTC-481]].
- **In principle (ruling 5):** a select-only edit of the Zone 7 and Zone 9
  decide-by files (each loads the dish's session date; no opt-out, block or
  send logic changes); moving decide-by-clock's "byte-identical" check the
  narrowest way (`neededBy` keeps its old line word for word as the fall-back
  and holds no offset); "Answers by" as the next answer day still to come,
  each dish counted from its own session (whole-event dishes from the event's
  last day); the moment at 1pm NZ on the session's date (noon in winter, the
  instant an event's last day is stored as), never later than the event's.
  The final word waits for this ticket's plan.
- **The words (ruling 4):**
  - E, the guest's dish: "Christmas lunch, Thu 25 Dec" beside the calendar
    mark; "Thu 24 Dec" when the name is the date. The host's link preview the
    same, in brackets.
  - F, the board: a small grey tag after a dish's name in a person's list,
    "Christmas lunch" (or its date when the name is the date). A whole-event
    dish has no tag.
  - G, the draft first line, an event over more than one date: "We're doing
    Christmas from Thursday, 24 December to Saturday, 26 December, at Mum's."
    One date: as today, "We're doing Christmas on Wednesday, 23 December, at
    Mum's." The rest of the draft unchanged; plain characters only.
  - D: no new words; "Kate needs to know by <day>." and "Answers by <day>"
    keep their words, only the day moves.
