## ✅ SCOPED 2026-09-20 — SEVEN FOUNDER RULINGS

### 1. Reach of a no — PER EVENT

An unsubscribe applies to one event only. Alice's future invitations to the same person for a
different event still send. Storage is one row per `(personId, eventId)`.

**Overturned** the executor's proposal of per-host. The founder's ground, in short: the person is
saying no to *this event*, not to *this host*.

### 2. Mechanism — LINK AND HEADER, BOTH

Every guest-bound email carries:

- An unsubscribe link in the body, pointing at `/unsubscribe/<token>`.
- A `List-Unsubscribe` header, and its `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`
  companion (RFC 8058), so Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook show their own "Unsubscribe" button at
  the top of the message and the person can act without opening the mail.

Both, because header-only misses clients that do not render it and body-only misses the users
trained to trust the top button.

### 3. Reach across channels — CHASE-WIDE NO

An email unsubscribe for an event stops the *automatic* chase on **every** channel for that event,
including the text chase. Ruling O read straight (*"A no to one channel is treated as a no to
being chased"*), applied to the email side. If the person has no other reachable channel they
appear on the host's list with the why-line *"chose not to receive email about this event; not
chased on any channel."*

### 4. Manual nudge — AD'S OVERRIDE SURVIVES

The by-hand nudge is host-initiated, not the automatic chase, so ruling AD (*"The by-hand nudge
sends, and says what it is overriding"*) survives an email unsubscribe. The nudge sends by text
if there's a usable phone that isn't SMS-opted-out. The UI tells the host what they are
overriding: *"You're nudging this person by text. They unsubscribed from your emails for this
event."* If there is no usable text channel, the nudge is refused with the same reason.

### 5. Wrap-up thank-you — STOPPED, NO FALLBACK

The wrap-up thank-you is automatic and email-only. An email unsubscribe for the event stops it.
No text version is built to keep the send alive — that is the *"silently keeps sending"* pattern
the founder named on [[GTC-324]].

### 6. Account mail — MAGIC LINK AND WELCOME MAIL EXEMPT

`sendMagicLinkEmail` and `sendWelcomeEmail` in `src/lib/email.ts` are not host-to-guest mail —
they are direct responses to something the account holder just did (signed in, created an
account). Neither carries an unsubscribe link, and neither reads the opt-out state. Same reading
"transactional" mail gets exempted in commercial-mail law.

### 7. UEMA 2007 — BUILD TO IT

Whether the NZ Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 strictly applies to a friend's invitation
sent from Gather's domain is uncertain — a court could read either way. Build to it regardless.
Its three requirements are almost free to meet:

- **Functional unsubscribe** — met by rulings 1–3 above.
- **Sender identification** — met by *"Alice via Gather"* in the from-line, per THE VOICE
  (2026-09-13).
- **Valid physical address** — Gather's registered postal address in the footer of every
  guest-bound email, sourced from a single config constant.

Named here so the three do not get quietly removed later by someone tidying the footer.

## ⚠ CORRECTIONS 2026-09-20 — RULED AFTER CC'S PLAN SURFACED SEVEN FINDINGS

CC produced a plan against the SCOPED section above and raised seven items that
needed rulings before code. **Four of them were factual errors in this ticket

Acceptance sections carry pointer banners on the bullets amended here.

### R1 — the invitation itself, on the mini-send path, REFUSES rather than falling to text

A person can only have an email opt-out for an event if they've already received
an email for it. So the initial press cannot hit this. But a **mini-send** for a
late arrival CAN, if the person was on the same event before, unsubscribed, was
removed, and re-added.

**Ruled:** the mini-send invitation refuses in the same way the chase does. The
person appears on the host's list with ruling 3's why-line. Falling to text
would be the *"silently keeps sending on a different channel"* pattern
[[GTC-324]] was raised against.

### R2 — findNudgeCandidates GETS THE GATE TODAY; the acceptance bullet is reworded

Ruling 3 says chase-wide. Today's chase runs at `findNudgeCandidates` in
`src/lib/sms/nudge-eligibility.ts`. The acceptance's *"No file under
`src/lib/sms/` is edited"* was too broad — Do-Not-Touch Zone 7, per
GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md, is the `SmsOptOut` model and the `Person.smsOptedOut`
column, not the whole directory.

**Ruled:** `findNudgeCandidates` gains the gate. Placed above the don't-chase
mark and below the SMS opt-out check, matching `nudge-mark.ts`'s house order.
The Acceptance bullet is reworded to: *"Zone 7 untouched — `SmsOptOut` model
and `Person.smsOptedOut` column are unchanged; the two opt-out modules
(`opt-out-service.ts`, `opt-out-keywords.ts`) are unchanged."*

### R2b — findProxyNudgeCandidates AND findDecideByFollowupCandidates too

Both are automatic chases. Ruling 3 reaches them.

**Ruled:** all three finders gate on the email opt-out, same skip reason. On
the proxy path the opt-out that applies is the **recipient's** (the household
contact receiving the reminder), not the subject's.

### R3 — the wrap-up thank-you has a TEXT LEG; ruling 5 is corrected with the right premise

I told the founder at scoping that wrap-up was email-only. It isn't —
`generateWrapUpLinks` picks `channel: 'sms'` for people with a usable phone
(47 of 333 in `gather_dev`). And `dispatchPendingWrapUpMessages` has an
SMS-failure email fallback at line 285. Ruling 5 was ruled on wrong premise.

**Re-ruled:** an email opt-out stops **every EMAIL leg** of the wrap-up:
- the primary email send (for a person whose wrap-up channel is email);
- the SMS-failure email fallback (for a person whose primary was SMS and
  failed);

and leaves the **primary text send alone** (chosen because the person has a
phone, not as a fallback for a blocked email). Ruling 5's *"no fallback"*
stands as ruled — it means no text channel is manufactured to keep an email
send alive, not that an existing text send is killed by an email opt-out. The
person said no to email; a text thank-you isn't email.

### R4 — accept CC's engineering call

The unsubscribe route is two files:
- **GET** at `src/app/unsubscribe/[token]/page.tsx` — the confirm page;
- **POST** at `src/app/api/unsubscribe/[token]/route.ts` — writes the row.

Next's App Router cannot serve both from one `page.tsx`, and
`tests/security-route-scan.ts` walks `src/app/api` only. The handler is
public and is added to `route-classifications.json` with that classification.
No CSRF token — RFC 8058 one-click is a cross-origin POST by design; the
worst a forged POST does is unsubscribe its own victim from one event.

### R5 — the token is a NEW HMAC-signed stateless token, not the existing guest-link shape

I wrote *"the same one-shot signed shape as the guest-link tokens the
invitation already carries — see `src/lib/tokens/`"*. Wrong on both counts:
`src/lib/tokens.ts` is a **file**, and `generateToken` makes 32 random bytes
stored as an `AccessToken` row — **Zone 3 (Do-Not-Touch)**. Neither signed
nor one-shot.

**Ruled:** new module at `src/lib/unsubscribe-token.ts` with:

- `mintUnsubscribeToken(personId, eventId)` — base64url payload plus base64url
  HMAC-SHA256 over it, keyed on a new `UNSUBSCRIBE_TOKEN_SECRET`.
- `readUnsubscribeToken(token)` — recompute, compare with `timingSafeEqual`,
  return `{ personId, eventId }` or `null`.
- **No expiry** — a way out that stops working is not a way out.
- **Fails closed** when the secret is unset, following [[GTC-270]]'s
  precedent for `CRON_SECRET`.
- `UNSUBSCRIBE_TOKEN_SECRET` added to `.env.example` and to
  `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`'s environment table.

**Zone 3 untouched.**

### R6 — the board MAPS TO NULL; the amber-with-no-why gap is filed as a follow-up
