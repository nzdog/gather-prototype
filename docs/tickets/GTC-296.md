# GTC-296 — Every email carries a way out

**Status:** Open — filed from the founder rulings of 2026-09-14, not started, **not scoped** |
**Branch:** TBD
**Severity:** not set at filing — the founder's to set.

**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from ruling Q in [[GTC-189]] (*Founder rulings — third
pass*). This ticket records the ruling and what it meets. It proposes no shape.

> EVERY EMAIL CARRIES A WAY OUT — the invitation and both reminders. Gather has no email opt-out
> today, so this is new work.

---

### What does not exist today — verified at `5aeaf0b`

- **Opt-out is SMS only.** `Person.smsOptedOut`, `Person.smsOptedOutAt` and the `SmsOptOut` model
  in `prisma/schema.prisma` are the whole of it, and `OPT_OUT_KEYWORDS` in
  `src/lib/sms/opt-out-keywords.ts` reads inbound texts. Do-Not-Touch Zone 7 names those and
  nothing about email.
- **No email opt-out state exists for any sender to read.** The senders are `sendNudgeEmail`,
  `sendWelcomeEmail` and `sendMagicLinkEmail` in `src/lib/email.ts`.

### The three emails the ruling names — none of them built

- **The invitation** — the ask by email, [[GTC-189]] slice 5.
- **Both reminders** — the chase's day-four and day-seven email legs, [[GTC-189]] slice 8. The
  chase sends SMS only today ([[GTC-178]]'s machinery).

### ⚠ Two guest-facing emails exist today, and the ruling does not name them

The ruling's headline is *"every email"*; its list names three, none of which is built. A guest can
receive two emails from the tree today:

- **The manual nudge** — `sendNudgeEmail`, from the route in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/[personId]/nudge/route.ts`, when `chooseManualNudgeChannel`
  returns `email`.
- **The wrap-up thank-you** — `sendNudgeEmail`, from `src/lib/wrap-up.ts`.

Whether either carries the way out is the founder's. `sendWelcomeEmail` (to the host, at event
creation) and `sendMagicLinkEmail` (sign-in) are account mail rather than messages to guests, and
are named only so the question is visible.

---

### Consequences recorded — not scoped

- **An email no is a no to being chased.** Ruling O of the same pass: *"A no to one channel is
  treated as a no to being chased."* Read as written, a person who takes the email way out is not
  then chased by text. Confirm at scoping; recorded so it is met rather than discovered.
- **The invitation carries it from the first send.** [[GTC-189]] slices 5, 6 and 7 land together
  and send the invitation. If the ruling binds the first send, this ticket is a precondition of
  that release, as [[GTC-294]] and [[GTC-191]] are. **Not added to the build shape here** — the
  founder's call.
- **Where the gate sits.** The house order for SMS — opt-out first, then the don't-chase mark — is
  recorded in `src/lib/eligibility/nudge-mark.ts`. Where an email opt-out sits is scoping.
- **Zone 7 adjacency.** An email opt-out is the same kind of suppression as Zone 7's and is not
  inside it. Whether `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` gains a zone for it is a decision for when it
  exists.
- **The environment.** THE VOICE (2026-09-13) sends from Gather's verified domain. The dev
  environment has none and its Resend key does not authenticate ([[GTC-247]]), so a live test
  waits on that.

### Unknowns

1. **The mechanism** — the stored state, the link, and whether a header-level unsubscribe

2. **The reach of a no** — per event, per host, or global. `Person` is shared across events.
3. **The manual nudge and the wrap-up thank-you** — above.
4. **Whether New Zealand's Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 applies** to a host's
   invitation sent from Gather's domain. Not established.
