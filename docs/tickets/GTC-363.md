### Where this comes from

The founder walked the Moment flow himself on 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04. Cowork merged what he
found, and a list of 23 tweaks he sent on 2026-10-05, into 33 numbered items (kept in the Cowork
project doc `claude/walkthrough-2026-10-03.md`). He sorted them on 2026-10-05: 29 before launch.
Cowork proposed building them in seven batches, lightest first, so that anything that runs long is
at the end. This is batch 1: eight items that are wording, or a line in the menu lists. The item
numbers below are the walkthrough list's; his answers are quoted from the sort.

### The items (what the code does today: Cowork, read 2026-10-05 at `b6bcd15`)

#### Item 6 — explain "Who should Gather talk to for this household?"

Ruling: *"Use it as written"*. The line: *"Gather sends this person the asks for the household's
children, and tells them what the others in the household have been asked to bring. Adults with
their own email or mobile still hear from Gather themselves."*

Today the question is asked in two places. Moment 1's household form
(`src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx`, the household contact picker) has under it:
*"Everything for this household goes to one person. Leave it on the main contact unless someone
else is the better ear."* The pre-flight's step 3 (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx`)
asks the same question for each household with nothing under it. For the plan: where the line goes
(the walkthrough item names both places), whether it replaces Moment 1's two sentences, and a check
that it is true of what Gather does since [[GTC-356]].

#### Item 7 — the board's summary: "I'm looking after N"

The founder's words, 2026-10-03: *"I don't like "Gather is on X" Change it to "I'm looking after
XX""*, so *"2 need you. Gather is on 23. 1 settled."* becomes *"2 need you. I'm looking after 23. 1
settled."*

Today: `src/components/glance/strip.ts` (`Gather is on ${summary.withGather}.`), with a comment in
`src/lib/glance/state.ts`. These are [[GTC-192]]'s words, pinned in `tests/glance-grid-test.tsx`
and named in assertion labels in `tests/glance-read-test.ts` and `tests/board-not-chased-test.ts`.
His ruling replaces GTC-192's words: the pins move to the new words (not weakened), and GTC-192
records the change.

#### Item 15 — make it clear that each entry is a household

His words: *"Need to clarify household when adding people."*

Today, after the host's own household (whose screen does say "household"), Moment 1's form opens
*"Who's coming to [event]? Add them here — just a name and how to reach them. You can sort out
what they're bringing later."*, then asks *"Anyone else in this group?"*, with buttons for a
partner, a kid with a job, a kid without a job and a guest. The column beside it is headed *"Ducks
in row so far"* (`src/components/plan/HouseholdCardList.tsx`). Nothing says that one entry is one
household, and the form says "group" where the rest of Gather says "household". New words to be
proposed in the plan and ruled.

#### Item 19 — turkey for Christmas

His words: *"Add turkey to Christmas"*.

Today Christmas's "Traditional roast" dishes (`src/lib/ai/plan-option-tree-config.json`) are Glazed
ham, Roast lamb, Roast chicken, Roast beef and Roast pork. The AI's instructions
(`src/lib/ai/prompts.ts`) say NZ Christmas mains are ham and lamb, not turkey, and that turkey may
appear when the host picks it, so a turkey choice fits them as they stand. The words (for example
"Roast turkey") and where it sits in the list, to be ruled.

#### Item 22 — kiwifruit off the classic pavlova

His words: *"Delete kiwifruit from classic pavlova"*. Ruling: *"All seven lists"*. No new words
were given; the sort's example was "Classic pavlova with cream".

Today *"Classic pavlova with cream and kiwifruit"* is a dessert choice in seven event types:
Christmas, adult birthday, casual BBQ, engagement party, Easter, anniversary and farewell. No test
pins it. For the plan: the new words, to be ruled, and what happens to an event whose stored
choices name the old words.

#### Item 26 — Entrée & Starters up front for Christmas

Ruling: *"Yes, show it up front"*.

Today, for Christmas, "Entrée & Starters" sits behind *"Show 3 more food categories"* in Moment 2's
questions. ⚠ For the plan: an event type's `defaultCategories` decide both what the questions show
up front and what plan generation always takes into account (`src/lib/ai/plan-input.ts`: *"defaults
are always engaged"*). Adding it there would put starters in every Christmas plan, even when the
host picks none. The plan asks the founder which he wants: shown up front only, or also always
planned.

#### Item 25 — a notes section

His words: *"A notes section - eg do you want any leftovers tomorrow for how many people etc…"*.
Ruling: *"Rename and lift the existing box, with leftovers as the example"*.

Today the food part of Moment 2's questions ends with the "📝 Other" section (*"Anything else
Gather should know about? Music, decorations, specific equipment, venue notes..."*), stored as
`EventSetup.otherNotes` and sent to the AI as the host's other food notes. Its new name, its
example and where it moves to, to be ruled.

#### Item 20 — an "Other" box in each section

His words: *"Each section should have other box"*.

Today every question in a Moment 2 section already has a free-text box
(`src/components/shared/OptionTree.tsx`, used only by `Moment2Step1Modal.tsx`), labelled only by
its grey placeholder: for Mains, *"Describe your mains..."* and *"Specify dishes..."*. The ask is
that it reads as "Other". The words, to be ruled.
