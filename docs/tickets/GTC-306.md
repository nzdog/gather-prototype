# GTC-306 — Sarah is never asked again about Ollie

### Signal

**Expected**
A child's maybe gets the same single decide-by follow-up any other maybe gets, and it goes to the
adult who carries the child's ask — the same adult the ask itself went to.

**Actual**
`findDecideByFollowupCandidates` in `src/lib/sms/decide-by-eligibility.ts` applies the child rule
through `isMessageableRole` and `CHILD_SKIP_REASON` from `src/lib/eligibility/child-exclusion.ts`,
and adds the skip rather than routing the follow-up anywhere. Nothing reaches the household
contact. The maybe expires unasked, and `DECIDE_BY_EXPIRED` then reddens the child's row for a
question nobody was given a chance to answer.

**Smallest failing case**
A child holding one item, carried by their household contact, whose carrier answers *maybe* on the
child's behalf. Run the decide-by follow-up sweep at the follow-up point. No message is produced
for anyone.

---

### Why the child rule is not what is wrong here

**Nothing in this ticket sends to a child, and the child rule does not move.** Moment 4 §10.6 is a
hard constraint and `child-exclusion.ts` is the single place it lives. The defect is that the
finder treats *"the assignee is a child"* as *"there is nobody to message"*, which was true before
the carried ask existed and is not true now: the ask for that row already went to an adult, and the
follow-up has the same addressee.

This is the same shape ruling R closed for the chase. `findProxyNudgeCandidates` and the ask both
learned to route a child's row to an adult; the decide-by finder did not.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
`findDecideByFollowupCandidates` in `src/lib/sms/decide-by-eligibility.ts` and whatever in
`src/lib/sms/decide-by-scheduler.ts` and `src/lib/sms/decide-by-sender.ts` has to carry the
carrier's identity rather than the assignee's.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. **Zone 7 is live** — the follow-up is an
  SMS and opt-out applies to the carrier exactly as it does to any recipient.
- `isMessageableRole` / `CHILD_SKIP_REASON` in `src/lib/eligibility/child-exclusion.ts`. The child
  rule is imported, not restated and not weakened; a child is still never a destination.
- The decide-by clock itself ([[GTC-175]] / D2). This ticket changes who is asked, not when.

### Unknowns

1. **One follow-up or one per row.** A carrier holding her own maybe and a child's maybe could get
   one message naming both, or two. The ask composes one message per recipient (ruling D); whether
   the follow-up inherits that is not ruled.
2. **What the message says.** The decide-by follow-up has its own words in
   `src/lib/sms/decide-by-sender.ts`, and none of them say *"on Ollie's behalf"*. Ruling Z rules who
   is asked, not what is said. The slice 2 words are the register to match.
3. **A carrier with no usable phone.** The follow-up is SMS. Under [[GTC-189]] ruling Y such a
   carrier is handed over at the send and is chased by nothing, so the follow-up does not reach her
   either. Whether that is the right answer for the follow-up as well as for the chase is not ruled.
4. **Whether the host as carrier is followed up.** Slice 1 answer 1 ruled she is not chased. The
   follow-up is not the chase, and A2's warning against widening applies.
