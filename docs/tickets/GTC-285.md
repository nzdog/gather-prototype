# GTC-285 — the second stranding: Moment 1 done, no EventSetup, wrong destination

**Status:** Open — needs scoping when pulled | **Effort:** TBD

*Found during [[GTC-235]]'s investigation, 2026-09-12. Upstream of [[GTC-235]] and
[[GTC-237]] and on neither.*

## Signal

**Expected:** a host who has entered her households and stopped can get back to Moment 1 from
her events list.

**Actual:** `/plan/events` routes on `event.setup` — `router.push(event.setup ?
/plan/${id}/setup : /plan/${id})`. The **only** write to `EventSetup` in the tree is the
debounced save in Moment 2 Step 1 (`POST /api/events/[id]/setup`, called from
`Moment2Step1Modal`). So an event where Moment 1 is done and Moment 2 has not started has no
`EventSetup` row, and the list sends her to the V1 dashboard — a surface built for a different
flow, showing her households as bare people rows.

**Smallest failing case:** pay, enter one household, go to `/plan/events`, click the event.

## Why [[GTC-235]] does not fix it

[[GTC-235]] made the setup route rehydrate, so arriving at `/plan/[id]/setup` now lands her
correctly on Moment 1. It did not change what the events list decides, and the list is where
she starts. The two fixes meet only if she reaches the route at all.

## Candidate directions, not a ruling

- **Widen the list's discriminator.** `_count.teams` is already selected and `setup` already
  narrowed; a household count would be a third field on `EVENTS_LIST_WIRE_SELECT`. The rule the
  list wants is the same one `resolveSetupStage` already answers — it should not grow a second,
  differently-shaped copy of it.
- **Or write `EventSetup` earlier.** Creating the row at Moment 1 would make the existing
  ternary correct, but `EventSetup` is the V1/V2 discriminator in thirteen `!event.setup` guards
  on the V1 dashboard, in `regenerate-plan`'s 404, and in this list. Moving when it is created
  moves all of that at once. Cheaper-looking and much wider.

Whichever is chosen, "which surface does this event belong to" should be one function with a
name, for the same reason `resolveSetupStage` is.
