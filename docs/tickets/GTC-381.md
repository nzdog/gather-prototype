### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-12 at `c586eb0`)

**35 - why an event still in Moment 1 opens on the old dashboard.**
- Gather tells a Moment-flow event from an old-style one by whether an `EventSetup` row exists
  (`src/lib/events/home-href.ts`, `eventHomeHref`: no row means `/plan/<id>`, the old dashboard).
  The page `src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx` branches on `event.setup` in dozens of places.
- `POST /api/events` (`src/app/api/events/route.ts`, the transaction around L202) creates the Event
  and the host's EventRole only. No `EventSetup` is made. The only writer of `EventSetup` is
  `POST /api/events/[id]/setup` (`route.ts` L259 and L280), which Moment 2's questions call. So
  after New Event and during all of Moment 1 the event has no row.
- New Event itself sends the host to `/plan/<id>/setup` (`src/app/plan/new/page.tsx` L175), so she
  starts right. Your Events (`src/app/plan/events/page.tsx`) routes by `eventHomeHref`, so coming
  back to an event that has only been through Moment 1 lands on the old dashboard. Anyone given the
  plain address, as Claude Code gave him, gets the same.
- The old dashboard for a no-row event shows the old flow (its checklist, "Generate Plan", and so
  on) - which GTC-149/GTC-357 hide only when a row exists.

**36 - the menu bar.**
- One component, `src/components/shared/Navigation.tsx`, rendered once from `src/app/layout.tsx`.
  Items today (L89 to L95): Home, Your Events, Past Events, New Event, Billing; then the email and
  Sign Out. Below 1280px it is the logo and a menu button (GTC-358). It hides itself only on
  `/h/`, `/c/` and `/p/` pages.
- The bar is covered, not absent, on the Moment screens that are `fixed inset-0 z-50 bg-white`:
  `SetupOpeningScreen.tsx` L28, `Moment2Opening.tsx` L40, `Moment1Summary.tsx` L202, and the setup
  page's own overlays (L947 loading, L971 her household's step, L1069 the Moment 1 form), plus
  `Moment2Step1Modal.tsx` L693 (Moment 2's questions). The plan view, Moment 3, the pre-flight and
  the board are ordinary pages and show it. GTC-367 gave these overlays a "<- Your events" link
  (`STRIP_WORDS.YOUR_EVENTS`) as their way out.
- Each overlay carries its own top row today (the way-out link and the event's name card), laid out
  for `max-w-5xl`. Revealing the bar means either lowering the overlays below it, or turning them
  into ordinary pages; the first is smaller.
- `/plan/templates` (Past Events) is the old templates page; per the 10 Oct talk-through it is hidden
  at launch (its "Use this again" fails for every real host, GTC-372).

**52 - the old dashboard as a host meets it** (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx`, reached from the
board's "Invites & people" and Your Events' "Invites & people"). On a Moment-flow event it still
says: the status chip "CONFIRMING" (L1562 to L1564); "View as Host" (L1586); "Save as Template" (L1657, from
CONFIRMING); the cards "It's away" (L1762), "Wrap up" with "Send thank-yous ->" (L1804 to L1823),
"Event Details" reading "<n> guests" with no number on a Moment-flow event (L1843) and the date in
the browser's own format (L1845, `toLocaleDateString()`: 12/25/2026 on his), "People" with
"0 coordinators, 5 participants" (L1860 to L1866), "Items & Quantities" (L1881), "Teams" (L1901),
"Plan Status" (L1921), "Invite Links" (L1955) and "Revision History" (its card at L1978, hidden for a row by the card's own gate and by `hiddenTabs` at L690). The
page itself branches on `event.setup` to hide the V1 pieces already.

**The two doors into Teams.**
- Pre-flight step 1: each loose item is an `<a href="/plan/<id>?expand=teams" target="_blank">` in
  `src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx` (L555), with a note underneath (L572 onward) saying
  it opens the Teams section in a new tab and "there is no link straight to a single item yet".
- The board's "and N more unassigned": `unassignedDoorHref` in `src/components/glance/strip.ts`
  (L395), used at `GlanceBoard.tsx` L517; same destination, same tab.
- `?expand=teams` is read by the old dashboard (L362 to L367, `validSectionIds`). Moment 3 ("Change
  who's on what") now has the item-in-the-middle arrival from [[GTC-380]]: `itemDoorHref` in
  `home-href.ts` goes to `/plan/<id>/setup?item=<itemId>`, honoured only on Moment 3.

### Cowork's first pass at the answers (PENDING the founder)

1. **Item 35 - the smallest honest fix.** Make `eventHomeHref` (and the old dashboard's own
   branching) agree that an event made through New Event is a Moment-flow event from the moment it
   exists. Options for the plan to weigh: (a) create the `EventSetup` row when the event is created
   (one extra row per new event, nothing migrated, existing events untouched; no schema change);
   (b) keep the row's absence and instead route `/plan/<id>` to `/setup` for a draft event with no
   teams and no items; (c) a new marker on Event (a migration; heavier). Cowork leans to (a), but the
   plan must read every place that tests `event.setup` (17 `.setup` reads in the dashboard page, and others in
   the setup page, the board page, Your Events, Moment 2's modal, `entry-stage.ts`, `check.ts` and
   `ask-preview.ts`; counted by grep on 12 Oct, to be re-read by the plan) and every suite that creates events by hand without a
   row.
2. **Item 36 - the bar.** Per his two rulings: Your Events and New Event stay; Home goes; Billing
   and Sign Out move under the email (a small dropdown on a computer, inside the existing menu on a
   phone); Past Events hidden. The plan must settle how the overlays make room (lower them under the
   bar, or become pages), what the bar does on the opening screen's fade-in, and whether "Home goes"
   also drops the logo link (the logo to `/` is a separate thing and stays).
3. **Item 52 - tidy, not move.** Cowork's list of what to tidy on a Moment-flow event, for him to
   rule: drop the status chip's raw "CONFIRMING"; drop the "guests" line that has no number; show
   the date as Event Details elsewhere writes it ("Sun 8 Nov 2026"); drop "View as Host" and "Save as
   Template" (the second fails for real hosts anyway, GTC-372); say "hosts and guests" not
   "coordinators, participants"; rename "Teams" and "Wrap up" in Gather's own words. Which of these
   he wants is a ruling.
4. **The two Teams doors.** Cowork's lean: point both at what the Moment flow already has -
   the pre-flight's loose items to Moment 3 at that item (`itemDoorHref`, same tab, with the "<-
   Back" path already there), and the board's "and N more unassigned" to Moment 3. That retires the
   "new tab" and the "find it under its team" note, and leaves `?expand=teams` serving only the old
   dashboard's own use. It changes words and pinned tests ([[GTC-366]]/[[GTC-373]] fences, and the
   pre-flight's note).
