Found (checked in the repo at `916baec`):
- Nothing sends a message to a host as a test today. The two guest senders that carry the host's
  voice, `sendAskEmail` and `sendChaseEmail` (`src/lib/email.ts`, both through `sendHostVoiced`),

  send will produce). Before the press the link in it is the stand-in `LINK_AT_PRESS`
  ("[link issued at the press]"); no token exists yet (tokens are issued at the transition and at
  the press, GTC-189). A test email cannot honestly carry a working guest link.
- The live switch (GTC-274) stops every send outside production: `getResendClient` throws
  `LiveSendsOffError` unless `GATHER_LIVE_SENDS` is exactly `on`, and "never the dev server, never
  a test suite" may turn it on. So the feature can never be seen working on Nigel's Mac, and a
  suite can prove it only behind `liveBehindTrap` with a stub that stops every request. The real
  proof (the email arriving) is on production after deploy, or one named live-run script on
  Nigel's word, to his own address.
- On the dev server the send would stop at the switch and report "not sent". What the host reads
  in that case is a new sentence.
- Existing walls that apply: the live switch, the send wall in the browser look (GTC-366), the
  dialog rule (GTC-368, which is a rule for the *look*: answer only "Leave site?" and log every
  dialog), Zone 9 (email opt-out and block, `EmailOptOut`), Zone 7 (text opt-out). Zone 4
  (Stripe), Zone 6 (security suite), Zones 1 to 3 are not touched by this item as scoped.
- Any route that sends is new unless it extends an existing one. A new route is a stop (ask
  Nigel first), and `security:routes` counts handlers (143 today, 2 UNPROVEN).
