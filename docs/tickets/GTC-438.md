# GTC-438 - The summary's contact Save never fails silently

> Filed by Claude Code on 31 Oct 2026. Found while planning
> [[GTC-433]]; the founder asked for its own ticket the same day. Not
> built.

## What happens today

On Moment 1's summary screen (src/components/plan/Moment1Summary.tsx),
someone listed without an email or phone can be opened, given one, and
saved with the row's "Save" button. `handleSaveContact` sends the whole
household as `PUT /api/events/[id]/households/[householdId]`, then:

- On a good reply it updates the list and closes the row.
- On any other reply (a 400 for a malformed email, a 500, a 404) it
  does nothing: no words, the row stays open as if still waiting, and
  what she typed is kept but nothing says it was not stored.
- On a network failure the `await fetch` throws inside try/finally with
  no catch: the button comes back, nothing is shown, and the browser
  gets an unhandled rejection.
- The row's email and phone boxes have no checks of their own, so a
  malformed email reaches the server and fails there, silently.

The same silent failure GTC-433 fixes for "Save changes", on a second
screen.

## The fix (proposed)

- `handleSaveContact` gains a catch, and treats a reply that is not OK
  as a failure: the row stays open with what she typed, and under its
  Save button, in the form's red line, "That didn't save — please try
  again." (the words the other Save buttons use). A new press clears
  them first.
- The row's two boxes are checked with the form's own `validateEmail`
  and `validatePhone` (exported since GTC-432) before the PUT; a
  failing box shows its words under it and nothing is sent.
- No server change. The PUT stays one transaction, so a failure stores
  nothing.

## Tests (proposed, in test:household-save)

- Source: the catch, the not-OK branch, and the two checks before the
  fetch.
- Chrome at 390 on a fixture with a household missing a contact: the
  PUT answered 500 in the page (the suite's stub), then a network
  failure: the words show, the row stays open with what was typed, 0
  unhandled rejections, nothing stored; then a real save stores it.
  "kid@test" typed: the email words show and no request is sent.
- Red before: on the current file the failure cases show no words and
  one unhandled rejection.

## Effort

Low: one file, one handler and two checks, about four browser checks.
