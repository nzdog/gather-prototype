### Where this comes from

Batch 4 of the seven in [[GTC-189]]'s Fourth ruling. Four items on two screens: the pre-flight
(32) and the board (9, 8 and 11). The item numbers are the walkthrough list's (the Cowork project
doc `claude/walkthrough-2026-10-03.md`). [[GTC-363]]'s, [[GTC-364]]'s and [[GTC-365]]'s changes on
these screens stay as built.

### The items (what the code does today: Cowork, read 2026-10-06 at `796c446`)

#### Item 32 — a way to the first unticked check

His words: *"Go to unchecked in preflight"*.

Today the pre-flight (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx`) has five steps, each with its
own box: *"Checked"*, except step 4's, which reads *"Settled"* (W9, ruled 2026-09-27). Send stays
greyed until all five are ticked and reads *"Send — finish the five checks first"*, without saying
which is left. On a phone the five steps run several screens long.

For the plan: a way from the greyed Send to the first step not yet ticked, how it says which step,
and where it takes her (scrolled to, and its box in reach); any words are W-lines. It never presses
or ticks anything itself. Before the press only: after it, the pre-flight's address shows *"Who I
chase"* ([[GTC-329]]).

#### Item 9 — a key to the board's colours

From his walk of 2026-10-03: a key to what the board's colours mean.

Today the board (`src/components/glance/GlanceBoard.tsx`, [[GTC-192]]) paints one strip per person:
red (needs her, and the only strip that opens something), amber (Gather is chasing them), green
(settled), and two greys: not chased (reminders off, marked "don't chase", or someone Gather invites
and never reminds) and out (*"— out"*). Nothing on the board says what the colours mean. Above the
grid, the summary reads *"2 need you. I’m looking after 23. 1 settled."*

For the plan: where the key sits and its words, in the board's own voice (W-lines), and how it stays
quiet. [[GTC-192]]'s Ruling 1: *"anything that would make the host lean in does not belong on this
screen"*. A change to the board's design gets a dated note in [[GTC-192]] and in
`docs/design/moment4-glance-reference.md`, as [[GTC-363]] did.

#### Item 8 — when answers are due, and the days to go behind a button

Ruling: *"When answers are due (“Answers by Fri 18 Dec”)"*; and in his words: *"Days to teh event
as a button the host can click to see if htey want"*.

Today the board shows no dates, on purpose. [[GTC-192]] refuses a countdown (Moment 4 §3, and Ruling
1; the mockup's "12 days to go" was dropped), and the person panel is never handed a date
(`readingPanelFor` in `src/components/glance/reading.ts`, the "date fence"). The board's first line
is *"[event name] · [N] households"*. A maybe's decide-by is worked out per item: the item's
needed-by (its drop-off time, else the event date) less an offset (the item's, else the event's,
else five days), in `src/lib/decide-by.ts`. Guests read it through `formatDecideByDay`, a weekday in
NZ time, with the day and month once it is more than a week away.

For the plan:

- what "Answers by" means for the whole event, since decide-by is worked out per item, and where it
  sits; its words and date format are W-lines;
- the button that shows the days to the event, hidden until she taps it, as his words say; its
  words and what it shows are W-lines;
- the board is a server component with no hooks (phase 2's property, asserted in
  `tests/glance-grid-test.tsx`). The button keeps that: for example a native disclosure, or an
  island beside the board as the replay and the live poll are;
- his ruling lifts [[GTC-192]]'s refusal for these two things only; dates for people stay fenced.
  Every assertion that pins the refusal is listed in the plan with what changes and why, quoting
  the ruling, and nothing else about the fence moves. Dated notes in [[GTC-192]] and the design
  reference.

#### Item 11 — print for the fridge, from the board

From his walk of 2026-10-03, of the old dashboard's printable list: *"It's good."* So the fix is a
way to it from the Moment flow.

Today the only print is on the old dashboard (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx`): the Items &
Quantities panel's *"Download PDF"* opens a window with a table per category (item, quantity,
assigned to, status) and calls the browser's print. Nothing in the Moment flow leads to it. The
board's doors sit at its foot: *"Change who I chase"*, *"Change who's on what"* and *"Invites &
people"* ([[GTC-357]]).

For the plan:

- where the way to it goes and its words (W-lines);
- whether it opens the old dashboard's panel, or prints straight from the board (the print moved
  into one shared module that the old dashboard uses too);
- ⚠ the print writes item, person and event names into its page without escaping them
  (`${item.name}` and the rest, written with `document.write`). If the plan reuses it, the names
  are escaped;
- no new API route: it reads from an existing one. A new route joins the security route list
  (Zone 6), which needs the founder's explicit approval; if the plan needs one, stop.

### Not in this ticket

Batches 5 to 7 ([[GTC-189]]'s Fourth ruling lists them). Otherwise the board's people, colours,
words and doors are unchanged, and so is the pre-flight's.

### Stops

- **Words:** every new or changed sentence a host reads is proposed in the plan as numbered lines
  (W1, W2, …) and built only once the founder has ruled on them.
- **Looking on screen** is allowed, behind the three safeguards in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`
  (*"Looking on screen — three safeguards"*). A scratch fixture of its own (example.com addresses,
  no phones). The board needs a sent event: its fixture is written directly in the database with
  nothing queued (no OutboundMessage or InviteEvent rows), never by pressing Send. After the look:
  no AI request in the dev log, `aiCallsUsed` 0, the fixture counted while it exists and removed by
  id, the totals as found, the server stopped. Never pressed: Send, *"Send it again"* or any door
  that sends, *"Generate plan →"*, *"↻ Regenerate this category"*, Moment 3's *"Move on →"*, New
  Event. The print's own dialog is never confirmed.
- **No test is weakened.** A test changes only where the founder's ruling changes what it pins
  (item 8), and every such change is listed in the plan first. **NOTHING SENDS FOR REAL.** **No
  migration expected**; if one is needed, stop. **Zones** as `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md` names
  them: none of these files is in one, and zones 7 and 9 (opt-out and email-block) are read-only; a
  new API route touches Zone 6 (see item 11); if the plan finds otherwise, stop.
- **Read the assertions first.** About thirty suites read the board or the pre-flight, among them
  `test:glance-grid`, `test:glance-read`, `test:glance-replay`, `test:board-door`,
  `test:after-the-press`, `test:preflight-withholding`, `test:board-not-chased` and
  `test:walkthrough-batch1`. A plan that changes what they drive reads every assertion in them that
  calls it.
