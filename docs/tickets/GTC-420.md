## What this ticket does, in plain English

A host who chose "invites only" (nobody brings anything) sees, on the
"Before you send" page, a shaded line saying so. Until now the only way
back to making a plan was a small grey link at the very top. Now the
shaded line has a clear button, "Make a plan after all →", which goes to
the same place as that small link: Moment 2's opening, where "Let’s do
this →" brings the plan back. The button is there only until she
presses Send: after that the invitations have said what they said.

## The founder's rulings (28 Oct 2026, in Cowork)

- On the pre-flight page, where an invites-only choice is shown (today
  the line about a plan that is put away), add a clear button "Make a
  plan after all" followed by the right-arrow character the app uses. It
  goes to Moment 2's opening, where "Let's do this" brings the plan back,
  the same route the small link "Back to What's the plan?" takes.
- BEFORE SEND ONLY. The earlier ruling (GTC-374): "any time before she
  presses Send. After that the invitations have said what they said, so
  it's fixed." So the button must not show once the invitations have
  gone. Use whatever the page already knows about whether Send was
  pressed; do not invent a new state. Prove in the suite that the button
  is absent after Send.
- Nothing else on the pre-flight changes in this ticket. Tweak 9 (step 5
  and Send) is a later ticket in Part B and must NOT be started now.

## The words, old and new

```
old  Invites only: nobody’s asked to bring anything, just whether they
     can come.
     Your plan is put away. Go back to “What’s the plan?” to bring it
     back.                                  (only when a plan was put away)
new  the same two lines, and under them a button:
     [Make a plan after all →]              (goes to /plan/<id>/setup?at=opening)
```

The arrow is the literal "→" the app's buttons use. The button is an
ordinary link styled as a button (sage, white words), like the small
link above it: a full page load, the same as the small link.

## The states, before and after

- Invites only, before Send, a plan put away: before, the line and "Your
  plan is put away ..."; after, the same and the button.
- Invites only, before Send, no plan ever made: before, the line; after,
  the line and the button ("Let’s do this →" there starts a plan).
- Invites only, after Send: before and after the same, the after-Send
  view; no line, no button.
- A planned event (not invites only), before or after Send: unchanged,
  no line, no button.
