### Context

Hinge spec §2: the press commits the release (no recall, no undo, at the mechanism level too —
ruled gap #1); the lock as a ledger (A2's mechanism, GTC-168); universal, silent, per-change
versioning; the two-sentence threshold script (*"You can still change anything — I'll just keep
the history." / "You'll start to see replies coming in. I'll track them and flag anything that
needs you."*); the atomic-feeling rollout — mechanically many messages over an hour with retries,
experientially one act, one sentence, one handover.

  apparently-atomic rollout.
