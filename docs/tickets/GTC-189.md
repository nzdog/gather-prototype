### Context

Hinge spec §2: the press commits the release (no recall, no undo, at the mechanism level too —
ruled gap #1); the lock as a ledger (A2's mechanism, GTC-168); universal, silent, per-change
versioning; the two-sentence threshold script (*"You can still change anything — I'll just keep
the history." / "You'll start to see replies coming in. I'll track them and flag anything that
needs you."*); the atomic-feeling rollout — mechanically many messages over an hour with retries,
experientially one act, one sentence, one handover.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- The press action itself: triggers A2's lock, anchors a send timestamp on the Event, dispatches
  H2-composed (GTC-187) messages to all confirmed recipients (per C1's toggles, GTC-172) in an
  apparently-atomic rollout.
- Surfaces the two-sentence threshold script at the moment of commitment.
- Refuses a roadmap/options door showing the machine's plan (cadences, escalation, tone-over-time)
  per the spec's explicit refusal — the compressed two-sentence script carries the feeling without
  the inventory.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`, especially SMS opt-out (Zone 7).
- The pre-flight (I1, upstream) and the minutes-after screen (I3, downstream) — this ticket is the
  press itself, the threshold moment.

### Dependencies

A2 (GTC-168), H2 (GTC-187), I1 (GTC-188).

### ⚠ Inherited from Epic A: mini-sends, and the affordance waiting on them

**This ticket owns mini-sends** (Hinge §2, gap #5), and two things are parked until it does.

`PersonEvent.sentAt` exists and is written ([[GTC-168]], [[GTC-196]]): the press stamps every
existing member at once, and anyone added afterwards gets their own send date.
`isMiniSend = personEvent.sentAt > event.sentAt` needs no extra field. What does **not** exist is the
machinery that actually *sends* to those people — the three-movement message on their own clock,
which is this ticket's.

**Auto-Assign is hidden on a sent event because of that gap** ([[GTC-201]], ruled 2026-08-03). It is
a **UI absence, not a server block** — the route allows and records it, because Moment 4 §7 forbids
hard-blocking the host. But post-send it creates N asks at once, and until mini-sends work nobody it
asks would ever be told. Asking someone silently is worse than not offering the button.

**When this ticket lands (with [[GTC-178]] / E1 for the cadence those clocks run on), delete the
`!isSent &&` condition in `src/components/plan/PeopleSection.tsx` and the tombstone comment above
it.** The comment names this ticket as its expiry. The ledger side is already done: the batch carries
one why as one changeSet.
