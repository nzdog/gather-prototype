## What and why

The public sample is live in production (4 Nov 2026). Two lines a
visitor sees promised that everything typed into a sample "is deleted
the next day". That is true of Gather's database, but plan-making
sends what a visitor types to Anthropic, which keeps it under its own
terms (GTC-447 launch register, A1). This ticket changes the words of
both lines, and nothing else.

- W58, the privacy line under "Create" on /demo/new
  (`PRIVACY_LINE` in src/app/demo/new/page.tsx).
- W59, the refusal shown when a visitor tries to delete a sample copy
  (`SAMPLE_COPY_DELETE_WORDS` in src/app/api/events/[id]/route.ts,
  sent in the 403's `message` and shown as a toast on the events
  page). Ruled into this ticket by the founder (Q1): two lines that
  disagree on what is deleted is worse than one late fix.

Backups (GTC-447 A2) are not in this ticket: the new words say Gather
deletes it, which is true of the live database and claims nothing
about backups. The founder checks Railway's backups.

## The words

Old, as approved in GTC-392 (Q16) and GTC-449:

- W58: "Only you can see your sample. It, and everything you type into
  it, is deleted the next day."
- W59: "A sample event can't be deleted, but there's no need: it, and
  everything you type into it, is deleted the next day. To start
  again, sign out and try a new sample event. You can still remove
  anyone you added."

New, the founder's words (4 Nov 2026), exact. W3 in
tests/demo-public-test.ts reads the W58 line below:

- W58 (privacy, /demo/new): "Only you can see your sample. Gather
  deletes it, and everything you type into it, the next day. Plans are
  made with an AI service."
- W59 (sample copy delete refusal): "A sample event can't be deleted,
  but there's no need: Gather deletes it, and everything you type into
  it, the next day. To start again, sign out and try a new sample
  event. You can still remove anyone you added."

Only W59's first sentence's middle changes; the rest is as it was. The
403, the reason SAMPLE_COPY, the other refusals and the events page are
unchanged.
