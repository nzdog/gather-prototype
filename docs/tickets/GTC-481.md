# GTC-481 - Multi-day: guests answer per session, and attendance and dishes stay consistent

Base: origin/main 6e84218 (25 Nov 2026, the merge of pull request #123,
GTC-485). The founder's multi-day rulings are in [[GTC-476]]; the foundation
(the "not at this session" table, the session routes and the `GATHER_SESSIONS`
switch) is [[GTC-478]]; Moment 1's sessions card, who comes to which session
and the date rule are [[GTC-479]]; Moments 2 and 3 by session are [[GTC-480]];
the deadline, the guest page's date, the board's tag and the invitation's range
are [[GTC-485]]. In the code a session is a `Day` row; the screens say
"session" (Q1). All dates and times are NZ time.

Everything here is behind `GATHER_SESSIONS`. With it off, nothing a host or
guest sees, receives or is nudged about changes.

## The founder's words and rulings that govern this ticket (from the brief)

- 22 Nov: "During multi day events, we invite Sure and Jason, She will come for
  all, he will come for one day but not the other two"
- Q4: who comes to which session ships together with sessions.
- Q3: one invitation covers all sessions.
- GTC-478 choice A: SessionAbsence stores who is NOT at a session; no rows means
  every session.
- GTC-485 decision 1: nothing new is sent when a dish's session moves after the
  send.
- Every session date is read through `src/lib/sessions/dates.ts`.

## What planning found (25 Nov 2026)

- No migration is needed: the "not at this session" table (`SessionAbsence`) is
  GTC-478's and is in both databases; GTC-479's host route writes it.
- A guest's "no" today is a No on a dish: the dish turns red on the board and
  the host finds someone else. The whole-event "Can't make it" shows only once
  every dish is No, or the guest holds none ("No worries — still coming?"). It
  stays as it is.
- The nudges nudge only about unanswered dishes (`nudge-eligibility.ts`, "any
  row of their OWN still PENDING"), and the maybe follow-up looks only at
  maybes. So a per-session decline is an ordinary No on each of the guest's own
  dishes on that session, plus the mark: no board, nudge or follow-up file
  changes, and Zones 7 and 9 are not touched.
- The guest route's reply (`GET /api/p/:token`) is pinned in about a dozen
  older checks (demo-board R3, R4, R6 and R8 against a server built at 5f64ce6;
  share-a-dish P1 to P7; sessions-guests-clock K13 and E2). It is not edited.
  The guest's sessions come from a new address of their own,
  `/api/p/:token/sessions`, asked only while the switch is on, through a new
  layout that hands the switch to the page as the setup layout does.
- The guest page is held by nine older checks (tweaks-e P1 and layer B, tweaks-i
  P1, P2 and layer B, demo-board G1 and W2, sessions-guests-clock K14 and E3);
  two of them compile the page against a fixed list of imports. So the page
  gains two pieces only (one import, one placement); the card, its state, its
  words and its one request are in a new component.
- GTC-480's session plan already fetches `GET /api/events/:id/sessions`, which
  returns the households and the marks, and keeps only the sessions. Keeping
  the rest gives Moment 3 its warning and the tabs their headcounts, with no
  new request and no route change.
- The why box asks by the ledger's own rule (`whyTrigger`), so a session's date
  moved after the send asks through one case in `src/lib/ledger.ts`. The two
  existing EDIT_DAY checks (ledger-why-scope's list, sessions-foundation L1)
  pass no field and still read "never asks".

## The plan (as approved, 25 Nov 2026)

The full plan, with every hunk laid out by prettier, is the plan of record given
to the founder; what follows is its substance.

**A. The guest page.** A card on the guest's own page, shown only while the
switch is on, the event has two or more sessions and the guest has not said
"Can't make it" to the whole event (never on the host's link preview): "You're
down for Christmas lunch and Boxing Day dinner.", then each session with its
date. A session they are down for has "Can't make it" (not on their last one);
one they can't make shows "Not coming" and "I can make it". "Can't make it" on
a session where they hold dishes not yet No asks first.

The new address `src/app/api/p/[token]/sessions/route.ts`, both handlers 404
and touching nothing while the switch is off:

- GET: the event's sessions in order, each with its words, whether this guest
  is marked off it, and B's reading.
- PUT `{ dayId, coming }`. Order of checks: the switch; the link (resolveToken,
  PARTICIPANT only, else 403); the guest's own place on that event (else 404);
  the input (400); the session on the event this link opens (400, nothing
  written); already so (200, nothing written); the last session (409).
- One transaction. Not coming: the mark; each of the guest's OWN dishes on that
  session not yet No answered No through `recordAssignmentAnswer` (the same
  write and history line a tap on its No makes); one EDIT_ATTENDANCE ledger
  line with the guest as the one who acted. Coming again: the mark removed and
  the same line; the dishes stay No. After the write, what a tap logs (one
  RESPONSE_SUBMITTED per dish turned No). Nothing is sent.
- The person is read from the link, never from the body; the carried-child
  resolver is never called, so GTC-191's reach is not widened.

New files: `src/app/p/[token]/layout.tsx` (the switch read on the server after
`connection()`, handed to the page, no element) and
`src/components/sessions/GuestSessions.tsx` (the card; with the switch off it
returns nothing before any request).

**B.** `sessionAttendance(whole, notAtSession)` in the new pure file
`src/lib/sessions/answers.ts`: a mark reads NO for that session; with no mark
the whole event's reading stands. `deriveAttendance` is not touched.

**C. Moment 3's warning.** Before a session's dish is given or moved to someone
marked as not at that session, and before a share's copy goes to them, a box
asks "Give anyway" or "Pick someone else". "Want me to suggest?" never suggests
such a person for that dish. It reads the marks the session plan already
fetched; no new request.

**D. A headcount per session** on Moments 2 and 3's tabs: Moment 1's count
(every named person, plus each household's kids without jobs) less the named
people marked as not at that session; kids without jobs drop out only where
every named person of their household is marked off it. After the date on the
tab; "Whole event" gets none.

**E. A session's date moved after the send** on Moment 1's card asks the
existing why box first, by T5 (the event's own date rule). A rename, a reorder,
or any change before the send never asks. Nothing is sent.
