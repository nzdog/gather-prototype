### Signal

**Expected**
[[GTC-189]] ruling A2 admits exactly one case where the host receives an ask: she is another
household's picked contact and a child of that household holds an item. That message exists and is
in Gather's voice alone.

**Actual — nothing composes it, and two lines inside the movement that survives are wrong for her.**

- **Nothing composes it.** Slice 2 composes nothing for her. Slice 3 shows her row on the pre-flight
  and deliberately leaves it uncomposed (`LINK_NONE` / `NONE_HOST_CARRIER` in
  `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview-compose.ts`, Evidence (slice 3) choice 2) precisely because
  decision 20 was open.
- **⚠ The speaker line names her to her own face.** `askSystemVoice` in
  `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts` opens every message *"Hi - Gather here, helping
  {hostFirstName} with this one."* Sent to the host that reads *"helping Kate with this one"* — to
  Kate.
- **⚠ The check-back promise is false for her.** The same function ends every non-itemless message
  *"I'll check back if I haven't heard from you."* Slice 1 answer 1 ruled the host as carrier is
  **not** chased, and `chooseChaseRoute` in `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` answers `NONE`
  with `HOST_AS_CARRIER`. A promise nothing will keep is what Hinge §5's *"continuity by
  declaration"* exists to prevent — the nudge is meant to arrive as a kept promise.

**Neither line is ruled.** Ruling AB rules which movements survive. What Gather says inside the
surviving movement, to the host, is this ticket's and needs the founder.

---

### What ruling AB does NOT cost — checked, and it is the cheap half

`movementsOf` in `src/lib/messages/compose.ts` drops an empty or whitespace-only movement rather
than rendering a gap. A `MovementContent` with `authorLine: null` and an empty `handover` therefore
produces exactly one movement through the **unchanged** `ASK_REGISTER`.

**So ruling AB needs no second register, no change to `MOVEMENT_ORDER`, and no change to
`compose.ts`.** It is a caller that does not exist. That is worth stating plainly because ruling W's
coordinator message ([[GTC-299]]) genuinely does need a new register, and the two should not be
confused: this one is a subset of the ask, not a different kind of message.

- `ASK_REGISTER`, `MOVEMENT_ORDER` and `composeMessage` in `src/lib/messages/compose.ts`.

### Unknowns

   not ruled.
2. **The check-back line.** It must go or change, because it is false for her. What replaces it —

3. **Whether she is asked her own attendance.** Ruling AA ([[GTC-307]]) asks a carrier with nothing
   of her own whether she can come. She is the host. Not ruled, and A2's warning applies.

   holds.** She is the person pressing the button. Sending her an email at the moment she presses
   send is a product question the ruling does not touch.
