## SCOPED 2026-10-02 — five founder rulings (in Cowork)

*Asked one at a time in Cowork on 2026-10-02, after [[GTC-290]] landed (`d6e7dc1`, hash in
`24371f0`). Questions and answers verbatim; each chosen option is recorded with the description the
founder saw.*

Context given, in summary: since [[GTC-288]] Gather keeps every text reply it can tie to a guest
and an event by TNZ's MessageID, and shows none, so the pre-flight tells the host a text reply
won't reach her. A guest who answers by text reads as silent: the reminders go on, and "gone quiet"
([[GTC-251]]) can turn them red.

**Q1.** *"GTC-350, question 1. When a guest replies to one of Gather's texts with anything other
than STOP or START, what should happen to their reminders?"* Options: *Stop; they're mine
(Recommended)*; *Keep reminding them*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Stop; they're mine
(Recommended)"** — *"Gather stops reminding them. Their card turns red and shows their words and
when they came, because only you can read what they said. To let Gather carry on, you hand them
back for 1 to 3 more reminders, as you can from "gone quiet". A reply like "please don't text me"
stops the texts too. A guest who has already answered stays green, with the reply shown."*

**Q2.** *"Question 2. How much of what they wrote should you see, and where? Today the board shows
no history and no dates: you ruled it a glance, not a message log. The plan page's guest details
already list each message Gather sent them."* Options: *Latest on board, all on plan
(Recommended)*; *Every reply on the board*; *Latest reply only*. The founder chose, verbatim:
**"Every reply on the board"** — *"Tap their red card and you see every reply they've sent, newest
first, with when each came. Everything is in one place, but the board gains a message history with
dates, which your earlier rulings kept off it."* Not the recommendation, which read: *"Tap their
red card on the board and you see their latest reply, in their words. The plan page's guest
details list every reply with when it came, beside the messages Gather sent them. The board stays
a glance, and the full record is on the plan page."*

**Q3.** *"Question 3. Gather texts from a number TNZ shares with other businesses. Rarely, a reply
meant for another business, like "Y" to a dentist's reminder, is handed to Gather because Gather
texted that guest last. It would show on your board, and by question 1 it would stop their
reminders. It works the other way too: a reply meant for you can go to the other business. What
should GTC-350 do about this?"* Options: *Show it anyway (Recommended)*; *Wait for the dedicated
number*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Show it anyway (Recommended)"** — *"It's rare, and you'll
usually recognise a stray message. You accepted the same risk for STOP when you put the dedicated
number on hold. If a stray reply stops a guest's reminders, you hand them back to Gather."*

**Q4.** *"Question 4. Before the press, the pre-flight tells you where replies go. It reads
"Replies to an email come to [your email]. A text reply won't reach you — I have no way to pass it
on." Which new second sentence should it have?"* Options: *Usually; reminders stop
(Recommended)*; *No "usually"*; *Shorter*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Usually; reminders stop
(Recommended)"** — *""A text reply usually comes to your board, and I stop reminding whoever sent
it." It says "usually" because a few replies go to another business on TNZ's shared number. That
keeps the sentence true, which is why you chose the current wording."* The preview he selected:
*"Replies to an email come to you@example.com. A text reply usually comes to your board, and I
stop reminding whoever sent it."*

**Q5.** *"Question 5, the last. The privacy page says "If you reply to one of our texts, we keep
your reply with the event it was about, and delete it with that event's data." Now the host will
see the reply, so the page should say so. Which wording?"* Options: *"who invited you"
(Recommended)*; *"the event's host"*. The founder chose, verbatim: **""who invited you"
(Recommended)"** — *""If you reply to one of our texts, we show your reply to the person who
invited you, keep it with the event it was about, and delete it with that event's data." It
matches "whoever invited you" in the opt-out line just above it."*

### What the rulings settle

- **Unknowns 1 and 2:** Q1 and Q2. A reply ends Gather's chase of that guest and makes them the
  host's, red; the host can hand them back. Every reply shows on the board, newest first, in the
  guest's words, each with when it came.
- **Unknown 3:** Q4, the pre-flight's new sentence, verbatim.
- **Unknown 5:** Q5, the privacy page's new bullet, verbatim. Keeping a reply with its event and
  deleting it with that event's data were ruled at [[GTC-288]]; deleting event data automatically
  is [[GTC-352]], not this ticket.
- **Unknown 7:** Q3. A reply TNZ's shared number hands Gather is shown like any other.
- **Unknowns 4 and 6** were not asked. The plan proposes them (notes 7 and 8 below).
- **Time:** the 1 Oct ruling lets this ticket wait if time runs short before 25 Oct. Scoped on
  2 Oct, with the rest of the chain done, it does not wait.

### For the plan (Cowork's notes, not rulings)

1. **Which replies count.** Only `TextReply` rows. [[GTC-288]] keeps a reply only when TNZ's
   MessageID ties it to one of Gather's texts and it came from the number that text went to; STOP
   and START are never kept as replies. Nothing is guessed. A reply belongs to the person the text
   went to, for that text's event.
2. **What "stops reminding them" stops.** Every reminder, hand-back reminders included. Whether a
   reply also stops the maybe's "please decide" follow-up and the thank-you is for the founder at
   the plan: propose a default and ask. A reply is not an opt-out: Zone 7 is not written, and STOP
   stays the only way to stop every text. Q1's "stops the texts too" was said of the reminders.
3. **Which chase a reply ends.** The one running when it arrived. A hand-back after it starts a new
   chase, which only a later reply ends. A reply from a guest who has answered ends nothing.
4. **The board.** A new red reason for a guest who replied, placed among the other red reasons (it
   must beat "gone quiet", which is false of somebody who replied), with why-line words the founder
   rules at the plan: propose them.
5. **Q1's "with the reply shown" for a guest who has answered.** Today only a red strip opens
   (`src/components/glance/PersonSurface.tsx`). Propose how an answered guest's replies show on the
   board, for the founder's ruling.
6. **Ruling 1's fence, relaxed by Q2, for replies only.** `PersonSurface.tsx` says "No history, no
   message log, no counts of anything", and `tests/glance-fence.ts` keeps behaviour and dated facts
   off the board. Q2 puts every reply, with when it came, on the board. Show the exact change to the
   fence and its tests for approval, and keep everything else out: no opens, views or delivery
   instants, and no phone number (the board carries no contact detail).
7. **Unknown 4, the replay ([[GTC-335]]).** GTC-335's ruling replays once anything that happens to a
   guest's messages while the host is away, at the time Gather recorded it. A reply that turns a
   card red reads as covered, replaying at `TextReply.receivedAt`. Confirm, or ask.
8. **Unknown 6, a child's ask.** A child is never texted, so a reply comes from the grown-up whose
   text carried the ask, and is kept against them. Default: it shows on that grown-up's card.
   Whether it also ends the chase for the children whose asks that text carried is for the plan to
   propose, under the carrier rulings ([[GTC-336]], ruling S).
9. **The hand-back ([[GTC-251]] slice 251c)** refuses anyone not "gone quiet" (`NOT_GONE_QUIET`).
   Q1 extends it to a guest who replied, with the same 1 to 3 reminders and three-day spacing.
10. **The words.** `TEXT_REPLY` in `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview-compose.ts` becomes Q4's
    sentence, and the "DO NOT ADD YET" note above it is replaced. The privacy page's bullet becomes
    Q5's, and its "Last updated" date moves. Tests pin both: list the moved assertions.
11. **A stray reply (Q3)** is treated like any other. Nothing tries to detect one.
12. **No migration is expected:** `TextReply` already holds the event, the person, the words and
    `receivedAt`. If the plan needs one, say why.
13. **The RED list** names every check that can only pass once the work is committed:
    carried-child-door F, and if `prisma/` changes, board-not-chased G and the zone checks in
    gone-quiet and decide-by-email. [[GTC-290]]'s plan missed three of them.
