### Signal

Today a text reply that isn't STOP reaches no one, and the pre-flight tells the host so: *"A text
reply won't reach you — I have no way to pass it on."* (`TEXT_REPLY` in
`src/lib/preflight/ask-preview-compose.ts`, ruled at [[GTC-189]] slice 3). An email reply already
reaches her: the same line begins *"Replies to an email come to <User.email>."*

[[GTC-288]] keeps every reply it can match to one of Gather's texts by TNZ's `MessageID`, from its
one database change, and shows none. This ticket shows them to the host on the guest's card, and
rewrites `TEXT_REPLY`.

### Unknowns

1. What the card shows: the words, when they came, and which of Gather's texts they answered;
   the latest reply or every one.
2. Whether a text reply changes how Gather chases the guest. Today a guest who answers by text
   reads as silent, so the reminders go on and "gone quiet" ([[GTC-251]]) can turn them red. The
   founder's.
3. The pre-flight's new wording in place of `TEXT_REPLY`. The founder's words.
4. Whether a reply that arrives while the host is away replays once on the board ([[GTC-335]]).
5. How long a guest's words are kept, and what the privacy page says about them
   (`src/app/privacy/page.tsx`; [[GTC-288]]'s plan proposes its first changes).
6. A reply to a message that carried a child's ask: whose card it shows on.
7. Until the dedicated shortcode is live (about the end of October), TNZ attribute a reply on the
   shared shortcode to whichever customer texted that number last (C1). A reply meant for another
   business, sent after Gather's text, would reach Gather and could show on a host's board.
