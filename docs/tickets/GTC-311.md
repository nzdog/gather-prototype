**Filed on the founder's instruction**, from [[GTC-189]] *Founder ruling AG (Nigel, 2026-09-15) —
supersedes ruling Y*:

> THE RULING: the chase channel is a default I can override, per person, on the pre-flight.
>
> - Default: chase by text. Someone with no usable phone gets no chase and comes to my list —
>   wide, as ruled Y.
> - But the pre-flight shows me each of those people, and I can tick "chase by email" on any of
>   them. Ticked, they are chased by email at day four and seven like anyone else.
> - Unticked, they come to my list — and now because I looked at them and decided, not because a
>   rule swept them there.
>
> WHAT THAT FIXES: nobody is red who has not failed. A person I have chosen to chase is amber,
> because he genuinely is being chased. A person I have chosen to handle is mine, because I chose
> it.

Tree read at `5fe9e22` on `feat/moment-one-redesign`. gather_dev measured read-only 2026-09-15.

### Signal

**Expected**
At the pre-flight the host sees everyone the chase cannot text, and decides per person whether
Gather chases them by email or hands them to her. That decision survives to day four.

**Actual**
None of it exists. There is no control, nothing to store the answer in, and the chase cannot read
one.

- **No storage.** `PersonEvent` in `prisma/schema.prisma` carries `nudgeMark`, `sentAt`,
  `firstNudgeSentAt` and `secondNudgeSentAt`, and nothing that says which channel the chase should
  take for this person.
- **No control.** `GET` in `src/app/api/events/[id]/pre-flight/message/route.ts` assembles and
  answers; its `PATCH` writes one value, `Event.askAuthorLine`, last-write-wins. `MessageStep` in
  `src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx` renders a list to read.
- **No reader.** `findNudgeCandidates` in `src/lib/sms/nudge-eligibility.ts` filters
  `person: { phoneNumber: { not: null } }` in SQL, and `sendNudge` in `src/lib/sms/nudge-sender.ts`
  calls `sendSms` alone. The chase has no email leg at all.
- **The preview does not even know who the population is.** `readAskPreview` in
  `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview.ts` routes every membership through `chooseAskRoute`;
  `chooseChaseRoute` in `src/lib/eligibility/channel-chooser.ts` has **no caller anywhere in
  `src/`**, verified at `5fe9e22`.

### ⚠ Read decision 25 before scoping this

[[GTC-189]] decision 25 asks whether the default this ticket overrides is the right one, and it is
open. Measured read-only against gather_dev on 2026-09-15, reusing the real chooser:

- **253 of 266 recipients would be tickable — 95.1%.** Seven have a usable phone.
- **333 `Person` rows; 12 carry a `phoneNumber`; 9 of those are usable NZ numbers.**
- **On the ten paid events, 52 adult guests carry one usable phone between them.**
- **35 `Person` rows hold a valid `+64…` number in the legacy `Person.phone` column with
  `phoneNumber` NULL, and no row has both** — ruling C's two-columns finding, counted. Every sender
  reads `phoneNumber`, so all 35 are invisible to the chase and all 35 sit in the tick population
  today. Reading that column would leave roughly 82%.

gather_dev is fixtures and the magnitude is an artifact of the seeds. **The shape is not:** in
`Moment1InputForm` both Email and Phone are `SkipForNowField`, only the name is required, and the
ask is email-first — so a host who gives addresses and no numbers has a product that works until
this screen.

**If decision 25 changes the default to an event-level switch, the per-person column below is still
needed for the exceptions, but the screen is a different screen.** Do not build the forty-row
version until 25 is ruled.

### Scope

**Fix here:**
- **Storage.** One nullable column on `PersonEvent`. See *Storage*, below.
- **The control.** The pre-flight shows every person the chase cannot text, with a per-person tick,
  and persists it.
- **The reader.** The chase honours the tick: ticked, an email leg at day four and day seven;
  unticked, nothing, and the person is on the host's list.

**Do not touch:**
- All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. **Zone 7 is live and unresolved here** —
  whether an opted-out person can be ticked at all is [[GTC-189]] decision 24, and until it is ruled
  this ticket may not offer the tick on an opted-out row.
- **`nudgeMark` and `resolveNudgeOffsetDays`.** The don't-chase mark is a different axis — how hard,
  not on which channel — and must not be folded into this one. Two axes in one enum is the shape
  ruling C measured going wrong.
- **The ask.** Ruling AG governs the chase only. `chooseAskRoute` is unchanged and an emailed adult
  is still emailed.
- **[[GTC-192]] Ruling 14's grey.** A ticked or unticked person who is also marked don't-chase stays
  grey, by ruling T's ground.

---

### Storage — the shape, and which migration

**A nullable column on `PersonEvent`.** The grain is exactly right: one row per (person, event),
`@@unique([personId, eventId])`. The precedent is its neighbour. `PersonEvent.nudgeMark`'s own
docstring makes the argument this ticket would otherwise have to make again:

> ON PersonEvent, NOT Person, and that is the whole point. The mark is a per-EVENT hosting
> judgement: Kate may go gentle on her mother for the wake and not for the birthday. Putting it on
> Person would reproduce exactly the global-for-a-per-event leak that `Person.inviteAnchorAt` and
> `Person.nudge24hSentAt`/`nudge48hSentAt` above are both retained-but-unwritten monuments to.

**Nullable, not a boolean with a default**, for the same reason `nudgeMark` is: *"NULL means NO
MARK — no opinion — and it is not a stored 'normal'."* Three states are needed because decision 23
exists — she ticked, she looked and did not tick, and **she was never asked** (a person added after
the press). A two-valued column cannot tell the third from the second, and the third is exactly
where the behaviour is unruled.

**It is NOT part of slice 4's outbound record.** That row describes a message that was sent — event,
membership, kind, channel, provider, provider message id, accepted at, rejected at, why. This is a
standing host decision that exists before anything is sent, one per membership rather than one per
message, with a different lifetime. Putting it there would make an instruction into a property of a
message.

**But it should ride in slice 4's migration.** Slice 4 is *"Schema, alone"*, already scheduled and
already required to be rehearsed against a production copy per the Deploy caveat in
`GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. A second migration for one nullable column buys nothing and costs a
second rehearsal — the same reasoning ruling W recorded for [[GTC-299]]'s third `kind`, *"cheaper to
meet before that migration than after."* **Executor's recommendation, not a founder ruling.**

### What this does to slice 3, which is built and committed

The change is one of kind, not of size: **the pre-flight stops being a screen she reads and becomes
one she decides on.**

- **A second write path, at a different grain.** The route's `PATCH` writes one event-level value,
  last-write-wins — the wrong contract for forty independent per-person switches.
- **It needs `chooseChaseRoute`**, which nothing calls. Shared with [[GTC-305]].
- **`HostListLine` changes species.** It is a line with one why from `hostListReason` in
  `src/lib/preflight/ask-preview-compose.ts`. Under AG the list holds two kinds of person — cannot
  be asked at all, and can be asked but not chased — and some rows carry a control. Slice 3's line
  under the heading, *"Gather will not message these people"*, is false of the second kind.
- **The words need a ruling.** Every word on that screen was founder-ruled at its slice, and
  `ADULT_WHY` / `CHILD_WHY` keep six deliberately-unreachable entries so no case renders blank. AG
  needs new ones and they are not ruled.
- **Step 5's five checks may mean something different.** `allChecked` gates Send on five *"I have
  looked"* acknowledgements; a step she must decide on is not a step she confirms she read.
