## What this ticket does, in plain English

The bar at the top already shows "Your Events" and "New Event" (on a
computer in the bar itself, on a phone at the top of the menu; GTC-381).

so a signed-in host saw it twice, in two different capitals. This ticket
takes the account-menu copy out. Nothing else changes.
