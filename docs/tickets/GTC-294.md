### Founder ruling (Nigel, 2026-09-13)

Option (a) of the two put to the founder in [[GTC-189]]'s audit:

> A coordinator who owns an item is a guest with an item and a job, and the job should not cost

So a coordinator holds a PARTICIPANT token **as well as** their COORDINATOR one.

---

### Signal

**Expected**
An adult who owns an item can be sent a working ask link, whatever else they do on the event.

**Actual**
A coordinator has no `/p/` link to send. `ensureEventTokens` in `src/lib/tokens.ts` issues
PARTICIPANT tokens in its step 4 only where `pe.role === 'PARTICIPANT'` **and** the person is
not in `coordinatorIds`, which it builds from every team's `coordinatorId` and every
`role: 'COORDINATOR'` membership. The file says so in its own words: *"Coordinators do NOT get
PARTICIPANT tokens."* A coordinator holds only `/c/{token}`, a different page.

**Why it matters now.** [[GTC-189]]'s recipient and channel model (founder ruling, 2026-09-13)
messages every adult who owns an item directly, with a link to the guest tap page. For a
coordinator who owns an item, that link cannot be built. `GET` in
`src/app/api/events/[id]/pre-flight/message/route.ts` already shows this: it lists coordinators
and their link stays the stand-in string forever, because it reads only PARTICIPANT tokens.

**Reachable, not hypothetical.** `PeopleSection`, mounted on the plan page, offers the
Coordinator role and `AssignCoordinatorsModal`. In gather_dev (as of 2026-09-13), 7 coordinator
memberships hold items, all on "Henderson Family Christmas 2025".

---

### Why after [[GTC-262]] — the ordering is load-bearing

[[GTC-262]] is open and High: `GET /api/gather/[eventId]/directory` and
`POST /api/join/[token]/claim` are unauthenticated and hand out whatever token a person holds,
preferring PARTICIPANT and falling back to COORDINATOR.

Issuing coordinators a PARTICIPANT token **changes what those two surfaces leak** — they would
start returning the participant link rather than the coordinator one — which moves GTC-262's
blast radius while GTC-262 is still open. Closing GTC-262 first means this ticket lands against
a surface whose behaviour has already been decided.

### What issuing the token changes — check each, do not assume

- **The chase finders open to coordinators.** `findNudgeCandidates` in
  `src/lib/sms/nudge-eligibility.ts` and `findDecideByFollowupCandidates` in
  `src/lib/sms/decide-by-eligibility.ts` both gate on a PARTICIPANT token for this event. A
  coordinator becomes a live auto-nudge and decide-by recipient. **That is correct under the
  ruling** — they own an item and are chased like any other adult — but it is a behaviour change
  to [[GTC-178]]'s and [[GTC-175]]'s shipped machinery and must be asserted in a test, not
  inferred from this sentence.
- **Zone 1, the role-scoped session cookies.** GTC-001's fix (no ticket file in `docs/tickets/`) separates host and participant
  sessions. A person who can open both `/p/` and `/c/` will hold two scoped sessions. Read the
  cookie scoping and confirm they do not collide. Do not change it here.
- **The shared-link claim list.** `src/app/join/[token]/page.tsx` lists
  `people where role: 'PARTICIPANT'`, so a coordinator is still not a claimable name. Whether
  they should be is not decided here.
- **No constraint change.** `AccessToken`'s `@@unique([eventId, personId, scope, teamId])` already
  admits both rows: the PARTICIPANT token carries `teamId: null`, the COORDINATOR one carries the
  team.
  ⚠ **CORRECTED 2026-09-18 — RIGHT CONCLUSION, WRONG REASON, AND THE REASON MATTERS.** The two
  rows are admitted because **`scope` differs**; `teamId` is not what distinguishes them. What
  `teamId` actually does here is the opposite of reassuring: the index is a plain one
  (`indnullsnotdistinct = f`, queried against PostgreSQL 16.14), so Postgres treats its NULLs as
  **distinct** — and it therefore does **NOT** prevent two PARTICIPANT rows for one person on one
  event. `skipDuplicates: true` on the `createMany` buys nothing there. The only guard against a
  duplicate ask is the in-memory `tokenExists` check, which is why step 4b iterates
  `personEvents` (unique on `personId, eventId`) rather than looping a set of people, and why
  idempotency is asserted rather than assumed. Measured live in the new suite's layer 0.
- **The two existing sweeps stay exactly as they are.** The COORDINATOR prune at the top of
  `ensureEventTokens`, and the `role: 'HOST'` PARTICIPANT revocation [[GTC-256]] phase 3 added
  under narrow founder approval. Neither is widened by this ticket.

### ⚠ The key — `pe.role`, never `coordinatorIds`, and the reason is a measurement

`coordinatorIds` is built from `Team.coordinatorId` unioned with COORDINATOR memberships
and carries **no role filter**, so it contains hosts. Measured in `gather_dev` before any
code was written:

```
Henderson Family Christmas 2025 (CONFIRMING)
  team "Mains" -> coordinatorId = Sarah Henderson
  Sarah's PersonEvent.role = HOST, and she IS Event.hostId
  she holds HOST + COORDINATOR, no PARTICIPANT
```

Keyed on `coordinatorIds` this ticket mints **her** a PARTICIPANT token, which
[[GTC-256]] Ruling 5 forbids — and nothing downstream would have caught it, because
`findNudgeCandidates` and `findDecideByFollowupCandidates` gate on the **token** and never
read `role`. Withholding it is the entirety of Ruling 8's mechanism.

⚠ **AND THE HOST SWEEP WOULD NOT HAVE SAVED IT, because of the order.** GTC-256's
revocation runs *before* `existingTokens` is read, which is before the creation block. A
token minted in the same call survives that call; the next call revokes it and the creation
block mints a fresh one. Mint, revoke, re-mint, on every call, with the host a live
auto-nudge and decide-by recipient in every window between — a defect that never fails.

⚠ **She is a live shape, not a seed artifact.** Two routes write a host as a team's
coordinator:

- `src/app/api/templates/[id]/clone/route.ts` sets `coordinatorId: hostId` on **every**
  team it creates, then writes her membership as `role: 'HOST'`.
- the `CREATE_TEAM` action in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/conflicts/[conflictId]/execute-resolution/route.ts` does the
  same for one team — reachable from `ResolveWithAIModal`.

Sized honestly: **one** such team in `gather_dev` today (`source: MANUAL`). No
`source: TEMPLATE` teams exist and no frontend call site for the clone route was found, so
that path is live code that has not run here; the conflict path has a call site. The
generator is clean — `resolveGeneratedTeamCoordinatorId()` returns `null`, checked.

Founder ruling on the shape, 2026-09-18: *"KEY ON THE ROLE, never on coordinatorIds. Your
measurement is the ruling... Record Sarah Henderson as the worked case and build the
fixture the way the clone route builds one — that is the assertion I most want in the
suite."* Done: the new suite's fixture makes the host `Event.hostId`, `role: 'HOST'` and a
