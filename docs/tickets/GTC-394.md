## The rollback structure

- Built on a NEW branch, `feat/demo-tweaks`, made from `463be1f` (the
  tip of `feat/demo-sandbox`, GTC-392 part B with its backfill).
- A local tag, `demo-ab-good`, names `463be1f`: the last version the
  founder is happy with. Not pushed.
- `feat/demo-sandbox` is never committed to, merged into, moved or
  force-pushed. No `git fetch --prune`; main, legacy-master and the
  remote-tracking names are not touched.
- One commit for this ticket first, then one commit per tweak in the
  order below (tweak 1 is part (a) only in this run), each made when
  that tweak's own checks are green. Staged by name, never the two
  untracked scripts. A last commit updates this ticket. No push, no tag
  push, no merge, no pull request: the founder pushes himself.

  or reset to `demo-ab-good`) or any one tweak (`git revert <hash>`).
  The report lists, for every commit, the files it touched and which
  later commits touch the same files.

## The eight tweaks, as scoped (founder's first prompt, 19 Oct 2026)

1. THE HOST'S CARD STANDS OUT (real events and samples). One shared
   definition used everywhere the host's own household or person card
   is drawn: a soft tint of the accent colour, a solid accent border,
   and the existing "You" pill made stronger (filled accent, white
   text). The word stays "You". Layout only.
2. THE TRANSITION PAGES, MUCH SIMPLER (real events and samples).
   Moment 1's (`SetupOpeningScreen`) and Moment 2's (`Moment2Opening`)
   full-screen pages become only the moment's name as a big heading,
   the two paragraphs ("What this does", "When it's done", unchanged)
   and the one button ("Ready to start herding", "Let's do this").
   Going from these two pages only: the four-step strip, the
   herd-of-cats line, and on Moment 2 the two quieter choices. Staying,
   as navigation: "Your events", the event's details, Moment 2's "Back
   to the people". "Write the list myself" and "Skip the plan: invites
   only" MOVE into Moment 2's questions (`Moment2Step1Modal`): same
   words, lines, handlers, routes, states and conditions.
3. THE FIRST PAGE: TWO BUTTONS, AND SARAH ON THE NEW BOARD.
   `src/app/demo/page.tsx` becomes the heading "Gather" and two
   buttons, "Sarah demo" and "Try it yourself". Everything else on the
   page goes (every section, the views' links, the Reset button). The
   routes behind them are NOT touched. "Try it yourself" does what the
   development-only button does today and stays development-only until
   part D (Q5). "Sarah demo" does what "Open Planning Dashboard" does
   today but goes to `/plan/{eventId}/glance`. The session route is not
   edited.
4. "ADD A CROWD" MUCH MORE OBVIOUS (samples only). A filled accent
   button, full width, larger text, in a soft tinted box with its line,
   at the top of the household list. Words and rules unchanged.
5. "FILL IN TYPICAL ANSWERS": OBVIOUS, OPENS, RANDOM, A NOTE (samples
   only). The same treatment, at the top of Moment 2's questions. It
   opens every section it filled (and "Show more" if needed). Random
   styles and dishes from each question's own choices, a random one to
   three dietary needs, one of three sentences for each job, and about
   one section in three gets a short note (never more than two, never
   in a section she answered). Takes a random source (Math.random by
   default). Fills only what is empty; Casual BBQ with no kind; never
   presses Generate; never touches the AI count; nothing new on the
   server.
6. "WHAT YOU'VE PICKED" AS A SPREADSHEET (real events and samples). A
   table with the print's look (`src/lib/print/item-list.ts`): the
   section, what is picked, what was typed. `picksSummary()` and its
   output kept exactly; a sibling function returns the rows from the
   same helpers. Works at phone width.
7. STEP 4: THE MESSAGE FIRST, SIMPLE AND THE SAME EVERY TIME (real
   events and samples; the screen only). The message first, as one
   readable email (the subject, then the text), then the controls in
   one fixed order: Your line, Read it as, the nudge choice, the people
   left to her. The voice labels and the opt-out line stay, quieter.
   What guests receive is NOT changed: `composeAsk`, `composePreview`,
   the message builder, the drain and every sender untouched. Step 4's
   stores, fingerprint and tick unchanged.
8. THE PRETEND LIST SAYS "INVITE ONLY", AND THE LINK GOES (samples
   only). "Name · by email — Invite only" where it said "— nothing";
   the "Open step 4" link and its wiring go; "Each would be the message
   in step 4, word for word." stays.

## Found before the build (19 Oct 2026, read only)

- Tailwind's `content` globs cover `src/pages`, `src/components` and
  `src/app` only, so a class written only under `src/lib` is never
  built. The shared host-card definition therefore lives under
  `src/components`.
- An opacity modifier on a CSS-variable colour builds nothing here:
  `bg-accent/10`, `border-accent/40` and `hover:bg-accent/5` produce no
  CSS (checked with the Tailwind CLI against the repo's config). So
  today's "You" pill tint and the pale border on "Add a crowd" and
  "Fill in typical answers" never rendered. The tints in this ticket
  use the `sage` scale (`bg-sage-50`, `bg-sage-100`), which does build.
- Sarah's board, read in process against `gather_demo` with nothing
  written (`readEventGlance` and `GlanceBoard` rendered): 0 households,
  43 people in "Not in a household", summary 5 need you / 8 with Gather
  / 30 settled. Its links: "Change who's on what" and "and 11 more
  unassigned" to `/plan/{id}/setup`; "Invites & people" and "Change
  these details" to `/plan/{id}` (the old dashboard).
