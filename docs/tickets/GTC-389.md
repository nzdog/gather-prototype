# GTC-389 - A job given to a child after the press reaches nobody

> Stub, filed 16 Oct 2026 on Nigel's ruling at GTC-387 Q15: "Keep the
> button, file the gap (Recommended)". Not scoped, no severity, not a
> precondition of GTC-387 or anything else.

## The gap (found planning GTC-387, read at `1444687`)

- GTC-387 gives the board's "kid without a job" pop-up a button, "Give them
  a job", to Moment 3. Before the press that is fine: the job travels in the
  grown-up's invitation when Send is pressed.
- After the press, nobody is told. A child is never messaged; their job is
  carried in the grown-up's message. The grown-up already has an invitation
  row, and the late-arrival sweep (`enrolMiniSends`, `src/lib/press/
  dispatch.ts`) enrols only people with no invitation row at all. So the job
  appears on the board and in Moment 3, and the grown-up hears nothing.
- The same is true of any job given to a child after the press, from any
  screen; the pop-up only makes it easier to reach.

## Not decided here

Whether the grown-up should be told, how (a second message, the next
reminder, a line on the board), and by which channel. Any answer is a send
change and needs its own plan.
