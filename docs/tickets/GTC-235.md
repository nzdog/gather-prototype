# GTC-235 — A generated V2 plan cannot be reopened — CLOSED

**Status:** Complete | **Branch:** feat/moment-one-redesign
**Shipped in one commit with [[GTC-237]]** — see "Why these are one change".

*Surfaced by [[GTC-233]] (the V2 route extraction), 2026-08-22. Pre-existing — GTC-233 moved
this code without changing the behaviour, and deliberately did not fix it.*

## Signal

**Expected:** a host who generated a plan can come back to it. Kate opens her event tomorrow
and sees the plan she approved.

**Actual:** the plan view opened only as the tail of an in-session generation. Navigating to
`/plan/[eventId]/setup` for an event whose plan already existed showed `SetupOpeningScreen`
— "Ready to start herding" — and then Moment 1. There was no path back to the plan short of
generating again, which costs an AI call and, before [[GTC-237]], ate her edits.

**Smallest failing case:** generate a plan, reload the page.

## Root cause

`setShowMoment2PlanView(true)` had two call sites in `EventSetupPage`
(`src/app/plan/[eventId]/setup/page.tsx`), and **both were callbacks of
`Moment2Step2Skeleton`** — `onReady` and the fallback `onApprove`. Nothing set the flag on
mount. The flag was pure session state with no hydration, and `showSetup` initialised `true`
unconditionally.

**Nothing new had to be stored.** The flow's position was already derivable from data that
exists: `EventSetup` is written by Moment 2 Step 1, `Household` rows by Moment 1, and item
provenance by generation and by the item PATCH. Two of the three inputs were already on the
wire in the two requests the page made on mount — `EVENT_WIRE_SELECT` names `setup` so the
events list can route on it, and `GET /api/events/[id]/items` selects no subset, so every
`Item` scalar including `source` comes back. Nothing asked.

## The fix

`resolveSetupStage` in `src/lib/setup/entry-stage.ts` — one function with a name, per this
ticket's own instruction, because every future Moment reads it:

- no `EventSetup`, no households → `opening`
- no `EventSetup`, households exist → `moment1`
- `EventSetup`, no generated items → `moment2-step1`
- `EventSetup` and generated items → `plan`

**"Already generated" means ITEMS, not teams.** Teams are the cheap test and the wrong one:
V1 events have teams and so does a hand-built plan. `hasGeneratedPlan` reads `source` and
counts `HOST_EDITED` as generated output, because the item PATCH flips `GENERATED →
HOST_EDITED` in place and a host who rewrote every row would otherwise read as having no plan.

**A V1 event at the V2 URL resolves to `opening`, unchanged** (founder ruling 4,
2026-09-12). `EventSetup` is the V1/V2 discriminator everywhere else in the tree — the events
list routes on it, `regenerate-plan` 404s without it, the V1 dashboard's `!event.setup` guards
hide the V1 pipelines with it — and it is load-bearing in the rule's first line. The case is
named and asserted rather than fallen through.

**The households fetch is conditional.** With an `EventSetup` row the rule never reads the
household count, so the common path still makes exactly the two mount requests [[GTC-233]]
verified.

### Ruling 2 — closing the opening screen required opening another door

Founder ruling, 2026-09-12: GTC-235 may retire the opening screen as the entry default **only
if the plan view gains a route to Moment 1 in the same change.**

The investigation found that the opening screen was the *only* door back to Moment 1 from
anywhere in Moment 2. `Moment2Opening` takes only `onStart` and has no back; Step 1 cancels to
that opening; the plan view's back goes to Step 1. So the single way a host added a household
after generating was to reload, land on the opening screen, and press Start. Rehydrating
without replacing that route would have fixed a stranding by removing a capability, which is
the worse trade.

`Moment2PlanView` therefore takes `onEditGuests` as a **required** prop. The control is the
headcount already in its summary line — "based on 17 guests" — because the headcount is the
Moment 1 fact, and the control belongs on the thing it changes rather than in new chrome.

#### Required, not optional — a condition made into an invariant

Ruling 2 made the Moment 1 route a **condition** on this ticket: rehydration may land only if
the route back lands with it. Typing `onEditGuests` as required rather than optional makes it
an **invariant**. Those are different strengths, and the difference is the whole reason it is
typed this way.

A condition is remembered. It holds for as long as someone recalls why it was imposed — and
the thing it guards against is precisely a future change that does not recall it, because the
route back to Moment 1 is not visible from inside Moment 2 and its absence raises no error.
GTC-233 removed a fall-through and left a dead end for the same reason: nothing failed.

An invariant is enforced. `onEditGuests?: () => void` would compile with the prop dropped, and
the plan view would ship once more with no way back to Moment 1 and a green build. Required,
it will not: the component cannot be rendered without someone deciding where that control
goes. The ruling is thereby carried by the type system rather than by anyone's memory of
2026-09-12, which is the only form in which a condition like this survives contact with a
later ticket.

`Moment1Summary`'s continue now has two correct answers and reads `hasGeneratedPlan` to choose
between them: forward to Moment 2's opening on the first pass, back to the plan on a return
visit. Sending a returning host to Moment 2's opening would have walked her at the Generate
button she has no reason to press.

### Ruling 3 — the links

Founder ruling, 2026-09-12: the smallest set, and **not** the pre-flight or the glance. Both
are V1-era answers to Moments 3 and 4; linking them moves the dead end rather than removing it,
and the pre-flight's Send is hard-disabled until [[GTC-189]].

- **Plan view → `/plan/[eventId]`**, labelled "Invites, people and reminders". A link she
  chooses, not a fall-through: [[GTC-233]]'s Option A stands and is not reopened. What Option A
  left behind was a plan view with no exits at all, on an event whose invites, people and
  nudges still live only on that dashboard.
- **The events list gains a second affordance per row.** `router.push(event.setup ? setup :
  dashboard)` gave each event exactly one destination, so a V2 event could not reach the
  dashboard from the list at all.
- **An exit from Moment 1's input phase.** Every Moment 1 screen is `fixed inset-0 z-50` and
  covers the global navigation, which already carries "Your Events". The plan view is
  `min-h-screen`, so the nav is visible there and needed nothing. Moment 1 is the overlay the
  entry rule now *holds* a half-finished host inside across a reload, so it is the one that
  needed a way out. The short-dwell overlays were left alone.

### Overturned deliberately, and annotated rather than deleted

[[GTC-233]] ruled "arriving at this route IS the request to start setup, so the opening screen
shows unconditionally". That was a decision, not an oversight, and it is reversed as one. The
comment at `showSetup` records the reversal and its date in place; [[GTC-233]]'s Scenario 2
evidence is annotated on that ticket as superseded.

## What this does NOT store

Approving the plan remains the one beat in the flow that writes nothing — no column, no
status, no ledger row. It is deliberately not an input to the entry rule and must not become
one by accident. It does not need to be: approving keeps the host on the plan view, so `plan`
is the right answer either side of it.

The Step 2 preview's `dietaryCoverage` and `thingsToConsider` are response-only and are not
persisted by this ticket. A reload during generation still loses them; landing on the plan view
is the better answer than rebuilding them at the cost of another AI call.

## Why these are one change

[[GTC-237]] and this ticket are independent in code — different files, no shared symbol — but
not in effect, and the ordering is not symmetric. Before this fix, the rehydration bug is what
*drove* hosts into the destructive rerun: the only way back to a plan was to walk Moment 2
again and press Generate. Shipping this ticket alone would have removed the reason to press
Generate while making revisits routine, landing hosts on a plan view two clicks from the
destructive path, with more accumulated edits to lose. Founder ruling, 2026-09-12: they ship
together, so 237's guard cannot lag.

## Do not touch

All Do-Not-Touch Zones in `GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`. None were modified. `?setup=true` was
not reintroduced in any form; the entry rule reads stored state, not the URL.
