# GTC-422 - Ticket D, the send gate (tweak 9)

> Filed by Claude Code on 29 Oct 2026, from the founder's Batch 2 Part B
> prompt (tweak 9). Branch feat/tweaks-send-gate, made from

## What this ticket does, in plain English

On the "Before you send" page, step 5 ("Ready", the step that holds
Send) used to have its own tick box, and Send stayed grey until all five
boxes were ticked. Now step 5 has no box: it is just the place where
Send sits. Send turns on as soon as steps 1 to 4 are ticked, and while
it waits it says "Send — finish the four checks first". Pressing Send
is unchanged: one press, no recall.

## The founder's rulings (28 Oct 2026, in Cowork)

- Today step 5, "Ready", reads "The last look is done." and has its own
  box; Send reads "Send — finish the five checks first" until all five
  boxes are ticked.
- New: step 5 is drawn WITHOUT a box (a new option on the step
  component, off by default so nothing else changes), its words as they
  are today. Send turns on when steps 1 to 4 are ticked. Send's tail
  becomes "finish the four checks first", after the same dash character.
  The shared pre-flight libraries are not touched.
- THE PRESS IS ONE ACT WITH NO RECALL: first read and write here whether
  the SERVER also checks the ticks or the five steps before it sends. If
  the server requires step 5, or anything else that would now differ,
  HARD STOP for this ticket.
- Earlier rulings this reverses, recorded here: GTC-377 ("Send stays
  exactly where it is in Ready, untouched, greyed until all five are
  ticked") and the five-checks wording in GTC-366. The founder rules:
  four ticks, step 5 without a box.
- The suite and the walk-throughs must prove, in a browser on the demo
  database, WITHOUT EVER PRESSING SEND: with steps 1 to 4 ticked Send is
  enabled; with any ONE of steps 1 to 4 left unticked (each of the four
  separately) Send is disabled and says the four-checks words; with none
  ticked it is disabled; step 5 has no box; nothing else on the page
  changes.

## The server (read before any edit, at GTC-421's tip): it does not check

- The Send route, src/app/api/events/[id]/send/route.ts (POST, 68 lines),
  reads the session (`requireEventRole(eventId, ['HOST'])`) and the event
  id from the address. It reads no body: the page's POST sends none. It
  calls `pressSend` and answers with its outcome.
- `pressSend` (src/lib/press/press.ts) refuses only on the event's state:
  `EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ALREADY_SENT`, `NOT_CONFIRMING`,
  `HOST_HAS_NO_ACCOUNT`, `DEMO_EVENT`, and inside its transaction
  `RECIPIENTS_UNAVAILABLE`, `LINKS_NOT_ISSUED`, `NO_RECIPIENTS`. It
  reads nothing about the steps or the ticks.
- The ticks live only in the browser: src/lib/preflight/tick-memory.ts
  says "the server keeps none of the host's ticks". No route under
  src/app/api reads a tick or `READY_PRINT`.
- So nothing on the server requires step 5 or the five ticks, and
  nothing there would differ. No HARD STOP. Nothing under src/app/api or
  src/lib is edited.

## Where the change is (read before editing, at GTC-421's tip)

src/app/plan/[eventId]/pre-flight/page.tsx:
- line 402, `allChecked`: `[1, 2, 3, 4, 5].every((n) => checked[n] ||
  settled[n])` becomes `[1, 2, 3, 4].every(...)`. The rest of the line
  (`!fourPending && ...`) is unchanged.
- line 536, the line under Send that names the first unticked step:
  `firstUnticked({ ...checked, ...settled })` becomes
  `firstUnticked({ ...checked, ...settled, 5: true })`. Why: with no box
  on step 5 there is nothing there to tick, so the line must never send
  her to step 5. Without this, once steps 1 to 4 were ticked Send would
  be on AND the line "Go to step 5: Ready ↑" would still show, pointing
  at a box that is gone. With it, the line names the first of steps 1 to
  4 not ticked, exactly as before, and is absent once Send is on. The
  shared `firstUnticked` (src/lib/preflight/next-check.ts) is not
  touched.
- line 950, step 5's `<Step n={5} ...>`: gains `noBox`. Its title
  ("Ready"), its blurb ("The last look is done."), `checked` and
  `onCheck` stay as they are (unused without a box; batch16 S5 still
  finds `onCheck={(v) => tick(5, v)}`).
- line 1009, Send's words: `' — finish the five checks first'` becomes
  `' — finish the four checks first'` (the same em dash, U+2014). Send's
  `disabled`, its classes and its `onClick={press}` are unchanged; the
  press handler is unchanged.

src/components/preflight/PreflightStep.tsx (NAMED HERE BEFORE ANY EDIT:
the one component that draws a pre-flight step):
- a new prop `noBox` (default false). Line 168: the header's box, drawn
  as `{folds ? (<div data-tick-slot ...>{box}</div>) : (box)}`, becomes
  `{folds ? (...) : noBox ? null : (box)}`. Only a step that does not
  fold can go without a box (Ready never folds); the foot box, its
  handler and every line dt-5 counts are untouched.

none       grey, "...five checks first"       grey, "...four checks first"

Send turns on with steps 3 and 4.
