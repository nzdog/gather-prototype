# GTC-358 — on a phone, the menu bar widens every signed-in page

> ⚠ **PRECONDITION OF THE DEPLOY — founder ruling, 2026-10-03**, verbatim below and in [[GTC-189]]'s
> gathered list.

**Status:** Open — **scoped 2026-10-03 (two founder rulings below), with [[GTC-357]]; plan next.**
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

Found by Claude Code while taking [[GTC-355]]'s Q20 screenshots, 2026-10-03. Its report, verbatim:
*"On a 390px phone, the site's top navigation widens the page to about 773px. Fixed bars then
stretch to that width, and a narrow capture cut Moment 3's footer in half. Moment 2's plan view
measures exactly the same, so it's not new."* GTC-355's Evidence records it under "Not done".

### What the code shows (Cowork, read 2026-10-03)

- The bar is `src/components/shared/Navigation.tsx`, rendered by the root layout
  (`src/app/layout.tsx`) on every page except the token views — `/h/` (the host's link), `/c/` (a
  coordinator's) and `/p/` (a guest's) — which hide it. So guests' and coordinators' pages are not
  affected, and every signed-in page is: the setup flow (Moments 1 to 3), the pre-flight, the board,
  the V1 dashboard, Your Events, Billing and the home page.
- It lays out the logo, five links (Home, Your Events, Past Events, New Event, Billing), the
  signed-in email and Sign Out in flex rows that never wrap, with no small-screen layout (no `sm:`
  or `md:` classes).
- Measured at 390px: the setup flow (Moments 2 and 3) at about 773px ([[GTC-355]]'s screenshots),
  and the pre-flight at 844px ([[GTC-356]]'s), so something on the pre-flight is wider than the bar.
  The other pages are not measured. *(Updated 2026-10-03 at scoping: the measurements, and the
  button's words, "Sign Out".)*

### The ruling

Asked in Cowork, 2026-10-03, verbatim:

> On a phone, the menu bar at the top of every host page (Home, Your Events, Past Events, New
> Event, Billing, your email, Sign out) sits in one row and makes the page about twice as wide as
> the screen. Guests' pages hide that bar, so guests aren't affected. Must this be fixed before the
> 25 October launch?

The founder's answer, verbatim: **"Yes, before launch (Recommended)"**. The option read: *"Add it
to the launch must-dos alongside GTC-355 to 357. Without it, a host on a phone sees every page,
Moment 3 and the board included, run off the side of the screen. It's likely a small change to the
menu bar alone."*

## SCOPED 2026-10-03 — two founder rulings (in Cowork, verbatim)

**R1.** *"Now GTC-358. On a phone, the menu bar along the top (Home, Your Events, Past Events, New
Event, Billing, your email and Sign Out, all in one row) makes every signed-in page about twice as
wide as the screen. The pre-flight measured 844 pixels wide on a 390-pixel phone. What should the
bar become on a phone?"* Options: *A menu button (Recommended)*; *Icons only*. The founder chose,
verbatim: **"A menu button (Recommended)"** — *"On a phone the bar shows the Gather logo and a menu
button (three lines). Tapping it opens the five links, your email and Sign Out. On a computer the
bar stays as it is."* Settles open item 1; the width at which it changes is the plan's to propose.

**R2.** *"The menu bar alone made the setup pages 773 pixels wide, but the pre-flight measured 844,
so something on the pre-flight itself is also wider than a phone. Should GTC-358 also fix anything
else that spills off a phone screen on the pages a host uses?"* Options: *Yes, the host's pages
(Recommended)*; *Just the menu bar*. The founder chose, verbatim: **"Yes, the host's pages
(Recommended)"** — *"After the menu fix, Claude Code measures each page a host uses at phone width
(Moments 1 to 3, the pre-flight, the board, Your Events) and fixes whatever still spills over, such
as the pre-flight. Each fix is listed for you in the plan."* Settles open item 3.

> *Correction, 2026-10-03 (Cowork's, not a ruling):* R2's question said "something on the
> pre-flight itself is also wider than a phone". The plan measured otherwise: the 844px against
> 773px came from the signed-in email's length, and with the bar hidden the pre-flight is exactly
> 390px. What does spill without the bar is Your Events (517px, each row's buttons) and the old
> dashboard (396px, its header buttons), which the plan fixes under R2.

Open item 2 is settled by scoping the two together: one plan for [[GTC-357]] and this ticket, a
commit each. Open item 4 is the plan's to propose.
