# GTC-358 — on a phone, the menu bar widens every signed-in page

> ⚠ **PRECONDITION OF THE DEPLOY — founder ruling, 2026-10-03**, verbatim below and in [[GTC-189]]'s
> gathered list.

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-03 in Cowork on founder ruling. Not scoped.
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

Found by Claude Code while taking [[GTC-355]]'s Q20 screenshots, 2026-10-03. Its report, verbatim:
*"On a 390px phone, the site's top navigation widens the page to about 773px. Fixed bars then
stretch to that width, and a narrow capture cut Moment 3's footer in half. Moment 2's plan view
measures exactly the same, so it's not new."* GTC-355's Evidence records it under "Not done".

### What the code shows (Cowork, read 2026-10-03)

- The bar is `src/components/shared/Navigation.tsx`, rendered by the root layout
  (`src/app/layout.tsx`) on every page except the token views — `/h/` (the host's link), `/c/` (a
  coordinator's) and `/p/` (a guest's) — which hide it. So guests' and coordinators' pages are not
  affected, and every signed-in page is: the setup flow (Moments 1 to 3), the pre-flight, the board,
  the V1 dashboard, Your Events, Billing and the home page.
- It lays out the logo, five links (Home, Your Events, Past Events, New Event, Billing), the
  signed-in email and Sign out in flex rows that never wrap, with no small-screen layout (no `sm:`
  or `md:` classes).
- Measured at 390px only on the setup flow (Moments 2 and 3). The other pages are not measured.

### The ruling

Asked in Cowork, 2026-10-03, verbatim:

> On a phone, the menu bar at the top of every host page (Home, Your Events, Past Events, New
> Event, Billing, your email, Sign out) sits in one row and makes the page about twice as wide as
> the screen. Guests' pages hide that bar, so guests aren't affected. Must this be fixed before the
> 25 October launch?

The founder's answer, verbatim: **"Yes, before launch (Recommended)"**. The option read: *"Add it
to the launch must-dos alongside GTC-355 to 357. Without it, a host on a phone sees every page,
Moment 3 and the board included, run off the side of the screen. It's likely a small change to the
menu bar alone."*

### Open, to scope with the founder one at a time

1. What the bar becomes on a phone (for example, its links behind a menu button), and the width at
   which it changes.
2. The overlap with [[GTC-357]], whose scoping may change this bar (where the board lives; V1 links
   such as Past Events and New Event): scope the two together, or this one first.
3. Once the bar fits, anything else on a signed-in page that is wider than a phone: measure the
   setup flow, the pre-flight, the board and Your Events at 390px.
4. How it is pinned: a width check at 390px on each of those pages, against the dev server, with
   nothing sent.
