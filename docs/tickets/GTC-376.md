# GTC-376 — a plan changed after the hold is never checked again

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-09 on the founder's instruction at [[GTC-374]]'s plan
(ruling 4: *"the re-check gap filed as a ticket for after launch"*). After launch. Not scoped.
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

Holding the plan (Moment 3's "Move on →", [[GTC-360]]) runs the gate once
(`runGateCheck` in `src/lib/workflow.ts`): an unacknowledged critical conflict, or a critical item
with a placeholder quantity, keeps her in Moment 3. A plan made or changed after the event is held
is never checked again: `holdThePlan` (`src/lib/moment3/hold.ts`) goes straight on for an event that
is not DRAFT (GTC-360 Q4, *"never held twice"*), and nothing else runs the gate before Send.

[[GTC-374]] makes the gap easier to reach: an invites-only event is held when she chooses it, and
can still start a plan before Send (Q5 there, ruled "accept" for launch).

### With it: C12 of GTC-374

PlanSnapshot (and `Event.planSnapshotIdAtConfirming`) and `Event.structureMode` are written by the
hold and never read. "Locks structure" locks nothing: no route refuses plan-making or plan-editing
on a held event ([[GTC-169]] removed `canMutate()`: "the lock is a ledger, not a wall").

### Unknowns

- Whether a second check belongs on "Move on →" for a held event (`POST
  /api/events/[id]/gate-check` exists, HOST only; on a held event it also reports
  UNSAVED_DRAFT_CHANGES), or at the press.
- Whether PlanSnapshot and structureMode should be read by something, or retired.
