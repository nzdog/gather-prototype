## What this ticket does, in plain English

Once a guest has answered "are you coming?", her answer stays on her
page as two buttons, Yes and No, hers marked (GTC-419). Until now, if she
had said yes and then held a dish (or had a dish she had not answered),
pressing No did nothing she could see: the server refuses "not coming"
from a guest who still holds a dish, so the page quietly read itself
again and nothing changed.

Now, when she presses No, the page first says No to each of her own
dishes that is not already a No, one at a time, exactly as if she had
tapped No on each dish; then it says she is not coming. Her host sees
the dishes come free. A child's dishes on her page are never touched.
If anything fails part way, the page stops, reads itself again, and
says "That didn't go through. Please tap No again."

Two smaller changes: the box now marks the answer the server actually
holds for her (an accepted dish counts as yes), so the page can no
longer say "We'll miss you" and "You're all set" at the same time; and
when she has said No and every dish of hers is No, a line under "We'll
miss you at the event!" says "We've marked your items No, so your host
knows to find someone else."

## The server (read, not changed)

- `PATCH /api/p/[token]` (the attendance answer, `{ attending }`) answers
  409 unless `isAttendanceAskable(her own assignments)`: no ACCEPTED, no
  PENDING, no MAYBE. Her own rows only (`personId` = the link's person).
- `POST /api/p/[token]/ack/[assignmentId]` (a dish answer, `{ response }`)
  takes ACCEPTED, DECLINED or MAYBE for her own row, or a carried child's
  row; it has no lifecycle gate (GTC-169: answers after the send are the
  point).
- Neither route reads the event's status: a closed event behaves as an
  open one, on the page and on the server.
- `deriveAttendance`: an accepted dish is YES, above any stored answer;
  then the stored answer; then MAYBE or all-declined is UNKNOWN.

## Where the change is (read before editing, at bcc3662)

One file: `src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx`.

1. Two hooks after the page's own: `notComingFailed` (the failure line)
   and `notComingBusy` (a press under way).
2. A new handler, `handleNotComing`, after the card toggles. The page's
   read (`fetchData`), the attendance answer (`handleAttendanceAnswer`)
   and the dish answer (`handleResponse`) are not changed by a character.
3. `shownAnswer`, worked out once above the page's markup: YES when the
   server's derived attendance is YES, otherwise her stored answer.
4. The answered box (shown, as before, whenever she has a stored answer):
   its heading, its line and its two buttons follow `shownAnswer`; the
   new line under "We'll miss you at the event!"; No calls
   `handleNotComing`; the failure line after the buttons.

The first-time box ("Are you coming?" / "No worries — still coming?",
"Yes, still coming", "Can't make it") is not changed: it shows only when
every dish of hers is already No (or she has none), so its "Can't make
it" has nothing to decline and sends what it sent.

### The calls, old and new

```
the dish answer (unchanged; a tap on a dish's No sends this):
  POST /api/p/<link>/ack/<id>  {"response":"DECLINED"}, then GET /api/p/<link>
the attendance answer (unchanged):
  PATCH /api/p/<link>  {"attending":false}, then GET /api/p/<link>

the answered box's No, before:
  PATCH {"attending":false}, GET
the answered box's No, now (handleNotComing):
  for each of HER OWN rows not DECLINED, in the page's order:
    POST /api/p/<link>/ack/<id> {"response":"DECLINED"}  (stop on failure)
  PATCH /api/p/<link> {"attending":false}  (only if every decline went)
  GET /api/p/<link>                        (always, once, at the end)
```

At most one call per undeclined dish of hers and one attendance call per
press: one pass over her rows, and a second press while one is under way
returns at once.

### The words, old and new

```
new: We've marked your items No, so your host knows to find someone else.
     (under "We'll miss you at the event!", when her answer reads No,
      she has at least one dish and every dish of hers is No)
new: That didn't go through. Please tap No again.
     (in the answered box, after a failed call, until her next No)
kept: Great — we'll see you there. / Your host knows to expect you.
kept: Thanks for letting us know / We'll miss you at the event!
kept: Yes / No; Changed your mind? Tap a different answer. ...
kept: Yes, still coming / Can't make it / the itemless words (batch7 R7)
```

A choice in the ruled line's condition: "when she holds at least one
item and her answer is No" is drawn only when every dish of hers is
DECLINED, so it never says "We've marked your items No" while a dish
added later still waits on her (state 13 below).

## The states, before and after (the calls are the box's No unless said)

```
 1 no dishes, not asked yet: "Are you coming?" box. Unchanged.
   Yes, still coming: PATCH true. Can't make it: PATCH false.
 2 no dishes, answered Yes: Yes marked. No: PATCH false, as before.
 3 no dishes, answered No: No marked. Yes: PATCH true, as before.
 4 only declined dishes, not answered: "No worries — still coming?".
   Unchanged.
 5 only declined dishes, answered Yes: No: PATCH false only (nothing to
   decline, none re-sent). As before.
 6 only declined dishes, answered No: before "We'll miss you at the
   event!"; now also "We've marked your items No, so your host knows to
   find someone else." Yes: PATCH true, as before (dishes stay No,
   ruling 4).
 7 a dish accepted, never asked: no box, "You're all set". Unchanged.
 8 answered Yes, then a dish accepted: Yes marked. Before, No sent PATCH
   false, the server said 409, nothing changed. Now No: POST DECLINED
   (that dish), PATCH false, GET; she reads state 6.
 9 answered No, then Yes on a dish (stored No beside an accepted dish;
   the server reads her as coming): before, the box said "Thanks for
   letting us know / We'll miss you at the event!" with No marked AND
   "You're all set. See you there." showed below it; its Yes sent PATCH
   true and got 409. Now Yes is marked, the box says "Great — we'll see
   you there." with "You're all set" (no "We'll miss you"); Yes, marked,
   sends nothing; No: POST DECLINED (the dish), PATCH false, GET.
10 answered Yes, then a dish given and not answered (pending): before No
   got 409. Now: POST DECLINED (that dish), PATCH false, GET.
11 a pending dish, never asked: no box. Unchanged.
12 answered Yes, then Maybe on a dish: before No got 409. Now: POST
   DECLINED (that dish), PATCH false, GET.
13 answered No, then a dish given and not answered: No marked (as
   before); the new line NOT shown (that dish is not No); Yes: PATCH
   true (the server still says 409, as before). Nothing new is sent.
14 her own dishes and a child's carried dishes: only HER rows are
   declined (in the page's order); the child's rows are never sent;
   a row of hers already declined is not re-sent.
15 a closed event: the same as 14 (the page does not read the status;
   neither route gates on it).
```

Any failure (a refused call or the network): the calls stop at the
failing one, the page is read again, the failure line shows; the
attendance No is never sent after a failed decline. A corner: in state 9
with two or more dishes to decline, if a later decline fails after an
earlier one went through and no accepted dish is left, the page reads
her stored No again, so No is already marked; the remaining dish shows
its own Yes, No and Maybe. Nothing wrong is sent; she answers that dish
on its card.

No state sends a wrong call: a Yes press never declines anything (it
sends what it sent); a child's row is never in the list; a row already
declined is never re-sent.
