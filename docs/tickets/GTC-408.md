## Flags for the founder

1. Nothing links to the cancel page. Stripe's checkout sends a
   cancelled payment to /plan/new?canceled=true
   (src/app/api/billing/checkout/route.ts line 51), and no file in src
   links to /billing/cancel. The page is reached only by typing its
   address. Its "Try Again" goes to /billing/upgrade.
2. The old domain still appears in six places under src, all the same
   fallback sender, "Gather <noreply@gather.app>", used ONLY when the
   EMAIL_FROM setting is missing. Not changed, as ruled. They are the
   "from" address of every Gather email:
   - src/lib/email.ts line 177, sendMagicLinkEmail: the sign-in link
     email.
   - src/lib/email.ts line 329, hostVoicedFrom: the address part of
     every guest email sent in the host's name (the ask, the chase,
     the decide-by email) and of the host's own test of one. Her name
     replaces the display part; the address after it is this one.
   - src/lib/email.ts line 405, sendNudgeEmail: the nudge to a guest.
   - src/lib/email.ts line 473, sendWelcomeEmail: the "your event is
     ready" email after payment, with its sign-in link.
   - src/app/api/auth/claim/route.ts line 79: the "claim your Gather
     host account" email with its sign-in link.
   - src/lib/sms/tnz-account-alert.ts line 99: the alert email to the
     founder when the TNZ text account has a problem.
   In plain English: if EMAIL_FROM is ever missing where Gather runs,
   every one of those emails would claim to come from
   noreply@gather.app, a domain that is not the founder's. Resend would
   most likely refuse them (the sending domain is not verified), so
   sign-in links and welcome emails would stop arriving, rather than
   go out under someone else's name. While EMAIL_FROM is set, the
   fallback is never used.
3. No other place under src holds the old support address
   support@gather.app: the cancel page was the last one.
