# GTC-342 — after the press, "Review and send" comes back for a late arrival and sends nothing

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-09-29 at [[GTC-329]] on founder instruction. Not scoped.
**Not a deploy precondition unless the founder rules it one.**

---

### Signal

`InviteStatusSection` (`src/components/plan/InviteStatusSection.tsx`) shows "Review and send"
while `hasUnsentPeople` is true: someone reads NOT_SENT. `/api/events/[id]/invite-status` derives
NOT_SENT from the legacy `Person.inviteAnchorAt` being null. Nothing in that gate asks whether the
event has already been sent.

So after the press, a person who reads NOT_SENT brings the button back. Seen at [[GTC-329]]'s walk
on 2026-09-29, on a post-press fixture with one person's anchor cleared: `hasUnsentPeople` true,
`notSent` 1.

- **Before GTC-329** the button led to the pre-flight's step 5, whose Send the press refuses
  `ALREADY_SENT`.
- **Since GTC-329** it leads to the post-press surface, "Who I chase", which has no Send. The words
  promise a send the page does not offer.

People added after the press get their own mini-send (`enrolMiniSends` in
`src/lib/press/dispatch.ts`), not a re-press, so this button has nothing of its own to do once the
event is sent.

### Unknowns

1. Which add paths leave a late arrival NOT_SENT. `capturePerson` and `reconcileMembers` in
   `src/lib/households/` stamp `Person.inviteAnchorAt` for a person added after the press; whether
   the batch import and the guest self-join (`src/app/api/join/[token]/claim/route.ts`) do has not
   been checked. If none leaves it null, this is only reachable when an anchor is missing.
2. What the host should see instead: nothing, or the mini-send's own state for that person. That
   touches [[GTC-321]]'s open question about "I sent them myself".
