### What was found
Plan generation completes in `handleGeneratePlan` (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx:544`). After success, it refreshes all data, closes expansion modals, and shows a toast — but provides no guidance on what to do next. The dashboard renders cards for teams, items, and people, with unassigned items visible but no actionable prompt.

The teams expansion modal (`expandedSection === 'teams'`) is the correct destination — it surfaces the team board where the host can assign items to people. This is opened via `handleExpandSection('teams')`.

Best placement for the CTA: between the AI generation loading banner and the card grid, so it's the first thing the host sees after generation completes.
