# GTC-458 - The /demo explainer: Gather styling, a fold-out per step, real screenshots

**Status: built, not committed.** Waiting for the founder's word to commit. Not pushed, not merged.
This ticket is built on top of GTC-457's commit (`ec32a6d`, PR #90) and replaces its component and
words; it does not stand without it.

All dates are NZ time.

## What and why

The founder (8 Nov 2026), looking at GTC-457's explainer on the live /demo page: "This is very
boring page. Please style it using Gathers design styles. Have all the paragraph headings as
titles that expand when clicked. Add some screen shots, especially of the Add People buttons and
suggest answers."

## What changed

- **Styling from the theme's own tokens, nothing new added.** The page takes `warm-white` for its
  background (was `gray-50`), the warm greys for text and borders, `sage` for the accent, and the
  heading is set in the serif the wordmark uses (`font-serif`, Source Serif 4 then Georgia, as in
  the brand lockup). No colour, font or Tailwind setting was added.
- **One fold-out per step.** GTC-457's single closed row is gone. The page now shows the heading
  "How Gather works, step by step", the introduction (always showing), and five fold-out rows:
  Start your event, and Moments 1 to 4. Each is closed on every visit, with a numbered round badge
  (a start mark for the first), a small label ("Moment 1") over the title, and the shared
  "Show" / "Hide" cue. Several can be open at once (GTC-364's rule), as in the pre-flight.
  A step's title is the old heading split in two: "Moment 1: Who's coming?" is now the label
  "Moment 1" and the title "Who's coming?".
- **Five screenshots**, real captures of the live sample taken 8 Nov 2026, cropped, in
  `public/explainer/` (about 200 KB in all). They load only when scrolled near.
  - Moment 1: the four buttons "Add Partner", "Kid with a job", "Kid without a job" and
    "Add Guest"; the "Add a crowd" button beside the list; the summary after the crowd is added.
  - Moment 2: the "Fill in demo answers" button; the screen after it, with the answers in
    "What you've picked".
  - Moments 3 and 4 have none. Both need a generated plan, which makes an AI call, so they were not
    walked.
- **Words.** Moment 1 gained one sentence naming the four buttons (read off the live screen, not
  recalled). The introduction gained "Tap a step to open it." Everything else is GTC-457's, now
  with a caption under each picture. `src/lib/demo/explainer-words.ts` holds the steps as blocks
  (paragraphs and figures, in order) so a picture sits beside the sentence it illustrates.
- `src/components/demo/DemoExplainer.tsx`: rewritten. `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on each
  row, closed text `aria-hidden`, a plain `<img>` with its size, `loading="lazy"` and words for a
  screen reader. No hooks beyond a set of open steps; no setting read; no request.
- `src/app/demo/page.tsx`: background, heading and container width only. `isDemoStartOn()`,
  `TRY_IT_YOURSELF` and the button are unchanged.

No schema, no migration, no route, no sign-in code, nothing in a Do-Not-Touch Zone. The middleware
does not match `/explainer/...`, so the pictures are served to a signed-out visitor.

## Decisions to look at

1. **The wrapper row is gone.** GTC-457 had one closed row holding everything. The founder asked
   for the headings to be the titles that expand, so the five steps now sit on the page, closed,
   under the heading and introduction. If the single wrapper row is wanted back, the five steps go
   inside it; it is a small change.
2. **The screenshots go stale.** They are pictures of today's screens. Retake the one whose screen
   changes (the words file lists them).
3. **On a phone the two widest pictures are small.** Their text is hard to read at 390 px wide.
   Tap-to-enlarge was not built.
4. **The sample the screenshots were taken from** was created on the live site on 8 Nov 2026 with
   the page's own pre-filled made-up host. It is private and the sample's clean-up job deletes it.
