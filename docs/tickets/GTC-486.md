## Why

The /demo page has a "Try it yourself" button and, under it, the explainer's
steps. Its introduction ended "Tap a step to open it.", which points visitors
at the steps (fixed explainer content that starts nothing) and away from the
button. On Sunday 22 Nov 2026 the logs showed 13 loads of /demo and 2 samples,
neither with a guest (weak evidence; see the planning report of 26 Nov). And
the logs could not tell "never pressed the button" from "pressed it and left
at Create", because the sample's start page (/demo/new) wrote no visit line.

This ticket makes the button the page's main instruction, cuts the
introduction to one line, adds a second button at the foot of the steps, puts
the campaign line under the heading, and counts /demo/new as a fourth page,
with the privacy page saying so in the same commit.

## Readings taken (each is the plain reading; say if one is wrong)

1. **The page name is `sample`.** The line reads `[page-visit] sample
   <source>`, for example `[page-visit] sample internal` after a press of "Try
   it yourself" (a full page load from /demo, so its Referer is Gather's own)
   and `[page-visit] sample direct` for a typed or bookmarked address. Chosen
   because it is one word, like the other three, and no other page's name
   starts with it: a reader that matches `[page-visit] demo` by its start
   would also count `demo-new`, but never `sample`.
2. **Counted only when the page opens.** The layout reads the demo start
   switch first (GTC-447) and answers not-found when it is off; the count
   comes after that, so a closed demo's not-found is never counted.
3. **No tag on the sample page, held by the counter itself.** The tag is
   written for the home page and /demo only (ruling Q8 of 19 Nov said so for
   those two); the counter no longer treats "not the new-event page" as
   "may carry a tag".
4. **The introduction is replaced whole** by "Rather read first? Each step
   below shows what happens." The old paragraph's other sentences (the working
   copy, you're the host, Gather is your assistant, four steps called Moments)
   go with "Tap a step to open it." The step titles still say "Moment 1" and so
   on; nothing now introduces the word "Moments".
5. **The D line and the line under the button show only where the button
   does**, in the switch's on-branch, as the explainer does since GTC-457. With
   the switch off the page is still the heading and "Under construction",
   exactly as GTC-430 ruled.
6. **The apostrophe.** The D line was drawn "You’re onto it. Finally, you can
   show them." with the page's own curly apostrophe, as the explainer under it
   is written ("You’re the host"). The founder kept the curly one (27 Nov) and
   then ruled new words for the line (27 Nov): it is now drawn "Because those
   potatoes won’t bring themselves to lunch.", curly as ruled.
7. **The date** was "Last updated: 26 November 2026", the day of the ruling.
   The founder ruled on 27 Nov 2026 for the deploy day: it is now "Last
   updated: 27 November 2026", in the page and in its three suites.
8. **The words live in demo/page.tsx** beside its other two (`TRY_IT_YOURSELF`,
   `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`), as `LEAD` and `START_LINE`; the introduction stays in
   explainer-words.ts.
