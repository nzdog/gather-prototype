# GTC-357 — a way into the board

> ⚠ **PRECONDITION OF THE DEPLOY — founder ruling, 2026-10-03**, verbatim in [[GTC-189]]'s gathered
> list: chosen with [[GTC-355]] and [[GTC-356]] as *"A way into the board"*.

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-03 in Cowork on founder ruling. Not scoped.
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

The founder, 2026-10-02, verbatim: *"What i really want is for the moment flow to go from 1 through
to 4 with the hinge"*.

The board, `src/app/plan/[eventId]/glance/page.tsx`, is URL-only: nothing in the app links to it
(its own page header; [[GTC-192]] decision 4, "Where the screen lives", still open; [[GTC-328]]).
After the press the pre-flight's address becomes the smaller "Who I chase" surface ([[GTC-329]]),
with no way on to the board. A host reaches Moment 4 only by typing its address, so none of what
the board shows (the reds, the replies, the hand-backs, the arrival replay) reaches her.

### Open, to scope with the founder one at a time

1. Where the board lives ([[GTC-192]]'s open decision): a tab on the plan, the page that replaces
   the V1 dashboard for Moment-flow events, or its own destination.
2. After the press: does the host land on the board?
3. Coming back later: where a sent Moment-flow event opens from Your Events and the home page
   (today a Moment-flow event opens the setup flow, `/plan/[eventId]/setup`).
4. The V1 dashboard for Moment-flow events: a back room reached from the board, and its V1 leftovers
   (the five-step setup checklist and the "assign items to your team members" banner).
5. Co-hosts: the board already opens for them, without guests' reply words ([[GTC-350]] Q-F).
