# GTC-357 — a way into the board

> ⚠ **PRECONDITION OF THE DEPLOY — founder ruling, 2026-10-03**, verbatim in [[GTC-189]]'s gathered
> list: chosen with [[GTC-355]] and [[GTC-356]] as *"A way into the board"*.

**Status:** Open — **scoped 2026-10-03 (two founder rulings below), with [[GTC-358]]; plan next.**
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

The founder, 2026-10-02, verbatim: *"What i really want is for the moment flow to go from 1 through
to 4 with the hinge"*.

The board, `src/app/plan/[eventId]/glance/page.tsx`, is URL-only: nothing in the app links to it
(its own page header; [[GTC-192]] decision 4, "Where the screen lives", still open; [[GTC-328]]).
After the press the pre-flight's address becomes the smaller "Who I chase" surface ([[GTC-329]]),
with no way on to the board. A host reaches Moment 4 only by typing its address, so none of what
the board shows (the reds, the replies, the hand-backs, the arrival replay) reaches her.

## SCOPED 2026-10-03 — two founder rulings (in Cowork, verbatim)

**R1.** *"GTC-357 first. Right now, after you press send you see "Sent to N people" with no way on,
and opening the event later from Your Events takes you back into the plan (Moment 3). The board,
which shows who's answered, who's red and any replies, has its own page, but nothing links to it.
Once the invitations have gone, where should the event open?"* Options: *On the board
(Recommended)*; *In the plan, with a button*; *Old dashboard, with a link*. The founder chose,
verbatim: **"On the board (Recommended)"** — *"Right after you press send, and whenever you open the
event again from Your Events, you land on the board. The plan, Moment 3 and "Who I chase" are
reached from there. The same for a co-host."* Settles open items 1, 2, 3 and 5.

**R2.** *"The old dashboard still holds things the Moment flow doesn't have yet: invites and share
links, people, reminders and conflicts. For Moment-flow events you reach it today from the "Invites
& people" button on Your Events. On a draft it still shows two leftovers from before the Moment
flow: the five-step setup checklist ("Create event … Run plan check") and the "Your plan is ready!
Next up: assign items to your team members" banner. What should happen to it for Moment-flow
events?"* Options: *Back room, leftovers gone (Recommended)*; *Back room, as it is*. The founder
chose, verbatim: **"Back room, leftovers gone (Recommended)"** — *"It stays, reached from the board
as well as from Your Events, for the things only it has. The checklist and the team banner stop
showing on Moment-flow events, because the Moment flow now covers those steps."* Settles open
item 4.

### What the rulings settle

- **Once the invitations have gone, a Moment-flow event's home is the board**, for the host and a
  co-host: she lands on it after the press, and opening the event again brings her there. Before
  the press it opens in the setup flow, as today. [[GTC-192]]'s open decision 4, "Where the screen
  lives", is settled by R1: the sent event's home.
- **From the board** she reaches the plan (Moment 3), "Who I chase" and the old dashboard.
- **The old dashboard stays as a back room** for what only it has (invites and share links, people,
  reminders, conflicts), reached from the board and from Your Events' "Invites & people".
  `SetupChecklistBanner` and `NextStepBanner` no longer show on Moment-flow events.

### For the plan (Cowork's notes, not rulings)

1. Every door that opens an event: Your Events' row click and its "Invites & people" button, the
   home page, the host preview link on a guest page (`src/app/p/[token]/page.tsx`), reuse from Past
   Events, the demo. Say which change and which stay.
2. The moment after the press ("Sent to N people" and the threshold lines on the pre-flight): does
   the page move on to the board by itself, or offer a button? Its words, for approval.
3. The words of the board's links (to the plan, "Who I chase" and the back room), for approval.
4. Whether the board keeps its address, `/plan/[eventId]/glance`.
5. [[GTC-358]] is planned with this ticket, in one plan with a commit each.

### Open, to scope with the founder one at a time
