# GTC-431 - A stopped Save names the problem and takes her to it

> Filed by Claude Code on 31 Oct 2026, from the founder's fix ticket
> "household form silently fails on Save". Branch fix/household-save
> from origin/main at 4f91128. The first of five tickets, one commit
> each: GTC-431 to GTC-435.

## Why

A production tester saved her own household with two adults on 30 Oct
2026 at 20:21 NZ time, added six children, and Save would not work.
Railway's HTTP log for the live deployment (1ae6b19c) shows her phone
(iPhone Safari) sent no request at all between that save and 20:29,
when she left for "Your events" and came back. No household request in
the window failed; no error line was logged. So the Save was stopped in
the browser by the form's own checks, whose only words sat under the
box that failed: with six child rows that box can be far above the
Save button, and nothing said so near it.

## The founder's ruling (31 Oct 2026)

1. When any form check fails on Save, show a message next to the Save
   button ("Please fix N things above"), scroll to the first failing
   box and focus it. Apply to all three Save buttons.
- At plan time: "Please fix 1 thing above" when N is 1.

## The change (src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx)

- Every Save path runs the checks through one function, `checksFail`:
  "Save & add another household", "Save changes", "Save and add
  everyone else →", and the setup page's "Save & move on →" (the form's
  handleDone). The checks themselves are unchanged.
- When a check fails: nothing is sent (as before); the errors are drawn
  at once (flushSync); the first box marked `aria-invalid="true"` in
  screen order, and visible, takes focus. Focusing it brings it into
  view. Every box with its own error now carries `aria-invalid` (nine
  places: name, the primary's email and phone, partner email and phone,
  a kid's name, email and phone, a guest's email and phone).
- Beside the Save buttons, where "That didn't save" already sits:
  `fixWords(N)`, as `role="alert"`, shown only after a stopped Save and
  while any box shows an error. N follows her fixes; it goes at 0.

Why focus and not a second scroll call: tweaks-b S1 (GTC-416 ruling 5)
pins the form's one scroll effect word for word and allows exactly one
`scrollIntoView` and one `matchMedia`. Focusing a box scrolls it into
view without animation, which also respects the reduced-motion setting.
