### The items (what the code does today: Cowork, read 2026-10-05 at `42205a4`)

#### Item 3, with item 21 — the screen jumps when a section opens

His words: *"When I click on desserts or something the screen jumps around"* (2026-10-03);
*"Dessert expand shifts the frame"* and *"Non and Alcoholic does too"* (2026-10-05); and, for Table
Snacks in the sort, *"It expanded like dessert adn non alocoholic drinks"*.

Two likely causes, read from the code and not yet seen on screen:

- **One section open at a time.** The modal keeps a single open section (`openAccordion`, one id),
  so opening Dessert closes the open section above it, and everything below moves up by that
  section's height.
- **A box sizes itself from its height before a choice.** `src/components/plan/AccordionShell.tsx`
  sets an open section's height from its content's `scrollHeight` as read while rendering, which is
  the height before the change, plus 40px. A choice that adds options (picking "Traditional roast"
  adds its five dishes) is cut off until the next render, which comes when the choice saves, about
  half a second later (the modal's 500 ms save); then the box grows.

⚠ `AccordionShell` is shared with the pre-flight's household rows ([[GTC-188]] extracted it for
that), so a change to it reaches the pre-flight too. The plan says what changes there.

#### Item 18 — the whole box opens on one click

His words: *"Entire lozenge eg: mains needs to be expandable on click"*. Asked what happened, his
answer in the sort: *"Clicking part of the box did nothing"*.

A click anywhere on a section's title row already opens it, so the dead part is to be found on
screen. One candidate, from the code: an open section has a small grey *"Still deciding?"* link at
its top. Once tapped, every choice in the section is greyed out and stops responding to clicks
(`opacity-50 pointer-events-none`) until *"✓ Still deciding — click to edit"* is tapped. A host who
taps it by accident finds the box dead. Another candidate is the cut-off in item 3.

#### Item 24 — dishes drop down right under their style

His words: *"Nested menu items drop down directly from parents"*.

Today `src/components/shared/OptionTree.tsx` (used only by Moment 2's questions) shows a section's
questions one after another. Picking a style ("Traditional roast") adds its dishes under a separate
question, *"Which dishes?"*, below the whole list of styles; with two styles picked, their dishes
are grouped under small grey headings. The ask: each style's dishes open right under that style.
The stored choices (`selections`, kept per question) need not change; this is how they are laid
out. For the plan: where the dishes' "Other" box goes, and what happens to a question that depends
on nothing (Alcoholic Drinks' third, *"Anything else to consider?"*).

#### Item 28 — the dietary needs summarised on the Dietary section's title row

Ruling: *"On the Dietary section’s title row"*.

Today the *"⚠️ Dietary requirements"* row shows *"Needs confirmation"* while unanswered and nothing
once answered; what was ticked shows only while the section is open. Dietary needs are kept for the
whole event, never per person. The summary's words (for example "No dietary needs", or the ticked
needs listed) are to be proposed in the plan and ruled.
