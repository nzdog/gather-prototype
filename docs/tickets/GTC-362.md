# GTC-362 — Back after signing in lands on the used sign-in link

**Status:** Open — a stub, filed 2026-10-04 on the founder's instruction (*"file it"*), from his own
walkthrough of the Moment flow. Not scoped.
**Not a precondition of the deploy** unless ruled one.
**Severity:** not set — the founder's to set.

---

### Signal

The founder, walking the Moment flow on a local fixture (2026-10-04), twice found his tab on
**"Link Already Used — This link has already been used. Request a new one."** while still signed in
(the bar showed his email): once halfway through Moment 2's menu choices, once in Moment 3 after
giving out items. Nothing he had done was lost — every save in the dev server's log returned 200.

### What the code and the log show (read 2026-10-04)

- The sign-in page, `src/app/auth/verify/page.tsx:37`, sends a signed-in host on with
  `router.push(data.redirectUrl)`. A push keeps the sign-in address (`/auth/verify?token=…`) in the
  tab's history, one step behind wherever she lands.
- A magic-link token is single-use. Returning to that history entry re-runs the page's effect, which
  posts the used token again and gets the "already used" refusal.
- Both times, the dev server's log shows a single request for the sign-in address straight after
  ordinary saves, with nothing before it that could have redirected there — the shape of a Back step.
  Nothing in the setup flow reloads the page or rewrites its address (checked: no `history.*`,
  `router.back`, `router.replace` or `location.reload` in the setup page or the Moment views).
- Likely trigger: on a Mac, a two-finger sideways swipe on the trackpad is Back in Chrome, easy to
  make while scrolling a wide area. An inference from the log; the browser itself was not observed.

### Who it reaches

Every host who signs in through an emailed link (the ordinary sign-in, `sendMagicLinkEmail`, and
the welcome email's link) and then presses Back, or swipes, on the first page she lands on.

### For the plan (Claude Code's note, not a ruling)

The likely fix is one line: `router.replace(data.redirectUrl)` instead of `router.push`, so the used
link never stays in the history. Zone 1 and 2 (session and the magic-link flow) are named in
`GATHER-BUILD-CONSTANTS.md`; this page is the client of the magic-link flow, so whether the change
sits inside Zone 2 is the founder's call. A test would pin that the page replaces rather than pushes,
and a browser walk (sign in, Back) would show the landing page instead of the refusal.
