### The items (what the code does today: Cowork, read 2026-10-06 at `8a852fd`)

#### Item 16 — people show in the column as they are added

His words: *"People should appear in ducks column when added straight way"* (2026-10-05). Asked
which of two things he saw, his answer in the sort: *"People I’d added didn’t show until I pressed
Save"*.

Today the column, *"Ducks in a row so far"* (`src/components/plan/HouseholdCardList.tsx`), lists
saved households only: the setup page's `households`, which grows when a household is posted. The
form (`src/components/plan/Moment1InputForm.tsx`) keeps the household being entered in its own
state (the main contact, a partner, kids with and without jobs, guests) until *"Save & add another
household"* or *"Save & move on →"*; only then does its card appear, at the bottom of the column.
On a desktop the column sits to the right of the form, sticky, scrolling on its own; on a phone it
sits above the form (`src/app/plan/[eventId]/setup/page.tsx`).

For the plan:

- how the household being entered shows in the column before it is saved, and how it reads as not
  saved yet (any words are W-lines);
- ⚠ on a phone the column is above the form, so a card that grows there while she adds people
  would push the form down as she types: the jump [[GTC-364]] removed. The plan says how it avoids
  that;
- whether a saved household being edited shows its unsaved changes the same way;
- where the new card sits, so it is in view on a long list;
- nothing is written to the database before she presses Save, as today, unless the plan proposes
  otherwise and it is ruled.

#### Item 27 — "Add item" more obvious

His words: *"Add item more obvious"*.

Today, on the plan view (`src/components/plan/Moment2PlanView.tsx`), each section ends with *"+ Add
item"* as small grey text, beside *"↻ Regenerate this category"*; *"+ Add category"*, at the very
bottom, is a full-width dashed button.

For the plan: how *"+ Add item"* becomes easy to see (for example the same dashed button as *"+ Add
category"*), kept apart from *"↻ Regenerate this category"*, which makes a paid AI call
(`regenerate-plan`). Any change of words is a W-line.

#### Item 4 — approve all, in "Who's on what?"

Ruling: *"Yes: one tap accepts all of Gather’s suggestions"*.

Today *"Want me to suggest?"* (`src/components/plan/Moment3AssignView.tsx`) shows Gather's
suggestions as faint names, each with ✓ (give) and × (dismiss); each ✓ is one save through the
existing route, `/api/events/[id]/items/[itemId]/assign`. A suggestion never goes to the host,
anyone marked *"Just attending"*, or a kid with a job for anything but a job
(`src/lib/moment3/suggest.ts`, [[GTC-355]]'s ruling Q11).

For the plan:

- the button's words and where it sits (W-lines);
- it accepts exactly the suggestions on screen and nothing else. Dealing out who brings what is
  the host's decision (founder Ruling 1, 2026-08-29, recorded in
  `src/app/api/events/[id]/people/auto-assign/route.ts`, which creates no assignments and is not
  for this);
- after the press each change asks the host why ([[GTC-202]]); one tap must not raise one question
  per item (`askForBatchReason` exists, from `useReasonPrompt`);
- what she sees if a save fails part-way, and what stays given;
- no new API route is expected. A new route joins the security route list (Zone 6), which needs
  the founder's explicit approval: if the plan needs one, stop.

#### Item 29 — an item changes colour once it is given

His words: *"When assigned should change colour"*.

Today an item no one holds has a dotted grey outline and grey text ending *"unassigned"*; a given
item has a plain grey outline, dark text and the person's name. No colour changes.

For the plan: the colour, shown in screenshots. ⚠ On the board green means *settled* (the person
said yes); a given item in Moment 3 isn't confirmed by anyone yet, so its colour shouldn't read as
settled. The ring that marks a picked item (`ring-accent`) must still stand out on a given one.

#### Item 30 — fewer taps: the person stays picked

His words: *"Check assignment action -"*. Ruling: *"Too many taps: keep the person picked"*.

Today each item takes two taps, a person then an item or an item then a person, and the pick
clears after each save (`setSelection(null)` in `assign`), so five items for one person take ten
taps. A tap made while a save is still going is ignored with no sign (`if (saving) return`). With a
person picked, tapping an item someone already holds moves the pick to that item. On a phone the
people are listed below the plan, and picking a person scrolls up to the plan, so the picked person
is out of view.

For the plan:

- the person stays picked after each item until she unpicks them: how she unpicks, and how she can
  tell who is picked while the people list is out of view (any words are W-lines);
- a tap made while a save is going is not lost without a sign;
- what tapping an item someone else holds does while a person is picked (today it must be cleared
  with × first);
- whether picking an item first, then a person, changes.
