### Where this comes from

Batch 6 of the seven in [[GTC-189]]'s Fourth ruling: the two searches. Item 23 is a search bar for
Moment 2's menu (the questions); item 13 is a search of the event. The item numbers are the
walkthrough list's (the Cowork project doc `claude/walkthrough-2026-10-03.md`, which is not in the
repo). Batches 1 to 5 ([[GTC-363]] to [[GTC-368]]) and [[GTC-369]] stay as built.

### The items (what the code does today: Cowork, read 2026-10-08 at `226898f`)

#### Item 23 — a search bar for the menu

His words, 2026-10-05: *"Search bar for menu"*.

Today Moment 2's questions (`src/components/plan/Moment2Step1Modal.tsx`) have no search. A dish
shows only once its section is open and its style is ticked ([[GTC-364]] nests each style's dishes
right under it). The lists come from `src/lib/ai/plan-option-tree-config.json` through
`src/lib/ai/config-loader.ts`: for each of the ten kinds of event, 14 sections, 68 top-level
choices (styles and the like) and 245 to 270 dishes under them. Some sections sit behind *"Show 3
more food categories"* (`getUpFrontCategories`), and a section can be *"Still deciding"*
([[GTC-364]]). Every tick saves through the questions' half-second save, and every way out of the
questions saves first ([[GTC-367]]).

#### Item 13 — a search of the event

Added after the walk, 2026-10-03: a search feature, to search the event (people and items). It came
the same night as item 12, a fuller board *"so a host can find who's on the turkey, or whether there
is a turkey at all"*, which was sorted after launch. Item 13 is the lighter answer to the same
need, before launch.

Today the board (`src/components/glance/GlanceBoard.tsx`, a server component with no hooks,
[[GTC-192]]) shows households and people, not items. Who's on what is answered only in Moment 3
(*"Change who's on what"*), the plan view and the printed list ([[GTC-366]]'s *"Print the list"*,
whose island fetches `GET /api/events/[id]/items` when tapped). The board's islands today:
`GlanceLive`, `GlanceReplay`, `PrintListDoor` and the event details ([[GTC-368]]). Its quiet rule
is [[GTC-192]]'s Ruling 1, *"anything that would make the host lean in does not belong on this
screen"*; the colour key, the days to go and the Moment words sit behind closed buttons for that
reason. Each person's strip carries `data-person-event-id`, and each household card
`data-household-card`.

### For the plan

Item 23:
- What the search covers (section names, styles, dishes, the Dietary choices), from the event's own
  kind only, and how a result reads (for example with the section and style it sits under).
- A tap on a dish ticks it and its style, opens its section and brings it into view. A dish in a
  section behind *"Show 3 more food categories"* brings that section out; a dish in a *"Still
  deciding"* section ends still deciding, as any pick there does ([[GTC-364]]). It saves through the
  same save as any tick, and nothing new reaches the server.

  many results show.
- Where the box sits on a computer and on a phone, without covering *"Generate plan →"* or the ways
  out. `test:walkthrough-batch2`'s header finder and the rule of no new `div.rounded-lg > button`
  ([[GTC-368]]'s plan) apply.

Item 13:
- The button's words, where it sits on the board (with the other closed buttons, for Ruling 1), and
  how it opens and closes on a computer and on a phone.
- What it searches (people and items by name; households?), and what each result shows: an item,
  who's bringing it or *"Nobody yet"*; a person, what they're bringing; also a person bringing
  nothing, a child carried by a grown-up, the host herself, and someone marked *"Just attending"*.
- A tap on a person takes her to their card on the board: how (a scroll and a ring, as
  [[GTC-366]]'s pre-flight line does).
- Where the data comes from: passed into an island by the board page, or fetched when it opens from
  an existing route, as the print door does. No new API route (that is Zone 6 and needs the
  founder's approval). The board stays a server component with no hooks; a new island joins
  `test:glance-read`'s fence (22 files today), as at [[GTC-366]] and [[GTC-368]], and passes
  `test:glance-grid`'s scans (no %, no countdown, none of its banned words).
- Co-hosts see the board too: say what the search shows them.
- It only finds: nothing in it changes anything, sends anything or asks why.

Both:
- Every sentence a host reads as numbered W-lines, for the founder's ruling.
- Size: if the two together are more than one ticket carries well, say so and propose a split
  before building.
