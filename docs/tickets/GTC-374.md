### Where this comes from

Batch 7 of the seven in [[GTC-189]]'s Fourth ruling: item 14, an "invites only" option, with no meal
plan and nobody asked to bring anything. Asked 2026-10-04; his list of 2026-10-05 asks again:
*"Suss out what it would take to not have people bring things"*. The item number is the
walkthrough list's (the Cowork project doc `claude/walkthrough-2026-10-03.md`, which is not in the
repo). Waitlist relevance, noted at the sort: three of the four events the first sign-ups named
weren't family meals. Batches 1 to 6 ([[GTC-363]] to [[GTC-368]], [[GTC-373]]) and [[GTC-369]] stay
as built.

### What the code does today (Cowork, read 2026-10-09 at `4c7381b`)

Much already works for a guest with nothing to bring:
- The invitation (`askSystemVoice` in `src/lib/messages/ask-register.ts`): a recipient with no
  items, no jobs and no carried child is told *"Nothing for you to bring."* between the greeting
  and *"I'll check back if I haven't heard from you."*, and is asked whether they can make it. Two
  suites pin the line: `tests/carried-ask-composition-test.ts` and `tests/household-list-test.ts`.
- The guest page ([[GTC-174]]'s itemless case), the reminders, and the board ([[GTC-305]]; Ruling
  16: undecided is amber, a yes is settled).

What stands in the way:
- **Holding the plan.** Moment 3's *"Move on →"* holds it ([[GTC-360]]; `src/lib/moment3/hold.ts`,
  `POST /api/events/[id]/transition`, `transitionToConfirming` in `src/lib/workflow.ts`). The gate
  refuses an event with no team (`STRUCTURAL_MINIMUM_TEAMS`) or no item
  (`STRUCTURAL_MINIMUM_ITEMS`); `tests/hold-the-plan-test.ts` reads both.
- **Nowhere to choose it, and nowhere to keep it.** `EventSetup` (`prisma/schema.prisma`) holds
  Moment 2's answers and `planApprovedAt`; nothing records "no plan". Moment 2's opening
  (`src/components/plan/Moment2Opening.tsx`) has *"Let’s do this →"* and *"← Back to the people"*.
- **The pre-flight's five steps** (`PREFLIGHT_STEP_TITLES` in `src/lib/preflight/next-check.ts`):
  *"What is still loose"*, *"Dietary needs"*, *"Who Gather talks to"*, *"The message, shown"*,
  *"Ready"*. The first two are about the plan. [[GTC-366]]'s *"Go to step N: …"* line counts them.
- **The Moments strip** (`stripDoors` in `src/lib/moments/strip.ts`, [[GTC-367]]) and the entry rule
  that decides where a returning host lands (`resolveSetupStage` in `src/lib/setup/entry-stage.ts`).
- **The board's search** ([[GTC-373]]) reads *"{Name}: Nothing yet"* for a person holding nothing,
  which on an event with no plan says nothing useful.

  as not needed. It stays familiar, and if she changes her mind, tapping one starts a plan."*

### For the plan

- **The choice:** its words and place on Moment 2's opening, quieter than *"Let’s do this →"*; what
  it does when tapped (holds the event with no plan and opens the pre-flight, or another way the plan
  proposes); and anything she must confirm first.
- **Where it's kept:** one new column (on `EventSetup` or `Event`; the plan says which and why).
  That is a migration: Zone 5's steps below.
- **The hold:** an invites-only event holds with no team and no item. Only those two structural
  checks are lifted, and only for an invites-only event; every other check stays. Say how, and what
  `hold-the-plan` then pins.
- **Changing her mind, before the send:** from invites only to a plan (where she lands, and that the
  event is still held or goes back); from a plan to invites only (what happens to a plan already
  made, its items and anyone already given one; nothing is deleted without a ruling). After the
  press it is fixed.
- **The invitation:** what an invites-only guest reads, by email and by text, in place of *"Nothing
  for you to bring."* (W-lines); the line stays as it is for a guest on a planned event who simply
  holds nothing. The household list ([[GTC-356]]) on an invites-only event.
- **The pre-flight:** the plan's two checks left out or settled for her, the steps renumbered or
  not, and [[GTC-366]]'s *"Go to step N"* line kept true.
- **The strip:** *"not needed"* for Moments 2 and 3 (W-line), and a tap that starts a plan, per the
  ruling; the entry rule's answer for an invites-only event.
- **The board and its search:** what a person reads in the search when there is no plan; anything
  else on the board that assumes a plan (the unassigned door, the print door).
- **Co-hosts**, and the old dashboard (V1), which should be unaffected.
- **Size:** if it is more than one ticket carries well, say so and propose a split before building.
