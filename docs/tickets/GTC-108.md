# [[GTC-108]] — Children count shouldn't need a separate confirm — CLOSED

**Status:** Complete | **Branch:** feat/moment-one-redesign | **Commit:** 061b750

### What was found

The "Add Children" sub-form had its own "Add" and "Cancel" buttons, requiring Kate to confirm the child count separately before saving the household. Partner and guest sub-forms didn't have this — they saved with the household.

### What was changed

Removed "Add" and "Cancel" buttons from children sub-form. The number input appears when Kate clicks "Add Children" and the value is included automatically when the household saves. "× remove" dismisses the section — same pattern as partner and guest. Removed `childCountConfirmed` state.

### Known limitations

None.
