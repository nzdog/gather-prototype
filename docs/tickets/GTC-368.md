#### Item 17 — the event's name throughout, with its details a tap away

Ruling: *"The event’s name"*; and in his words: *"A clickable event name that brings up a pop up
with the rest of the details"*.

Today the event's name shows on Moment 1 (*"Who’s coming to …?"*), its summary, the plan view
(*"Here’s what I’d suggest for …"*), small above the pre-flight's heading, and on the board's top
line. It is not on Moment 2's opening screen, its questions or Moment 3, and nowhere can it be
tapped. The event holds more than its name: its dates, the kind of occasion, a description, a venue
and its times, and the guest count (`Event` in `prisma/schema.prisma`); they are changed in the old
dashboard's Edit Event.

For the plan: where the name goes on the screens that lack it; what the pop-up shows, and its words
(W-lines); whether it only shows the details or lets her change them too; how it opens and closes
on a phone; and the board, where [[GTC-192]]'s rules apply.

#### Item 5 — what each Moment does, and what done looks like, in his words

Asked in Cowork on 2026-10-05, *"Before launch or after?"*; his answer: *"Before launch
(Recommended)"* (verbatim in [[GTC-189]]'s Fourth ruling). His words for each Moment, as he pasted
them on 2026-10-05, verbatim:

```
This is for moment 1
What this does
Gets everyone who's coming out of your head and into one list. Names, how to reach them, and who's in each household.
When it's done
You won't need to remember who you've asked. I've got the list, and I'll handle the invites and chase-ups from here.

Moment 2: What's the plan?
What this does
Gets the plan out of your head and onto the page. Tell me about the event and any dietary needs, and I'll draft the full list of what's needed: food, drinks and everything else. You decide what stays.
When it's done
You won't need to worry you've forgotten something. The whole plan is in one place, it's yours, and it's ready to hand out.

Moment 3
What this does
Puts a name next to every job. You decide who's on what, and I keep track of it all.
When it's done
Every job has an owner, and you're not carrying the plan anymore. I'll ask each person and follow up, so you don't have to.

Moment 4: Is everyone sorted?
What this does
Shows you where everything stands on one screen: who's said yes, what's covered, and what's still open. I chase anyone who hasn't replied and flag anything that needs you.
When it's done
You stop wondering. Every job is confirmed, and you know your event is sorted.
```

For the plan: where each Moment's two lines sit and when they show (for example "What this does"
when she arrives, "When it's done" when she finishes). His words are used as written; any heading,
label or adaptation around them is a W-line. On the board (Moment 4), [[GTC-192]]'s Ruling 1
applies.
