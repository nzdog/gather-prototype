## What this ticket does, in plain English

On the board, "See menu" and "See assignments" used to open a box over
the page. Now each one opens in place, right where it sits, like the
board's other "tap to see more" lines ("Days to go", "What this does").
Tap it again, or tap Close, and it folds away. What it shows is the same:
the same heading, the same lists, the same words.

The "Invites & people" link stays. The board has no other way to see a
guest's own link or whether a guest has opened their invitation, so
taking the link away would lose both.

## The founder's rulings (28 Oct 2026, in Cowork)

- Part 1, read-only, written here BEFORE any edit: find every way a
  host can reach, once an event has been sent, (a) each guest's personal
  link (to copy or see it), (b) each guest's invite status (sent,
  opened, answered) and (c) the "Add a way to reach them" box, WITHOUT
  the "Invites & people" door: on the board itself, or one tap away from
  it (the Moments strip, the after-Send view, a person's own surface).
  Say what the old dashboard does when opened and whether anything else
  on the board needs it.
- Part 2: the door is hidden on every event board ONLY IF (a) and (b)
  are both reachable another way as above, with the same function. If
  either is not, SKIP the hiding, leave the door as it is, and report
  exactly what is missing. "Invites, people and nudges" on Moment 2 is
  not touched.
- Part 3: "See menu" and "See assignments" fold out IN PLACE on the
  board, with the browser's own fold-out (details and summary), their
  words, order and contents unchanged. The board stays free of React
  hooks; no state is added. "Change who's on what" and "Print the list"
  stay as they are. "See assignments" stays hidden on an invites-only
  event, as today. The fold-out opens and closes with the keyboard and
  does not change what the pop-up showed. If it cannot be done without
  a hook, or the content cannot move unchanged, SKIP part 3.

## Part 1 - what a host can reach without the door (read at 4851b3f)

The door: src/components/glance/GlanceBoard.tsx, the `data-back-room-door`
link, words `BACK_ROOM_LINK` = "Invites & people", address
`backRoomHref(id)` = `/plan/<id>` (src/lib/events/home-href.ts). That
address is the old dashboard, src/app/plan/[eventId]/page.tsx.

(a) Each guest's personal link - NOT reachable without the door.

- The only screen that shows or copies a guest's own link is the old
  dashboard: page.tsx, "Individual Invite Links Section", one row per
  link with `{link.personName}`, `{link.url}` and a button "Copy Link"
  (then "Copied!"); also "Copy Directory Link" above it, and the
  `SharedLinkSection` component.
- The board: no link to any guest's address. The strips are names and
  state words only (strip.ts).
- A person's own surface on the board: PersonSurface.tsx (a red strip)
  says in its header "No history, no message log ... Not the messages
  Gather sent them, not when anything was opened or delivered"; it holds
  no guest link. GlancePersonReading.tsx (green and amber strips): its
  one link is "the ruled way to where a grey is changed"; no guest link.
- The Moments strip above the board (glance/page.tsx, `moment4Doors`,
  src/lib/moments/strip-hrefs.ts): Moment 1 (`/setup?at=people`), the
  plan view (`/setup?at=plan`), Moment 3 (`/setup`) and the pre-flight.
  None shows a guest link. After Send the pre-flight is AfterThePress
  (src/components/preflight/AfterThePress.tsx): the don't-chase mark, the
  per-person exception and the event switch; its only links go back to
  the board.
- Two taps away, two other links do lead to the same old dashboard, but
  neither is "one tap away" and neither is about invites: the plan
  view's "Invites, people and nudges →" (Moment2PlanView.tsx line 448,
  reached by the strip's Moment 2), and the event details' "Change these
  details" (src/components/shared/EventDetails.tsx, `DETAILS_WORDS.CHANGE`,
  reached by tapping the board's top line).

(b) Each guest's invite status (sent, opened, answered) - NOT reachable
without the door.

- Only the old dashboard shows it: page.tsx's link rows carry the marks
  titled "Responded", "Opened link", "Invite sent", "Not sent yet"; and
  `InviteStatusSection` (src/components/plan/InviteStatusSection.tsx,
  counts "Not sent", "Opened", "Responded") and `InviteFunnel`, both
  read from GET /api/events/<id>/invite-status.
- The board shows each guest's STATE as a colour, with the key "Needs
  you. Tap it for what to do." / "I’m looking after them: I’ve asked,
  and I’ll follow up." / "Settled. Nothing more needed from them." / "No
  nudges. I won’t follow them up." / "Out. Not coming." An answer turns
  a strip green or grey, so "answered" can be read; but "opened" is
  never shown, by ruling (PersonSurface's header, quoted above), and an
  amber strip does not say whether the invitation was opened.

(c) The "Add a way to reach them" box - reachable ON the board, for the
guests it is for.

- PersonSurface.tsx `contactBox`: on a red strip whose reason is "cannot
  be reached" (ruling M), the box's heading is `DOOR_WORDS.UNREACHABLE.EDIT`
  = "Add a way to reach them" (src/lib/press/resend-door.ts line 110),
  with an Email box, a Mobile box where a mobile may be added, and one
  Save (GTC-387 item 69).

What the old dashboard does when opened: page.tsx calls GET
/api/events/<id>/tokens, whose route runs `ensureEventTokens` (it mints
or revokes AccessTokens for everyone on the event, so it WRITES), then
lists the invite links; on a CONFIRMING event it also reads
/invite-status. Does anything else on the board need it? No: nothing
under src/components/glance, src/lib/glance, EventDetails or MomentArc
calls /tokens or /invite-status, and the press itself runs
`ensureEventTokens` inside its own transaction (src/lib/press/press.ts
line 264), so every guest sent at the press already has a link.

## Part 2 - hiding the door: SKIPPED

The ruling's condition is not met: (a) the guests' own links and (b)
whether each guest has opened their invitation are on the old dashboard
only. GlanceBoard.tsx's door, `BACK_ROOM_LINK` and `backRoomHref` are
unchanged; "Invites, people and nudges" is unchanged. Nothing was
edited for part 2.

What is missing, exactly, for the door to go:
- a way, on the board or one tap from it, to see and copy one guest's
  own link ("Copy Link" today);
- a way to see whether one guest's invitation was sent, opened or
  answered ("Not sent yet", "Invite sent", "Opened link", "Responded"
  today). The board already says "answered" through the colours; "sent"
  and "opened" have no place on it.

## Part 3 - the fold-outs: where the change is (read before editing)

The two pop-ups are drawn by src/components/glance/PlanLists.tsx, an
island beside the board (GTC-388). NAMED HERE BEFORE ANY EDIT: this is
the one component under src/components/glance this ticket edits, because
it draws both pop-ups. GlanceBoard.tsx itself is NOT edited: it already
renders `<PlanLists eventId={glance.eventId} className="..." />` first in
the door row, hidden on an invites-only event, and keeps no hook.

Today (4851b3f), PlanLists.tsx:
- two `<button type="button" data-plan-list-door="menu|assignments"
  aria-haspopup="dialog" aria-expanded=...>` with the words "See menu"
  and "See assignments";
- while one is open, a backdrop `<div data-plan-list-backdrop=""
  className="fixed inset-0 z-50 ...">` holding `<div role="dialog"
  aria-modal="true" aria-label={heading} data-plan-list={open} ...>`:
  a heading row (`data-plan-list-heading`, "The menu" or "Who’s on
  what", and a Close button, aria-label "Close") and the body (loading
  "Getting the list…", failed "That didn’t work. Close this and try
  again.", or the list);
- closing on Close, a tap on the backdrop and Escape (a `useEffect`
  key listener).

New:
- each door is a `<details data-plan-list-fold="menu|assignments"
  name="plan-lists">` with a `<summary data-plan-list-door="...">`
  carrying the same words and the board's same door class, plus the
  marker hidden as the board's other fold-outs hide it;
- opening one (a tap, or Enter or Space on its summary: the browser's
  own) reads the items, as the pop-up did, and the other one folds
  away (only one list at a time, as with the pop-ups);
- inside, IN PLACE under its summary, `<div data-plan-list=...>` holds
  the heading row and the body EXACTLY as the dialog held them: the same
  heading, the same Close button, the same loading, failed, empty and
  list markup. The list is drawn only while it is open, so the closed
  board still gains two lines and nothing else;
- Close folds it away and puts the focus back on its summary;
- the backdrop, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, `aria-haspopup` and the
  Escape listener go: they belonged to the pop-up. The state is the
  same three pieces (`open`, `items`, `failed`) and the same counter;
  the one `useEffect` is removed, so no state and no hook is added.
