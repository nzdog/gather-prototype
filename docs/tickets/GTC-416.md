## What this ticket does, in plain English

Three things a host sees while setting up:

- Moment 1: when she adds a partner, a kid with a job or a guest, the new
  block slides into view so its name, email and phone boxes all show
  (tweak 5).
- Moment 2: four small underlined text links become proper outlined
  buttons, in the look the app already uses for "Add Partner" (tweak 6).
  Their words do not change.
- Her own card ("You") is a deeper sage everywhere it shows (tweak 15).

Nothing is stored, sent or read differently. No behaviour changes but the
scroll.

## The contrast figure (tweak 15)

Worked out with the WCAG formula from the theme's colours (sage-200
#c7cdc6; Tailwind's grey scale). Text on the card:

```
text colour          on sage-100 (before)   on sage-200 (now)
gray-900 (names)          14.10                 10.96
gray-800                  11.67                  9.07
gray-700                   8.19                  6.37
gray-600                   6.01                  4.67
sage-800                   6.58                  5.11
gray-500 (small notes)     3.84                  2.99
gray-400                   2.02                  1.57
```

The main words on the card (names, gray-900) read at 10.96 to 1, well
past the 4.5 to 1 the usual standard asks of ordinary text. But most
cards also carry small grey notes in gray-500 (for example "you — never
messaged about your own event" in the pre-flight's rows), and those drop
from 3.84 to 2.99 to 1; they were already below 4.5 on sage-100, and are
further below now. The "You" pill (white on the accent) is 3.59 to 1, as
before; the accent border against the card is 2.22 to 1. The ruling was
the constant's line only, so nothing else was changed: flagged for the
founder (a later fix would darken those notes on her card, gray-600 reads
4.67).
